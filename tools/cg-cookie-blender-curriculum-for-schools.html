CG Cookie Blender curriculum for schools

A downloadable Blender curriculum for schools with videos and rubrics.

$ LicenseComputingLocal with optional cloud or accounts

Overview

CG Cookie offers a Blender curriculum for schools that is free to download. It has lesson plans linked to CG Cookie videos, instructor tips, student assignments, and learning targets. Search results describe eight courses and 90 hours on one page and a 30-hour curriculum on another. A free teaching guide covers classroom tips, rubrics, and project ideas.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn Blender through videos and set assignments.
  • Students build 3D models and animations.
  • Students practice skills tied to careers.

For teachers

  • Lesson plans link to videos.
  • The teaching guide includes rubrics and project ideas.
  • A standards framework cross-reference is included.

For schools

  • The curriculum download is free.
  • Unit 1 can use free content, but units 2 and 3 rely on paid content.
  • Group rates start at 14 USD per month per student, and instructor accounts are free, per a search snippet.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This curriculum has no AI. It builds the skill of spatial reasoning, as students shape objects in three dimensions. It also builds sequencing through animation.

Privacy and safety

Blender runs locally. CG Cookie videos are served by the vendor, and paid units need student subscriptions on the vendor platform. The research could not read the official page, which returned an error. The materials do not say whether accounts are needed, whether the internet is required, or what student data is collected. A teacher should read the privacy terms before students sign up.

How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Model a simple object from the unit 1 lessons.
  2. Add materials and lighting to a still life.
  3. Render a scene and write about the choices.
  4. Build a character and pose it.
  5. Present a 3D portfolio piece to the class.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Model a part for a simple machine.
  2. Measure a real object and model it to scale.
  3. Test a design and revise it once.
  4. Build a small product concept.
  5. Compare two designs for the same use.
How this can be used for Career and Technical Education
  1. Plan a short animation for a small business.
  2. Build a model for a product pitch.
  3. Create a portfolio of three models.
  4. Use a rubric to review a peer's work.
  5. List the career paths that use 3D skills.
