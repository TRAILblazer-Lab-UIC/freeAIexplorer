Particle IoT boards for education

Wi-Fi and cellular IoT boards that connect student projects to the internet.

ComputingSends student data to a company

Overview

Particle makes Wi-Fi and cellular IoT development boards such as the Photon 2 and the Boron. Its education program lets eligible schools apply for hardware. The page says approved applicants can receive up to 750 USD in hardware. It suits advanced high school students who connect sensors to the internet.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build devices that send data to the internet.
  • Students write code in a real developer tool called Particle Workbench, which is based on VS Code.
  • Lane Tech High School students connected sensors to the internet with a Photon.

For teachers

  • A classroom product helps teachers manage student devices.
  • The program offers a free plan with 100K cloud data operations per month.
  • The setup is advanced. Plan time and support.

For schools

  • The hardware grant can offset the cost of boards.
  • Board prices were not confirmed.
  • The school needs reliable Wi-Fi without a captive portal for the Photon 2.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in these boards. They connect sensors and devices to the Particle Device Cloud. Students build skills in sensors, data, and logic.

Privacy and safety

Student accounts are required, and the devices connect to the Particle cloud all the time. Account identifiers, device IDs, and device data go to the vendor. Location data is shared if devices send it. The privacy addendum lists 21 subprocessors including AWS, Google analytics, Stripe, and Anthropic for AI product features. It does not address students directly and defers to a minors section that the research did not see. A teacher should review the privacy terms with IT before creating student accounts.
