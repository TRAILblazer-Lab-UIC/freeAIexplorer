Overview

AutoGo is a programmable 1:24 scale self-driving car created from the Donkey Car project. A search snippet describes it as pre-loaded with self-driving programs and shipped with a guided educational resource. The facts come from a snippet. The directory page returned 404. Students use it to learn about AI step by step.

  1. Run a pre-loaded self-driving program and describe what the car does.
