NVIDIA Jetson AI Education resources (Jetson for AI Education)

An NVIDIA hub of teaching kits and course links for Jetson edge AI computers.

AIRobotics

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

NVIDIA hosts a hub of teaching material for its Jetson edge AI computers. It links the Deep Learning Institute Edge AI and Robotics Teaching Kit with lecture slides and hands-on labs. It also links an educator certification, university courses such as Duckietown, F1TENTH, and MuSHR, and community projects. The labs run on Jetson Nano hardware that schools buy separately.

Benefits

For students

  • Students work on deep learning and computer vision labs.
  • Students explore autonomous robotics with real hardware.
  • Advanced high school students can follow a linked high school curriculum example.

For teachers

  • The teaching kit includes lecture slides and hands-on labs.
  • A Jetson AI Ambassador educator certification is linked.
  • The hub links to JetBot, Duckietown, F1TENTH, and MuSHR course material.

For schools

  • The teaching materials are free. A Jetson Nano must be bought separately.
  • The hub is an index page and most courses target university level. The K-12 fit is partial.
  • The internet is needed for setup and downloads. IT should plan on days.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI includes deep learning, computer vision, and autonomous robotics. It runs on the Jetson board in the classroom. Students work through the labs, train and run models, and use the results in their own robot projects.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which accounts students need. The slides and labs appear to run on the local Jetson device after download, and the internet is needed for setup and downloads. The research did not fetch the linked teaching kit page and did not review the linked course platforms. A teacher should check which linked courses require accounts, such as the Duckietown edX course.
