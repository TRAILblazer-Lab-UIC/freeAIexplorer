Samsung Solve for Tomorrow AI Curriculum

A free AI ethics lesson on model cards and responsible use cards.

FREEAIStays on the deviceGood for AI beginners

Overview

Samsung Solve for Tomorrow: AI Foundations is a free AI curriculum built with the Mark Cuban Foundation. Lesson 6, Ethics Check, covers model cards and responsible use cards. Students study AI bias, look at a Google model card, and make their own card. The page does not state tools needed, grade, or license.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn to read a model card.
  • Students study AI bias in decisions about people.
  • Students create a model card or responsible use card for their own project.

For teachers

  • The download includes a plan, slides, pre-reading, templates, and homework.
  • Templates are PowerPoint files.
  • A course syllabus is also available.

For schools

  • The lesson is free.
  • No IT work is needed and no account is required.
  • The grade range is not stated. Samsung's contest range is grades 6 to 12.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

No AI tool runs in this lesson. The lesson teaches AI ethics, model cards, and bias. Students read a model card and write one for their own project. They build the skill of asking what data a model used and who it may harm.

Key terms on this page

Short definitions of words used above and below. Select a term for the full glossary entry.

AI bias
AI bias is a pattern of unfair or one-sided results from an AI system.
AI ethics
AI ethics is the study of how AI should be built and used fairly and safely.
Model card
A model card is a short document that describes an AI model.

Privacy and safety

Lesson 6 is a set of downloadable documents. The page states no accounts and no tools. The research did not read the other lessons. The materials do not state whether the internet is needed. The lesson links to a Google model card, which opens on Google's site. A teacher should check that the linked card opens on school devices. A teacher should also decide which student projects will get cards.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Read a Google model card and list what it says the model can and cannot do.
  2. Write a model card for a class project.
  3. Find an example of AI bias and explain who it affects.
  4. Fill in a responsible use card for an AI tool you use.
  5. Compare two model cards and decide which is clearer.
How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Read about an AI decision that affects people and discuss who is helped or harmed.
  2. Write a responsible use card for a school AI tool.
  3. Debate who should be responsible when an AI makes a harmful choice.
  4. Interview a classmate about how AI affects their daily life.
  5. Draft community rules for using AI fairly.
