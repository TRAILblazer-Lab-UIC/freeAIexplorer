Overview

PictoBlox Junior Blocks is a free iOS app with the subtitle Scratch Coding with AI and STEM. It is a block-based coding app for beginners. The listing says it covers coding concepts, AI and machine learning topics, and extensions for robotics. It supports the Quarky and Wizbot boards.

Benefits

For students

  • Students snap blocks together to build programs.
  • Students meet AI and machine learning topics.
  • Students can connect the app to Quarky and Wizbot boards.

For teachers

  • The app installs from the App Store.
  • The App Store rates the app 4+.
  • Teachers who know Scratch will find the blocks familiar.

For schools

  • The app is free and lists no in-app purchases.
  • It needs iOS 14 or later and about 330 MB of space.
  • The app store rating was 3.0 of 5 from 2 ratings. Review it before buying devices.
