Elecfreaks TPBot with AI Lens

A micro:bit smart car with a lens that recognizes colour cards.

AIComputingRoboticsGood for AI beginners

Overview

This is the TPBot micro:bit smart car with the PlanetX AI Smart Lens added. The lens recognizes colour cards using simple image recognition. In the CSER lesson, students run a sample program that reacts to red and blue cards. They then modify it to add a third colour or new behaviours. The lesson is written for Year 3 to Year 6.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see how a machine recognizes what it looks at.
  • Students follow the PRIMM steps of Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, and Make.
  • Students program the car in MakeCode blocks.

For teachers

  • The CSER lesson Robot eyes gives a ready plan.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is simple image recognition in the AI Smart Lens. It recognizes colours and runs on the device. Students run a sample program, then change it so the car reacts to a new colour. They use the changes to learn how a machine matches input to an action.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the internet is used at setup to load the MakeCode web editor. Recognition runs on the lens module, and the lesson describes no cloud service. The research did not confirm whether the lens stores images. The ELECFREAKS store policy covers store visitors only. The materials do not say whether Chromebooks work. A teacher should test the setup on the school's own devices and check what the lens records.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Predict what the car will do when it sees a red card and then run the sample program.
  2. Investigate how the code tells red from blue.
  3. Modify the program to add the green card.
  4. Make the car perform a new behaviour for each colour.
  5. Test the lens in bright and dim light and report what changes.
