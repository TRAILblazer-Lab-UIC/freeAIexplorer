Digital Technologies Hub Note the Music

A lesson idea that links music, code, and AI with LEGO, Google AI Duet, and Scratch.

FREEAIComputingUnverifiedGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is a lesson idea for Years 3 to 4 and 5 to 6 that links music, computing, and AI. An unplugged part uses LEGO bricks to build beats and compare musical notation to code. Six levelled plugged activities range from exploring Google AI Duet to programming a two-instrument duet in Scratch. Students compare the random function used to mimic AI with how AI Duet behaves.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build beats with LEGO before they use a screen.
  • Students compare notation with code.
  • Students see how a program that uses randomness differs from AI.

For teachers

  • The lesson links to Australian Curriculum Digital Technologies and Music codes.
  • Six activities run from easy to harder.
  • Pencil Code and GarageBand are optional extras.

For schools

  • The lesson is free. LEGO bricks are needed for the unplugged part.
  • It runs in a browser, including on a Chromebook.
  • No install is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Google AI Duet makes music in response to notes a student plays. The materials do not say where it runs and it needs the internet. In Scratch, students use the random function to mimic AI. They compare the two and talk about what makes real AI different.

Privacy and safety

The page mentions no logins and lists no account needs. AI Duet and Scratch are web tools, and the research did not review their data practices. The research did not check where AI Duet sends audio or key presses. The internet is needed at all times. A teacher should check the school's Google terms and confirm that AI Duet still works. A teacher should also use headphones in a shared room.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Build a four-beat pattern with LEGO bricks and clap it.
  2. Compare a LEGO pattern with musical notation.
  3. Play a short tune on Google AI Duet and listen to the reply.
  4. Program a two-instrument duet in Scratch.
  5. Change the notes in the duet and describe the new sound.
How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Compare a block of music code with a recipe of steps.
  2. Use broadcast messages to make two Scratch sprites play together.
  3. Add a random note change and describe the effect.
  4. List one way AI Duet differs from a random note.
  5. Explain how hard coding differs from learning from examples.
