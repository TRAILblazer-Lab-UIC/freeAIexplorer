Experience AI Discovering AI single lessons

Single Experience AI lessons on data and predictions for ages 8 to 16.

FREEAIUnpluggedNo device neededGood for AI beginners

Overview

Discovering AI is a set of single lessons from Experience AI. It introduces the main ideas behind AI to learners with little or no background. One version suits ages 8 to 12 and one suits ages 13 to 16. In the 8 to 12 lesson, students describe how AI systems use data to make predictions. They also name problems that AI can help solve.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn what data and predictions mean.
  • Students follow a prediction pathway step by step.
  • Students show what they learned in an exit ticket.

For teachers

  • The download has a lesson plan, slides, and activities.
  • Each age group has its own version.
  • The lesson needs no AI background.

For schools

  • The lessons are free.
  • A free Raspberry Pi Foundation login is needed to download.
  • The 8 to 12 activities run on paper, and the materials describe no student data collection.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The lesson has no running AI program. It teaches how AI uses data to find patterns and make predictions. Students describe the prediction pathway and talk about problems AI can help solve. They build skills in reading data and thinking about what a prediction means.

Privacy and safety

The 8 to 12 lesson runs on paper and the materials describe no student data collection. Teachers need a free Raspberry Pi Foundation account to download. They answer a few questions about how they will use the resources when signing up. The lesson page does not list student tools. Student data collection is not expected. The research did not read the 13 to 16 lesson or any privacy policy. A teacher should check which activities need a device and read the Foundation privacy terms before signing up.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Describe a time that a machine made a prediction, such as a song suggestion.
  2. Collect class data on a simple question and spot a pattern in it.
  3. Trace the prediction pathway from data to a guess on a diagram.
  4. Name a problem in your school that AI might help to solve.
  5. Complete the exit ticket and explain data in your own words.
