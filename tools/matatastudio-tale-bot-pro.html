MatataStudio Tale-Bot Pro

A button programmed robot for young children that moves, draws, sings, and records.

$$ComputingRoboticsStays on the device

Overview

Tale-Bot Pro is a button-programmed robot for young children. The site says it moves, draws, sings, dances, speaks and records, and supports 10 languages with an Interactive Map. Retailer listings say it takes up to 256 commands and records up to 30 seconds of audio. The site states Pre-K and up, and retailers give ages 3 to 5.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Tale-Bot Pro has no AI. It follows the buttons that children press. Children build the skill of sequencing, which means putting steps in the right order.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed and the robot works without the internet. The robot records audio. Retailer descriptions say recordings are stored on the robot, and the research did not confirm this. The site privacy policy was not read. The materials do not say whether the robot stores other data. A teacher should decide how to handle recordings of children. A teacher should also follow the retailer warning about small parts for children under 3.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Press buttons to make the robot move forward and turn.

  3. Predict where the robot will end up before it runs.
