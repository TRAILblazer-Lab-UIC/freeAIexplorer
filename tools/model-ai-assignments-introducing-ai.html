Model AI Assignments: Introducing AI

A no-technology card activity that introduces AI to learners aged 6 and up.

FREEAIUnpluggedNo device neededGood for AI beginners

Overview

Introducing AI is a no-technology activity that uses a printed deck of example cards and a worksheet packet. It runs 15 to 20 minutes and suits ages 6 and up, K-12. Teachers can use it to start a class or a unit. Search results link it to Duri Long's AI Matters column on unplugged assignments.

Benefits

For students

  • Students talk about where they have met AI.
  • Students pick cards for the technologies they have used.
  • Students get a friendly first look at AI.

For teachers

  • The activity needs no tech knowledge.
  • It works as a class or unit opener.
  • The 2021 page notes that it is very introductory. Later lessons can add nuance.

For schools

  • The materials are free.
  • The activity needs printing, glue, and colored pencils or markers.
  • No devices or accounts are needed. The internet is used only to download the packet.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The activity has no running AI program. It gives an overview of AI through example cards. Students pick the cards that match technologies they know and talk about them. They build basic AI literacy that later lessons can deepen.

Privacy and safety

The activity runs on paper, no account is needed, and no internet is needed. The materials describe no student data collection. The page is very introductory. A teacher should plan time to correct any ideas about AI that students bring.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Pick the cards that match technologies you have used and tell the class why.
  2. Sort the cards into groups by where you meet each one, such as home, school or phone.
  3. Describe one AI example on a card to a partner.
  4. Fill in the worksheet with your own example of AI in daily life.
  5. Make a class poster of where AI shows up at home and school.
