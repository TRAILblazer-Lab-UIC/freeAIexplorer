Blue Robotics BlueSTEAM Ahead BlueROV2 curriculum

A free modular curriculum for teaching with the BlueROV2 underwater robot.

Robotics

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

BlueSTEAM Ahead is a Blue Robotics education initiative that brings marine technology into classrooms. Search summaries say the company offers a free modular curriculum for the BlueROV2 and BlueBoat. It is tied to Ocean Literacy Principles and NGSS. The curriculum is a Google Doc linked from the program page. The first focus is high school.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn ocean science through hands-on robotics.
  • Students operate and study an underwater vehicle.
  • Students connect marine ideas with engineering.

For teachers

  • The curriculum is modular. Teachers can pick parts.
  • A guide helps choose between vehicles.
  • Blue Robotics offers education packages.

For schools

  • The curriculum is free per a search snippet.
  • The BlueROV2 itself starts around 4,900 USD per a search summary.
  • IT and facility planning are likely needed for water testing.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This curriculum has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is controlling a robot with sensors. Students observe how an underwater vehicle responds to their commands.

Privacy and safety

The pages read do not describe accounts, data collection, or whether the internet is needed. Google hosts the curriculum as a Google Doc. The research did not confirm that the robots are controlled from a local computer. No student data is described. A teacher should ask the vendor about the control software and check school device rules. Water activities need adult supervision and clear safety rules.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Read an ocean literacy principle and plan a question to explore with the vehicle.
  2. Record what the ROV camera shows in a water tank.
  3. Compare temperature and depth readings at two spots.
  4. Discuss how people use robots to study the ocean.
  5. Write a short report on what you learned.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Compare the BlueROV2 and the BlueBoat for a chosen task.
  2. Pilot the ROV through a simple course in a tank.
  3. Test how thruster changes affect movement.
  4. Sketch a tool to add to the vehicle.
  5. List the safety checks before each launch.
