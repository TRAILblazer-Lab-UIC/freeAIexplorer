Autodesk AutoCAD Education Plan

A free renewable AutoCAD license for eligible students and educators.

Computing

Overview

Autodesk offers AutoCAD free to eligible students and educators through its Education plan. The license is renewable and needs an Autodesk account with education verification. Autodesk uses the third party SheerID to check enrollment. A batch note suggests grades 9 to 12.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn a professional drafting tool.
  • Students practice precise design work.
  • Students can build skills for career paths.

For teachers

  • The tool fits engineering and CTE courses.
  • Search results say accredited middle and high schools count as qualified.
  • Each user can install on up to three devices per a search snippet.

For schools

  • The education license is free and renewable.
  • IT needs to install the software on Windows or Mac.
  • Autodesk pages block automated fetches. Verify terms directly with Autodesk.

Where is the AI in this product?

AI features were not checked. Autodesk pages blocked the research. Students build skills in measurement, precision and design. They draw plans with exact lines and dimensions.

Privacy and safety

Every student and teacher needs an Autodesk account and data goes to the vendor. The materials do not say what student data is collected. The research could not fetch the Autodesk privacy statement. A third party named SheerID checks enrollment, according to a search snippet. The minimum age was not confirmed. A teacher should confirm the minimum age and the privacy terms before students sign up.
