KerbalEdu

The former school edition of Kerbal Space Program, with uncertain availability today.

ComputingUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

KerbalEdu was the education edition of Kerbal Space Program. It launched around 2014 from Squad and TeacherGaming. It sold to schools at a discount with classroom tools. Search results show TeacherGaming planned to hand it to Private Division, and a forum thread says school licenses stopped working.

Benefits

For students

  • Students could build rockets and learn about orbits.
  • Students could test designs through trial and error.
  • The game ties physics to a hands on challenge.

For teachers

  • The edition came with classroom tools.
  • The game suits orbital mechanics lessons.
  • Teachers should note the tool is rated advanced for teachers.

For schools

  • Schools bought it at a discount, but the current price was not confirmed.
  • The current availability could not be confirmed.
  • IT must install it on each machine.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Fly a rocket to orbit and describe what holds it there.
  2. Compare orbits at two heights and note the time of each.
  3. Plan a trip to a moon and write the steps.
  4. Predict a landing spot and test it.
  5. Explain how gravity shapes a flight path.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Build a rocket and test its height.
  2. Change one stage and compare results.
  3. Redesign a rocket that fails.
  4. Plan a fuel budget for a trip.
  5. Present the design choices to the class.
