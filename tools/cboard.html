Cboard

A free open source communication app with symbols and speech.

Computing

Overview

Cboard is a free open source augmentative and alternative communication app for children and adults with speech or language impairments. Users tap pictures and symbols, and the app speaks them using text to speech. It runs in browsers and in apps for iOS, Android, Windows, and Amazon devices. Boards can be customized, and registered users can save them to an account.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can communicate by tapping symbols.
  • Students hear their choices spoken aloud.
  • Students can use the app in about 45 languages.

For teachers

  • Teachers can customize boards for each student.
  • The app is simple to learn.
  • It works on a tablet, a phone, or a computer.

For schools

  • The app is free to use, and a search result lists a Pro Yearly plan at 79 USD per year.
  • It can load offline after the first visit through a service worker. One source limits this to Chrome on desktop and Android.
  • A school can self-host it by building a Docker image from the repo.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI. Cboard uses text to speech to read symbols aloud. It builds the skill of choosing and ordering symbols to make a message.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are optional, and registered users can save boards to an account on the vendor cloud. Sign up uses Google, Apple, Facebook, or email. Unregistered users cannot save changes safely. The privacy page showed no policy text, and the research did not confirm what data is collected. The site notes cookies for experience and traffic analysis. A teacher should read the privacy policy directly and decide whether to use an account for a student.

How this can be used for Special Education and Accessibility
  1. Build a board with a student's favorite words and practice using it.
  2. Add a new symbol for a classroom item.
  3. Use the board to make a request during a lesson.
  4. Make a daily schedule board.
  5. Review the board with the student and remove words that are not used.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Use symbols to build a short sentence and read it aloud.
  2. Make a board for the characters in a story.
  3. Retell a story using symbols.
  4. Choose symbols for feelings and tell about a day.
  5. Build a board with words from a spelling list.
