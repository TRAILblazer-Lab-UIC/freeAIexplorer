Pimoroni Pico Explorer Base

An add-on board that gives a Raspberry Pi Pico a screen, buttons, and motor drivers.

Computing

Overview

Pico Explorer Base is an add-on board for a Raspberry Pi Pico. It has a 1.54 inch 240x240 display, four buttons, two H-bridge motor drivers, a piezo speaker, and a mini breadboard. It has two Breakout Garden I2C sockets. The Pico is not included and needs male headers.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can build projects with a screen, buttons, and motors.
  • Students use the mini breadboard for their own circuits.
  • Students can program in MicroPython or C/C++.

For teachers

  • The board is advanced and needs teacher skill.
  • The Pico needs soldered headers.
  • A MagPi review notes the only power option is 3V3.

For schools

  • A MagPi review listed the board at about 26 USD more than 5 years ago. The current shop page showed no price.
  • LEDs and jumper wires are not included.
  • The board needs only a computer with USB.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI for this board. It runs code that students write. They build skills in sensors, logic, and sequencing.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed and the board works with no internet. The board has no network hardware itself. The research did not examine a Pico W, which adds wireless. Student programs stay on the board and the computer. A teacher should supervise the soldering step and check the board for loose parts.
