AI4K12 Activity Resource Guides

Free teacher guides from AI4K12 for demos on speech, faces, chatbots, and images.

FREEAILocal with optional cloud or accountsGood for AI beginners

Overview

The AI4K12 Activity Resource Guides are teacher guides for teaching AI. Guides include SpeechDemo, Waveforms and Spectrograms, intelligent assistant guides, Chatbot with BERT, Markov Chain Demo, DiffusionDemo, and FaceDemo Part 1. Each guide is tagged by grade band and Big Idea. The guides are royalty-free under a Creative Commons license.

Benefits

For students

  • Students try real AI demos in the browser.
  • Students work with speech, faces, text, and images.
  • Students build Scratch assistant and chatbot projects in some guides.

For teachers

  • Each guide lists a grade band of 3-5, 6-8, or 9-12.
  • Guides are tagged by AI4K12 Big Idea.
  • A teacher picks a guide, downloads the PDF, and opens the linked demo.

For schools

  • The guides are free and the license is royalty-free.
  • Students need a browser, and Chromebooks are listed.
  • Scratch project files are hosted on Google Drive.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The guides cover speech recognition, face detection, chatbots, text-to-image diffusion, and Markov chains. Some demos run in the browser and some use an outside vendor's cloud. Students try each demo, observe the output, and then test its limits. The guides help students see how an AI system senses and responds.

Privacy and safety

The data flow is mixed. SpeechDemo uses Google Speech Recognition. Audio may go to Google. The Scratch variants run on outside sites such as Cognimates and ML for Kids. The research did not review the other demos. The materials do not say whether accounts are needed. A teacher should test each demo before class and note which ones use a camera, a microphone, or an outside server.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Use SpeechDemo to record a phrase and study its waveform and spectrogram.
  2. Try FaceDemo and discuss how a computer finds a face in an image.
  3. Chat with a Scratch based chatbot and list the rules it follows.
  4. Run the Markov Chain Demo and predict the next word before the demo shows it.
  5. Use DiffusionDemo to see how prompts shape an image and describe the results.
