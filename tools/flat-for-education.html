Flat for Education

A cloud music notation platform where students write and share scores together.

Computing

Overview

Flat for Education is a cloud-based music notation and composition platform for K-12 classes. Students write scores in a browser and can edit the same score together. Teachers comment on notes and watch live. It includes auto-graded theory worksheets, sight-reading, and performance assessments.

Benefits

For students

  • Students write real notation in a browser.
  • Students can work on one score together.
  • Students export their work as PDF, MIDI, MusicXML, or MP3.

For teachers

  • Teachers comment on notes and watch live.
  • Theory worksheets are graded automatically.
  • Lock templates limit the toolset for an assignment.

For schools

  • It connects to Google Classroom, Canvas, Teams, Schoology, and Moodle.
  • A free tier and paid plans exist. A search snippet showed about 6 USD per student per year for a UK teacher plan.
  • Student scores are saved in the cloud.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Flat has no AI. No AI features were found on the official page. The tool builds pattern and sequencing skills. Students place notes in order and hear the result.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are required and scores are saved in the vendor cloud. The data listed is accounts, rosters, scores, and usage data. The FAQ says Flat is FERPA and COPPA compliant and never sells student data, which are vendor claims. The general privacy policy says education users are covered by a separate policy. The research did not read that policy. The general policy names service providers and possible Google Analytics, and says data may be stored outside the user's country. A teacher should read the education privacy policy and decide how to sync rosters.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Use a lock template to write a four bar melody.
  2. Share a score with a partner and write a harmony.
  3. Complete an auto-graded theory worksheet.
  4. Record a sight-reading attempt and review it.
  5. Export a finished piece and play it for the class.
