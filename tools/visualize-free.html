Visualize Free

A free cloud charting app where users upload spreadsheets and build charts.

FREEComputingSends student data to a company

Overview

Visualize Free is a free, cloud hosted charting and analytics app from InetSoft. Users upload a spreadsheet and build charts with a drag and drop designer. Charts are private by default and can be shared by URL. The official page markets it to business users and web developers, with no stated K-12 focus.

Benefits

For students

  • Students turn spreadsheet data into charts without code.
  • Students can try different chart types quickly.
  • Students can share a chart by link.

For teachers

  • The designer uses drag and drop.
  • A Teachers First listing suggests grades 6 to 12.
  • Charts are private until a student shares the link.

For schools

  • The tool is free. A 2021 listing mentioned a paid upgrade at 70 USD per month, which may be outdated.
  • It runs in a browser and needs no install.
  • Data goes to the vendor cloud, which raises a privacy concern.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI. The tool draws charts from the columns students choose. Students build data skills as they pick which chart shows a pattern best.

Privacy and safety

Uploaded spreadsheets go to the InetSoft cloud, and the internet is always needed. A search snippet says the evaluation page asks users to register a free account before uploading their own data. The research did not read the privacy policy, and classroom suitability for students under 13 is unconfirmed. A teacher should upload only data with no personal information and review the terms first.

How this can be used for Math
  1. Upload a class survey spreadsheet and build a bar chart.
  2. Compare a bar chart and a line chart of the same data.
  3. Build a chart of weather data and describe the trend.
  4. Change the chart axes and explain how it changes the message.
  5. Share a chart with a partner and ask them to interpret it.
