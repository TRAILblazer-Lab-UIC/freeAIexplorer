Arm Robotics and Internet of Things course (Bit:Bot)

A 24 lesson Arm course on building an autonomous micro:bit car for a smart city.

FREEAIRoboticsStays on the device

Overview

This Arm course has 24 lessons. Learners create an autonomous car that interacts with a smart city. It starts with a micro:bit and Bit:bot robot, then moves to a race car and a modular smart track. Later lessons cover smart traffic lights, drones, collision avoidance, and AI and machine learning. Arm lists the program for ages 11 to 16.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build and program a car step by step.
  • Students try light following and collision avoidance.
  • Students finish with lessons on AI and machine learning.

For teachers

  • The course is a free download.
  • Lessons 1 to 6 start with the basics.
  • Materials can be adapted through GitHub.

For schools

  • The course costs nothing. Bit:bot and micro:bit hardware cost extra and no price is given.
  • Arm's UK discount through Rapid Online is UK only.
  • The course runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, and in a browser.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Follow Lesson 1 to learn what a micro:bit is and run a first program.
  2. Code a light follower and test it with a flashlight.
  3. Program the smart traffic light and explain its logic.
  4. Add collision avoidance and test it with obstacles.
  5. Work through the machine learning lesson and describe what a model learns.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Build the Bit:bot and test its motors.
  2. Assemble the modular smart track and test the car on it.
  3. Tune the light follower sensors for the room lighting.
  4. Design a city layout with traffic lights.
  5. Compare two obstacle layouts and record how the car responds.
