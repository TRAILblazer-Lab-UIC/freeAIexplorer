Google and Adobe AI in the Classroom courses

Free teacher courses on AI literacy from Google and Adobe.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

These are free online courses for teachers on AI literacy. The Google AI Educator Series has K-12 sessions of 10 to 15 minutes and a badge. Adobe offers Generative AI in the Classroom with Adobe Express. It gives a digital badge and a certificate for 30 minutes of learning. These are teacher courses and not student lessons.

Benefits

For students

  • Students gain from a teacher who understands AI tools.
  • Students may see clearer classroom rules for AI use.
  • Students can try Adobe Express with a teacher who has completed the course.

For teachers

  • The sessions are short and fit a busy week.
  • Teachers earn a badge, and the Adobe course gives a certificate.
  • Teachers learn prompts for Adobe Express generative AI.

For schools

  • Both courses are free.
  • The courses run in a browser and list Chromebook as a platform.
  • Staff can learn together and build a shared plan for AI use.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The courses teach AI literacy and generative AI. The tools run in the vendors' cloud. Teachers write prompts in Adobe Express and see how the tool responds. The details here come from search snippets. The cited article could not be fetched.

Key terms on this page

AI literacy
AI literacy is the knowledge and skill to understand, use, and judge AI.
Chromebook
A Chromebook is a laptop that runs ChromeOS, an operating system from Google.
Generative AI
Generative AI is AI that makes new content.
Prompt
A prompt is the request or question you give an AI tool.

Privacy and safety

Teachers need a Google or Adobe account, and the internet is always needed. Gemini and Adobe Express are cloud tools. The research did not read either vendor's privacy policy, and the materials do not say what data the courses collect. A teacher should check the vendor privacy terms and use a work account. Students should not use the Adobe tools until the school approves them.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Ask students to define generative AI after the teacher finishes the course.
  2. Run a class discussion on three rules for using AI in school.
  3. Have students compare an AI answer with a trusted source.
  4. Build a class poster of AI terms learned from the Google sessions.
  5. Ask students to write one question they still have about AI.
How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Use Adobe Express to make a poster from a written prompt.
  2. Compare two prompts that differ by one word and describe the images.
  3. Edit a generated image by hand and explain the changes.
  4. Write a label that tells viewers an image used generative AI.
  5. Discuss what makes an image feel original.
