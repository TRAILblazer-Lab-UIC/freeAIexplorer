AI Foundations (ISTE and IBM)

A free 15-hour online course on AI basics and ethics for high school students.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

AI Foundations is a free online course for high school students from ISTE and IBM. It takes about 15 hours and needs no programming. Topics include the basics of AI, design thinking with an AI Design Challenge, career pathways, and AI ethics. Students earn a digital badge by scoring 80 percent or higher on the final assessment.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn how machine learning, neural networks, and recommender systems work.
  • Students apply AI to a real-world problem in the AI Design Challenge.
  • Students earn a digital badge from IBM and ISTE.

For teachers

  • The course is self-paced and needs no programming skill.
  • Teachers can sign up as educators on the same free platform.
  • Organizations can register students in bulk through a form.

For schools

  • The course is free per the AI4K12 listing.
  • Students use a browser and need no special devices.
  • A teacher can handle the setup, and the listing shows no IT install.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This is a course about AI and does not run an AI tool for students. The course runs on IBM's cloud and students work through it in a browser. Students learn about machine learning, recommender systems, neural networks, and AI ethics. The course builds the skill of explaining how AI systems learn from data.

Privacy and safety

Students need a free IBM SkillsBuild account and the course runs on IBM's cloud. The high school program is for ages 13 to 18, according to the FAQ. The FAQ does not mention parental consent or student privacy protections. A search snippet says IBM may share personal information with subsidiaries and third parties worldwide. The research did not read the IBM privacy policy. A teacher should read that policy and the school administrator terms before students register.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Explain in plain words how a machine learning model learns from data.
  2. Compare how a neural network and a recommender system make their guesses.
  3. Pick a recommender on a favorite app and sketch how it might work.
  4. Debate one AI ethics issue from the course and share a class position.
  5. Draft a one-page plan that applies AI to a school problem.
How this can be used for Career and Technical Education
  1. Research an AI career path from the course and write a short job profile.
  2. Complete the AI Design Challenge for a problem in a local business.
  3. Interview a worker about how AI changes their job and share the findings.
  4. Present an AI design idea to the class as a short pitch.
  5. Add the digital badge to a student portfolio and write what it shows.
