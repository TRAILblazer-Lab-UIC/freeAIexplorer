NYC K-5 integrated CS tools by subject

NYC subject pages that list K-5 computer science units for science and social studies.

Computing

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

These subject pages on the NYC DOE CS4All K-5 site list integrated computer science units. Each unit has a grade band and a plugged or unplugged label. The science page lists Beebot Resources, Making Sense of Sensors, and Surf's Up, Waves All Day. The social studies page lists Beebot Resources and Computing Through Time, a 21 lesson unit.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn computer science inside science and social studies.
  • Students use unplugged activities and Bee-Bot robots.
  • Students sequence steps with mats and templates.

For teachers

  • The pages are a catalog of ready units.
  • Units list grade bands and plugged or unplugged labels.
  • Materials are in Google Drive folders and Google Slides.

For schools

  • The materials are free.
  • Most units are unplugged.
  • The Beebot resources need Bee-Bot robots.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

These pages have no AI content. The units build sequencing, which is putting steps in order. Students also practice working with sensors and data in simple ways.

Privacy and safety

The pages mention no student accounts. The materials are hosted on Google Drive and Google Slides, which teachers open from the web. The research did not open the individual units. The materials do not say whether the internet is needed, and the need can differ by unit. The research found no student data collection. A teacher should check that Google Drive files open on school devices and review each unit's tools first.

Ideas by subject

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Use a Beebot mat to program a path with a set of steps.
  2. Write unplugged steps for a partner to follow.
  3. Find a mistake in a sequence and fix it.
  4. Sort the steps of a daily routine into order.
  5. Explain how a robot follows instructions.
How this can be used for Science
  1. Program a Bee-Bot to move through a life cycle mat.
  2. Complete the Making Sense of Sensors unplugged mini unit.
  3. Use the Surf's Up unit to study how waves move.
  4. Act out a wave and describe its motion.
  5. Record what a sensor would notice in the classroom.
How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Work through a lesson of Computing Through Time and place events on a timeline.
  2. Program a Bee-Bot to move between landmarks on a community map.
  3. Write steps for a historical journey.
  4. Compare an old way and a new way to do a task.
  5. Present a timeline and explain the order.
