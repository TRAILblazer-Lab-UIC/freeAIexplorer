Code.org Digital Trails unplugged lesson

A 40-minute unplugged lesson on digital footprints and what is safe to share online.

Unplugged

Overview

Digital Trails is Unit 2 Lesson 1 of CS Fundamentals Course B 2020 and takes about 40 minutes. It was created by Common Sense Education. Students learn that sharing online leaves a digital footprint. In groups of four they act as detectives who follow the digital trail squares of two fictional animals, Mervin the Mouse and Ellie the Elephant.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn that online actions leave a trail.
  • Students sort information into what is OK and not OK to share.
  • Students work in small groups as detectives.

For teachers

  • The lesson has a clear Watch, Play, Review, and Wrap Up structure.
  • It includes a handout, slides, and a video.
  • The page labels the standard as CSTA 1A.

For schools

  • The lesson is free.
  • It is mostly paper based.
  • No IT help is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This lesson has no AI. It teaches about the digital footprint people leave online. Students practice data thinking as they sort information.

Privacy and safety

The activity uses cut out squares and a printed handout, and it collects no student data. The page links Google Drive and Google Docs handouts that teachers copy before sharing. The materials do not state whether an account is needed. The internet is used to open the slides and video. A teacher should preview the video and adjust the examples to fit the class.

How this can be used for Library and Media Literacy
  1. Follow the digital trail squares of Mervin the Mouse and list what you learn.
  2. Compare the trail of Ellie the Elephant with the trail of Mervin.
  3. Sort information cards into OK and not OK to share.
  4. Explain to a partner why one card is not safe to share.
  5. Make a classroom poster of the rules for staying safe online.
