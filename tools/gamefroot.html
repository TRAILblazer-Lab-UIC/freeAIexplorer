Gamefroot

A browser 2D game maker that uses block coding.

Computing

Overview

Gamefroot is a browser-based 2D game maker that uses a Scratch-like block coding approach. Students build and publish games. Teachers can manage student accounts, attach courses, and review progress according to a third-party listing. The company is based in Wellington, New Zealand. The official page returned only a title. Treat all details as unverified.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build 2D games from blocks.
  • Students publish their games.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not say whether Gamefroot has AI. The materials here do not describe any. The tool builds logic and sequencing skills through block coding.

Privacy and safety

Student accounts are required and work is stored with the vendor. The research found no Gamefroot privacy policy and could not read the official site. The materials do not say what student data is collected or how students under 13 are handled. Games can be published and student projects may be public, but this was not confirmed. A teacher should confirm the privacy terms and what publishing shares before students use it.
