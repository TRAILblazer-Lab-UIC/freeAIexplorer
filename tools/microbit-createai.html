micro:bit CreateAI

A free web tool where students train a motion model that runs on a micro:bit.

AIComputingGood for AI beginners

Overview

micro:bit CreateAI is a free web tool for training a machine learning model on movement data from a micro:bit. Students record short samples of actions such as clapping or waving. They train and test the model, open it in MakeCode, and download it to a micro:bit V2. The model then runs on the board.

Benefits

For students

  • Students record their own movement data.
  • Students train, test, and run a model on a real device.
  • Students use blocks in MakeCode with the model.

For teachers

  • The micro:bit Foundation provides a user guide and starter projects.
  • A seven lesson unit covers AI literacy and data literacy for ages 9 to 12.
  • Setup takes about an hour.

For schools

  • The tool is free and open source.
  • A micro:bit V2 is needed to run models. A V1 can only collect data.
  • Phones are not supported, and the tool is not supported in micro:bit classroom.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a motion classifier. Students train it in the browser and then it runs on the micro:bit. Students record actions, train a model, and then use it in a MakeCode program. They can add more samples to improve it.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the internet is used at setup. The Foundation privacy policy says CreateAI movement data are not sent to or stored by the Foundation. Projects are stored in the browser or app on that device. The tool embeds Microsoft MakeCode, which has its own privacy statement. CreateAI apps use Google Analytics on an opt in basis. The policy covers children under 13 by asking for parental consent. It does not mention COPPA. A teacher should remind students to save HEX files, and should check that classroom computers do not clear browser data between lessons.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Record clapping and waving samples and train a model to tell them apart.
  2. Test the model with a partner and record how often it is right.
  3. Add more samples and see how the accuracy changes.
  4. Use the model in MakeCode to show an icon for each action.
  5. Complete a lesson from the seven lesson unit on AI and data literacy.
