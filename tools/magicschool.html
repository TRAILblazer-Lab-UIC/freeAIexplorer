MagicSchool

A cloud AI platform with many teacher tools and teacher-led student activities.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

MagicSchool is a cloud AI platform for schools with 80+ teacher tools and 50+ student tools. Teacher tools include lesson plans, rubrics, quizzes, writing feedback, report card comments, an IEP generator, and an AI tutor. Student tools run as teacher-led activities with safer settings. It integrates with Google Classroom, Canvas, Clever, ClassLink, Schoology, and Microsoft.

Benefits

For students

  • Students use tools that a teacher sets up and watches.
  • Student tools run with safer settings.
  • Students get feedback on writing and practice help.

For teachers

  • Teachers can draft lesson plans, rubrics, and quizzes quickly.
  • The IEP generator and differentiation tools support special education work.
  • A free tier covers the teacher tools.

For schools

  • Plus costs $8.33 per user per month billed annually or $12.99 monthly. Enterprise for districts is custom priced.
  • Student tools need a paid or district plan and a school agreement.
  • Districts can set up SSO and rostering, with dashboards and custom data privacy agreements.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a set of text generation tools that run on the vendor's cloud. Teachers use them for lesson plans, feedback, and an AI tutor. Students use teacher-led activities with safer settings. Teachers review and edit the results to extend their own work.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are required and the internet is always needed. Student access runs through a school agreement, and students under 18 may use it only through a customer agreement. Data goes to the vendor cloud and to AI vendors listed in a subprocessor list, which the research did not read. With Google Classroom connected, it can read course data, submission metadata, and submission file contents. The policy says personal information and student data are not used to train AI models, and data sent through AI APIs is deleted within 30 days. Common Sense rated the category of AI teacher assistants as moderate risk and flagged IEP and behavior plan generators as high risk for bias. The research did not read the full Common Sense rating. A teacher should follow the rule against entering student identifying information and should review the school's agreement.

How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Receive writing feedback from a teacher-led tool and revise a paragraph.
  2. Use a rubric the class helped design to score a sample essay.
  3. Practice summarizing a reading with a tutor tool and compare it with a classmate.
  4. Rewrite a text for a different audience and discuss what changed.
  5. Review AI feedback and decide which comments to accept.
How this can be used for Special Education and Accessibility
  1. Ask for a reading passage at a simpler level and compare it with the original.
  2. Use a tool to break an assignment into short steps.
  3. Practice a skill with a tutor and note which hints help.
  4. Work with a teacher to set one goal and track progress.
  5. Choose a support from a short list and explain why it helps.
