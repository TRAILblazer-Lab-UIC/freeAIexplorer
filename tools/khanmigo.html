Khanmigo

An AI tutor and teaching assistant from the nonprofit Khan Academy.

AI

Overview

Khanmigo is an AI tutor and teaching assistant from the nonprofit Khan Academy. It is described as guiding learners toward answers and not handing them over directly. Teacher tools help with rubrics, lesson planning, and summaries of student work. Subjects named include math, science, coding, history, and humanities.

Benefits

For students

  • Students get a tutor that asks guiding questions.
  • Students can get help in math, science, coding, and humanities.
  • The vendor lists grades from elementary school through college.

For teachers

  • Teachers sign up free for the teacher tools.
  • Tools help with rubrics, lesson plans, and summaries of student work.
  • Teachers can use it to save planning time.

For schools

  • Student access comes through a district partnership or a parent subscription.
  • A parent subscription can cover up to 10 children.
  • The district agreement describes the district service as a premium subscription. IT should review it.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a language model tutor and planning assistant. It runs in the vendor's cloud. A signed district agreement says Khanmigo uses third-party large language models, and the provider is not named. Students chat with the tutor and use the hints to work out answers themselves.

Privacy and safety

Students need accounts, and the internet is always needed. A signed district agreement says the vendor collects account data, student work data, and use data that includes Khanmigo activity and chat content. It says Khanmigo uses third party large language models and does not name the provider. Learners are told not to include personal data in their inputs. Common Sense rates it low risk and raises concerns about the Historical Figures activity and students not fact checking. The public privacy policy did not load, and retention and use of chats to improve models were not read. A teacher should review the district agreement and the Common Sense assessment with IT.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Math
  1. Work a problem with Khanmigo and write down each hint that helped.
  2. Ask the tutor to check your steps on a practice set.
  3. Explain a math idea to the tutor in your own words and read its response.
  4. Create your own word problem and ask the tutor to critique it.
  5. Compare the tutor's method with your class method.
How this can be used for Science
  1. Ask the tutor questions about a lab and compare its answers with your notes.
  2. Plan an experiment with the tutor's help and list the variables.
  3. Ask the tutor to quiz you on a science vocabulary list.
  4. Check one claim from the tutor against a textbook.
  5. Write a summary of a topic after a chat with the tutor.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Share a draft paragraph and read the tutor's questions.
  2. Discuss a book with the tutor and note a new idea.
  3. Ask for feedback on a thesis statement and decide what to change.
  4. Practice a debate argument with the tutor.
  5. Write a reflection on how the tutor helped your writing.
How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Ask the tutor to explain a coding error and fix it yourself.
  2. Write a short program and ask the tutor for hints only.
  3. Ask the tutor to quiz you on loops and variables.
  4. Describe your program to the tutor and read its questions.
  5. Compare two ways to solve the same coding task.
