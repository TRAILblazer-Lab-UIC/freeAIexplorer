Digital Technologies Hub Your Digital Footprint Unplugged

A listing for an unplugged Code.org lesson on digital footprints for Foundation to Year 2.

Unplugged

Overview

This is a Digital Technologies Hub listing for a Code.org lesson for Foundation to Year 2. It covers vocabulary and activities about the digital trail people leave online. It also introduces safety techniques that reduce the risks of that trail. The listing links out to Code.org Course 2 and offers no downloads itself.

Benefits

For students

  • Young students learn words such as digital footprint.
  • Students learn about the trail they leave online.
  • Students learn safety techniques for young learners.

For teachers

  • The lesson maps to three Australian Curriculum codes on personal data and safe use of digital tools.
  • A search snippet says the Code.org version has a Follow the Digital Trail activity.
  • Common Sense Education has similar lessons for ages 7 to 8.

For schools

  • The lesson is free, according to the Hub listing. The page does not state a price.
  • It is mostly paper based.
  • No IT help is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This lesson has no AI. It teaches about digital trails and online safety. Students practice thinking about data as they sort what is safe to share.

Privacy and safety

The lesson is unplugged, and the Hub page describes no data collection. The Hub page links out to Code.org, and the research did not read that lesson. The materials do not state whether an account is needed on Code.org. The internet is used to open the lesson. A teacher should check what the Code.org lesson requires before class.

How this can be used for Library and Media Literacy
  1. Learn the words digital footprint and digital trail.
  2. Talk about what a footprint in sand tells you.
  3. Match each online action to a footprint on the trail.
  4. Sort pieces of information into safe and unsafe to share.
  5. Make a poster that shows one safety tip for being online.
