Overview

The rae is a small desktop robot from Luxonis built on its OAK vision technology. Launch coverage describes it as an open-source edge AI educational robot. The official page and a Hackster article could not be read. Most details come from search snippets. Treat all details as unverified.

Benefits

For students

  • Students may explore computer vision on a small robot.
  • Students may prototype ideas with the DepthAI tools.
  • Students see edge AI on a device they can hold.

For teachers

  • A search snippet of the Luxonis docs calls it a robot for evaluating the DepthAI ecosystem and rapid prototyping.
  • Launch coverage calls it open source.
  • The vendor does not say how teachers set it up.

For schools

  • A shop snippet shows 499 USD with VAT included and out of stock. Kickstarter pricing in 2022 started at 299 USD.
  • The vendor does not say what computers or accounts it needs.
