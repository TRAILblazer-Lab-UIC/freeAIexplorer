GNU Octave

A free language for numerical computing with plotting and linear algebra tools.

FREEComputingStays on the device

Overview

GNU Octave is a high-level language for numerical computation. It has a command-line interface and a batch mode. It covers linear algebra, nonlinear equations, integration, polynomials, and differential equations. Its language is mostly compatible with MATLAB. It suits advanced high school students.

Benefits

For students

  • Students solve math problems by writing short commands.
  • Students plot results and see the math as graphs.
  • Students gain skills that carry to MATLAB.

For teachers

  • A University of Minnesota open textbook introduces Octave for linear algebra and calculus students.
  • One tool covers many math topics.
  • It suits teachers comfortable with a command line.

For schools

  • The software is free under the GPL.
  • It runs offline after installation on Windows, Mac, and Linux.
  • IT will likely need to install it.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

GNU Octave has no AI. It builds computational thinking skills. Students turn a math problem into steps a computer can run. It runs on the local computer.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed and the internet is used only for setup. Octave runs locally and student files stay on the device. The research did not read a privacy policy. The tool has a steep learning curve and works from a command line. A teacher should try a lesson first and prepare some starter commands. A search found a blog that used it for high school algebra, but no formal school use was found.
