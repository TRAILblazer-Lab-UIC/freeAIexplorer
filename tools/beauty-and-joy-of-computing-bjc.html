Beauty and Joy of Computing (BJC)

A full-year AP Computer Science Principles course taught with the Snap! block language.

FREEAIComputingLocal with optional cloud or accounts

Overview

Beauty and Joy of Computing, or BJC, is an AP Computer Science Principles curriculum taught with the Snap! block language. The site offers labs, a teacher guide, lecture videos, slides on social implications, and summer professional development. The College Board endorses it as an AP CSP provider. It is written for grades 9 to 12.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn real programming ideas in a block language.
  • Snap! connects to robots and Arduino for hands-on projects.
  • Students study the social impact of computing along with the code.

For teachers

  • The labs and teacher guide cover a full-year course.
  • Summer workshops give teachers training.
  • The course aligns with AP CS Principles.

For schools

  • The course is free and the site content uses a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
  • It runs in a browser, including on a Chromebook.
  • Setup takes minutes and needs no IT help. Snap! source can be downloaded for offline use.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The course is mostly general computing. Its AI content is limited to lecture videos, and the course has no AI tools of its own. Students build skills in data and algorithms that support later AI learning. Snap! runs in the browser.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are optional and the internet is used at setup. Snap! can run offline from a downloaded copy. Cloud save is optional and project files go to the cloud only if a student saves them there. The resources page does not describe student accounts. The research did not check Snap! cloud privacy. A teacher should decide whether students save to the cloud or to their own computers. A teacher should also check the Snap! cloud terms before students sign in.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Build a first Snap! project from a BJC lab and explain each block.
  2. Create a game that uses a list and a loop.
  3. Watch an AI lecture video and write three questions about it.
  4. Write a short reflection on a social impact topic from the slides.
  5. Connect Snap! to a robot or an Arduino and make it respond to a block program.
