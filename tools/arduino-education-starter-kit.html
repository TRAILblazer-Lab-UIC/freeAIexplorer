Arduino Education Starter Kit

A classroom kit for up to 8 students with 4 Arduino UNO boards and guided lessons.

Computing

Overview

The Arduino Education Starter Kit is a classroom set for up to 8 students. It has 4 Arduino UNO R3 boards, 4 multimeters, 4 breadboards, batteries, wires, sensors and actuators in a reusable box. Online content has 9 guided lessons and 2 open ended projects, for up to 25 hours of learning. The page headline says K-12 while a student kit listing says ages 11+.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build circuits on a breadboard.
  • Students program real boards.
  • Students measure with a multimeter.

For teachers

  • The kit includes 9 guided lessons and 2 open projects.
  • An activation code under the lid unlocks the online content.
  • The kit was a finalist in the BETT Awards 2022.

For schools

  • The kit page lists no price.
  • A Classroom Pack of six kits was 401.38 EUR with VAT on the store page.
  • IT needs to install software, and a teacher needs an Arduino account to activate the kit.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The kit has no AI. Students build skills in logic, sensors and sequencing. They write code that reads a sensor and controls an output.

Privacy and safety

An Arduino account is required to activate the kit, and the online content lives with the vendor. The materials do not say what student data is collected. The Arduino policy collects account details and device and browsing data. It lists sharing with processors, Qualcomm Group companies, and advertising partners. Accounts under 14 need parental consent. A teacher should check the account rules and the age guidance before anyone signs up.

  2. Compare readings from a sensor in two places.
  3. Graph sensor readings over time.
