Fundacion Sadosky Program.AR curriculum sequences

A free Spanish book about how Argentina teaches computer science in schools.

ComputingUnplugged

Overview

This PDF is a 2023 book published by CAF and Fundacion Sadosky. It describes the Program.AR initiative that has pushed computer science into Argentine schools since 2013. The book covers the Pilas Bloques block-programming app, which has over 140 challenges and is said to work offline. It is a report about the initiative and does not hold lesson sequences.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can use the Pilas Bloques app, which the book describes as free and usable offline.
  • Students solve more than 140 block-programming challenges.
  • Spanish-speaking students can work in their own language.

For teachers

  • The book describes the Program.AR teaching manuals and teacher training.
  • It gives background on how a national program is built.
  • Better lesson material may sit in the Program.AR manuals.

For schools

  • The book is free and open access under a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license.
  • Pilas Bloques can be installed separately.
  • School leaders can use the book to plan a computer science program.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This book has no AI. It builds understanding of how schools teach computer science. Students who use Pilas Bloques practice sequencing and logic with blocks.

Privacy and safety

The book is a PDF, and the internet is used to download it. No account is needed. The book says Pilas Bloques is free and works without a connection, and the research did not test that. The book gives no detail on student data and the research found no privacy policy. A teacher should check the license terms before copying pages. A teacher should also check how Pilas Bloques saves work before students use it.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Use Pilas Bloques to solve a first set of block-programming challenges.
  2. Compare two solutions to the same challenge and choose the shorter one.
  3. Explain a block program to a partner in plain Spanish or English.
  4. Read a short section of the book and list the ideas that the program teaches.
  5. Design a challenge of your own for a classmate.
