Where is the AI in this product?

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Lay out a card sequence that moves a classmate across a floor grid.
  2. Predict where a sequence ends and then run it to check the prediction.
  3. Fix a card sequence that sends the walker to the wrong square.
  4. Use colored sticky notes to mark squares that must be visited.
  5. Write a short story where each card is an action by a character, and act it out.
