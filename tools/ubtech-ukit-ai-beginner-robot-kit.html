UBTECH uKit AI Beginner Robot Kit

A building kit with a voice-enabled control box and AI blocks for younger students.

Overview

The uKit AI Beginner is a building kit from UBTECH. Its control box has dual microphones, a speaker, and a Wi-Fi module. Retail listings say it has over 350 pieces, four servos, and 13 robot models. Students in grades 3 to 8 build a model and then use AI blocks for speech recognition and text to speech.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build a robot from a kit with many model choices.
  • Students program with blocks.
  • Students make the robot listen and speak.

For teachers

  • Blocks keep programming simple for beginners.
  • The kit offers 13 models. A teacher can plan several builds.
  • No independent review was found. A teacher should test the kit first.

For schools

  • School suppliers list the kit at 399.95 and 459.95 dollars.
  • Listings give two grade ranges, grades 3 to 8 and grades 3 to 5.
  • The vendor does not say whether the internet is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor lists AI blocks for speech recognition, text to speech, and natural language processing. The vendor does not say where these run, and the control box has a Wi-Fi module. Students drag blocks to make the robot hear a phrase and answer. They then change the blocks to extend what it says.
