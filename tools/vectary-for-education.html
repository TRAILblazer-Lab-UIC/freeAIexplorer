Vectary for Education

A browser 3D design and AR tool with a shared class workspace.

Overview

Vectary is a browser based 3D design and AR tool. Its education program describes a shared class workspace where students see each other's projects. The educator owns all workspaces and manages access. The education page returned an error when checked. These facts come from a search snippet.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build 3D models in a browser.
  • Students can view AR projects on a phone.
  • Students can see classmates' work in the shared workspace.

For teachers

  • The educator owns and manages every workspace.
  • Sample exercises for students are available.
  • Suggested uses include geometry animations.

For schools

  • Education access is described as free by third-party posts that may be dated.
  • Current terms are not confirmed.
  • The privacy policy says Vectary does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. A review is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Vectary has AI features, but they were not verified for education accounts. The AI runs in the vendor's cloud. The policy says users under 18 may not use the AI features. A teacher should confirm which AI features students can reach. Students would use any AI feature to draft a model and then edit it.

Privacy and safety

Students and teachers need accounts, and the internet is always needed. Vectary collects account name and email, optional profile and education details, posted content, AI inputs and outputs, and device and browsing data. The policy lists sharing with service providers, AI model providers, and advertising partners, and data may be stored outside the user's country. The policy says Vectary does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16, and users under 18 may not use the AI features. The research found no COPPA or FERPA statement, and it is unclear whether these rules cover school managed accounts. A teacher should ask Vectary how student accounts are handled before use.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Build a 3D model of a favorite object and view it in AR on a phone.
  2. Design a 3D sculpture and add color and texture.
  3. Create a small scene and describe the mood it sets.
  4. Redesign a classroom object in 3D.
  5. Share a model with the class and give kind feedback on two others.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Model a simple product and list the parts it needs.
  2. Change a dimension and observe how the design shifts.
  3. Build a model of a bridge or tower and name its supports.
  4. Design a phone stand and test the idea in a sketch.
  5. Present a design and explain two choices you made.
How this can be used for Math
  1. Build a 3D shape and name its faces, edges, and corners.
  2. Animate a shape to show a rotation or a slide.
  3. Measure the sides of a model and calculate its volume.
  4. Make two shapes that look alike and compare their sizes.
  5. Use a geometry animation to explain a theorem to the class.
