Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This lesson has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is sequencing. Students line up sound blocks in order and the robot plays them.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state whether accounts are needed or whether the internet is required. Edison robots are programmed from the EdWare app or browser editors. The research did not review the data handling of those editors. The lesson page was not read. A teacher should check the Edison editor rules and school device policy first. Sound levels should stay comfortable in the classroom.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Explore the robot's built-in sounds and describe each one.
  2. Build a short rhythm with the percussion features.
  3. Repeat a rhythm pattern and clap along.
  4. Change the order of sounds and describe the new rhythm.
  5. Perform your sequence for the class.
How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Build a sequence of sound blocks in EdBlocks.
  2. Run the program on the robot and listen.
  3. Debug a block that plays at the wrong time.
  4. Use a repeat block to loop a rhythm.
  5. Swap programs with a partner and describe what they do.
