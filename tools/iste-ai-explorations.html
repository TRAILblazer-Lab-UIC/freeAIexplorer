Overview

This is ISTE's AI resource hub, which redirects to iste-ascd.org/ai. It lists educator courses, a student AI lessons page, and free guides. The AI Lessons page offers student lessons from unplugged activities to building chatbots and simple video games. The hub page does not mention an item named AI Explorations.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The lessons cover AI literacy, AI ethics, and chatbots. The vendor does not say where any AI tools run. Many lessons are unplugged. Students build or test chatbots and simple games and then explain how they work. They use the ethics guide to discuss impact.

Key terms on this page

AI ethics
AI ethics is the study of how AI should be built and used fairly and safely.
AI literacy
AI literacy is the knowledge and skill to understand, use, and judge AI.
Chatbot
A chatbot is a program that holds a written or spoken conversation.
ISTE Standards
The ISTE Standards are guidelines for using technology in learning.
Unplugged activity
An unplugged activity teaches computing ideas without a computer.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Run an unplugged activity that sorts items by rules and describe the rules.
  2. Build a simple chatbot and test it with five questions.
  3. Make a simple game and explain one AI idea inside it.
  4. Compare your chatbot with a classmate's chatbot.
  5. Write a plan to improve one project.
How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Read the ethics guide and list two fair uses of AI.
  2. Debate one case where AI could harm a group.
  3. Write a class code for responsible AI use.
  4. Interview an adult about how AI changes their work.
  5. Create a poster on AI and fairness.
