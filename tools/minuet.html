Minuet

A free KDE app for ear training with intervals, chords, scales, and rhythm.

FREEComputingStays on the device

Overview

Minuet is KDE's music education application for ear training. It offers exercises on intervals, chords, scales, and rhythm, with keyboard and staff feedback. Newer versions add singing and clapping practice with microphone feedback. Students listen, answer, and see whether they were right.

Benefits

For students

  • Students train their ears with short exercises.
  • Students get instant feedback on a keyboard and a staff.
  • Students can practice singing and clapping with live feedback.

For teachers

  • Settings cover tempo, instruments, and test length.
  • Teachers can add their own exercises with JSON files.
  • A third-party review says it reinforces listening skills but does not replace a full music theory curriculum.

For schools

  • The app is free and open source.
  • It runs on Linux, Windows, and Mac.
  • IT may need to approve installs.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This tool has no AI. It builds the skill of hearing patterns in sound, such as intervals and rhythms. Students practice spotting patterns by ear.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed. The internet is used to download the app. The singing feature uses the microphone on the device. The research read no data collection statement for the app. The KDE apps privacy policy was not fetched. A teacher should test the microphone exercises and check the Mac nightly build before class.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Listen to an interval and name it on the keyboard.
  2. Identify major and minor chords by ear and record the score.
  3. Clap a rhythm and check the feedback.
  4. Sing a note and see how close the pitch is.
  5. Make a custom exercise file with a few favorite intervals.
