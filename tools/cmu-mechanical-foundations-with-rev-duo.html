CMU Mechanical Foundations with REV DUO

A three-unit mechanical design course with REV DUO kits and a culminating project.

Robotics

Overview

This is a mechanical design course with three units. The units are Strength, Stability and Balance, Motors, Transmissions and Mechanical Advantage, and Drivetrains. Each unit has an intro video, instructional content, evidence uploads, assessments and a culminating project. It uses REV DUO kits in two versions. One uses the REV Control Hub and Driver Hub. The other uses an Arduino and goBILDA power distribution boards.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build real mechanisms and test them.
  • Students upload evidence and take assessments.
  • FTC students can upload evidence toward SMART Robotics Technician credentials, per search results.

For teachers

  • Teachers can use CS2N to create groups and track progress.
  • A teacher professional development course is listed at 299 USD per a search result.
  • The course needs a teacher who is comfortable with robotics.

For schools

  • The course price was not confirmed.
  • Schools must buy REV DUO parts.
  • IT help is likely needed for accounts and kits.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This course has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is understanding how machines move. Motors, transmissions and drivetrains are the parts of many robots.

Privacy and safety

The course is hosted online on CS2N, which is a cloud service. Course pages say access and tracking go through CS2N, and the research did not confirm whether accounts are required. Evidence uploads go to the service. The CS2N policy lists name, email, username, group membership, and course activity. It says it does not sell or rent personal information. It allows use by children under 13 with consent from a parent, school, district, or teacher. It does not name COPPA or say where data is stored. A teacher should read the CS2N privacy policy and get approval from the school before creating accounts.
