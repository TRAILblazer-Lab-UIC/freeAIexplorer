Panorama AI Literacy Essentials for K-12

A free two-hour online course that teaches K-12 educators AI basics and ethics.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

This is a free, self-paced online course for K-12 educators that takes about two hours. It covers what AI is, ethical strategies for schools, real classroom examples, prompt writing, and evaluating AI output. It suits teachers and school leaders. Completers get a certificate.

Benefits

For students

  • Students gain from teachers who learn to guide AI use.
  • Students can see AI used with clear ethics and rules.
  • Students gain from classroom examples that teachers adapt.

For teachers

  • The course is self-paced and takes about two hours.
  • It teaches prompt writing and checking AI output.
  • Completers get a certificate for LinkedIn or professional development credit.

For schools

  • The course is free for individual educators.
  • Districts can buy a premium package with a district badge and progress reporting. The price is by quote.
  • No IT work is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The course teaches about AI and generative AI. It runs on the vendor's cloud as an online course. Teachers learn to write prompts and judge AI output, then use those skills in their own lessons.

AI ethics is the study of how AI should be built and used fairly and safely.

AI literacy is the knowledge and skill to understand, use, and judge AI.
Generative AI
Generative AI is AI that makes new content.
Prompt
A prompt is the request or question you give an AI tool.

Privacy and safety

An account is required through the enrollment form, and enrolling means agreeing to Panorama's privacy policy. The course is for educators, and the research found no student data involved. The course collects teacher enrollment details and stores them with the vendor. The research did not read the privacy policy. A teacher should read it before signing up with a school email.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Explain in your own words what AI is, using an example from the course.
  2. Test an AI tool with one question and judge the output with the course checklist.
  3. Sort everyday tools into those that use AI and those that do not.
  4. Write a short class rule for responsible AI use.
  5. Compare two replies to the same prompt and explain which is better.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Write a prompt, run it, and improve it twice.
  2. Compare a first prompt with a revised prompt and describe the change in output.
  3. Check an AI summary against the original text and mark errors.
  4. Write a prompt that asks for feedback on a draft and judge the advice.
  5. Draft a short guide on good prompts for classmates.
