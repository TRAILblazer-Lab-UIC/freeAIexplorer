Planner 5D for Education

A 3D home and interior design planner that is free for schools.

Computing

Overview

Planner 5D is a 3D home and interior design planner. The education page says it is now free for every school or educational institution worldwide. Schools apply for licenses through a Get licenses button, and each license goes to one student. Students design floor plans and rooms in a browser or an app.

Benefits

For students

  • Students design rooms and floor plans in 3D.
  • Students can work in a browser or on a phone or tablet app.
  • The page suggests use in STEM, art, architecture, and engineering.

For teachers

  • The tool is easy to teach.
  • The web version needs no plug-ins.
  • The page suggests problem based learning projects.

For schools

  • The vendor says it is free for every school worldwide. Other terms were not listed.
  • Each student needs a license and an account.
  • IT should review the vendor terms before rollout.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in Planner 5D for education. The tool runs in the vendor cloud. Students use it to plan and test layouts and build spatial reasoning.

Privacy and safety

Student accounts are required and designs are stored in the vendor cloud. The internet is needed at all times. The privacy policy says student data on education licenses is not sold or used for AI training or advertising. It also says the company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, and minors need guardian consent. The policy does not name COPPA. Data may go to listed sub-processors, and a search snippet names OpenAI among them. Any AI use is unconfirmed. A teacher should review the terms with IT and get any needed consent before students sign up.

How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Design a classroom floor plan to scale, as the education page suggests for architecture classes.
  2. Plan a small house and check that every door has clear space.
  3. Compare two layouts for a library corner and pick the better one.
  4. Add measurements to a plan and calculate the floor area.
  5. Present a design and explain the choices you made.
How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Design a room around a chosen color scheme.
  2. Decorate a space for a theme such as a school event.
  3. Create a model of a dream bedroom and describe the style.
  4. Compare a 2D plan with the 3D view and describe what changes.
  5. Make a walkthrough of a finished design for the class.
