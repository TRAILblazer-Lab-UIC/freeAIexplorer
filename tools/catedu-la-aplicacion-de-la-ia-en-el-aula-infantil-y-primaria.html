CATEDU La aplicacion de la IA en el aula infantil y primaria

A Spanish online book of unplugged and hands-on AI activities for young children.

AIGood for AI beginners

Overview

This is a Spanish-language online book from CATEDU and the third module of a training path on AI literacy. It covers AI-made teaching materials and age-appropriate explanations of algorithm, data, model, and bias. Chapters include early years games, unplugged activities, and a hands-on chapter with Teachable Machine and the Echidna board. The page marks the book as still in creation.

Benefits

For students

  • Young students learn how a machine can learn from examples.
  • Students explore decision trees and bias with activities away from the screen.
  • Students train simple models with images, sound, and gestures.

For teachers

  • The book gives strategies for each school cycle.
  • The lessons include Rule detectives, The decision path, and Create a fair AI.
  • Teachers need only to choose a chapter.

For schools

  • The book is free to read online.
  • Unplugged chapters need no devices.
  • The Echidna board is a separate purchase and the price was not checked.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Some chapters have no AI tool, and students learn how AI works through paper activities. The hands-on chapter uses Teachable Machine, which trains a model in the browser. Students teach the model with examples and then watch it classify new ones. This builds skills in sorting and pattern spotting.

Privacy and safety

Reading the book needs no account, and the internet is needed throughout. Unplugged activities need no device. The hands on chapter uses Teachable Machine and EchidnaLM, and the research did not check where their data goes. The notes list no student data collection for the book itself. A teacher should check that Teachable Machine is allowed by the school. A teacher should also avoid using photos of students as training data.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Play the rule detective game and guess the hidden rule from examples.
  2. Sort fish and not-fish cards and explain the rule used.
  3. Build a paper decision tree for sorting animals.
  4. Train a Teachable Machine model with three kinds of objects and test it.
  5. Discuss what happens when the examples are unfair and fix the set.
