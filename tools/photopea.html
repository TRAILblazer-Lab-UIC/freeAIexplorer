Photopea

A free browser image editor that opens PSD files and offers layers and masks.

FREEAIComputingLocal with optional cloud or accounts

Overview

Photopea is a free image editor that runs in a web browser. It opens and saves PSD files and reads PNG, JPG, PDF, and RAW. Students use layers, masks, adjustment layers, vector tools, and filters. The free version shows ads.

Benefits

For students

  • Students edit images with layers and masks.
  • Students can remove a background in one click.
  • A step-by-step learning section helps beginners.

For teachers

  • Nothing is installed and no account is needed.
  • The interface looks like other pro editors. Teachers can reuse existing lessons.
  • The learn page is available in English and Spanish.

For schools

  • The site is free with ads. A Premium plan exists, but no official price was read.
  • It runs on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, and Linux.
  • IT does not need to install anything.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Photopea has AI features for one-click background removal and text-based content replacement. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students can remove a background and then finish the edit by hand with layers and masks.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed. The privacy policy says files are processed on the device and never leave it. Without a login, only a preferences cookie is kept. With a login, the site stores a name, an email, and a copy of preferences. The materials do not state whether the AI features such as background removal send images to a server. The policy does not mention ads, age rules, or children's data, and the free version shows ads. A teacher should skip logins and test the AI features before students use them.
