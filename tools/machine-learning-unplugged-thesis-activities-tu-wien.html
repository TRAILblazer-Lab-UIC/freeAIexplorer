Machine Learning Unplugged thesis activities (TU Wien)

A diploma thesis with two hands-on activities on training trees and neural networks.

FREEAIUnpluggedNo device neededGood for AI beginners

Overview

This 2023 diploma thesis by Lukas Lehner was advised by Gerald Futschek at TU Wien. It designs and pilots two hands-on unplugged activities. One teaches how decision trees are trained and the other teaches how artificial neural networks are trained. The goal is to explain training without oversimplifying how machines learn. A 2025 follow-up paper reports a pilot of the decision tree activity with 56 upper secondary students.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn how decision trees are trained.
  • Students learn how neural networks are trained.
  • Students work with their hands, which keeps the ideas concrete.

For teachers

  • The thesis describes both activities in detail.
  • The design avoids giving single neurons unrealistic tasks.
  • The follow-up paper reports a real classroom pilot.

For schools

  • The thesis is free to download from the repository.
  • No computers, accounts, or internet are needed.
  • Required materials were not confirmed. Some prep time is likely.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The activities have no running AI program. They teach how decision trees and artificial neural networks learn from training data. Students act out the training steps by hand. They build skills in data and in seeing how a model improves as it trains.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Train a decision tree by hand from a small set of examples.
  2. Test the tree on new examples and mark its mistakes.
  3. Act out a small neural network in a group and pass values along the links.
  4. Adjust the weights after each mistake and see the output improve.
  5. Compare how a tree and a neural network learn from the same data.
