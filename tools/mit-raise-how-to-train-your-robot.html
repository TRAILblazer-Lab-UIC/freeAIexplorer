MIT RAISE How to Train Your Robot

A free 30-hour middle school curriculum on training robot companions with machine learning.

FREEAIRoboticsUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

How to Train Your Robot is a free 30-hour middle school curriculum on machine learning, bias, and human-AI interaction. Students design intelligent robot companions for real-world challenges and train them. Materials include an Educator Guide, a Student Workbook, and an Activity Programming Guide. Students program with Open AI Programming Blocks, a web-based environment hosted by MIT RAISE.

Benefits

For students

  • Students design a robot companion for a real-world challenge.
  • Students train machine learning models with blocks.
  • Students discuss bias and human-AI interaction.

For teachers

  • The Educator Guide, Student Workbook, and Activity Programming Guide are provided.
  • The curriculum is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0. The credit line must say it was created by the I2 Learning and Personal Robots Group at the MIT Media Lab.
  • It was piloted in three Massachusetts schools during STEM week in 2019, per a search snippet.

For schools

  • The curriculum is free. A robot is needed and a research paper reports about $40.
  • The setup includes robot instructions and a link to Amazon Future Engineer instructions.
  • The materials do not state account needs. IT should check.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is machine learning that students train with the Open AI Programming Blocks. The blocks run in a web browser hosted by MIT RAISE. The materials do not say clearly where the training runs. Students train their robot companion and then check how bias in the data changes its behavior.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which accounts students need. They also do not say where student data goes. A teacher should check the web environment's data terms and review any images or sounds students use for training.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Train a model with the Open AI Programming Blocks and test it with new examples.
  2. Add examples to a weak class and compare results.
  3. Find a case where the model is wrong and trace the cause.
  4. Compare two training sets and describe which model works better.
  5. Write the steps of how a robot learns from examples.
How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Discuss how a biased training set could harm a group of people.
  2. Interview a classmate about what a robot companion should do for them.
  3. Debate who is responsible when a robot gives a wrong answer.
  4. Write a rule for how a robot should treat private information.
  5. Compare two community needs and decide which a robot could help.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Sketch a robot companion for a real-world challenge.
  2. Choose the sensors the robot needs and explain each choice.
  3. Build a simple test for the companion and record results.
  4. Improve the design after the test.
  5. Present the final design in a short demo.
