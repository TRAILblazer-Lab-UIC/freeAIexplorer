Stanford CRAFT Can AI help with storytelling

A 45 minute Stanford CRAFT language arts lesson on AI and storytelling.

AIGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is a 45 minute AI and Language Arts lesson created by Imama Owaisi and adapted by Julie Braly. Students weigh the benefits and trade-offs of using AI in storytelling. The CRAFT project targets high school. Students read, discuss, and consider authenticity and originality.

Benefits

For students

  • Students think about who the author is when AI helps write a story.
  • Students read different views on AI and creativity.
  • Students practice weighing benefits against trade-offs.

For teachers

  • The lesson fits in a single 45 minute class.
  • A teacher guide and readings are included.
  • The discussion needs little technical setup.

For schools

  • The lesson is free.
  • It needs a browser and no software install.
  • IT involvement is minimal.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The lesson is about generative AI used in storytelling. The materials discuss AI authorship and show a tutorial on making AI animated story videos. The lesson does not run AI itself. Students read and talk about how AI can extend or replace their own creative work.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state whether accounts are needed or what data is collected. Students open readings and a video on outside sites. A teacher should check those sites against school rules. A teacher should also decide whether students will try any AI tool.

How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Read the article on AI and storytelling and write a one paragraph response.
  2. Discuss whether an AI assisted story is still original.
  3. Compare a short human written story with an AI written one.
  4. Write a class statement on when AI help in writing is fair.
  5. Plan a story by hand and list where AI could help and where it should not.
