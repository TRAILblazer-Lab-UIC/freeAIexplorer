Day of AI: Grade 3 Discovering Artificial Intelligence

A grade 3 Day of AI unit on how AI learns from data.

AIGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This grade 3 unit runs 5 to 6 class periods of 45 to 60 minutes. Lessons are What Is AI, Can Machines Really Learn, What Is a Learning Algorithm, How Do Machines Learn, Bias in Artificial Intelligence, and a Career Spotlight. In one lesson students train a model with labeled examples. They see how training data changes predictions.

Benefits

For students

  • Students train a model and see how examples shape its answers.
  • Students learn the idea of bias in plain terms.
  • Students meet real AI careers in a career spotlight.

For teachers

  • Slides come as a Google Slides copy and as an Office download.
  • An educator guide and student resources are on the site.
  • Setup takes only a few minutes.

For schools

  • The unit is free.
  • Students need a browser.
  • A teacher registers for a login link. IT has little to do.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Sort picture cards into two groups and explain the rule you used.
  2. Train a model with labeled examples and test it on a new picture.
  3. Change the labels on a few examples and describe what happens to the predictions.
  4. Find an example where the model has too few pictures and suggest how to fix it.
  5. Draw a poster that shows how a machine learns from examples.
