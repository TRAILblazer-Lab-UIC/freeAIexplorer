Stanford Digital Education high school AI curriculum

High school lesson plans from Stanford that pair with Google's AI Essentials course.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

The official website did not respond when we checked in November 2026. Confirm that this tool is still available before you plan with it.

Overview

Stanford Digital Education wrote high school lesson plans to go with Google's AI Essentials course. The first pilot ran in 2024 at North Star Academy Washington Park High School in Newark. A second pilot ran at Niagara Falls High School in New York. Students can work toward a Google Career Certificate in AI Essentials.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn AI skills that link to careers.
  • Students can earn a Google Career Certificate in AI Essentials.
  • Students follow a structured course of about 10 weeks.

For teachers

  • Lesson plans are written for classroom use.
  • The pilots show how a school can run the course.
  • The plans pair with an existing Google course.

For schools

  • The lesson plans are said to remain available.
  • Google gave Stanford 100 free licenses for the pilot. Later pricing is unknown.
  • The vendor does not say what IT work is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The materials teach AI literacy and AI skills for careers. The Google AI Essentials course runs on Google's cloud in a web browser. Students use the course and the lesson plans to learn how to work with AI tools.

Privacy and safety

The course runs on a Google course platform and needs the internet. The research did not confirm accounts or data terms. The materials do not state what student data is collected. A teacher should check Google's account terms and the school's data rules. A teacher should also ask about license costs before starting.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Describe in your own words what AI is and where you see it each day.
  2. Complete one module of the AI Essentials course and share what you learned.
  3. Try a prompt in an AI tool and refine it three times.
  4. Discuss the limits of AI answers with a partner.
  5. Build a short class poster on responsible AI use.
How this can be used for Career and Technical Education
  1. Interview an adult about how AI changes their job and write a summary.
  2. Make a list of career tasks that AI can help with.
  3. Practice writing clear prompts for a workplace task.
  4. Review a job posting and mark the AI skills it asks for.
  5. Create a plan for earning the AI Essentials certificate.
