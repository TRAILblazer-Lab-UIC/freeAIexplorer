Seeed Studio Wio Terminal

A microcontroller board with a screen and sensors that supports TinyML.

$AIComputingStays on the device

Overview

The Wio Terminal is a microcontroller board with a 2.4 inch screen. It has an accelerometer, microphone, speaker, light sensor, buttons, and a microSD slot. Students program it with Arduino, CircuitPython, MicroPython, or CodeCraft. Seeed documents TinyML projects with Edge Impulse and TensorFlow Lite.

Benefits

For students

  • Students use a board with a built-in screen and sensors.
  • Students can try gesture and sound classification with TinyML.
  • Students can choose among several programming languages.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is optional TinyML for gesture and sound classification. It runs on the board. Students collect sensor data, train a small model, and load it onto the board. They can then test how well it recognizes gestures or sounds.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed for the board, and the internet is used at setup. The board runs programs locally. Student data flows through the tools used with it, such as Edge Impulse or CodeCraft, which have their own terms. Wi-Fi code written by students can send data anywhere. A teacher should check those services before students sign up.
