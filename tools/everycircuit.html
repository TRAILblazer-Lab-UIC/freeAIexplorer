EveryCircuit

An interactive circuit simulator with animated voltage and current.

FREEComputingUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

EveryCircuit is an interactive circuit simulator with animated voltage and current displays. It runs in a web browser and as Android and iOS apps. Users build circuits, simulate in real time, and share them to a public gallery. The free tier limits your own circuits to 5 components.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see current and voltage move in real time.
  • Students build and change circuits quickly.
  • Students can browse circuits in the public gallery.

For teachers

  • The simulation shows what a circuit does before any parts are used.
  • Teachers can request a class license through the site form.
  • It works on phones, tablets, and computers.

For schools

  • The free tier limits your own circuits to 5 components.
  • Full features need a subscription or a one-time purchase. The educator license costs about 4 USD per semester per the official site.
  • The App Store version was last updated in April 2023. Maintenance is not confirmed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in EveryCircuit. The tool builds logic and cause-and-effect skills. Students change a part and watch how the circuit responds.

Privacy and safety

The tool runs in a browser or an app, and the internet is used at setup. An account is optional and is needed to buy, sync, or share. The Full Version includes cloud storage and device sync, and the research could not tell where that data is stored. Shared circuits go to a public gallery. The privacy policy page could not be read. The Apple privacy label, which Apple has not verified, lists email address, user ID, and product interaction data. A teacher should tell students what is public and check the privacy terms before anyone signs up.
