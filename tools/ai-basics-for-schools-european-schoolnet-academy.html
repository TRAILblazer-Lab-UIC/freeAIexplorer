AI Basics for Schools (European Schoolnet Academy)

A free online course that teaches primary and secondary teachers the basics of AI.

AIGood for AI beginners

Overview

AI Basics for Schools is an open online course from European Schoolnet Academy. It is for primary and secondary teachers of any subject and needs no prior AI knowledge. The course has three modules titled What is AI, AI in Education, and AI Benefits and Challenges. Teachers spend about 15 hours in total.

Benefits

For students

  • Students gain from teachers who understand what AI is and how it works.
  • Students get clearer guidance on the benefits and limits of AI tools.
  • Students see their teachers model careful, informed use of AI.

For teachers

  • The course is free to browse and needs no AI background.
  • Each module is estimated at about 5 hours.
  • A peer-assessed final activity earns a certificate.

For schools

  • The course costs nothing, although the course page does not state a price.
  • Teachers work online in a browser and need no IT install.
  • A staff group can take the course together and share what they learn.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This is a course about AI, and it does not run an AI tool for students. It runs on the vendor's cloud and teachers read and work through it in a browser. Teachers learn what AI is, how it is used in education, and what its benefits and challenges are. The course builds the skill of judging AI claims.

Privacy and safety

Teachers need a European Schoolnet Academy account and the course runs on the vendor's cloud. The course is for teachers and not for students. The research did not review the privacy policy. The materials do not state what data the site collects. The page lists 2021 dates. Current enrollment is unconfirmed. A teacher should read the site's privacy terms before registering with a school email.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Read Module 1 and write a plain-language definition of AI to share with a class.
  2. List three places students meet AI each day and discuss how each one learns from data.
  3. Use the AI in Education module to plan a class talk on how schools use AI.
  4. Hold a debate on one benefit and one challenge of AI named in Module 3.
  5. Turn what you learn into a short class poster on how AI makes decisions.
