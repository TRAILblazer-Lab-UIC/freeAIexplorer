Unplugged assignments for K-12 AI education

A short AI Matters article that introduces two unplugged AI activities for K-12.

FREEAIUnpluggedNo device neededGood for AI beginners

Overview

This is a short article in AI Matters volume 7, issue 1 from March 2021. It appears on pages 10 to 12. It introduces two unplugged activities for K-12 AI education that need no technology. Later papers describe one of them as using a semantic network to show how agents make connections.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can learn AI ideas with paper and pencil.
  • Students may build a semantic network, according to later papers.
  • Students practice linking ideas into a map.

For teachers

  • The article is short.
  • The activities need no technology.
  • The activity details are unconfirmed. Teachers should read the article first.

For schools

  • The article is free to read.
  • Paper is the only material listed.
  • No accounts or devices are needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The activities teach AI ideas such as semantic networks and knowledge representation. The knowledge representation point is unconfirmed. They run on paper with no computer. Students draw connections between ideas, which shows how an agent could store knowledge.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Draw a semantic network that links words about animals.
  2. Add a new connection to the network and explain why it fits.
  3. Trade networks with a partner and answer questions using only the map.
  4. Compare two networks for the same topic and note the differences.
  5. Describe how an AI agent could use a network to answer a question.
