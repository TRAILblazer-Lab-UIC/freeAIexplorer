LLMs Unplugged Fine-tuning and LoRA module

A paper module that adds new text to a finished model through an adapter sheet.

AIUnpluggedGood for AI beginners

Overview

The Fine-tuning and LoRA module is part of LLMs Unplugged. It runs 50 to 60 minutes, plus 20 for the flattering adapter. Students teach a finished model new text without retraining it. They keep new tallies on a separate adapter sheet that can be turned up or removed. They then compress the sheet into a few strips of numbers, as LoRA does.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see how a model picks up a new style from fresh text.
  • Students turn an adapter up or down and watch the output change.
  • Students learn the idea behind LoRA with paper strips.

For teachers

  • The module builds on a model the class already made.
  • The adapter idea gets a hands-on demonstration.
  • The page lists ages 9 and up.

For schools

  • The materials are free.
  • The module needs a grid, printed sheets, and dice.
  • No computers or accounts are needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The module has no running AI program. It shows fine-tuning by adding a second sheet of tallies to a finished model. It then shows LoRA by squeezing that sheet into a few strips of numbers. Students use the adapter and see how the model's text changes.

Privacy and safety

The module runs on paper. The pages read mention no devices or accounts. The research read no privacy policy for this item. The vendor does not describe any online part. The module's own page was not opened. A teacher should confirm the content license on the GitHub repository before sharing the sheets. A teacher should also choose the new text with care.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Add tallies from a new text to an adapter sheet and keep them apart from the base model.
  2. Turn the adapter strength up and down and compare the generated text.
  3. Remove the adapter and show that the base model is unchanged.
  4. Compress the adapter into a few strips of numbers and explain what was lost.
  5. Explain to a partner why an adapter saves time compared with full retraining.
