Make Wonder Blockly content in Spanish

Spanish student content in the Blockly web and iOS apps for Dash robots.

FREEComputingRoboticsSends student data to a company

Overview

A 2 April 2025 announcement says all student-facing content in the Blockly web and iOS apps is available in Spanish. It covers Coding Pathways, Math Activities, Science Activities, challenges, guidance and instructional videos. This includes premium content from a Make Wonder subscription. Students switch to Spanish through device or Chrome language settings.

Benefits

For students

  • Spanish speaking students read lessons in their own language.
  • Students use the same Dash activities as other students.
  • Students learn coding, math and science in Spanish.

For teachers

  • Teachers can set a language on a device with no new tools.
  • The Spanish content matches the English content.
  • Blockly already offered its core interface, blocks and puzzles in several languages.

For schools

  • Core Blockly is free.
  • Premium Spanish lessons need a Make Wonder subscription, and the price is not confirmed here.
  • Student data goes to the vendor.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in this feature. Dash follows the blocks that students place. Students build skills in sequencing and logic in Spanish.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are optional, and premium Spanish content needs a Make Wonder subscription through a teacher login. Student data goes to the vendor cloud, hosted on Amazon Web Services. The materials say program names, solutions and time in app are collected. The policy is the same as for the main platform. The company states COPPA compliance and no collection of kids' voices or images. The research did not confirm which features need the internet. A teacher should read the Make Wonder privacy policy before adding students.
