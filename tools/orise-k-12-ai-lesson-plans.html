Overview

ORISE publishes a set of free AI lesson plans as PDFs for grades 5 to 12. The page lists nine plans, including Predicting with Probability, Picture Perfect AI, Evaluating AI Output, Developing a Classroom AI Policy, and Ethical AI Use. Students explore how AI works, judge its output, and discuss ethics.

Benefits

For students

  • Students train an AI system by hand in Picture Perfect AI.
  • Students research AI in a career field and investigate an ethical issue.
  • Students practice judging AI output.

For teachers

  • The lessons are ready to print and teach.
  • The Ethical Usage lesson lists CCSS ELA and ISTE standards.
  • Teachers can use the policy lesson to build a classroom AI policy with students.

For schools

  • The lessons are free PDF downloads.
  • No IT work is needed.
  • The Ethical Usage lesson needs student devices with internet and sticky notes.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The lessons teach about AI. Picture Perfect AI has students train an AI system by hand. Evaluating AI Output has students judge the replies of an AI tool. The vendor does not say where any AI tool runs. Students use these activities to build their own judgment.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which accounts students need. The Ethical Usage lesson needs student devices with internet and lists tools such as ChatGPT and Canva. The lesson tells teachers to remind students not to enter personal or identifying information into AI tools. The research did not read the Picture Perfect AI PDF. Its tool is unknown. A teacher should check which websites and tools students open and whether the school allows them.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Train a simple AI system by hand in Picture Perfect AI and describe what it learned.
  2. Test an AI system with a tricky example and note where it fails.
  3. Research how AI is used in a career field and share one finding.
  4. Compare the replies of an AI tool to one question and rate each reply.
  5. Draft rules for a classroom AI policy and explain the reasons.
How this can be used for Math
  1. Work through the Predicting with Probability lesson and record each prediction.
  2. Compare predicted results with real results and describe the gap.
  3. Roll dice or draw cards many times and graph the outcomes.
  4. Explain how probability helps an AI system make a guess.
  5. Design a small game and calculate the chance of each result.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Research AI in a career field and write a short summary.
  2. Choose one of four ethical issues and write a claim with evidence.
  3. Debate an ethical issue in a small group and listen to each view.
  4. Write an exit ticket that states one new idea you learned.
  5. Draft a class AI policy in clear sentences and read it aloud.
