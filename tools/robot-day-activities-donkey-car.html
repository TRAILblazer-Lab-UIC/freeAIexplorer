Robot Day activities (Donkey Car)

A hands-on activity where students train a small car to drive itself from camera images.

Overview

This Robot Day activity page is about the Donkey Car, a small autonomous car built on an RC chassis with a Raspberry Pi 4 and a camera. Students drive the car around a track to collect images. The images train a neural network. The trained network then predicts speed and steering to drive the track without a human. The page is part of the AI Racing League project for high school and college students.

Benefits

For students

  • Students collect their own driving data.
  • Students train a neural network and watch it drive.
  • Students see how data quality affects the car's driving.

For teachers

  • The page lays out a clear cycle of drive, train, and test.
  • AI Racing League lesson plans are listed.
  • The page has sample text for 5th and 11th grade.

For schools

  • The page lists parts at about $310 per car. A college course listing showed about $300 each.
  • Cars are brought to Robot Day events.
  • Setup takes days and needs a Raspberry Pi 4 and Python skills. The materials do not state account needs.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Drive the car around the track and collect a set of images.
  2. Train a network on the images and test it on a fresh lap.
  3. Collect more data on a weak turn and compare the new results.
  4. Compare a model trained on clean driving with one trained on messy driving.
  5. Explain in a short note how images become steering decisions.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Assemble the RC chassis, Raspberry Pi 4, and camera.
  2. Calibrate the camera and test the video feed.
  3. Design a track with turns and mark safe zones.
  4. Measure lap time before and after training.
  5. Write a build and calibration guide for the next team.
