Oak National Academy GCSE Data science AI and machine learning unit

An eight-lesson Year 10 unit on AI and machine learning for GCSE computing.

AIGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is an eight-lesson Year 10 unit in Oak National Academy's GCSE AQA computing programme. Lessons cover Introduction to AI, Machine learning, Predictive vs generative AI, Social and ethical issues of AI systems, Data driven models, Approaches to training machine learning models, Classification, and Machine learning engines. Oak offers free videos, slides, and worksheets.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn the main types of AI and machine learning.
  • Students study classification and ways to train a model.
  • Students think about the social and ethical issues of AI.

For teachers

  • Each lesson comes with a video, slides, a worksheet, and a quiz.
  • Teachers can download slides and worksheets from the teacher view.
  • Oak says its curriculum drew on research from the National Centre for Computing Education.

For schools

  • The materials are free.
  • They run in a browser and IT involvement is low.
  • The unit lines up with GCSE AQA Computer Science.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The unit teaches about AI and does not run an AI tool. It covers predictive and generative AI, classification, and ways to train machine learning models. Students use the lessons to understand how models learn from data.

Privacy and safety

The lesson list page mentions no accounts for pupils. The lesson content did not load for the research. Any tools used inside lessons are unknown. The research did not read Oak's privacy policy. The internet is needed. A teacher should check the Oak terms and ask whether students can use the site without signing in.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Describe the difference between predictive and generative AI with an example of each.
  2. Sort a set of items into classes and explain the rule you used.
  3. Compare two ways to train a machine learning model.
  4. Discuss one social or ethical issue of an AI system.
  5. Explain how data driven models make predictions.
