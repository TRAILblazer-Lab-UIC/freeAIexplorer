Drone Dojo Light Show course

An advanced self-paced online course on building and programming a light show drone fleet.

ComputingRobotics

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is a self-paced online course on building and programming a fleet of light show drones around the Drone Dojo PiHawk kit. A 2024 review says the course teaches Skybrush software. Students solder and 3D print. The review calls it advanced and suited to a small motivated group such as an after-school club.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build real drones with soldering and 3D printing.
  • Students learn show software called Skybrush.
  • Students plan and fly a fleet together.

For teachers

  • The course is self-paced with about 6 hours of video per a 2024 review.
  • It suits a small club.
  • Lessons cover the PiHawk build and fleet flying.

For schools

  • A June 2025 review lists course access at 27 USD per month through the Blue Belt membership.
  • The kit costs about 1000 USD per drone. A 10-drone show needs about 10000 USD of gear.
  • Both sources come from one outlet and the prices are dated.
