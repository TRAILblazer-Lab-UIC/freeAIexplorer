Makey Makey GO

A small keychain-style Makey Makey that turns touches into key presses.

ComputingStays on the device

Overview

Makey Makey GO is a smaller, keychain-style version of Makey Makey. The discovery notes name it, but the official home page did not mention or price it. The Makey Makey family turns touches on conductive objects into key presses. Details here are limited to what was verified for the family.

Benefits

For students

  • Students connect everyday objects to a computer.
  • Students build their own touch inputs.
  • Students explore how conductive materials work.

For teachers

  • The kit is a compact input device.
  • The Makey Makey lessons are written for the Classic or the STEM Pack.
  • Setup takes minutes.

For schools

  • The price for GO was not found on the official site.
  • The device needs a computer with a USB port.
  • The listing may have changed or been discontinued.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Makey Makey GO has no AI. It builds skills in input and output. Students learn how a touch becomes a key press that a program can use.

Privacy and safety

The device works as an input device with no account and no internet. The research found no privacy page. The programs students use with it may have their own data practices. A teacher should check those programs before students use them.

How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Build a touch button from foil and a clip and test it.
  2. Design a controller for a simple game.
  3. Compare two materials for a touch pad and choose one.
  4. Fix a connection that does not register.
  5. Present the design and explain what you would improve.
How this can be used for Science
  1. Test which everyday objects conduct a signal.
  2. Sort objects into two groups and explain the pattern.
  3. Draw the path of the signal from touch to computer.
  4. Predict how water changes the result and test it.
  5. Write a short report on what you found.
