Hopscotch

A visual block coding app for making games and animations on iPad.

FREEComputingSends student data to a company

Overview

Hopscotch is a visual block coding app for making games and animations. The site lists downloads for iOS and Mac. A teacher review notes that students can build games only on iPad. The iPhone app plays games others made. The site has an Educators section with lesson plans and an Hour of Code page.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build games and animations with blocks.
  • Students can share projects they publish.
  • Students play games made by others.

For teachers

  • The Educators section has lesson plans.
  • Teachers can sign up as educators and create classes and student accounts.
  • An Hour of Code page offers a short starter.

For schools

  • The app is free with subscription and pass options.
  • Search results list Hopscotch for Schools at 9.99, but current prices were not on the pages read.
  • Chromebook support is not indicated. Students need an iPad to create games.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Hopscotch has no AI. It builds logic and sequencing skills. Students snap blocks together to make a character act. The app runs on the device and projects are stored in the vendor's cloud.

Privacy and safety

Student accounts are optional, and projects are stored in the vendor's cloud. The vendor collects a username, project data, usage data, device make and model, OS, language, and a generated device ID. The privacy policy names Amazon Web Services, Amplitude for analytics, and Help Scout as service providers. It states COPPA certification and says users must be over 13 to create an account, with an adult supplying the email and password. It does not mention FERPA, and published projects and usernames are visible to others. A teacher should review the policy, ask how deletion works by email request, and decide whether students may publish.
