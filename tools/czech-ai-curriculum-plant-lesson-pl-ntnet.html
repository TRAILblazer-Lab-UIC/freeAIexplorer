Czech AI curriculum plant lesson (Pl@ntNet)

A biology lesson where students check an app that names plants from photos.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

This Czech lesson is for ages 11 to 15 and lasts 45 to 90 minutes. Students use the Pl@ntNet app to identify a plant from a photo. They look at the plant's condition and placement and check the app's answer against online images. They finish by making an informational leaflet on plant care.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn to check an AI answer before trusting it.
  • Students practice careful observation of a real plant.
  • Students make a leaflet that teaches others how to care for the plant.

For teachers

  • The lesson plan fits a single biology class.
  • It needs a projector, a presentation, printed worksheets, pens, and scissors.
  • The lesson is available in English and Czech.

For schools

  • The lesson and the app are free.
  • Students need a device with internet access.
  • IT has little to do. A teacher should check whether the app may be used on school devices.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is an image classifier in Pl@ntNet that names a plant from a photo. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Students take a photo, read the answer, and check it against other images online. This shows them that the AI can misidentify a plant.

Privacy and safety

Plant photos are uploaded to the Pl@ntNet service for identification, which is a third party. The internet is needed at all times. An account is optional, and the lesson page names no accounts. The research did not review the Pl@ntNet privacy policy, and the materials do not state a minimum age. A teacher should check the app's privacy terms and tell students to photograph plants only and keep people out of the pictures.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Photograph a plant and record what the app says it is.
  2. Describe the plant's condition and placement and explain how they affect its health.
  3. Check the app's answer against online images and note any difference.
  4. Make a leaflet on caring for the plant with watering and light tips.
  5. Test the app with the same plant from two angles and compare the answers.
How this can be used for Library and Media Literacy
  1. Search online images to check whether the app's answer matches the plant.
  2. List which sources you trust for plant facts and say why.
  3. Write a short note on how to check any AI answer against another source.
  4. Compare two websites about the same plant and note which one is clearer.
  5. Add a credit line for each image used in the leaflet.
