Sphero Edu Jr app

A free block-coding app for young children that works with the Sphero indi robot.

ComputingRobotics

Overview

Sphero Edu Jr is a free block-coding app for the Sphero indi robot. Children drag and drop blocks to program the robot's lights, sounds, and movement. They can build mazes, games, and simple songs. The developer states the app works only with indi.

Benefits

For students

  • Young children drag and drop blocks to make a robot move and play sounds.
  • Children can build mazes, games, and simple songs.
  • The app also has a classic driving mode.

For teachers

  • An indi Educator Guide Book with 20 lessons supports the app.
  • The app is available in English and 14 other languages.
  • The app is free to download.

For schools

  • The app is free, but an indi robot is required.
  • The app runs on iOS 15 or later and on Android.
  • A teacher can install it in about an hour.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The app has no AI. Children place blocks in order and the robot follows them. They build early sequencing skills.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed. The policy says personal data is not collected through the app. It lists mobile data, toy data and crash reports that go to Sphero servers, which may be hosted by service companies. The policy says those third parties may not adhere to it, and it does not mention COPPA. The listed data includes device and toy identifiers, connection timestamps, accelerometer data and diagnostics. A teacher should read the privacy policy first and check with school leaders before using the app with children.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Drag blocks to make indi drive through a taped maze.
  2. Add light blocks so indi shows a color at each turn.
  3. Build a short song with sound blocks and play it.
  4. Change one block and tell a partner what changed.
  5. Make a simple game where indi must reach a goal.
