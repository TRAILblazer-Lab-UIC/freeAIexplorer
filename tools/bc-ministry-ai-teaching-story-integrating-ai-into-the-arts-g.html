BC Ministry AI teaching story integrating AI into the arts (grades 10-12)

A teaching story where drama and English students talk with an AI character chatbot.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

This teaching and learning story comes from the BC Ministry of Education and Child Care. It describes a grade 10 to 12 Drama and English Language Arts literary studies class. Students researched a literary or historical figure, then chatted with an AI character chatbot. They went on to make a dramatic piece such as a rap battle or short script.

Benefits

For students

  • Students talk with a chatbot to explore a historical or literary figure.
  • Students turn their research into a rap battle or short script.
  • Students practice citing AI use and judging AI output.

For teachers

  • The story includes student work samples and teacher reflections.
  • The teacher shares advice on privacy, bias, and citation.
  • A whole class demo can stand in until individual use is approved.

For schools

  • The story lists the tools as free and accessible, although their terms were not verified.
  • The class used district approved tools only.
  • The teacher named data privacy as a key concern.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a character chatbot and an optional AI lyric and song tool. Both run in a company's cloud. Students chat with the bot to learn about a figure. They then write and perform their own piece, and they judge and cite what the AI produced.

Privacy and safety

The chatbot and the song tool run in a company's cloud and need the internet at all times. The pages read do not name the tools and the research did not review their vendor terms. The document states that students could not input personal data. The first step was to pick district approved tools for student safety. The materials do not state whether the tools need accounts. A teacher should check district approval and the terms of each tool before class.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Theater and Media Arts
  1. Research a historical figure from verified sources and then chat with the character bot.
  2. Write a rap battle between two characters and perform it.
  3. Compare the chatbot's answers with the research notes and mark any mistakes.
  4. Write a short script that puts two figures in the same scene.
  5. Name the AI tool in the program notes and discuss why credit matters.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Read a literary text and list questions to ask a character chatbot.
  2. Evaluate the chatbot's answers against the text and cite the proof.
  3. Write a poem or lyric and compare it with an AI generated draft.
  4. Discuss bias in the way the bot portrays a character.
  5. Write a citation note that explains how AI helped with a piece of writing.
