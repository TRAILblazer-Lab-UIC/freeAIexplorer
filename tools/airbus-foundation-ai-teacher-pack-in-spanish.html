Overview

This is a Spanish-language PDF activity pack on AI for secondary teachers from the Airbus Foundation. Search results say lesson plans cover AI and Education, AI and Art, and AI and Disinformation. The pack links to FAIR ENOUGH, an interactive AI series. One activity includes a 30-minute debate.

Benefits

For students

  • Students discuss the social and ethical sides of AI.
  • Students practice critical thinking in a class debate.
  • Spanish-speaking students can work in their own language.

For teachers

  • The pack is a ready-made PDF with lesson plans.
  • A separate English PDF exists.
  • One activity gives a 30-minute debate follow-up.

For schools

  • The PDF is free.
  • Printed activities need no IT install.
  • The vendor does not say what devices students need for the linked series.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The pack teaches AI literacy, ethics, and generative AI. The vendor does not say that any AI tool is built into the PDF. Students read, discuss, and debate on paper, and the pack links to an interactive series online. The pack builds the skill of judging what AI can and cannot do.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Library and Media Literacy
  1. Study the AI and Disinformation lesson and list signs that a text or image is fake.
  2. Test a claim from a news story and trace it to its source.
  3. Create a short poster that teaches classmates to spot AI made fakes.
  4. Hold a 30-minute debate on who is responsible for AI made falsehoods.
  5. Write a short guide for younger students on checking online facts.
How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Work through the AI and Art lesson and discuss who the artist is.
  2. Compare a human made artwork with an AI generated one and list the differences.
  3. Debate whether AI art should be labeled and why.
  4. Sketch an artwork and describe how an AI tool might change it.
  5. Write an artist statement about using AI in creative work.
How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Hold the 30-minute debate on a social or ethical issue about AI.
  2. List groups of people affected by an AI tool and describe each effect.
  3. Explore the AI and Education lesson and discuss how AI might change school.
  4. Write a short policy for how a town should use AI.
  5. Compare views on AI from two sides of an issue.
