Overview

The Digital Technologies Hub is an Australian website of resources for teachers, students, and families. It covers curriculum guidance, planning, lesson ideas, courses, assessment, and an AI section. The Department of Education runs it, and Education Services Australia built it. Content is licensed CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise indicated.

Benefits

For students

  • Students meet lessons that range from Foundation to Year 10.
  • Students try unplugged activities that need no device.
  • Older students learn about AI through lesson plans and explainer videos.

For teachers

  • A scope and sequence for F-10 helps with planning.
  • Teaching pages recommend unplugged lessons and link to CS Unplugged.
  • Professional learning modules support teacher growth.

For schools

  • Access to the site is free.
  • The content is open under CC BY 4.0.
  • The site lists the Australian Curriculum: Digital Technologies. Teachers in other countries may need to adapt it.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The hub has an AI section with lesson plans, tools, and explainer videos. The hub itself is a website and runs in the browser. Students and teachers use the lessons to learn what AI is and how to use it with care.

Privacy and safety

The pages read did not mention accounts or student data, and the research did not read the linked privacy policy. The internet is needed for use. The site offers an email newsletter. The materials do not state which accounts students need. Individual lessons may link to outside sites with their own rules. A teacher should read the Hub privacy policy and review those outside tools before students use them.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Try a CS Unplugged activity linked from the hub and explain the idea it teaches.
  2. Follow an AI lesson plan and list three things AI does well and badly.
  3. Write step-by-step instructions for a daily task and test them with a partner.
  4. Sort a set of cards by a rule and describe the algorithm used.
  5. Watch an explainer video on AI and write one question it raises.
