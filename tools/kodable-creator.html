Kodable Creator

A block based game design course for grade 2 and up with an AI tutor.

Computing

Overview

Kodable Creator is a game design course for students in grade 2 and up. Students use drag and drop blocks that show real coding words and symbols. Each course has four missions named Debug, Elevate, Hack, and Master. Courses include High Flyer, Run and Jump, Capture the Flag, and Key Quest, then a Free Create mode.

Benefits

For students

  • Students design their own games with blocks.
  • Students move from blocks toward real coding words.
  • Students can ask a built in tutor for help.

For teachers

  • The Kodable site offers 11 free lesson plans.
  • Teachers add students through Google Classroom, Clever, or a class code.
  • Teachers assign courses and track the work.

For schools

  • One Kodable page says Creator is free for teachers in classrooms.
  • Another snippet says unlimited CatBot needs Premium+, and the plan details are unclear.
  • Earlier notes said school plans are paid. Confirm the price with the vendor.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

A search snippet reports a built in AI tutor named CatBot. It generates text answers to student questions and is capped at 5 questions per day. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Students can ask it for help and then apply the answer to their own game.

Privacy and safety

Student accounts are required, and the internet is needed at all times. Work and accounts live in the vendor's cloud. The privacy page says the game does not collect personally identifiable information from children. The CatBot tutor accepts student questions according to a search snippet, and its AI provider and the handling of those questions are unknown. A teacher should read the privacy terms. A teacher should also decide whether students may use CatBot and review what it says.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Finish the four missions of the High Flyer course and explain what each mission taught.
  2. Use the Debug mission to find and fix a mistake in a game.
  3. Design a level in Free Create mode and write the rules.
  4. Swap games with a partner and give one piece of feedback.
  5. Ask CatBot a question about a block, then test the answer in your own code.
