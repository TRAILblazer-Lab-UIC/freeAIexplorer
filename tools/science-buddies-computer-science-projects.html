Science Buddies computer science projects

A free library of computer science projects sorted by grade level.

FREEComputingUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

Science Buddies has a library of free computer science projects sorted by grade level from elementary to high school. Projects use languages and tools such as Scratch, Python, JavaScript, and the micro:bit. A project tool can filter by language and grade. Pages are web based with video walkthroughs on Scratch projects.

Benefits

For students

  • Students pick a project at their own level.
  • Students follow video walkthroughs on Scratch projects.
  • Students can choose Scratch, Python, or micro:bit work.

For teachers

  • Projects are sorted by grade and language.
  • Teachers can fill a unit with ready projects.
  • The official page returned an error to the research tool. This entry relies on search snippets.

For schools

  • Projects are free.
  • Hardware such as the micro:bit Go Bundle is sold separately.
  • Prices for kits were not found.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in this library. The projects build skills such as sequencing and logic. Students follow steps and then change the project to make it their own. That practice builds a base for later work with AI tools.

The page is a library of many projects, and the research could not read it. Site accounts are optional according to the research notes. The research did not read the privacy policy. The materials do not state where student work goes. Some projects link to outside tools such as Scratch. A teacher should check each tool and the site's terms before students sign in.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Use the grade filter to pick a project and follow its steps.
  2. Watch a Scratch walkthrough and then add a feature of your own.
  3. Try a Python project and change one value to see the result.
  4. Build a micro:bit project if the class has the hardware.
  5. Compare two projects and describe what each one teaches.
