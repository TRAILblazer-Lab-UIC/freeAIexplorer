Edison lesson plans and barcode activity book

Free Edison lesson plans and a barcode book of seven no-code robot activities.

ComputingRobotics

Overview

Edison offers free lesson plans, teacher guides and student worksheets for EdBlocks, EdScratch, EdPy and EdCreate. A separate activity book contains 15 barcodes that the robot scans to run preset programs. The book has seven activities, including clap-controlled driving, obstacle avoidance, line tracking and sumo. The barcode activities need no coding.

Benefits

For students

  • Students run robot programs by scanning barcodes.
  • Students try sumo, line tracking and obstacle avoidance.
  • Students move on to coding when ready.

For teachers

  • Lessons come as PDF and editable Word files.
  • Volunteer translations are available.
  • An Arizona STEM Acceleration Project lesson uses the barcode programs with fifth graders.

For schools

  • The lessons and the book are free. The Edison robot is sold separately and is required.
  • No account, internet or app is needed for the barcode book.
  • Printing needs matte paper and a printer.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

These lessons have no AI. The robot reads a barcode and runs a preset program. Students build skills in sequencing, sensors and logic.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed for the barcode book, and scanning needs no screen or internet. Writing programs for the remote barcodes and updating V3 firmware need a computer and the internet through the EdBlocks site. The research found no student data collected, and no privacy policy applies to the printed book. A teacher should note that no independent review of the book was found and should test each barcode first.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Scan a barcode and describe what program the robot ran.
  2. Try clap-controlled driving and explain how the robot listens.
  3. Follow an EdBlocks lesson plan and write a short program.
  4. Compare a barcode activity to a block program for the same task.
  5. Use the worksheets to plan a program before coding it.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Build a robot challenge from the sumo barcode and test it.
  2. Test obstacle avoidance with different objects and record the results.
  3. Try line tracking on a drawn path and describe how well it works.
  4. Use an Arizona STEM lesson idea with barcode programs.
  5. Redesign a course after a failed run and record the change.
