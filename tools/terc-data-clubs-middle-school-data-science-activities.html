TERC Data Clubs middle school data science activities

Three data science modules where middle schoolers explore topics they care about.

Overview

Data Clubs is a TERC program for middle school youth in rural and urban out of school settings. It gives learners simple tools to visualize and analyze data on topics they care about. The page lists three modules built on large datasets from government agencies and research groups. A search snippet says TERC lists the project as no longer active.

Benefits

For students

  • Students explore data on topics such as time use, ticks, and injuries.
  • Students use simple tools to graph and study large datasets.
  • Students ask their own questions of real data.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Math
  1. Graph a dataset and describe the pattern you see.
  2. Compare two groups in a dataset and say which is larger.
  3. Write a question that data could answer and test it.
  4. Explain how a graph could mislead a reader.
  5. Present your findings with a chart.
How this can be used for Science
  1. Study ticks and Lyme disease data and describe where cases are highest.
  2. Look at how the numbers change over time.
  3. Make a claim about the data and cite your evidence.
  4. List what extra data you would want next.
  5. Share your findings with the club.
How this can be used for Health and PE
  1. Explore injuries on and off the field and describe where they happen most.
  2. Compare injury data across different activities.
  3. Suggest one safety tip that the data supports.
  4. Make a poster of your findings.
  5. Discuss what the data cannot tell you.
