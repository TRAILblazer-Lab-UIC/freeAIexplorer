Day of AI: Grade 6 Behind the Screens, How AI Works

A grade 6 Day of AI unit where students train an image classifier.

AIGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This grade 6 unit runs about 5 class periods of 50 minutes. Lessons are Is It AI, Training a Machine, Where AI Knowledge Comes From, and AI, Work, and Who Pays the Price. One lesson covers what happens at the moments an AI gets an answer wrong. Students train and test an image classification model with labeled examples. They also look at text generating AI, bias, and effects on work.

Benefits

For students

  • Students train and test an image classifier.
  • Students learn why AI makes mistakes and where bias comes from.
  • Students discuss how AI changes work and who bears the cost.

For teachers

  • A slide deck and educator guide are on the site.
  • Setup takes only a few minutes.
  • The lessons are easy to teach with no coding.

For schools

  • The unit is free.
  • Students need a browser and internet access.
  • A teacher registers for a login link, and IT has little to do.

Where is the AI in this product?

The unit teaches supervised learning, image classification, text generation, and bias. The vendor does not say where the training tool runs. Students label example images, train a classifier, and test it. They then use the results to judge when an AI answer can be trusted.

Classifier
A classifier is an AI model that sorts inputs into groups.
Image classification
Image classification is an AI task that puts a single label on a whole picture.
Labeled data
Labeled data is data that comes with the correct answer attached.
Model accuracy
Accuracy is the share of answers an AI model gets right.
Model training
Training is the process where an AI model learns from examples.
Supervised learning
Supervised learning is machine learning from labeled examples.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Decide which everyday tools use AI in the lesson Is It AI and explain each choice.
  2. Train an image classifier with labeled examples and test it on new pictures.
  3. Feed the model a poor set of examples and record how the accuracy changes.
  4. Compare how a text generating AI answers two different questions.
  5. Discuss one job that AI could change and who would be affected.
