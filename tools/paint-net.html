Paint.NET

A free Windows image and photo editor with layers and effects.

FREEComputingStays on the device

Overview

Paint.NET is an image and photo editor for Windows. It has layers, unlimited undo, effects, and plugins. It began as a free alternative to Microsoft Paint. Students use it to edit photos and make digital art.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn layers and adjustments in a simple program.
  • Students can undo any number of steps and try new ideas.
  • Students can apply effects to photos and drawings.

For teachers

  • The program is easy to learn for most teachers.
  • A 2007 post on the Paint.NET blog describes a middle school teacher whose students used it beyond Microsoft Paint.
  • Plugins add extra tools when needed.

For schools

  • The download is free. The Microsoft Store version is a paid purchase.
  • It runs on the local computer.
  • It works on Windows only. It does not run on Chromebooks or Macs.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in Paint.NET. It works through manual editing tools. Students build an eye for detail and learn how layers stack, which links to the idea of building an image in steps.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the internet is used at setup. The privacy policy link returned a 404 error. The research read no policy. The materials do not state whether the program sends usage data, and the research could not confirm whether update checks send data. A teacher should ask IT to check this before a full rollout.

How this can be used for Visual Arts
  1. Edit a photo with layers and describe each change, as middle school students did in the blog report.
  2. Create a poster by combining text and an image on separate layers.
  3. Apply two effects to a drawing and compare the results.
  4. Use a plugin to try a new brush or effect.
  5. Make a before and after pair for a class gallery.
