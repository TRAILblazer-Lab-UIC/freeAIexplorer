Amii AI Literacy for K-12 Educators

A free AI literacy course and learning kits for K-12 educators from Amii in Alberta.

FREEAISends student data to a companyGood for AI beginners

Overview

Amii offers a free open-access AI literacy course for K-12 educators called AI for All Essentials for Educators. It has bite-sized 30-minute lessons. Chapters 1 to 3 are available and chapters 4 to 6 are coming soon. Amii also offers K-12 AI Learning Kits with teacher handbooks, lesson plans, slide decks, assessments, and videos aligned to Alberta.

Benefits

For students

  • Students benefit from teachers who understand how AI learns and decides.
  • Students can use kits made for the elementary classroom and for junior high.
  • Students learn about AI risks and ethics.

For teachers

  • Lessons are short, about 30 minutes each.
  • The course comes in English and French.
  • The kits include handbooks, slide decks, learning tasks, and assessments.

For schools

  • The educator course is free.
  • Course packages let school boards load content into an LMS.
  • The page does not state a cost for kits.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This is a course and kit set about AI and does not run an AI tool for students. The course runs on the vendor's cloud. It covers how AI learns and makes decisions, AI risks, generative AI, and ethics. The vendor does not say where any classroom tools run, and the lessons build the skill of explaining how AI learns from data.

Privacy and safety

The course site states that it has no mandatory logins or personal data collection. That is the vendor's own statement. The course runs on the vendor's cloud and needs the internet. Learning kits are requested through an application form. The research did not review how that form handles data. A teacher should check the form terms. A teacher outside Canada should check that the Alberta standards fit the local plan.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Use Chapter 2 to explain to the class how AI learns from examples.
  2. Role play how an AI makes a decision and list what data it needs.
  3. Discuss the risks and realities of AI from Chapter 3 and share class views.
  4. Make a poster that explains generative AI in plain words.
  5. Use an elementary kit activity to build a simple model by hand.
