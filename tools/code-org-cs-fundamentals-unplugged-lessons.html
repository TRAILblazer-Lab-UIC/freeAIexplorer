Code.org CS Fundamentals Unplugged lessons

A free library of over 40 unplugged lessons from Code.org CS Fundamentals.

ComputingUnplugged

Overview

Code.org offers a library of more than 40 unplugged lessons drawn from its CS Fundamentals courses. The activities run without computers in the room. Each can be used alone or alongside related computer science lessons. Code.org uses unplugged lessons to introduce each idea before the online puzzles.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn computing ideas through games and group tasks.
  • Students try lessons such as My Robotic Friends and Digital Trails.
  • Students meet each idea before they see it on a screen.

For teachers

  • The library has more than 40 lessons to choose from.
  • Each lesson stands alone or pairs with online lessons.
  • Lessons come from the CS Fundamentals courses.

For schools

  • The lessons are free.
  • The activities need no devices in the room.
  • IT staff have little to do beyond allowing the downloads.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This library has no AI. It builds computing skills such as sequencing, debugging, and understanding data. Students practice these skills with paper, cups, and group games.

Privacy and safety

The lessons run on paper, and no student data is collected. The materials do not say whether a Code.org account is needed. A teacher should check the account terms before students use the online lessons. The internet is used only to open or download the lesson plans.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Play My Robotic Friends and write a program for a classmate to follow.
  2. Debug a program that fails and find the step to fix.
  3. Explore Digital Trails and discuss what information a device keeps.
  4. Match each unplugged lesson to the online puzzle that follows it.
  5. Teach one unplugged activity to a younger class.
