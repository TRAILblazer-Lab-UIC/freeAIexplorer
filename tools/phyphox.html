phyphox

A free phone app that turns built-in sensors into a physics lab.

Computing

Overview

phyphox is a free app from RWTH Aachen University. It turns a smartphone into a physics lab. Experiments read sensors such as the accelerometer and microphone. Students collect data and export it in common formats.

Benefits

For students

  • Students run real experiments with a phone they already use.
  • Students see live data from sensors.
  • Students can export results for a spreadsheet.

For teachers

  • A remote control feature lets a second device with a browser view and control a running experiment.
  • A web editor lets teachers build custom experiments.
  • A teacher blog finds the interface dense for younger students. Plan strong guidance.

For schools

  • The app is free and open source.
  • It needs no internet during use.
  • The paper says one wifi router can serve many students for remote control.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in phyphox. It records sensor data on the phone. Students build skills in sensors and data as they collect and read graphs.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed and the app works with no internet. The privacy policy says the app does not explicitly collect personal data. Measurements stay on the device unless exported. Before an experiment sends data over a network, the app lists what could be sent, such as a device ID, microphone recordings, location, and sensor data. Third party experiments fall under that party's policy. The website keeps IP addresses in server logs for up to 90 days. A teacher should check what students share when they export or send data.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Measure acceleration while walking and describe the graph.
  2. Use the microphone to record the sound of a tuning fork or a tone.
  3. Drop a phone onto a soft surface and study the acceleration data.
  4. Compare two students' data from the same motion.
  5. Export a data set and write a short lab conclusion.
