CodeGrade

A paid coding platform with an autograder, browser editor, and AI assistant inside the LMS.

FREEAIComputingSends student data to a company

Overview

CodeGrade is a commercial platform for teaching programming. It combines an autograder, a browser code editor with an Ubuntu terminal, and an AI assistant. It runs inside Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, or Brightspace and supports more than 175 languages. It is aimed mainly at university and advanced secondary computer science courses.

Benefits

For students

  • Students submit and resubmit code in the browser and see test results.
  • Students get line-level feedback from instructors.
  • Students can ask a configurable AI assistant for help.

For teachers

  • A block-based test builder makes autograding easier.
  • Rubrics, inline feedback, and a plagiarism checker support grading.
  • Teachers can see AI assistant conversation logs.

For schools

  • The homepage says CodeGrade is free for up to 50 students with no credit card. That may suit one K-12 class.
  • Paid tiers were not listed on the page read.
  • IT will likely need to approve the LTI link with the school LMS.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

CodeGrade has an AI coding assistant for students and AI-graded prompt exercises. The AI runs in the vendor's cloud. Teachers configure the assistant and can read the conversation logs. Students use it for hints and then rewrite the code in their own way.

Privacy and safety

Students sign in through the LMS, and the internet is needed at all times. The vendor collects IP addresses, full names, email addresses, LMS IDs, code submissions, and AI assistant conversation logs. The policy says data is processed only within the European Union and is not given to third parties other than hosting companies. A separate FERPA statement says CodeGrade acts as a school official and deletes records on request. The policy does not mention AI providers, and the research did not confirm where assistant prompts are processed. A teacher should review the privacy terms with the school before students use it.
