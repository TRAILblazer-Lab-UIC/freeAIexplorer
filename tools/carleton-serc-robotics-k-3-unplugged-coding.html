Carleton SERC Robotics K-3 Unplugged Coding

A SERC page with slides, assessments, and unplugged maze activities for grades K-3.

ComputingRoboticsUnplugged

Overview

This SERC page by T. Moffatt offers strategies and resources for teaching robotics to grades K to 3. The downloads are a K-3 Robotics PowerPoint, a teacher needs pre-assessment, a post-assessment, and a Word file of unplugged activity mazes. The page was published in August 2021. The page does not describe the mazes in detail.

Benefits

For students

  • Students practice coding ideas on paper mazes.
  • Students need no screens or robots.
  • Students work at their own level in grades K to 3.

For teachers

  • Pre and post assessments help teachers see progress.
  • A slide deck gives a ready start.
  • Teachers print the mazes and are ready to go.

For schools

  • Everything is free.
  • No devices are needed in class.
  • IT is not involved.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The materials have no AI. Students build sequencing and logic skills as they work through unplugged mazes. They learn to give step by step directions.

Privacy and safety

The materials are paper and slides, and the internet is used to download them. The page does not mention accounts or state a data policy. The research did not open the slides or the activity file. The page records no provenance for this item. A teacher should read the files before sharing them with students.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Trace a path through a printed maze and write the steps in order.
  2. Give a partner step by step directions to solve a maze.
  3. Find the bug in a set of directions that hits a wall.
  4. Rewrite a long set of directions using fewer steps.
  5. Make a new maze and trade it with a classmate.
How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Plan a path before moving and compare it to the actual path.
  2. Test a plan on a maze and record what to fix.
  3. Design a maze with one obstacle and share it.
  4. Explain how a robot would follow the same steps.
  5. Take the pre and post assessments and talk about what changed.
