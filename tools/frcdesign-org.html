FRCDesign.org

A free learning guide for designing FIRST Robotics Competition robots in Onshape.

FREERoboticsSends student data to a company

Overview

FRCDesign.org is a learning guide and technical reference for designing FIRST Robotics Competition robots in Onshape. It has a Learning Course, a Design Handbook, mechanism examples and CAD best practices. A 2025 ASEE conference paper describes it as an open source, free course for advanced CAD and robot design in high school robotics. Students model robots in the Onshape cloud CAD tool.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn real CAD skills used by robotics teams.
  • Students study mechanism examples with breakdowns.
  • Students practice design ideas before they build.

For teachers

  • An Educator's Guide section supports teaching.
  • The Design Handbook works as a reference wiki.
  • Later course stages were still marked work in progress in the paper.

For schools

  • The course is free.
  • Students need Onshape accounts and the internet.
  • IT help is likely needed for account setup.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This resource has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is modeling in 3D. Students design parts in a computer program that checks their work.

Privacy and safety

The FRCDesign.org site is a static reference. The CAD work happens in Onshape, a cloud service. Student accounts are needed and the internet is always needed. Onshape stores the account and the CAD models. The research did not read the Onshape privacy policy or its terms for students under 13. A teacher should read the Onshape privacy terms and get school approval before creating accounts. Check the age rules for accounts.

How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Complete the first CAD lessons in the Learning Course.
  2. Model a simple mechanism from the examples.
  3. Follow CAD best practices when you name and organize parts.
  4. Use the Design Handbook to plan a robot subsystem.
  5. Share your design with a teammate for feedback.
