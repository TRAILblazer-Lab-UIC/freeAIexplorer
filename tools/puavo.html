Puavo

School device and account management software with a Linux desktop for schools.

Computing

Overview

Puavo is management software with an operating system for schools. Puavo Web is a web application for managing user accounts and devices across organisations and schools. Puavo OS is a Debian based desktop system for primary and secondary schools. The Finnish company Opinsys develops it, and it is used in Finnish schools and a few schools in Germany and Switzerland.

Benefits

For students

  • Students sign in to a familiar desktop with GNOME, Firefox, and LibreOffice.
  • Students can use a restricted exam mode.
  • Students can use guest logins.

For teachers

  • Teachers do not manage the system. District IT handles it.
  • The desktop works on older computers.
  • The documentation is partly Finnish.

For schools

  • The source code is free software.
  • It runs on most PC hardware, including computers up to about ten years old.
  • Opinsys sells consulting and services, and prices are not published.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in Puavo. It manages accounts and devices. Students build practical skills in logging in and managing files on a shared system.

Privacy and safety

Puavo runs on the school's own servers and uses accounts that administrators create for all users. The hosting depends on the deployment and the research did not confirm who hosts it. The materials do not state what student data is collected or whether the internet is needed. No privacy policy was found. A teacher should ask IT who can see student accounts and how data is stored.
