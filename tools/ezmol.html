EzMol

A web wizard for viewing protein structures and making images of them.

Computing

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

EzMol is a web based wizard for viewing protein and nucleic acid structures and producing images of them. It is built on 3Dmol.js and works through eight steps from upload to render. Users upload a PDB file, open a saved EZM file, or enter a four letter PDB ID to download from the RCSB Protein Data Bank. It is aimed at UK A-level students, roughly US grades 11 to 12.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see proteins in three dimensions.
  • Students can style residues, side chains, surfaces, and labels.
  • Students export images for reports.

For teachers

  • A linked tutorial covers primary to quaternary protein structure with haemoglobin.
  • A SAPS molecular modelling practical is linked to the A-level practical skills endorsement.
  • A schools portal covers EzMol and BioBlox.

For schools

  • The tool is free.
  • It needs only a modern browser with WebGL.
  • No install is needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in EzMol. The tool builds data and pattern skills. Students look at a structure and spot features such as helices and surfaces.

Privacy and safety

The site is always online and no account is needed. The terms page says Google Analytics monitors visitor traffic. The page does not describe how uploaded structures are stored or handled. A batch note said uploads are processed on a university server, and the research did not confirm that. A teacher should check the site terms before students upload files. A teacher should also use public PDB IDs for class work.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Enter the PDB ID for haemoglobin and describe what you see.
  2. Compare primary to quaternary structure with the tutorial.
  3. Change the style to show side chains and note the difference.
  4. Label a feature and explain its job.
  5. Export an image and write a caption.
