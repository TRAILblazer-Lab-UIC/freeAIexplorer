Hello Ruby

Picture books and free printable activities that introduce young children to computing.

ComputingUnplugged

Overview

Hello Ruby is a picture book series by Linda Liukas that introduces computing ideas to young children. The official page lists five titles. They are Adventures in Coding, Journey Inside the Computer, Expedition to the Internet, Robot's First Day of School, and a bind-up. It also lists free printable activities for schools and parents, a free educator's guide, and one-minute videos.

Benefits

For students

  • Students meet computing ideas through stories.
  • Students try low-tech activities that build logic.
  • Students at ages 4 to 10 can use the materials per one listing.

For teachers

  • A free educator's guide is open ended.
  • Printable worksheets are free.
  • One reviewer says the books work best with an adult guiding the activities.

For schools

  • The activities are free, and one book is listed at 19.99 dollars.
  • The activities need only a printer.
  • No devices or accounts are needed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This series has no AI. It builds logic and computing vocabulary. Students learn how computers work through stories and low-tech puzzles.

Privacy and safety

The books and printed activities keep student work on paper. No account is needed, and the internet is used only to download the activities. The videos are on YouTube and the research did not review them. The research did not read a privacy policy for this item. The official page gives no ages. A teacher should check that the content fits the class and preview the videos before showing them.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Do a printable logic activity and explain your steps.
  2. Draw a picture of how a computer works after a read aloud.
  3. Write a set of steps for a simple task such as brushing teeth.
  4. Spot a pattern in an activity and describe the rule.
  5. Watch a one-minute video and show the idea with paper.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Listen to a Hello Ruby story and retell it in order.
  2. Draw a character from the book and write a sentence about her.
  3. Predict what happens next in the story.
  4. Write a short story of your own about a robot.
  5. Discuss which part of the story taught a computing idea.
