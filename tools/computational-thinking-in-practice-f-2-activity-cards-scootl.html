Computational thinking in practice F-2 activity cards (Scootle)

Printable activity cards that teach six computational thinking ideas through daily routines.

FREEUnpluggedNo device needed

Overview

This is a printable set of activity cards for parents and teachers. It offers simple ideas for each of six aspects of computational thinking. It covers Foundation to Year 2 and draws on everyday routines such as following a recipe, getting dressed, and following game rules. The Digital Technologies Hub lists it as a lesson idea PDF under CC BY 4.0.

Benefits

For students

  • Students practice thinking skills with routines they already know.
  • Students need no devices.
  • Students can continue the activities at home.

For teachers

  • The cards cover six aspects of computational thinking.
  • They match Australian Curriculum Digital Technologies codes.
  • Teachers can share them with parents.

For schools

  • The download is free.
  • It is paper based and needs no devices.
  • No IT help is needed.

Privacy and safety

The cards are on paper and collect no student data. The materials do not state whether an account is needed. The internet is used only to download the PDF. The Scootle page returned an access error. A teacher should use the Hub listing to get the file. A teacher should also check the license before sharing copies.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Tell the steps of getting dressed in order.
  2. Follow a simple recipe and name each step.
  3. Spot a pattern in the rules of a game.
  4. Break a routine such as packing a bag into smaller parts.
  5. Fix a set of steps that is out of order.
