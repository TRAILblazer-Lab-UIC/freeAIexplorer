NAO (Aldebaran)

A humanoid robot promoted for children with autism and for coding lessons.

Overview

NAO is a humanoid robot promoted for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. The special education page lists emotional recognition and expression, motor skills, communication games, and daily routine support. Programming uses Choregraphe software. The aldebaran.com address now redirects to maxtronics.com. Aldebaran entered receivership in 2025 and Maxvision bought its core assets in July 2025.

Benefits

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor lists emotion recognition and speech interaction. The materials do not say where the AI runs. Students and teachers use Choregraphe to build the robot's behaviors. The robot responds to speech in the activities.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. The vendor does not say whether the internet is needed. NAO has a camera and a microphone. The special education page does not cover privacy, and generative AI features may use cloud services. The research did not verify that. How this can be used for Special Education and Accessibility
  1. Use a communication game to practice greetings with the robot.
  2. Practice naming emotions by asking the robot to act out a feeling.
  3. Follow the robot's daily routine steps on a picture schedule.
  4. Copy the robot's movements to practice motor skills.
  5. Take turns giving the robot simple spoken commands.
How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Build a short program in Choregraphe that makes the robot wave.
  2. Add a speech step to the program.
  3. Test how the robot responds to different phrases.
  4. Change the order of steps and predict the result.
  5. Present a short robot show to the class.
