Study tools by kennedyraju55 (Ollama and Gemma 3)

Five free Python study tools that run on a local model through Ollama.

FREEAIUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is a set of five open source Python tools by one developer. They are Study Buddy Bot, Textbook Summarizer, Language Learning Bot, Research Paper QA, and Reading List Manager. They use Streamlit screens and a local language model through Ollama. The article names Gemma 3 and the Study Buddy README names Gemma 4.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can make quizzes, study plans, and flashcards from their notes.
  • Students can summarize a PDF chapter.
  • Students can practice a language and get grammar correction.

For teachers

  • Five tools cover study, reading, language, and research needs.
  • The author states that student interactions never leave the device.
  • Setup is technical and suits a teacher who likes to tinker.

For schools

  • The tools are free.
  • A laptop that can run a local model is needed. A related article says 8 GB of RAM or more.
  • IT will likely need to approve the install.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a language model that runs on the device through Ollama. It writes quizzes, summarizes chapters, corrects grammar, and answers questions about papers. Students use the output to study and then check it against their own notes. The tools are a personal project.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed. The internet is used at setup. The author says interactions never leave the device and that Study Buddy Bot keeps history only in memory. These are the author's own claims and the research did not verify them. There is no privacy policy. A teacher should check the answers. A local model can make mistakes. A teacher should also try each tool before students use it.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Summarize a textbook chapter with the tool and compare it with your own summary.
  2. Ask Study Buddy Bot for a quiz on a short story and take it.
  3. Make flashcards for vocabulary words from a novel.
  4. Use Reading List Manager to plan a month of reading.
  5. Ask Research Paper QA a question about an article and check the answer against the text.
How this can be used for World Languages
  1. Write a paragraph in the target language and read the grammar corrections.
  2. Ask the Language Learning Bot to quiz you on ten new words.
  3. Practice a short dialogue with the bot and note mistakes.
  4. Keep a list of corrections and review it each week.
  5. Compare the bot's grammar advice with your textbook.
