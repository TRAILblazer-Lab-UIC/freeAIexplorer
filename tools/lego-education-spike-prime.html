LEGO Education SPIKE Prime

A retired LEGO Education hub kit for middle and high school with blocks and Python.

ComputingRoboticsStays on the device

Overview

SPIKE Prime is a hub-based LEGO Education robotics kit for middle and high school, coded with blocks or Python in the SPIKE App. The product page marks it as retired, with company sales ending June 30 2026. The app is supported through June 30 2031 with no new features after June 30 2026. LEGO Education Computer Science and AI is the stated successor line.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build robots and code them with blocks or Python.
  • Students can move from blocks to text code.
  • Students use sensors in science investigations.

For teachers

  • The app runs on web, iOS, Android, Chromebook, Windows and macOS.
  • Teacher Resources lessons stay available until June 30 2031.
  • The kit is eligible for FIRST LEGO League through the 2027-2028 season.

For schools

  • The official page lists no price. The kit is now resale only.
  • Projects save locally and no accounts are needed.
  • Spare parts are stocked for two years after the last sale.

Privacy and safety

The SPIKE App privacy policy says no account or login is required. Written or coded work and voice recordings are stored only on the device. Anonymous app performance data may be gathered in some regions and can be turned off. The app reads the device IP address to reach LEGO servers for updates and region. The policy does not mention AI lessons or COPPA, and local running of AI lessons was not confirmed. A teacher should check that school devices can pair with the hub.
