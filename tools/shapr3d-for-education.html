Shapr3D for Education

A CAD modeling app with a free one-year Pro license for schools.

FREEComputingSends student data to a company

Overview

Shapr3D is a CAD app for modeling on iPad, Mac, Windows, and iPhone. Its education offer gives active students and staff at accredited institutions a free educational version of Shapr3D Pro. The license lasts one year and can be renewed while the user is eligible. Students model parts and designs in 3D.

Benefits

For students

  • Students get the Pro version at no cost.
  • Students model 3D objects with an iPad and a pencil or a computer.
  • Students practice product design skills.

For teachers

  • A middle school technology teacher reported using it on an iPad for class.
  • Reviewers say it suits product design more than architecture.
  • Each student must apply. Plan the requests ahead.

For schools

  • The education license is free. Use must be educational and non commercial.
  • Shapr3D reviews requests within 3 business days.
  • IT may need to help with school emails and device installs.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Shapr3D has no AI. It is a modeling tool. Students build skills in spatial thinking and in planning a design step by step.

Privacy and safety

An account is required, and the internet is needed at all times. The vendor collects name, email, password, IP address, device details, approximate location, site activity and proof of school enrollment. The policy lists sharing with analytics, advertising and other providers. Data is mainly stored in Ireland and the European Economic Area. The policy does not address children or minors. A teacher should review the privacy policy with IT before students apply.

How this can be used for Engineering and Design
  1. Model a simple object such as a phone stand, as a middle school class did on iPad.
  2. Measure a real item and model it at the same size.
  3. Redesign a part to make it stronger and compare the versions.
  4. Draw a sketch and extrude it into a 3D shape.
  5. Present a design and explain each choice.
