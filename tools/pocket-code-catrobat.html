Pocket Code (Catrobat)

A free mobile app for building games with visual blocks on a phone.

Computing

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

Pocket Code is a free open source mobile app for building games and apps with a Scratch like block language called Catrobat. Students build programs directly on a phone or tablet. The Android version is called Catroid. An online community site hosts sample programs.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can code on a phone or tablet.
  • Students make games, animations, and apps.
  • Students can look at sample programs from the community.

For teachers

  • The block language is familiar to teachers who know Scratch.
  • Teachers install the app on shared devices.
  • Both Android and iOS apps are available.

For schools

  • The app is free and the code is open source.
  • The apps are on Google Play and the Apple App Store.
  • Chromebooks that run Android apps may work, but this is untested.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI in Pocket Code. Students build programs from blocks. They build sequencing and logic skills.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which accounts students need or where data goes. An account may be needed to share to the community site, and the research did not verify this. The privacy policy could not be read because of a certificate error. A teacher should check the privacy terms and decide whether students should share publicly.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Build a small game that moves a sprite across the screen.
  2. Add a block that plays a sound on touch.
  3. Use a loop to repeat an action.
  4. Open a sample program and describe how it works.
  5. Test a classmate's game and suggest one change.
