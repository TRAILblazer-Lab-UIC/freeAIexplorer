Model AI Assignments: AI and Employment Lesson Plan

A one hour discussion lesson on how AI may change work and jobs.

AIUnpluggedGood for AI beginners

Overview

This one hour lesson is by Cunyan Ma, Daniella DiPaola, and Cynthia Breazeal. It is a reflective look at how AI may affect employment. Students discuss four possible effects, which are replacing workers, augmenting work, creating jobs, and emphasizing human-centered skills. The 2023 Writers' Guild of America strike is the main case study. It is written for grades 9 to 12 and all subjects.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see AI as a system that affects people and work.
  • Students role-play workers or decision-makers who draft AI policies.
  • Students reflect on their own future work.

For teachers

  • The pack has a teaching script and slides.
  • The page says the lesson is non-technical and suits teachers without an AI background.
  • Two worksheets support the discussion.

For schools

  • The download is free.
  • The lesson needs no hardware and no internet.
  • No student data is collected.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The lesson has no running AI program. It treats AI as a socio-technical system and looks at labor and ethics. Students study how AI might replace, assist, or create work. They use the case of the writers' strike to form and defend policies.

Privacy and safety

The lesson runs on paper, no account is needed, and no internet is needed. The materials describe no student data collection. A teacher should prepare for strong opinions about jobs and fairness. A teacher should also set respectful rules for the role play.

Ideas by subject

Select a subject to see five classroom ideas.

How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Role-play a worker or a decision-maker and draft an AI policy for a workplace.
  2. Fill in the infographic worksheet on the four effects of AI on work.
  3. Study the Writers' Guild strike and list what each side asked for.
  4. Compare which jobs AI might replace and which it might assist.
  5. Write a take-home reflection on how AI could change a job you might want.
How this can be used for English and Language Arts
  1. Write a short statement from the point of view of a striking writer.
  2. Complete the demands and responses worksheet in clear sentences.
  3. Write a persuasive essay on one of the four effects of AI on work.
  4. Prepare a short talk on your policy ideas.
  5. Compare two news reports on the strike and note the difference in tone.
