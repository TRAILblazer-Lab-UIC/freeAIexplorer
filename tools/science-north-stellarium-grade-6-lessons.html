Science North Stellarium grade 6 lessons

A two-part grade 6 sky science lesson series built on Stellarium Web.

Computing

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

This is a two-part Grade 6 Earth and Space Science lesson series on Stellarium Web. Students explore the night sky at different places on Earth. They find the Milky Way, the Orion Nebula, planets, and southern constellations. A handout with exercises goes with the lessons.

Benefits

For students

  • Students see how the sky looks different from different latitudes.
  • Students learn why red light is used for star watching.
  • Students without internet can still answer the questions using the pictures provided.

For teachers

  • The handout gives ready-made exercises for the lesson.
  • The Part 2 offline lesson plan works on paper with no device.
  • Both parts are listed in English and French.

For schools

  • The lessons are free.
  • No student account is needed.
  • The paper route needs no devices and no IT help.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This lesson series has no AI. Stellarium Web is a planetarium that draws the sky. Students build pattern spotting skills as they match star groups to constellations and track how planets move.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed. The lesson uses the Stellarium Web site in a browser, and the materials mention no accounts. The research did not read the site's own data handling. The offline route uses printed pictures. A teacher should check that stellarium-web.org is not blocked on school devices and review the site's privacy terms with IT.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Open Stellarium Web and find Polaris from two different locations, then compare how high it sits in the sky.
  2. Locate the Milky Way and the Orion Nebula and sketch where each one appears.
  3. Track a planet across several nights and describe its path.
  4. Look for southern constellations and explain why northern viewers cannot see them.
  5. Use the offline pictures to answer handout questions and explain the answers to a partner.
