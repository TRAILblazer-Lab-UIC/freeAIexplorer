RAISE Playground

A free block-based platform for building projects with machine learning models.

FREEAIComputingRoboticsUnverifiedGood for AI beginners

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

RAISE Playground is a block-based programming platform for using machine learning models, robotics, and AI engines to make projects. The site lists image classification, text classification, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, music generation, affective computing, gesture recognition, social robotics, and microcontroller robotics. It links to three curricula named RAICA, Dancing with AI, and How to Train Your Robot. The curricula are written for middle school.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build projects with real machine learning models.
  • Students try ideas from music generation to gesture recognition.
  • Students can link projects to robots and microcontrollers.

For teachers

  • Three linked curricula give ready lessons.
  • The materials use a CC-BY-NC license.
  • A search snippet says the RAICA Picture This module targets ages 11 to 14.

For schools

  • The platform is free. Commercial use requires contacting the team.
  • It needs the internet at all times.
  • Account requirements were not confirmed.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The platform offers image classification, text classification, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, music generation, affective computing, and gesture recognition. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students use blocks to train and test models. They then use the results in a project.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state which accounts students need or where student data goes. The platform needs the internet at all times. The pages read have no privacy policy link, and the Terms of Service page returned 404. The research did not confirm whether the machine learning models run in the browser or in the cloud. A teacher should check the account rules and ask what happens to images and sounds. A teacher should test the first project before class.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Start the RAICA Picture This module and train an image classifier.
  2. Test the classifier on new pictures and note the errors.
  3. Train a text classifier on short class sentences.
  4. Use gesture recognition to control a sprite.
  5. Explain how more examples change the model.
How this can be used for Music
  1. Use the Dancing with AI curriculum to make a dance for a robot.
  2. Try music generation and describe the style of the result.
  3. Use gesture recognition to start a beat with a body move.
  4. Compare an AI made tune with a tune a student wrote.
  5. Discuss who should get credit for AI made music.
