MolView

A free web app that shows molecules as interactive 3D models.

FREEComputingUnverified

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

MolView is a free web app for chemistry education. Students draw a structural formula or search PubChem, RCSB, and other databases. They then view the molecule as an interactive 3D model. It also shows mass, IR, and predicted NMR spectra, protein structures, and crystal unit cells.

Benefits

For students

  • Students turn a 2D drawing into a 3D model.
  • Students explore spectra, proteins, and crystals.
  • Students export images or MOL, PDB, and CIF files.

For teachers

  • Teachers use it in organic chemistry curricula, according to a EuChemS magazine article.
  • The tool has several 3D render engines.
  • It needs no install or account.

For schools

  • The app is free.
  • It works on Chromebooks in a browser with WebGL.
  • IT work is minimal.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This app has no AI. It converts drawings to models using chemistry data. Students build skills in spatial reasoning and in reading data.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the internet is needed at all times. Searches send queries to public chemical databases. Drawn structures are handled in the browser. The research found no privacy policy and did not review analytics or cookies. A teacher should check the site's privacy practices and preview the databases students search.

How this can be used for Science
  1. Draw water and view it in 3D.
  2. Compare the 3D shapes of two molecules and describe the difference.
  3. Search a medicine and list its parts.
  4. View a protein and name its main features.
  5. Export a model as an image and use it in a lab report.
