Minecraft Education lessons library

A web library of lessons for Minecraft Education that can be searched.

Computing

Overview

This is a web library of lessons for Minecraft Education. A search snippet says it hosts hundreds of standards aligned lessons. Teachers can search by student age, subject, and standards. Playing most lessons needs a licensed Microsoft school account.

Benefits

For students

  • Students learn inside a game world they know.
  • Lessons cover topics from Washington state history to ancient history, according to one review.
  • Students explore worlds that fit a class topic.

For teachers

  • The library can be searched by age, subject, and standards.
  • Demo lessons are available without an account.
  • The lesson pages are free to browse.

For schools

  • Most lessons need a Minecraft Education license.
  • Microsoft 365 Education A3 and A5 plans include it.
  • The IT administrator assigns the license.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor materials do not describe any AI. The library offers lessons that students play inside Minecraft Education. Teachers should treat these details as provisional. The official page could not be read.

Privacy and safety

Students and teachers need a school Microsoft 365 account, and the data goes to the vendor. A search snippet says the demo needs no account but cannot save worlds. The research did not read the privacy policy. The lessons page and support article timed out or returned a 403. The research did not confirm internet needs or what student data is collected. A teacher should ask IT to review the privacy terms and account settings before use.

How this can be used for Social Studies
  1. Explore a world about Washington state history and write three facts students find.
  2. Visit an ancient city world and compare it to a modern town.
  3. Build a model of a historic building and label its parts.
  4. Write a journal entry from the view of a person who lived in the era.
  5. Make a map of a world and explain how geography shaped the people there.
