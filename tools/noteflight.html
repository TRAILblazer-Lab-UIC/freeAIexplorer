Noteflight

An online music notation editor with a classroom platform called Learn.

Computing

Overview

Noteflight is an online music notation editor for composing, playing back, and sharing music. It offers free, Premium, and Learn plans. Noteflight Learn is a teaching platform that gives teachers and students a private site with assignments and groups. Learn syncs with Google Classroom and other learning management systems.

Benefits

For students

  • Students write music and hear it play back.
  • Students share scores with others.
  • Students work on assignments inside Learn.

For teachers

  • Learn provides assignments and groups.
  • It syncs with Google Classroom and other systems.
  • A SoundCheck add on assesses performance, and Learn has Essential Elements exercises.

For schools

  • A free plan exists, and Premium and Learn plans are paid.
  • Teachers create the Learn accounts for students.
  • IT may need to allow Noteflight domains.

Privacy and safety

Accounts are required, and the internet is needed at all times. Basic and Premium accounts collect a name, contact details, a username, a password, an IP address, uploaded scores, and comments. That policy says those accounts are not intended for children under 13. Noteflight Learn follows a separate Student Data Privacy Policy, and the research did not read it. Data goes to the vendor and its contractors and affiliates. A teacher should read the Learn student privacy policy and the school's data rules before students sign up.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Write a four measure melody and play it back.
  2. Complete an assignment sent through Noteflight Learn.
  3. Arrange a simple tune for two instruments.
  4. Share a score and give feedback on a classmate's work.
  5. Use an Essential Elements exercise to practice a new rhythm.
