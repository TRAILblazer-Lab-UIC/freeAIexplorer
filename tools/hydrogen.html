Hydrogen

A free open-source drum machine and pattern sequencer.

Computing

Overview

Hydrogen is an open source drum machine and pattern sequencer. It works as both a drum sequencer and a drum synth. It offers MIDI support, many drumkits, and control of instrument, sample, and note properties. It was created by Alessandro Cominu. Students program drum patterns on Linux, macOS, or Windows.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build drum patterns step by step.
  • Students hear their patterns play right away.
  • Students try different drumkits and sounds.

For teachers

  • It suits a music technology lab.
  • It supports MIDI and OSC control.
  • Time stretching and pitch shifting are included.

For schools

  • The software is free.
  • It runs offline on Windows, Mac, and Linux.
  • IT may need to approve the install on managed machines.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

Hydrogen has no AI. It builds rhythm and pattern skills. Students place sounds on a grid and listen for repeating patterns. It runs on the device.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the program runs locally. The internet is used for setup. No privacy policy was found. The official site failed to load with a certificate error during the research. A teacher should download the installer from a source the school trusts. A teacher should also plan how students save and export files and check headphone use in the lab.

How this can be used for Music
  1. Program a four-beat rock pattern with a kick and a snare.
  2. Change the tempo and describe how the feel changes.
  3. Swap a drumkit and compare the sound.
  4. Build a pattern with eight steps and add a fill.
  5. Pair a pattern with a melody played on another instrument.
