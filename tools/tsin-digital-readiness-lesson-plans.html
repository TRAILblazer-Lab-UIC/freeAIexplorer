Overview

These are lesson plan sets from the Tennessee STEM Innovation Network for grades K-2, 3-5, and 6-8. A search snippet says they are aligned to the Tennessee computer science standards. The same snippet says the grades 6 to 8 lessons are built on the free Code.org curriculum. The official pages returned 404 during research. The details are not confirmed.

Benefits

For students

  • Students meet computer science ideas at their grade level.
  • Students in grades 6 to 8 may use the free Code.org curriculum, per a search snippet.
  • Lessons are planned around computational thinking.

For teachers

  • A companion CS Integration Guidebook walks teachers through bringing computational thinking into class.
  • The plans are aligned to the Tennessee computer science standards, per a search snippet.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Give a partner step by step directions to draw a shape, as a K-2 activity.
  2. Sort a set of cards by a rule and name the pattern.
  3. Write a set of steps for a daily routine and test it.
  4. Use the Code.org tools in grades 6 to 8 to build a short program.
  5. Find a mistake in a set of instructions and fix it.
