Live Background Removal Lite (OBS)

A free OBS filter that removes webcam backgrounds without a green screen.

FREEAIStays on the device

Overview

Live Background Removal Lite is an effect filter for OBS Studio. It removes the background from portrait sources such as webcams. Students use it in OBS to produce video without a green screen. The project describes it as fast and light on resources.

Benefits

For students

  • Students can place themselves in front of any image or scene.
  • Students need no green screen or special lighting.
  • Students practice building video scenes in OBS.

For teachers

  • A teacher can set up a simple video studio with a webcam.
  • The filter is added to an existing webcam source in OBS.
  • It is free to use.

For schools

  • The filter is free and open source.
  • Releases exist for Windows, macOS, and Ubuntu.
  • It runs on the device, but IT will likely need to approve OBS and the download.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The AI is a portrait segmentation model that finds a person in the camera view. It runs on the local device. Students use it to cut themselves out of a video and place a new background behind them, then judge how well the cutout works.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed, and the internet is used for setup. The plugin processes video locally according to its description, but network behavior was not verified. No privacy policy was found. A teacher should check where students save recordings and who can view them.

How this can be used for Theater and Media Arts
  1. Record a short news report with a student in front of a chosen background.
  2. Test the filter in bright and dim light and note the changes.
  3. Build a weather report scene with a map behind the presenter.
  4. Create a scene change by swapping two backgrounds during a recording.
  5. Review a clip for cutout flaws and write notes on how to fix them.
