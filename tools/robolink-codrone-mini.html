Robolink CoDrone Mini

A small low-cost programmable drone from Robolink, coded with Blockly.

ComputingRobotics

The privacy details for this item are unverified. Check which tools and accounts students will use before class.

Overview

CoDrone Mini is a lower cost, small programmable drone from Robolink. Each kit includes the drone, a remote and a battery. It is programmed with Blockly, and some listings mention Python. The Robolink guide page fetched did not cover the Mini. It is a different product from CoDrone EDU.

Benefits

For students

  • Students code a small drone.
  • Students see blocks control real flight.
  • The kit includes a remote for first flights.

For teachers

  • The kit is lower cost than larger classroom drones.
  • Each kit holds the drone, the remote and a battery.
  • Blockly gives an easy start.

For schools

  • Listings run from 89.99 USD at a closeout to 165.35 USD.
  • A 12 kit set was 1079.88 USD at one reseller per a search snippet.
  • Setup details are unknown.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The vendor does not describe any AI. Students build sequencing and logic skills as they write block code for the drone. They also test and refine their programs.

Privacy and safety

The materials do not state whether accounts or the internet are needed. They also do not state what data is collected. A teacher should ask Robolink what app and account the Mini uses. Set flight rules for the room before students fly.
