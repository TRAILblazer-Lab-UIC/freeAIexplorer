Arduino Nano family

A set of small Arduino boards in the Nano size that fit a breadboard.

ComputingStays on the device

Overview

The Arduino Nano family is a set of small microcontroller boards sold in the Arduino store. The listing includes the Nano R4, Nano ESP32, Nano Every, Nano Matter, Nano RP2040 Connect, Nano 33 IoT and Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2. The boards use the Nano form factor that fits a breadboard. Students program the boards over USB from a local computer.

Benefits

For students

  • Students build small circuits on a breadboard.
  • Students can choose boards with Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
  • Students practice real text coding.

For teachers

  • The family has many models for different projects.
  • Headers or no headers options are available.
  • The boards are advanced. Teachers should expect a steep start.

For schools

  • Board only prices run from about 10 to 43 EUR.
  • The boards work offline after setup.
  • No account is needed and projects stay on the device.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

The boards have no AI. Students build skills in sensors, sequencing and logic. They write code that reads a sensor and acts on it.

Privacy and safety

No account is needed and the research found no student data collection. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth models can connect to services if students program them to. Arduino Cloud is optional and needs an account. Student code stays on the computer and the board. A teacher should keep wireless features off unless a lesson needs them. A teacher should also check that school computers allow USB devices.
