Rainbow Resource coding cards grades 3-6

A bilingual card set for unplugged coding games that direct people or robots.

$ComputingUnpluggedNo device needed

Overview

This is a commercial set of Activity, Challenge and Movement cards for unplugged coding. Students pick an Activity card and use Movement cards to complete the task. Then they use Challenge cards to test their skills. The cards can direct people or robots and are printed in English and French. Rainbow Resource lists the brand as SI Manufacturing and the grade range as 3 to 6.

Benefits

For students

  • Students plan a sequence of moves before they act.
  • Students can direct a classmate or a robot with the same cards.
  • Students can switch between English and French.

For teachers

  • One listing describes 3 instruction cards, 10 activity cards, 10 challenge cards and 90 movement cards.
  • The cards give ready-made tasks and need no lesson writing.
  • No setup steps are listed.

For schools

  • The set is low cost. Rainbow Resource listed it from 5.89 to 12.95 USD when checked.
  • The cards need no devices or internet.
  • No student data is collected.

Getting started checklist

Where is the AI in this product?

This card set has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is sequencing. Students write a list of moves and watch a person or robot follow it.

Privacy and safety

The cards are physical. No accounts are needed and no student data is collected. The materials do not mention any app. A teacher should check that students have room to move safely when they act out the code. Check any robot you pair with the cards for its own privacy rules.

How this can be used for Computer Science
  1. Pick an Activity card and use Movement cards to direct a partner across the room.
  2. Use Challenge cards to test your sequence and fix any mistakes.
  3. Direct a classroom robot with the same Movement cards.
  4. Trade a sequence with another pair and follow it exactly.
  5. Do one activity in French and one in English and compare the commands.
