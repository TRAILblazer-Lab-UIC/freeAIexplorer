How to use this site
  1. Tell us about your class. Pick a grade, a subject, and the devices you have.
  2. Choose a type of technology, or leave them all open.
  3. New to AI? Turn on the beginner view. It shows easy tools where students can watch AI at work.
  4. Read the little labels on each card. They show cost, technology type, and where student data goes.
  5. Open a tool to read a plain description, a setup checklist, privacy notes, and five ideas for each subject.
  6. Select the star to save a tool to My List. Print a plan or share it with a colleague.

What is local AI?

AI is software that can recognize, sort, or create things. It can run in one of two places.

Cloud AI

Like mailing a letter. Your words travel to a company's data center far away.

  • Runs in large company data centers
  • Often uses closed models that outsiders cannot inspect
  • Student words and work go to a third-party company
  • The company may keep or reuse what students type

Local AI

Like a calculator on your desk. It works right here, on a school computer, tablet, or robot.

Benefits of local AI

  • Student work stays at school
  • Often free
  • Often works without internet
  • Often uses open models anyone can inspect

Both kinds of AI have a place. Some company AI services are very useful, and many schools use them well with the right agreements.

Local AI gives teachers another option with real benefits. Students can use AI to extend what they can do. They keep their own creativity and critical thinking at the center.
