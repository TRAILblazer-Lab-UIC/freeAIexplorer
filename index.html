How to use this site
  1. New to AI? Press For AI Beginners. You will see easy tools where students can watch AI at work.
  2. Open I'm looking for something specific. Pick your grade, subject, devices, internet, budget, and privacy needs. The best matches rise to the top.
  3. Narrow the list with the filters. Choose a type of technology, a subject, or a kind of resource such as a lesson or a robot.
  4. Read the badges on each card. The colored badge shows where student data goes. Green means it stays on the device.
  5. Open a tool page. Each page explains the tool in plain words. It lists benefits, a setup checklist, privacy notes, and five ideas for each subject.
  6. Select the star to save a tool to My List. Print a "Try It Out" plan or share your list with a colleague.

Every page was researched and written with help from AI. Check details with each provider before class. Read why AI that stays in school matters or how we built this site.

Why choose AI that stays in your school?

Most AI chat tools send every word to a company's computers. Local AI runs on devices your school already owns. Student work stays in the room.
