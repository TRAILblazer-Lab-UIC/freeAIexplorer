## Overview
Loti-Bot is a programmable floor robot from TTS that is presented as the next step after Bee-Bot, Blue-Bot, and Rugged Robot. Per search results it has programmable color LEDs, bumper and proximity sensors, and an adjustable pen holder. Students program it over Bluetooth with block-based coding in the TTS Loti-Bot app. One distributor targets grades 3-4.

## Benefits for students
- Students build block programs that move a floor robot.
- Students use sensors to make the robot react.
- Students draw paths with the pen holder.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app is listed for tablets, laptops, Chromebooks, and PCs using Chrome or Edge.
- Teachers can move from Bee-Bot to a robot with more features.
- Setup takes about an hour.

## Benefits for schools
- Search results list the robot at about 195 to 197 USD.
- The app runs on devices the school may already own.
- One listing says iPad only on iOS.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Loti-Bot on the TTS site.

## Where is the AI?
Loti-Bot has no AI. The robot runs the block program students build in the app. Students build skills in sequencing and logic as they test and fix each program.

## Privacy and safety
The research read no privacy policy. All facts come from search snippets of vendor and retailer pages. The materials do not state whether an account or the internet is needed. The research did not confirm whether the app is a web app or stores data. Check the app's privacy details and Bluetooth needs before class.
