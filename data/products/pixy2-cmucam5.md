## Overview
Pixy2 is a small smart camera that processes images on board at 60 frames per second. It can be taught to detect objects with a button press. It also tracks lines, detects intersections and reads road sign barcodes. It connects to Arduino and Raspberry Pi over SPI, I2C, UART or USB.

## Benefits for students
- Students give a robot the power to see colors and lines.
- Students teach the camera an object with a button press.
- Students read camera data on an Arduino or Raspberry Pi.

## Benefits for teachers
- The camera does its image work on board.
- Libraries exist for Arduino and Raspberry Pi.
- FIRST Robotics teams use it with a roboRIO.

## Benefits for schools
- The price is not shown on the official page.
- It is sold through Amazon and distributors.
- Hardware and code are open source.

## Where is the AI?
The page says color learning and line algorithms run on the camera. It does not use the term machine learning, and the vendor describes no AI. Students build skills in sensing, data and pattern spotting as they teach the camera an object.

## Privacy and safety
The camera works on the device. No account or internet is needed. The materials document no student data collection. A teacher should remember that the camera sees the room. Keep it pointed at the project and away from people.
