## Overview
EasyEDA is a free online tool for schematic capture, circuit simulation, and PCB layout with 3D preview. It also has a desktop client with offline modes. The site lists an Education Edition, free courses, and grants. The grade range is not stated, and the policy says users under 16 are outside its target audience.

## Benefits for students
- Students draw a circuit and see it as a 3D board.
- Students simulate a circuit before building it.
- Students practice real electronics design steps.

## Benefits for teachers
- The Education Edition lets a school invite student accounts.
- Free courses are listed on the site.
- The desktop client can store projects locally.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- PCB manufacturing and parts are paid services.
- The policy says the services are not directed at users under 16. The minimum age for student accounts under the Education Edition was not confirmed.

## Where is the AI?
The materials do not say that EasyEDA has any AI. The vendor does not describe AI features here. The tool builds logic and design skills. Students connect parts into a working circuit.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are required, and the site is always online. The policy lists IP address, username, email, encrypted password, uploaded projects and design files, and device and usage data. Data is stored in Frankfurt and may be transferred to affiliates in Mainland China. Cookie data goes to advertising and analytics platforms including Google, Facebook, TikTok, Microsoft, LinkedIn and Reddit. The policy says the services are not directed at users under 16 and that the company does not knowingly collect children's data. A search snippet says the education agreement makes the school or teacher the data controller. It also asks for parental consent for students under 13. The desktop client can keep projects on the computer. A teacher should check the education agreement and the cookie settings before inviting students.
