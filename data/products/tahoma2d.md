## Overview
Tahoma2D is free software for 2D and stop motion animation. It is based on OpenToonz. It supports brushes with tilt support, cutout animation, special effects nodes, and export to mp4 and gif. It can capture stop motion frames from DSLR cameras and webcams.

## Benefits for students
- Students can draw with brushes and manage color palettes.
- Students can capture stop motion frames with a webcam.
- Students can export their work as mp4 or gif.

## Benefits for teachers
- One program handles drawn animation, cutout animation, and stop motion.
- Rhubarb Lip Sync can match mouth shapes to speech automatically.
- Tahoma2D is a fork of OpenToonz aimed at stop motion work.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and the code is open.
- It needs 64-bit Windows 8.1, 10, or 11, or macOS 15 or later.
- IT approval for installs may be needed. No internet is needed after install.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download Tahoma2D from its official site.
- [ ] Check that each computer runs 64-bit Windows or a recent macOS.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve and install it.
- [ ] Connect a webcam if you plan stop motion work.
- [ ] Draw a short test animation with the brush tools.
- [ ] Try a stop motion capture with a small object.
- [ ] Export a short clip as mp4.

## Where is the AI?
Tahoma2D has no AI. The vendor lists automated lip sync as a feature and does not call it AI. Students still choose the art and timing. They practice sequencing as they plan motion over time.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and no internet is needed after install. Files are saved on the device. The site FAQ says the project does not collect any data on users, which is a vendor statement. The research found no separate privacy policy page. Decide where students store their finished films and ask IT to confirm the app makes no network calls.
