## Overview
Data Science Foundations is a free grades 9 to 12 course from EVERFI. It has four units: Data Science Basics, Collecting Cleaning and Validating Data, Analyzing and Visualizing Data, and Reporting and Acting on Data. There are four lessons of about 30 minutes each, roughly 2 hours in total. The course is asynchronous with built-in assessments and real-time grading.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how data is collected and cleaned.
- Students practice analyzing and visualizing data.
- Students work at their own pace.

## Benefits for teachers
- Lessons are graded in real time.
- Teachers receive lesson plans, activities, and discussion guides.
- Teachers request free access with a form.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is always free for teachers and funded by partners and sponsors.
- It runs in a browser and works on Chromebooks.
- It is offered in English and Spanish.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in this course. The course builds data skills. Students learn to collect, clean, analyze, and report on data.

## Privacy and safety
Teachers request access with their name, school, state, role, grade level, and work email. The materials do not state how student accounts work. The course runs on EVERFI servers with grading. The vendor likely stores student progress. The research did not read the student data terms. A teacher should read the privacy terms and ask EVERFI how student accounts, grades, and class data are stored.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Learn how data is collected and describe one way that can go wrong.
2. Clean a small data set and list the fixes made.
3. Make a chart from a data set and say what it shows.
4. Compare two sets of numbers using an average.
5. Write a short report on a finding.

### cte
1. Explore a career that uses data and describe its daily tasks.
2. Practice presenting a data finding as if to a manager.
3. Plan a small data project from question to report.
4. Check a data set for errors the way an analyst would.
5. Write a short job ad for a data worker.

### social_studies
1. Use data to look at an economic trend and describe it.
2. Compare prices over time and explain the change.
3. Discuss how data shapes choices in a community.
4. Check a news claim by looking at the data behind it.
5. Report on how data could help a local business.
