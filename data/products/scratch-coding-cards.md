## Overview
Scratch Coding Cards are a deck of project idea cards by Natalie Rusk and the Scratch team. A search result describes the 2016 deck as 75 laminated cards and a Scratch 3.0 edition as 76 cards. Students follow the cards to build projects in Scratch on a device. The card contents were not checked directly.

## Benefits for students
- Students follow a card at their own pace.
- Students build projects with blocks.
- Students work from a printed guide beside the screen.

## Benefits for teachers
- A card deck needs little preparation.
- The cards can be placed on tables for self-directed work.
- The teacher learning curve is easy.

## Benefits for schools
- A print deck is listed at 24.95 USD in a search result.
- The materials do not say whether a free PDF exists.
- Scratch runs in a browser.

## Where is the AI?
The cards have no AI. Scratch is a block coding tool. Students build sequencing and logic skills as they follow each card.

## Privacy and safety
The cards are paper. Scratch runs in the browser, and its data handling was not read for this entry. The materials do not state whether an account is needed or what data is collected. A teacher should read the Scratch privacy policy and check whether students will save projects online.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Pick a card and build the project it describes.
2. Change one block in the finished project and note the effect.
3. Combine two cards into a single project.
4. Trade cards with a partner and build a project from their card.
5. Explain to the class how the blocks in a card project run.
