## Overview
Tinkering Fundamentals: Circuits is a beginner online course on Coursera from the Exploratorium. Instructors are Mike Petrich, Karen Wilkinson, and Luigi Anzivino. It has six modules about 7 hours long. Learners try circuit boards, scribbling machines, paper circuits, sewn circuits, and MaKey MaKey, then build an action plan and a tinkering journal for peer review.

## Benefits for students
- Learners handle real circuit materials.
- Learners make scribbling machines, paper circuits, and sewn circuits.
- Learners try MaKey MaKey and take apart a toy.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course is aimed at educators and enthusiasts.
- Activity guides are in the course resources.
- Teachers finish with an action plan.

## Benefits for schools
- The page says it is included with Coursera Plus.
- Assignments and a certificate need the Certificate option. Free audit details are not fully stated.

## Where is the AI?
The course has no AI. It builds circuit and sensing skills. Learners link a battery, wire, and part into a working circuit.

## Privacy and safety
The course runs on Coursera and each learner needs a Coursera account. The internet is always needed. The data collected is a learner account and course progress. The research describes the learners as adult educators and did not read the Coursera privacy policy. A teacher should read the Coursera privacy policy before enrolling. Students under school care should not join until the school has reviewed it.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Build a circuit board with a battery and a light.
2. Make a scribbling machine with a small motor.
3. Take apart a toy and sketch its circuit.
4. Make a paper circuit with a switch.
5. Plan a class activity and note the materials needed.

### science
1. Test which materials carry electricity.
2. Predict whether a circuit will light a bulb and then check.
3. Sew a circuit and describe the current path.
4. Use MaKey MaKey to find what conducts.
5. Keep a tinkering journal of questions and results.
