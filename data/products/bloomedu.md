## Overview
BloomEDU is a mobile app that identifies wildflowers from a photo. It then uses quizzes and spaced repetition to help users remember the names. It covers more than 46,000 wildflower species across the 50 U.S. states. It is a consumer app and the research found no classroom features.

## Benefits for students
- Students photograph a flower and get a name.
- Students practice names with quizzes.
- Offline regional packs allow quizzes without a connection.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app is easy to learn.
- Quiz modes cover state, family, and season.
- Identification shows confidence labels.

## Benefits for schools
- A free plan allows 3 identifications per day and one offline state pack.
- Premium costs $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year.
- Teachers must install the app on devices.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a plant image identification model. The photo is sent to a server in a company's cloud. Identification needs a connection. Students take a photo, read the name and the confidence label, and then check the answer in a field guide.

## Privacy and safety
BloomEDU sends each plant photo and a location rounded to about 1 km to the vendor server, which passes the photo to OpenAI. The policy says the server does not save photos or coordinates. The app also uses Amplitude analytics and Sentry error reports, which may include a short screen replay. The internet is needed all the time. Accounts are optional, and guest data stays on the device. The policy says the app is not intended for children under 13, and the App Store rates it 13+. A teacher should read the policy before any student uses the app, and should use a shared teacher device for younger grades.
