## Overview
Maixduino and MAix Bit are older Sipeed development boards. They use the Kendryte K210 RISC-V chip. The Sipeed wiki lists both and links to MaixPy documentation. The vendor page read did not give specs, price, or status. An Elektor store page lists the Maixduino among edge AI products.

## Benefits for students
- Students can program a board built for machine vision.
- Students learn embedded programming with MaixPy.
- Students see AI run on a small chip.

## Benefits for teachers

## Where is the AI?
The discovery notes say the K210 chip supports machine vision. The vendor page read does not confirm this. The AI would run on the board itself. A teacher should verify what the board can do before planning lessons.

## Privacy and safety
The research reviewed no privacy information for these boards. The materials do not state what data is collected, whether accounts are needed or whether the internet is needed. The boards are described as running AI on the device. A teacher should test the board and any camera features before students use them.
