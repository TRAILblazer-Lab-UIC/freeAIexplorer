## Overview
Mathigon is a free online site of math courses, tools, and manipulatives. It is now part of Amplify, a US curriculum company, and its Polypad tool moved to polypad.amplify.com. Users can sign in with Google, Microsoft, or email. Teachers get a dashboard to follow student progress.

## Benefits for students
- Students explore math with interactive pages.
- Students use tools such as Flash Cards, Sequences, Graph Theory, Origami, and Symmetry.
- Students use Polypad to move virtual manipulatives.

## Benefits for teachers
- A teacher dashboard follows student progress.
- A search snippet says the dashboard can import a Google Classroom roster.
- Teacher resources are available.

## Benefits for schools
- The site is described as free, and press says Polypad and Mathigon resources stay free.
- It runs in a browser and needs no install.
- Offline use was not confirmed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the Mathigon site in a browser.
- [ ] Try a course and a Polypad tool yourself.
- [ ] Decide if students need accounts. Accounts are optional.
- [ ] Review the Amplify Customer Privacy Policy.
- [ ] Set up a teacher account and a class if you want the dashboard.
- [ ] Check whether students need to sign in at all.
- [ ] Share a link to a course with the class.

## Where is the AI?
Mathigon has no AI. The vendor describes no AI features. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Students explore patterns and test ideas with the tools.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are optional and are used for saving progress. The site collects account and progress data and sends it to the vendor, Amplify. The internet is needed at all times. Sign in through Google or Microsoft is offered. The Amplify policy says it handles student data as a FERPA school official and relies on the school for COPPA consent for children under 13. It says it does not sell student data or use it for targeted advertising. The research inferred that this policy applies to Mathigon accounts. The materials state no minimum age. A teacher should read the Amplify privacy policy, ask about a data privacy agreement, and use the site without accounts for younger students.
