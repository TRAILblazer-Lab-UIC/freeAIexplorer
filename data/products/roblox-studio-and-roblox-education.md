## Overview
Roblox Education offers free software and curriculum for students of all ages. Its page lists computer science, digital citizenship, and entrepreneurship among subjects. Resources include educator onboarding, free lessons, developer onboarding, a list of learning games, and a directory of educators. Students build experiences in Roblox Studio on the Roblox platform.

## Benefits for students
- Students build games in a tool that many already know.
- Students can learn coding in a space that feels familiar.
- Students can explore digital citizenship and entrepreneurship topics.

## Benefits for teachers
- Educator onboarding and lessons are free.
- A directory of educators lets teachers find peers.
- Curated learning games add variety.

## Benefits for schools
- The education page says software and curriculum are free.
- Roblox Studio runs on Windows and Mac.
- IT will likely need to install it.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Roblox Education on the Roblox site and open the educator onboarding.
- [ ] Ask IT and school leaders to review the privacy policy and account rules.
- [ ] Check the age rules. The minimum age is 13.
- [ ] Read which tools and accounts each lesson needs.
- [ ] Ask IT to install Roblox Studio on a test computer.
- [ ] Try one free lesson yourself.
- [ ] Decide how students will sign in before class.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in this offer. Students build skills such as logic and design. They write code that controls a game and then test it. That practice builds a base for later work with AI tools.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed, and the internet is needed at all times. The research could not read the Roblox privacy policy. Search snippets say it sets stronger privacy settings for users under 13. A Common Sense snippet says it is unclear whether managed classroom accounts exist. The education page does not state whether accounts are required. Student work and account data go to the vendor. A teacher should read the privacy policy and the education pages before students sign up.
