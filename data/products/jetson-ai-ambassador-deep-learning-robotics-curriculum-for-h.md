## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Watch the video and list the deep learning steps the teacher describes.
2. Train a small image model on the Jetson Nano and test it on new pictures.
3. Compare the model's mistakes on two kinds of images.
4. Explain in a short note how a neural network learns from examples.
5. Discuss how deep learning helps a self-driving vehicle see the road.

### engineering
