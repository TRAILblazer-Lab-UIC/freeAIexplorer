## Overview
Writing Coach is an AI agent from ibl.ai that reviews student writing and gives revision guidance. It reads the assignment prompt first and leads with strengths. It does not rewrite student work, and it flags possible plagiarism patterns for teacher review. Feedback depth scales by grade.

## Benefits for students
- Students get feedback that starts with their strengths.
- Students keep ownership of their writing.
- Feedback fits the grade level.

## Benefits for teachers
- It reviews against rubrics such as 6+1 Traits, PARCC, and state rubrics.
- It pulls submissions from Canvas, Google Classroom, and Schoology.
- It can comment inline in Google Docs or Word.

## Benefits for schools
- The page lists no prices. Cost is usage-based by tokens, or the school can self-host and pay for GPUs.
- Engineers help connect the learning system and the identity provider.
- The vendor claims PII detection and redaction.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a writing feedback agent that also flags plagiarism patterns. It runs in the vendor's cloud or on a school server. Students read the comments and revise their own drafts. Teachers review any flagged items.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed, and the internet need depends on deployment and model choice. Student writing goes to the system. The vendor says names and personal details are not retained across sessions and mentions COPPA, PII redaction, and audit logging. All of that is a vendor claim and none of it was verified. The tool can run in the vendor cloud or on a school server, and cloud models such as Claude, GPT, or Gemini receive student text when selected. A teacher should ask for the data privacy agreement, find out which model is used, and review each plagiarism flag by hand.
