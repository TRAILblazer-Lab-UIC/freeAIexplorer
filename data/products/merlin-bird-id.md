## Overview
Merlin Bird ID is a free field guide app from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. It identifies birds from photos with Photo ID, from recordings with Sound ID, and from a short question wizard. Region bird packs can be downloaded for use without internet. It runs on iOS and Android.

## Benefits for students
- Students identify birds they see and hear.
- Sound ID shows a live spectrogram of a bird call.
- Students can work outdoors with no signal after downloading a pack.

## Benefits for teachers
- Cornell classroom pages use the app in birding and citizen science lessons.
- A shared group account option exists for classes.
- Lessons work for grades 3-5 and beyond.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free.
- It runs on phones and tablets that the school already has.
- Regional packs let outdoor lessons run offline.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Merlin Bird ID in the App Store or Google Play.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the app on school devices.
- [ ] Install the app.
- [ ] Create an account with email.
- [ ] Review the shared class account option in the FAQ.
- [ ] Download the bird pack for your region.
- [ ] Test Sound ID outside with a short recording.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a set of models that classify bird images and bird sounds. They run on the device after the bird pack is downloaded. Students photograph or record a bird, read the suggestions, and then check them against what they observe.

## Privacy and safety
An account is required, and the internet is needed for setup. The FAQ says the app can be tried for 5 days before the email address must be confirmed. Cornell says it will not sell or give the email address to others. The FAQ describes a shared account for classes whose sightings are visible only to signed in users of that account. The materials do not state what other data the app collects. The Cornell privacy policy returned a 403 and was not read. The research did not confirm age specific rules for children under 13. A teacher should read the privacy terms and decide how students will log in.
