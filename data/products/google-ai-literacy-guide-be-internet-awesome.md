## Overview
The Google AI Literacy Guide is a supplementary curriculum for grades 2 to 8. It is part of Be Internet Awesome. The educators page says it has lesson plans and activities on machine learning, data, and algorithms. A search snippet lists three lessons called What is AI, Digital Citizenship and AI, and Responsible AI Use. The guide does not teach students to use specific AI tools.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn the basics of machine learning, data, and algorithms.
- Students think about digital citizenship in a world with AI.
- Students practice critical thinking about AI use.

## Benefits for teachers
- The guide is a ready PDF with three lessons.
- It works as a supplement to other lessons.
- Teachers do not need to teach any AI tool.

## Benefits for schools
- The PDF is free.
- The educators page says the curriculum needs no login. No hardware is required.
- The materials describe no student data collection.

## Where is the AI?
The guide has no running AI program. It teaches how machine learning, data, and algorithms work. Students also learn to use AI responsibly. They build skills in asking where data comes from and in judging when to trust an AI answer.

## Privacy and safety
The guide runs on paper and the materials describe no student data collection. The Be Internet Awesome page says its curriculum does not require personal information or a login. That statement sits in the curriculum section and may not name this guide. The research could not read the PDF. Google publishes the guide, and the vendor does not say how it uses download data. A teacher should read the lessons and check that the examples fit the class.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Explain machine learning to a partner using an example from the guide.
2. Sort a set of cards by a rule and describe the rule as an algorithm.
3. Collect class data on a simple topic and talk about what it shows.
4. Write step-by-step instructions that a machine could follow.
5. Make a poster that shows how data helps a machine learn.

### library_media
1. Discuss what makes a good digital citizen when using AI.
2. List rules for responsible AI use and compare them with a partner.
3. Check an AI claim against a trusted source.
4. Role-play a situation where a student must decide how to use AI fairly.
5. Create a short guide for younger students on using AI wisely.
