## Overview
The Raspberry Pi 500 is a complete computer built into a keyboard. It has a 2.4GHz quad-core Arm Cortex-A76 processor, 8GB of RAM, dual 4K HDMI outputs, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Gigabit Ethernet, and a horizontal 40-pin GPIO header. The unit includes a 32GB microSD card. A Desktop Kit adds a mouse, a 27W power supply, an HDMI cable, and the Beginner's Guide.

## Benefits for students
- Students get a complete computer in one keyboard.
- Students can use the GPIO header for physical computing.
- Students use Raspberry Pi OS, which is preloaded.

## Benefits for teachers
- The preloaded card cuts setup work.
- The Desktop Kit includes the cables and power supply.
- The launch article does not position the Pi 500 as an education product.

## Benefits for schools
- The launch article lists USD 90 for the unit and USD 120 for the Desktop Kit.

## Where is the AI?
The Raspberry Pi 500 has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is working with sensors and logic. Students connect parts to the GPIO header and write code to read them. That practice shows how computers sense and respond.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The computer runs Raspberry Pi OS locally, and the maker collects no usage data from the device. The internet is needed to update the system or install packages. The Raspberry Pi privacy policy does not address children or school accounts. A teacher should set a strong password and check wiring rules before students use the GPIO header. A teacher should also check the price. The listed prices have changed.
