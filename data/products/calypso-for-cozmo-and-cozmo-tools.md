## Overview
Calypso is a language for programming intelligent robots such as Cozmo. This curriculum has 16 modules, including Perception, Recognizing Faces, State Machines, and Speech and Hearing. Modules 15 and 16 have no links yet. Extras include a Keep-Away Game example, printable markers, and video series for early and upper elementary.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how a robot perceives the world.
- Students try face recognition and speech ideas.
- Students learn state machines through hands-on work.

## Benefits for teachers
- The modules give a clear order of lessons.
- Laws of Calypso and flashcards support review.
- AI4K12 lists it for all ages.

## Benefits for schools
- Cost is not stated.
- A Cozmo robot is required.
- The grade bands are only hinted by two video series.

## Where is the AI?
The AI includes perception, face recognition, and speech and hearing, taught through the robot. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students program the robot to use what it senses. They then test and improve the program.
