## Overview
This Career Girls lesson plan has students watch a 4:29 video of women role models talking about AI careers. Students define artificial intelligence as a class and circle vocabulary from the video. They fill in an Exploring AI Careers worksheet that compares job titles and responsibilities. The plan closes with a discussion and research.

## Benefits for students
- Students meet role models who work in AI.
- Students learn words such as algorithm, data, machine learning, and neural networks.
- Students list AI careers and describe what each job does.

## Benefits for teachers
- The plan needs only a video, a worksheet, and a projector.
- Preview takes only a few minutes.
- The follow-up research can use books, magazines, or the internet.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson is free.
- No AI tool is needed.
- It is a 2020 resource aimed at girls' career exploration.

## Where is the AI?
The lesson has no AI tool and students do not use any AI software. The AI is only the topic. It builds the skill of understanding AI vocabulary and how jobs use data and algorithms. Students extend their own career ideas by listing the skills each job needs.

## Privacy and safety
The lesson plan itself asks for no student data. The video streams from the internet, and the research did not check the video site's account or data policy. The materials do not state whether an account is needed to view it. A teacher should preview the video and check that it loads on school devices. A teacher should also check that the video site is not filtered.

## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Watch the video and fill in the worksheet with two job titles.
2. Compare the duties of two AI careers in a table.
3. Circle the vocabulary words and write a definition for each.
4. Research one AI career and present three facts to the class.
5. Write a letter to a role model with one question about her work.
