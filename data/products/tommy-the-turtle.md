## Overview
Tommy the Turtle is a free early coding game for young children from Zyrobotics. Kids tap command buttons to move a turtle. The commands include forward, backward, grow, shrink, turn, spin, and jump. It introduces sequences and loops. A challenge mode offers puzzles. The developer says ages 3 and up and Common Sense Media says ages 5 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Young children program by tapping, with no typing.
- Children learn sequences and loops through play.
- Challenge mode offers puzzles.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can use the app as an Hour of Code lesson, per a search snippet.
- It is easy for teachers to learn.
- It suits K-2 classes.

## Benefits for schools
- A review says the app is free with no ads or in-app purchases.
- It needs an iPad or iPhone. The Android version was removed from Google Play on 2021-04-10.
- The current App Store listing was not confirmed.

## Where is the AI?
Tommy the Turtle has no AI. Children tap commands in order to move the turtle. They build sequencing and loop skills.
