## Overview
The Crumble Controller is a small programmable board with crocodile clip connection pads. Redfern Electronics makes it for primary-age and design and technology projects. Students connect LEDs, motors, switches and other add-ons. They program it from the free Crumble software.

## Benefits for students
- Students connect parts with crocodile clips and need no soldering.
- Students drive motors and LEDs from their own code.
- Students add simple electronics to a design project.

## Benefits for teachers
- It is described as built for design and technology projects.
- The board connects to a computer by USB.
- The software is free.

## Benefits for schools
- The starter kit is listed at about 30 GBP.
- No account is needed and no internet is needed.
- It works with Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook.

## Getting started
- [ ] Buy the Crumble Controller or a starter kit.
- [ ] Install the Crumble software or open Crumble Code in a USB-capable browser.
- [ ] Connect the controller by USB.
- [ ] Attach an LED with crocodile clips.
- [ ] Run a first program that turns on the LED.
- [ ] Add a motor and test it.
- [ ] Note that the legacy ChromeOS app is available through the Chrome Admin Console until February 2027.

## Where is the AI?
The Crumble Controller has no AI. It runs the program that students load on it. Students build skills in sequencing, outputs and logic.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the research found no student data collected. The board is programmed over USB from a computer, and no network features are known. No privacy policy was read. Programs stay on the computer and the board. A teacher should check the battery pack and wiring before class.
