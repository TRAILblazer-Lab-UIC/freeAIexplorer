## Overview
SmolChat is an open source Android app for running GGUF language models on a phone through llama.cpp. Users add or remove models, edit system prompts, and adjust settings such as temperature and min-p. A task mode generates responses without saving messages. Chats are stored in a local database.

## Benefits for students
- Students chat with a model that runs on their own phone.
- Students can change system prompts and settings and see the effect.
- Students learn how model choice changes the answers.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can show how temperature changes a reply.
- Any GGUF model can be loaded.
- The app shows Markdown and highlighted code.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free and open source.
- Chats stay on the device after setup.
- IT may need to help. Sideloaded installs may not suit managed devices.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find SmolChat on Google Play, GitHub Releases, or Obtainium.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install on school Android devices.
- [ ] Install the app.
- [ ] Download a small GGUF model while online.
- [ ] Add the model in the app.
- [ ] Go offline and try a short chat.
- [ ] Try the task mode for responses that are not saved.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small language model that chats. It runs on the Android device through llama.cpp. Students write prompts, edit the system prompt and settings, and use the replies to extend their own work.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The internet is used at setup to get the app and models. The repository privacy policy says the app does not obtain information when used and shares no data with third parties. It does not address network activity or analytics, and the research could not read the Google Play data safety section. The policy says the app is not knowingly used to collect data from children under 13, which is a data rule and not a minimum age. A teacher should remember that output quality depends on the model chosen.
