## Overview
ML-Machine is a free web tool from Aarhus University for recording micro:bit movement data and training machine learning models on it. It needs at least one micro:bit and can connect over Bluetooth. Aarhus also publishes a teaching unit with a teacher's guide, student activities, and slides. The micro:bit Foundation later released CreateAI as an evolution of it.

## Benefits for students
- Students record their own movement examples.
- Students train a model and see live predictions.
- Students use the board's Bluetooth, lights, buttons, and sound.

## Benefits for teachers
- Aarhus offers a downloadable teaching unit.
- The tool was tested in Danish public schools through the ultra:bit project, per a search snippet.
- Teachers who want a newer tool may prefer CreateAI, which has an MIT license and a privacy policy.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- At least one micro:bit is needed, and two are recommended per a search snippet.
- The materials do not say how much the internet is needed after setup.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find ml-machine.org and the Aarhus teaching unit page.
- [ ] Compare it with micro:bit CreateAI before you choose.
- [ ] Gather micro:bit boards with Bluetooth.
- [ ] Open the tool in a browser.
- [ ] Pair a micro:bit by Bluetooth.
- [ ] Record examples for two actions and train a model.
- [ ] Download the teaching unit files.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a motion classifier. Students train it from examples recorded on the micro:bit. The vendor does not say where the training runs. Students record, train, and watch the live prediction, and then add examples to improve it.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need or where data goes. The research found no privacy statement, and the ml-machine.org homepage returned no readable content. The CreateAI launch article says CreateAI needs no login. That does not prove how ML-Machine behaves. The research did not confirm offline use or data handling. A teacher should read the site terms and check whether the tool saves any data in the browser or online. CreateAI is the successor and has a published privacy policy.
