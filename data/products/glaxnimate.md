## Overview
Glaxnimate is an open-source desktop app for vector animation and motion graphics. It supports tweening, precompositions, and Python scripting. It exports Lottie, animated SVG, animated WebP, GIF, video, and spritesheets. Version 0.6.0 in March 2026 was its first stable release as part of KDE.

## Benefits for students
- Students draw shapes and animate them with tweening.
- Students export animations as GIF or video to share.
- Students can work offline.

## Benefits for teachers
- The interface is easy to learn.
- Light and dark themes help with screen comfort.
- Python scripting is available for advanced students.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- No account is needed and no internet is needed after install.
- IT can install it from the Microsoft Store, Flathub, a Linux package manager, or a macOS build.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Glaxnimate on the KDE site.
- [ ] Choose the right install source for your computers.
- [ ] Ask IT to install it on school machines.
- [ ] Open the app and draw a simple shape.
- [ ] Add a keyframe and animate the shape across the screen.
- [ ] Export a short GIF and play it.
- [ ] Note that the app is aimed at Lottie and UI animation more than classic cartoon animation.

## Where is the AI?
Glaxnimate has no AI. It builds animation skills. Students plan motion in steps and keyframes. It runs on the device.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and no internet is needed after install. Student files stay on the device. Glaxnimate is a local desktop app and the research did not read a privacy policy. A teacher should decide where students save files and which formats they can share.
