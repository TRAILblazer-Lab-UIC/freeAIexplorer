## Overview
EWsim.io is a free browser-based mixed-signal circuit simulator. Launch coverage says it runs analog and digital circuits together and supports firmware for AVR, PIC, 8051, and Arduino, with over 200 components. The site also has an HDL playground for Verilog and SystemVerilog. It was announced in July 2026 and is aimed at engineers and students more than K-12.

## Benefits for students
- Students test analog and digital circuits in one place.
- Students run microcontroller firmware in the simulator.
- Students try Verilog and SystemVerilog in a playground.

## Benefits for teachers
- It is free per the site title and launch coverage.
- It opens in a browser with no install described.
- It suits advanced high school work.

## Benefits for schools
- The tool costs nothing.
- It needs only a computer with a modern browser.
- It is a very new product and browser support was not tested.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in EWsim.io. The tool builds logic and systems thinking. Students link parts and watch how signals move.

## Privacy and safety
The site is always online and runs in the browser. The materials do not state which accounts students need. Launch coverage says there is no sign-up barrier, but the site does not state whether accounts exist. The research could not read the privacy policy on electronicwings.com. Whether circuits are stored on the vendor server is unknown. A teacher should open the site, read the terms, and test whether work can be saved.
