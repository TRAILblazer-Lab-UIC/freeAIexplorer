## Overview
This is a series of five IBM activity kits listed on the AI4K12 site and posted on May 15, 2024. The kits are Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Train Your Computer With Machine Learning, What's Possible with AI TJBot, Chatbots for Good, and Summit Supercomputer and AI. Age ranges run from about 11 to 14 for some kits and 14 to 19 or adults for others.

## Benefits for students
- Students try AI ideas in hands-on activities.
- Students can build with a TJBot robot.
- Students learn about chatbots and supercomputers.

## Benefits for teachers
- Kits include facilitator guides and online demos.
- Resources download from a Box.com repository.
- Teachers can pick the kit that fits the age group.

## Benefits for schools
- The kits are described as free to access.
- The TJBot kit may need a physical robot that costs extra.
- Requirements for each kit were not confirmed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the AI4K12 listing and find the IBM kit pages.
- [ ] Check the IBM pages, which may have moved.
- [ ] Choose a kit that fits the age group.
- [ ] Download the kit from the Box.com link.
- [ ] Read the facilitator guide.
- [ ] Ask IT to allow the online demos.
- [ ] Run one kit with a small group.

## Where is the AI?
The kits cover machine learning, chatbots, and AI basics. The vendor does not say where the demos run. Students train a model, build or talk to a chatbot, and test what a TJBot can do. They use these tasks to see how AI learns from data.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts or tools students need, and the research did not fetch the IBM kit pages. The data handling of the demos was not reviewed. The machine learning kit page is named machine-learning-for-kids, which suggests a cloud hosted tool, but that was not confirmed. A teacher should open the kit pages and demos first and check what each one asks for.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Work through the Introduction to Artificial Intelligence kit and list what you learned.
2. Train a model in the machine learning kit and test it on new examples.
3. Design a chatbot for a good cause and write sample questions.
4. Explore what a supercomputer does for AI and write a summary.
5. Compare a chatbot reply with a human reply to the same question.

### engineering
1. Build a TJBot and describe each part.
2. Plan a task for TJBot and write the steps it should follow.
3. Test the robot and record what works and what fails.
4. Redesign one part of the robot to improve it.
5. Present the robot to the class and explain how it uses AI.
