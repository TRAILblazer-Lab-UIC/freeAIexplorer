## Overview
CASA Code is a UnidosUS computer science program for Latino middle school students. The toolkit introduces STEM and computer science. It gives guidance on engaging Latino families. A search result says it includes three sample lessons, a binary code worksheet and a family coding night. The PDF was too large to fetch. These details come from search results.

## Benefits for students
- Students try an unplugged computer science activity.
- Students work through a binary code worksheet.
- Students can share their learning at a family coding night.

## Benefits for teachers
- The toolkit gives lesson plans and a family night plan.
- It includes guidance on engaging Latino families.
- A teacher should read the PDF first to confirm what it contains.

## Benefits for schools
- The PDF is free.
- The materials are paper based.
- No devices or IT help are needed.

## Where is the AI?
This toolkit has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is understanding binary data. Students see how information can be written as ones and zeros.

## Privacy and safety
The toolkit is a PDF that is printed for use. No accounts are needed and the research found no student data collection. The research could not read the PDF. Lesson details are unconfirmed. Check any family night sign-up process for how contact details are stored. Keep family names and contact details out of shared documents.
