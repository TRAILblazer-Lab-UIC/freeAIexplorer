## Overview
This is a free self-paced online course for teachers with about 20 hours of training and six chapters. The chapters cover computer science and robotics, the Thymio robot, first steps in programming, Scratch, Thymio Studio, and classroom activities. EPFL and Inria produced it. FUN issues completion certificates.

## Benefits for students
- Students gain from a teacher who has practiced with the robot.
- Students can try activities built for primary and secondary classes.
- Students can program in Scratch and Thymio Studio.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course runs at the teacher's own pace.
- It includes educational activities and teaching tracks.
- Teachers receive a certificate of completion from FUN.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free.
- A Thymio robot costs extra and no price was read.
- The course comes in English and French.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Thymio MOOC on the EPFL courseware site.
- [ ] Register for an account on the site.
- [ ] Enroll in the course.
- [ ] Plan about 20 hours over several weeks.
- [ ] Install the free Thymio Suite software.
- [ ] Get a Thymio robot for the practice activities.
- [ ] Finish a chapter and note ideas for your class.

## Where is the AI?
The course has no AI. It teaches teachers how to program the robot and plan lessons. Students later build sequencing and logic skills.

## Privacy and safety
The MOOC page requires registering and signing in, and the course runs on a vendor cloud. The research did not read the platform privacy policy. The materials do not state what data the courseware collects or whether the internet is needed. The research did not review the tutorial part. Read the platform privacy details before registering. Check whether the course works on the school network.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Try the first steps in programming activities from chapter three with students.
2. Build a Scratch program that controls the robot.
3. Use Thymio Studio to program a simple behavior.
4. Compare two ways to program the same task.
5. Write a short lesson plan from the classroom activities chapter.

### engineering
1. Study how the Thymio robot senses its surroundings.
2. Test the robot's sensors and record what each one detects.
3. Design a short challenge that uses two sensors.
4. Compare the robot's behavior in two different rooms.
5. Present a robot design to the class and explain how it works.
