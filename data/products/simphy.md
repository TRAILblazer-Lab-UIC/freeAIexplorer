## Overview
SimPhy is a physics simulation tool for building 2D and 3D simulations with drag and drop. Its site lists modules for optics, virtual labs, mechanics, 3D simulations, fields and forces, and circuits. Teachers can build their own simulations or use a library of ready-to-run ones. It targets teachers and students.

## Benefits for students
- Students run and change physics simulations.
- Students see forces, optics, and circuits in action.
- Students can build a simulation of their own.

## Benefits for teachers
- A library of ready-to-run simulations saves prep time.
- Teachers can build custom simulations by drag and drop.
- The vendor claims 256 simulations, which is unverified marketing.

## Benefits for schools
- The site offers Try for Free and Buy Pro options.
- The software runs on the computer after download.
- IT must install it.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download SimPhy from the SimPhy site.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Choose the free trial or check the Pro price.
- [ ] Install it on one test computer.
- [ ] Open one ready-to-run simulation and change a setting.
- [ ] Build one simple simulation of your own.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI. SimPhy runs physics simulations. Students build skills in spotting patterns as they change a setting and watch the result.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state whether accounts are needed or what data is collected. The software runs on the computer and the internet is used only at setup. A teacher should check the privacy policy and ask IT what the install collects.
