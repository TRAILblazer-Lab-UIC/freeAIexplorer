## Overview
SOLIDWORKS Design Standard for Students is a free Windows desktop CAD download. Students sign up through a form and receive a download link and a serial number. Students also get guest access to My SolidWorks video lessons and tutorials. The vendor blog says certification exams cost extra.

## Benefits for students
- Students use real CAD software at no cost.
- Students learn from video lessons and tutorials.
- Students can work on their own computer after the download.

## Benefits for teachers
- Students sign up on their own.
- Tutorials support self-paced learning.
- The teacher learning curve is moderate.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free. Certification exams cost extra.
- Worldwide free availability was reported to begin on 2026-07-01.
- IT approval may be needed. The software runs on Windows only.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the SOLIDWORKS student sign-up form.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install on school devices.
- [ ] Have each student sign up, accept the license terms, and receive the serial number.
- [ ] Download the installer and enter the serial number.
- [ ] Stay online for the setup.
- [ ] Open a My SolidWorks tutorial.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in these materials. SOLIDWORKS is a CAD tool. Students build skills in spatial thinking and in planning a part.

## Privacy and safety
An account is required, and the internet is used at setup. Students sign up through a form that sends personal details to the vendor. The research reviewed no privacy policy. A third party summary says commercial use is not permitted. A teacher should read the privacy terms and the license terms before students register.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Follow a My SolidWorks tutorial and model a simple part.
2. Measure a small object and model it at the same size.
3. Sketch a shape and extrude it into a 3D part.
4. Combine two parts into a simple assembly.
5. Present the model and explain each step.
