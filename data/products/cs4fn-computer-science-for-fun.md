## Overview
CS4FN is a computer science outreach project at Queen Mary University of London. It publishes a magazine and a website of articles and activities for school students. Paul Curzon and Peter McOwan created it in 2005. A spin-off called A Bit of cs4fn targets primary schools.

## Benefits for students
- Students read articles that explain computer science ideas.
- Students try activities that connect to the articles.
- Primary students have their own spin-off, A Bit of cs4fn.

## Benefits for teachers
- A teachers area lets UK schools request printed copies.
- The articles can be read online or downloaded as PDFs.
- The magazine is printed twice a year.

## Benefits for schools
- The magazine is free to subscribing UK schools.
- Free PDFs have been reported.
- The vendor does not describe any account or device needs.
