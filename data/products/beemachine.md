## Overview
BeeMachine is a platform for recording bee sightings. It uses computer vision to identify bee species from photos. Sightings go into an open database for wild bee conservation. Classes can create or join projects, view shared sightings, and download reports.

## Benefits for students
- Students photograph bees and see species suggestions.
- Students add to a real conservation database.
- Students can compare sightings with classmates in a group project.

## Benefits for teachers
- A class project shows shared sightings and statistics.
- Teachers can download reports.
- The app is easy to learn.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is listed as free in the App Store.
- It works on the web and on iOS and Android phones.
- Sightings go to an open database. Student photos and locations may become public.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find BeeMachine on the web or in an app store.
- [ ] Read the privacy terms and the open database notes.
- [ ] Sign up with a teacher account.
- [ ] Create a class project and invite members.
- [ ] Decide what location settings students may use.
- [ ] Practice taking clear photos on school grounds.
- [ ] Review each sighting before it is shared.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is an image classifier that names bee species from photos. The vendor does not say where the model runs, and the data flow points to the vendor's cloud. Students take photos, read the suggestions, and decide whether to accept them in their own survey work.

## Privacy and safety
The App Store label lists precise and coarse location, name, email, photos, and user ID linked to the user. The label is self reported and Apple does not verify it. Sightings feed an open database. The privacy page returned 404 and the research did not read a policy. The materials do not state whether accounts are needed. A teacher should check what becomes public before students post any photo or place.
