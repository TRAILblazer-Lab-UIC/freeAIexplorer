## Overview
This is a set of five downloadable guides of hands-on AI projects. The guides are Elementary Educator, Secondary Educator, Elective Educator, Computer Science, and AI Ethics. Projects range from unplugged activities to building chatbots and simple video games. Each guide comes in English, Spanish, and Arabic.

## Benefits for students
- Students can read lessons in their home language.
- Students try hands-on projects from unplugged to chatbots.
- Students discuss AI ethics.

## Benefits for teachers
- Guides are organized by audience, which makes them easy to pick.
- Unplugged projects need only printing and simple materials.
- Teachers can share the Spanish or Arabic edition with families.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Run an unplugged project and explain the rules that the class followed.
2. Build a simple chatbot and test it.
3. Make a small game that uses one AI idea.
4. Compare two chatbots and describe their differences.
5. Plan a new project for a younger class.

### world_languages
1. Read a short project in Spanish or Arabic and explain it in English.
2. Translate a project step into a second language and compare with the guide.
3. Teach an AI term in a home language to a classmate.
4. Write chatbot replies in two languages and compare them.
5. List words that are hard to translate in the guide.

### social_studies
1. Read the AI Ethics guide and name two risks.
2. Debate one case where AI helps and one where it harms.
3. Write rules for fair AI use in your school.
4. Ask a community member how AI affects them.
5. Make a poster that explains AI and fairness.
