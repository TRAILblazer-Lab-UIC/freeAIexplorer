## Overview
My Robotic Friends is Lesson 1 of CS Fundamentals 2018 Course E and takes about 60 minutes. Groups of four split into pairs. Each pair writes a program from six allowed symbols for the other pair's robot to follow when stacking cups. The class then discusses the hardest parts of writing and following instructions.

## Benefits for students
- Students act as both programmer and robot.
- Students practice debugging by raising a hand to report bugs.
- Students use only the symbol key, which teaches exact instructions.

## Benefits for teachers
- The plan includes a symbol key and a stacking ideas sheet.
- Standards cited include Common Core Grade 4 and NGSS 3-5.
- The lesson ends with a debugging discussion and a journal.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson is free.
- The main supply is 10 disposable cups per group of four.
- Paper trapezoid templates work in place of cups.

## Where is the AI?
This lesson has no AI. It builds programming and debugging skills. Students write a program, run it on a human robot, and fix the bugs.

## Privacy and safety
The lesson runs on paper and with cups, and no student data is collected. No account and no internet are needed after the download. A teacher should remind students that the robot follows only the written symbols. The programmers stay silent while the robot works.
