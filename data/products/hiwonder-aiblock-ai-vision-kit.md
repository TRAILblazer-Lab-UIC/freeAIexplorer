## Overview
The AiBlock AI Vision Kit is an ESP32 robot kit with 8 AI vision modes. The listing says they work without internet. Modes include face recognition, line following, color recognition, and tag recognition. Students program it in Scratch blocks with WonderLab or in Python with the Hiwonder Python Editor. The listing describes it as K-12 for ages 8 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Students see a robot recognize faces, colors, and tags on its own.
- Students can start in Scratch blocks and move to Python.
- An optional voice module adds custom spoken commands.

## Benefits for teachers
- Two programming paths fit mixed skill levels.
- The robot connects by USB with no account.
- The kit suits grades 3 to 12 according to the extract.

## Benefits for schools
- The price was not confirmed. One reseller lists it in rupees.
- The kit works with no internet after setup.
- WonderLab runs on Windows, and IT will likely install it.

## Where is the AI?
The kit has 8 AI vision modes, including face, color, and tag recognition, and an optional voice module for speech recognition. The AI runs on the robot hardware. Students program what the robot does after it sees something. They can add custom voice commands with the voice module.

## Privacy and safety
The listing says the vision modes work with no internet, and the AI runs on the robot. The research read only one reseller page. It did not confirm whether the voice module is fully offline or whether a mobile app or account is needed. The vendor does not say what data the software collects, and the research did not review a privacy policy. A teacher should check the WonderLab install and test the face recognition mode with consent in mind.
