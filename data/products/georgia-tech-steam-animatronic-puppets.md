## Overview
This is a grade 9 to 12 STEAM unit that combines mechatronics, theater arts and music technology. Students design and build a jump-scare style animatronic puppet like those in haunted house exhibits. The parts list includes a pneumatic system and an Adafruit Audio FX Player. The unit runs 2 to 4 class periods, and a lesson package is available for download.

## Benefits for students
- Students combine mechanics, theater and sound in one build.
- Students show their puppets to the school at lunch.
- Students design a surprise that fits a story.

## Benefits for teachers
- A lesson package is available for download.
- The unit fits in 2 to 4 class periods.
- The GoSTEAM program is a Georgia Tech partnership with Gwinnett, Clayton and Atlanta schools.

## Benefits for schools
- The cost was not confirmed.
- Schools must source pneumatic parts, an audio player and craft supplies.
- The build needs no internet.

## Where is the AI?
This unit has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is controlling a machine with sensors and signals. Students trigger motion and sound at the right time.

## Privacy and safety
The puppets run on the device and the page names no software or accounts. The page describes no student data collection. A teacher should review air pressure safety before students use the pneumatic system. Check the sound levels and the scare level with the school before the showcase. Use eye protection while building.

## Ideas by subject
### theater_media
1. Design a puppet character and write a short scene for it.
2. Plan the timing of the jump scare.
3. Build the puppet body from mounting board, glue and tape.
4. Perform the puppet for classmates.
5. Show the puppets to the school at lunch.

### engineering
1. Build a pneumatic arm that moves the puppet.
2. Test the air system and fix any leak.
3. Adjust the speed of the motion.
4. Mount the parts so they stay in place.
5. Write a short list of design improvements.

### music
1. Choose a sound for the jump scare.
2. Load the sound on the Audio FX Player.
3. Match the sound to the puppet's motion.
4. Test different volumes and pick the best one.
5. Compare how music and silence change the scare.
