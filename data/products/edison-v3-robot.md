## Overview
Edison is a programmable robot for students aged 4 to 16 according to the maker. It works with LEGO bricks, is rechargeable and has no loose parts. It can be programmed with barcodes for a screen-free option. It can also be programmed with the free EdBlocks, EdScratch and EdPy languages.

## Benefits for students
- Students can start with barcodes and no screen.
- Students move on to blocks and then to text code.
- Students build with LEGO bricks.

## Benefits for teachers
- Free lesson plans, teacher guides and activity sheets are available.
- A teacher reported using EdScratch and barcodes with Grade 5 and 6 students.
- One robot grows with the class.

## Benefits for schools
- A single robot costs 59 USD and the Class 30 pack costs 1199 USD on the global site.
- The global site and an Australian site differ in currency and shipping.
- The apps need an internet connection per the EdScratch FAQ.

## Where is the AI?
Edison has no AI. The robot follows barcodes or programs that students build. Students build skills in sequencing, sensors and logic.

## Privacy and safety
The robot runs programs on board, and barcode use needs no software. The online programming apps need no account, and optional registration collects name and email. The apps need the internet to open. The vendor says no student data is collected by default and states SOPIPA compliance. A teacher should read the current privacy policy and decide which language fits the age of the class.
