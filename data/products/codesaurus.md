## Overview
Codesaurus is a print and play board game for ages 5 and up with 2 to 4 players and 15 to 30 minutes of play. Players move dinosaur pieces with command cards that work like lines of code. The first to reach the nest wins. The author is Dana Phelps, and the game is a remix of OctoStudio Remix.

## Benefits for students
- Students plan moves with command cards that act like code.
- Students race to the nest with a clear goal.
- Students can later move to OctoStudio with early coding ideas in mind.

## Benefits for teachers
- The page offers printable instructions, a 20 by 20 inch board, command cards, and tokens.
- A game lasts 15 to 30 minutes.
- The page suggests playing before OctoStudio.

## Benefits for schools
- The game is free to print.
- Cardstock and printing costs apply.
- Optional 3D printed dinosaur pieces are available for schools with a printer.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Codesaurus on the page by Dana Phelps.
- [ ] Download the PDFs for the instructions, board, cards, and tokens.
- [ ] Print the pages on cardstock.
- [ ] Tape the six board sheets into one board.
- [ ] Cut out the command cards and tokens.
- [ ] Read the rules and play a trial round.
- [ ] Optional step is to print 3D dinosaur pieces.

## Where is the AI?
This game has no AI. It builds sequencing, which is the skill of planning commands in order. Students use command cards to run a short program for a dinosaur.

## Privacy and safety
The game is paper based, and no student data is collected. No account is needed, and the internet is used only to download the files. A teacher should check the print quality and the age range before class. The vendor materials do not describe other safety steps.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Lay out command cards to move a dinosaur toward the nest.
2. Predict where a card sequence ends before you play it.
3. Fix a card sequence that sends a dinosaur the wrong way.
4. Compare plans with a partner and choose the shorter one.
5. Play Codesaurus first and then try OctoStudio to see the same ideas.
