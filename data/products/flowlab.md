## Overview
Flowlab is a browser-based game maker. Students build games with a visual, flow-based logic editor. A teacher plan adds a class dashboard for student projects. Search results report that no prior programming is needed. The official pages returned errors when fetched. Details come from search snippets.

## Benefits for students
- Students build playable games in a browser.
- Students plan game rules with visual logic.
- Students save projects after signing in.

## Benefits for teachers
- A teacher dashboard shows student projects.
- A search snippet reports roster import from Google Classroom and LTI.
- Teachers create a class and students join it.

## Benefits for schools
- A free tier exists. A search snippet lists a Teacher Plan at 42 USD per month for one teacher and 25 students.
- Projects are stored on Flowlab servers.
- It runs in a browser and works on Chromebooks.

## Where is the AI?
Flowlab has no AI. It builds logic and sequencing skills. Students decide what happens when a player acts and set the rules.

## Privacy and safety
Student accounts are required and projects are stored on Flowlab servers. The materials do not say who holds the accounts or what student data is collected. The research could not fetch the privacy policy and the official pages returned errors. Games can be published and shared publicly, which parents and teachers should consider. A teacher should read the privacy terms and set the sharing options before students publish. Confirm all details on the site.
