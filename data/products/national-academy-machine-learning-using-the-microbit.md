## Overview
This is a six-lesson Year 9 computing unit from Oak National Academy. Pupils learn about machine learning with the BBC micro:bit. They gather movement data, train and test a model, and build an app that uses its predictions. They finish by documenting the model with a model card.

## Benefits for students
- Students record and label their own movement data with a micro:bit.
- Students train, test, and improve a classification model.
- Students learn to judge a model by writing a model card.

## Benefits for teachers
- The unit follows a clear order of six lessons.
- Slides and worksheets are free to download.
- The unit lines up with the England KS3 computing curriculum.

## Benefits for schools
- The lessons are free.
- Each pupil or pair needs a micro:bit.
- Pupils need a browser device for the online model tool, and a teacher should plan about an hour of setup.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a classification model that students train on movement data from the micro:bit. The model is trained and tested in the browser. Students collect examples, check how well the model predicts, and improve it with better data. They then build an app that uses its predictions.

## Privacy and safety
Students record movement data with the micro:bit and train a model in an online tool called CreateAI. The materials do not say where CreateAI processes or stores that data. The research did not check its data handling. The internet is needed throughout, and the Oak pages read needed no teacher login. A teacher should test CreateAI before class and confirm what it stores.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Name three AI applications that students meet each day and explain what each one predicts.
2. Record micro:bit movement data for two actions, such as shaking and tilting, and label each sample.
3. Train a model and test it on new movements, then count how many it gets right.
4. Improve the model by adding more varied examples and compare the results.
5. Build a small app that uses the model, then write a model card that lists its limits.
