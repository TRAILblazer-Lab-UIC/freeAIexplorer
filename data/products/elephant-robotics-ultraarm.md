## Overview
The ultraArm is a desktop robot arm from Elephant Robotics. The official site lists the P1 and P340 models. A sponsored IEEE article says the P340 has a metal body, stepper motors, and repeatable accuracy of 0.1 mm. It works with five add-on kits for vision and picking, conveyor belt, slide rail, drawing, and laser engraving.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a real arm that draws, picks, and engraves.
- Students can add a vision kit to explore machine vision.
- Students can use Python, C++, Arduino, or ROS2, according to search snippets.

## Benefits for teachers
- Five add-on kits give many project paths from one arm.
- The Elephant Luban software makes G-code for drawing and engraving.
- Software download links exist on the vendor site.

## Benefits for schools
- Launch pre-orders were $649 for the standard kit and one listing showed $699. Current prices were not confirmed.
- The arm needs a computer, and details were not confirmed.
- No independent hands-on reviews were found. IT should plan on a teacher or IT install.

## Where is the AI?
The arm itself has no required AI. The vision and picking add-on kit adds machine vision. The materials do not say where that vision runs. Students who add the kit can use it to detect objects and then program the arm to pick them.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. They also do not say what data the software collects or whether the internet is needed. The only privacy policy found appears to cover an Elephant Robotics app. It says the app may collect personal information and log data and uses Google Play Services. It also says the app is not directed at children under 13. The research did not confirm that it applies to the ultraArm software. A teacher should ask the vendor about data handling and keep hands clear of the arm while it moves. The laser engraving kit needs extra safety rules.
