## Overview
CAD Dimensions offers a free laser cutting course guide for educators in STEM, CTE, and creative programs. The page says it contains lesson plans on design, materials, and laser operation, defined learning outcomes, project-based assignments, safety guidelines, and cross-disciplinary applications. The download link is sent after the visitor fills out a form. The page names no grade levels and no required software.

## Where is the AI?
This guide has no AI. It teaches students to design for a laser cutter. They build skills in design, sequencing, and precise measurement.

## Privacy and safety
The guide is a vendor resource, and the download link is sent after the visitor fills out a form. The form collects teacher contact details for a sales lead, and the research did not list the form fields. CAD Dimensions is a CAD and 3D printing reseller. The page says submitted information will never be sold to third parties and links a privacy policy, which was not read. The guide itself is gated, and the research did not read it. A teacher should use a work email and read the form's privacy terms. A teacher should also follow laser safety guidance with students.

## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Read the safety guidelines and write a short safety checklist.
2. Compare materials that are safe to laser cut.
3. Plan a small product that a shop could make with a laser.
4. Cut a test piece and measure its accuracy.
5. Present a finished project and explain the steps.

### engineering
1. Draw a part in design software for a laser cutter.
2. Choose a material and explain why it fits the part.
3. Test two settings and compare the cut quality.
4. Fix a design that did not fit together.
5. Document the design and the settings used.

### visual_arts
1. Sketch a pattern for a laser cut piece.
2. Cut a simple pattern and describe how it looks.
3. Layer two cut pieces to make a picture.
4. Compare paper and wood cut art.
5. Display the pieces and describe the design choices.
