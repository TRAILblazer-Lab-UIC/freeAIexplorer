## Overview
NeuroViz is an interactive tool for exploring how fully connected neural networks train. Users adjust the architecture, activation functions, learning rates, and datasets. They then watch activations, weight updates, and loss as training runs. The site returned almost no readable text. Details come from a preprint paper.

## Benefits for students
- Students see a network learn as it trains.
- Students change settings and watch the loss respond.
- Students connect activations and weight updates to the final result.

## Benefits for teachers
- The tool gives a visual aid for teaching how training works.
- A paper reports a 31-participant comparison with six existing tools and a SUS score of 80.97.
- The paper is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.

## Benefits for schools

## Where is the AI?
The tool trains small neural networks and shows what happens inside. The vendor does not say where the training runs. Students change the network design and the learning rate, then watch how the activations and loss change.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need or where any data goes. The site is a JavaScript app and its text could not be read. A teacher should open the site first, look for any sign-in or data collection, and ask the authors if anything is unclear.
