## Overview
TunePad is a free online platform for making music with Python. It offers beginner tutorials and advanced production tools. The site says students from middle school to college have used it in schools, camps, and clubs. It was developed as a companion to EarSketch with more focus on notes and beats. Everything runs in the web browser.

## Benefits for students
- Students write Python and hear music right away.
- Students follow step by step tutorials on the Learn page.
- Students can share songs and code.

## Benefits for teachers
- Educators can email support@tunepad.com for curriculum materials.
- A 10 week curriculum was used in fifth grade classes in Evanston/Skokie District 65.
- A case study covered middle schoolers in a TunePad coding camp.

## Benefits for schools
- The platform is free with optional donations.
- It works in any browser with audio output, including on Chromebooks.
- It needs the internet at all times and accounts.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the TunePad site and try the Learn tutorials.
- [ ] Read the privacy policy before students make accounts.
- [ ] Email the support address for curriculum materials.
- [ ] Create teacher and student accounts.
- [ ] Check that each device has audio output or headphones.
- [ ] Teach one tutorial and watch how students respond.
- [ ] Plan a block of weekly sessions.

## Where is the AI?
TunePad has no AI. Students write Python code that makes sounds, and the code runs in the browser. They practice sequencing, loops, and pattern building.

## Privacy and safety
A username and password are required. Optional details include email, age, gender, and country. The site also logs songs, code, IP address, browser type, and pages visited, and it uses Google Analytics. All of this goes to the vendor. The privacy policy is dated 2018 and does not address minors, schools, COPPA, or FERPA. Read the policy and limit optional personal details.
