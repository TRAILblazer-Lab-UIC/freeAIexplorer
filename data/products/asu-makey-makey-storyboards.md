## Ideas by subject
### ela
1. Plan a short fictional story with a beginning, middle and end.
2. Draw each scene on a storyboard panel.
3. Write the words or sounds for each panel.
4. Trade storyboards and give feedback on the story.
5. Revise the storyboard after feedback.

### computer_science
1. Connect a Makey Makey to a computer and test one touch point.
2. Map each panel to a key press.
3. Fix a touch point that does not respond.
4. Draw a diagram of the circuit you built.
5. Explain how a touch becomes a computer input.
