## Overview
This is a short article in AI Matters volume 7, issue 1 from March 2021. It appears on pages 10 to 12. It introduces two unplugged activities for K-12 AI education that need no technology. Later papers describe one of them as using a semantic network to show how agents make connections.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Draw a semantic network that links words about animals.
2. Add a new connection to the network and explain why it fits.
3. Trade networks with a partner and answer questions using only the map.
4. Compare two networks for the same topic and note the differences.
5. Describe how an AI agent could use a network to answer a question.
