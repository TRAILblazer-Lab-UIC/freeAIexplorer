## Overview
AutoLearn is an education module built on DonkeyCar, an open source small self-driving car platform. It uses the Chameleon Cloud testbed to teach cloud computing, edge devices, and AI. Students configure a car, collect driving data, and train models on cloud GPUs. The paper appeared at the EduHPC-23 workshop in 2023.

## Benefits for students
- Students move from a real car to cloud training.
- Students learn how edge and cloud computing work together.
- Students see their own driving data become a model.

## Benefits for teachers
- The module covers autonomous vehicles, cloud, and AI together.
- A published paper describes the design.
- Pathways guide students through the steps.

## Benefits for schools
- Cost is not verified. It needs a small DonkeyCar kit.
- It needs a Chameleon Cloud account.
- Setup is advanced, and IT help is needed.

- [ ] Train a model on the cloud GPUs.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a driving model trained on cloud GPUs and run on the car. Students collect data on the car and train in the cloud. They then load the model back on the car and test it. The notes here are based only on search snippets.

## Privacy and safety
An account and constant internet access are needed. Driving data goes to the Chameleon Cloud testbed. The research could not verify account details because the paper was too large to fetch. The materials do not state what other student data is collected. A teacher should read the Chameleon Cloud terms before students sign up and confirm who owns the data that students upload.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Register a car on the testbed and describe each step.
2. Drive laps to collect data and note how much you recorded.
3. Train a model on cloud GPUs and compare it with a smaller local run.
4. Test the model on the car and record where it fails.
5. Explain in writing how data moves from the car to the cloud and back.
