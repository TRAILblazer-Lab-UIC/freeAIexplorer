## Overview
Neuronify is an educational app that simulates neural circuits with the integrate-and-fire model. Users drag and drop neurons and other circuit parts. They watch how a change to one connection affects a whole network. It builds intuition for neural phenomena without programming. It is a biological neuron simulator and does not train machine learning models.

## Benefits for students
- Students build circuits by dragging neurons onto a canvas.
- Students watch how one connection changes a whole network.
- Students can use camera-based sensing and touch control.

## Benefits for teachers
- No programming is needed to run a lesson.
- The app suits a science class on the brain and nerves.
- The link to AI is conceptual, which gives a way to discuss artificial neurons.

## Benefits for schools
- The site does not state a price and links to a Patreon support page.
- Installers exist for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iOS.
- Code has seen little recent activity, and IT must install the app.

- [ ] Plan a short activity that asks students to predict what a change will do.

## Where is the AI?
Neuronify has no AI of its own. It simulates biological neurons with fixed math rules. It builds an idea that supports AI learning, which is how connected units pass signals through a network. Students change a connection and watch the result.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used only to download the app. The pages describe no accounts or data upload. The research found no privacy policy and did not test the app for data use. The materials do not say what student data is collected. A teacher should ask IT to check the app after install and keep student saved files in a safe place.
