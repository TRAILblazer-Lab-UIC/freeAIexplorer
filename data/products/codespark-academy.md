## Overview
codeSpark is a game-based learn-to-code app for young children. It uses drag-and-drop puzzles with no reading in the early levels. Later levels teach loops, conditionals, variables, and debugging. The product page lists ages 3 to 10. BEGiN acquired codeSpark in 2021, and codespark.com now redirects to the BEGiN site.

## Benefits for students
- Young children can play without reading.
- Students move from sequencing to loops and variables.
- Students learn through puzzles and games.

## Benefits for teachers
- A search snippet says teachers create a class in a dashboard.
- Students join with a classroom code.
- The app suits teachers new to coding.

## Benefits for schools

## Where is the AI?
This app has no AI. It builds skills in sequencing, loops, and conditionals. Students arrange picture blocks to guide a character.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed, and the data goes to the vendor cloud. BEGiN family products collect child progress and activity, first name or nickname, age, and birth month and year. The BEGiN policy covers several brands. It says the HOMER product follows COPPA and is kidSAFE certified, and it says child activity information is not shared with third parties for marketing. It also says advertising and analytics sharing may count as a sale under California law. The research read only part of the policy and did not confirm which section applies to codeSpark classroom accounts. A teacher should read the BEGiN privacy policy and choose nicknames that do not show a student's real name.
