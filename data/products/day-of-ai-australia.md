## Overview
Day of AI Australia is a free national program for students in Years 1 to 10. It runs with MIT RAISE and is delivered locally by UNSW. Search results describe lesson streams for Years 1 to 2, 3 to 4, 5 to 6, and 7 to 10. Each stream covers what AI is, how machines learn, and ethics. The program also offers an AI Trivia game and AI Student Senate guides.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn what AI is and how machines learn.
- Students talk about ethics at a level that fits their year.
- Students can play an AI Trivia game.

## Benefits for teachers
- Lessons come with objectives, vocabulary, steps, timings, and slides, according to search results.
- Streams fit Years 1 to 10.
- Lessons are aligned to Australian Curriculum version 9, according to search results.

## Benefits for schools
- The program is free.
- A teacher registers for free to get the lesson plans.
- The materials do not say which devices or accounts students need.

## Where is the AI?
The program teaches about AI and does not run an AI tool. Students learn what AI is, how machines learn, and how to think about ethics. They use these ideas to talk about AI systems they see in daily life.

## Privacy and safety
Teachers register to get the lessons, and the program page says login and registration are available. The materials do not state what student data is collected or where it goes. Student tools vary by lesson, and the research did not itemize them. The research did not read a privacy policy. A teacher should read the program's privacy terms and check whether students need accounts for any activity.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Sort objects into groups and explain the rules you used as a first lesson on how machines learn.
2. Play the AI Trivia game and list one new fact you learned.
3. Describe an example of AI you see at home or at school.
4. Compare how a person and a machine learn to spot a cat.
5. Discuss a fair rule for using AI and write it on a class chart.
