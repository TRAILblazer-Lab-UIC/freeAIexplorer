## Overview
Sweet Home 3D is a free interior design application. Students draw house plans in 2D and arrange furniture, with a live 3D view beside the plan. The vendor runs a school page that suggests a house plan, a redesigned classroom, and a sustainable home. It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux and is available in 29 languages.

## Benefits for students
- Students place walls, doors, and furniture and see the result in 3D right away.
- Students turn a flat plan into a space they can look around.
- Students can work in their own language. The app comes in 29 languages.

## Benefits for teachers
- The vendor school page offers project ideas for students of all ages.
- A French teacher blog describes a three session sequence built around the software.
- Teachers in France, Ecuador, and Italy are reported to use it.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and the code is open.
- It runs on the device and the internet is needed only at setup.
- A graphics card with 3D support helps the 3D view. IT will need to install it on each computer.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download Sweet Home 3D from the vendor's site and read its school page.
- [ ] Ask IT to install it on each computer.
- [ ] Check that each computer has graphics that support 3D.
- [ ] The school page routes downloads through a form. Ask IT to review it.
- [ ] Draw a small room and add furniture to learn the tools.
- [ ] Look at the 3D view and walk through the room.
- [ ] Choose a first project, such as a classroom redesign.
- [ ] Paid versions may exist on app stores. Stay with the free download.

## Where is the AI?
Sweet Home 3D has no AI. Students make every choice about walls, doors, and furniture. They practice spatial thinking and learn to turn a plan into a model.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed for local use and the internet is used only at setup. Plans are saved on the device. The privacy page says the iOS and macOS apps include Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. It does not describe data collection in the desktop or Android versions. Check which version students will use and where they save their plan files.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Draw a complete house in 2D and check it in the 3D view, as the vendor school page suggests.
2. Redesign the classroom with a new layout and explain each choice.
3. Measure a real room and draw it to scale.
4. Test two furniture layouts and decide which works better.
5. Design a sustainable home and list its features.

### cte
1. Create a floor plan for a small business and place the rooms.
2. Design a home for a client with a stated need and present the plan.
3. Import a new furniture model and add it to the catalog.
4. Compare your plan with a classmate's and give feedback.
5. Make a portfolio page with 2D and 3D views of your best design.
