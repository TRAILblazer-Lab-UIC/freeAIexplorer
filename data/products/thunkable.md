## Overview
Thunkable is a mobile app builder that describes itself as AI powered. Users describe an app in plain language and an AI Builder makes screens, layout, and logic. They then refine the app with a visual editor or by editing code. Apps can be published to the iOS and Android stores and to the web.

## Benefits for students
- Students turn an idea into a working app on a real phone.
- Students can refine the app with blocks or code.
- Apps can use device features such as the camera, GPS, and Bluetooth.

## Benefits for teachers
- Thunkable for Education offers a 115-page project based curriculum.
- Teacher training is available.
- Version history lets students go back to an earlier version.

## Benefits for schools
- A free plan exists, but it has only public projects and 2,000 AI tokens.
- Paid plans range from 18 to 189 USD per month.
- The privacy review is a concern. Schools should ask about a data agreement.

## Where is the AI?
The AI Builder runs in the vendor's cloud. It makes screens and logic from a prompt and can make checklists and images. Students describe the app they want and then edit what the AI produced. The edits are where they learn how the app works.

## Privacy and safety
An account and internet access are required. Thunkable collects account details, email, IP address, device data, usage activity, and approximate location. For schools it also collects student email addresses and activity. Data goes to service providers, advertising partners, and analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. It may be stored anywhere in the world. The policy says the services are not directed to children under 13 and the school is responsible for consent. The policy does not mention AI tools, Common Sense has not rated privacy, and the research found no signed student data privacy agreement. The free plan makes projects public. Ask Thunkable for a data agreement and check project visibility before students publish.
