## Overview
Cornelius Composer is a music notation editor aimed at elementary students. Notes turn into animated creatures when the score is zoomed in. Students enter notes and play the score with instruments or solfege voices. They can edit while it plays. The app imports and exports MusicXML and MIDI and exports PDF.

## Benefits for students
- Students see notes as friendly creatures.
- Students hear their score at once.
- Students can edit while the music plays.

## Benefits for teachers
- Note colors can match boomwhackers.
- Colorblindness display options are listed.
- Teachers can import and export MusicXML and MIDI and export PDF.

## Benefits for schools
- The Windows listing shows a free download.
- The App Store lists in-app purchases of about 4.99 to 7.99 USD to unlock the full app.
- A separate free Schools edition exists for volume purchase on iOS.

## Getting started
- [ ] Install from the Microsoft Store on Windows.
- [ ] Install from the App Store on iPad or iPhone.
- [ ] Look into the Schools edition for volume purchase.
- [ ] Enter a short melody and play it back.
- [ ] Try the solfege voices.
- [ ] Set note colors to match classroom instruments.
- [ ] Check whether the app is still updated. The last version seen is dated April 2022.

## Where is the AI?
This app has no AI. It builds skills in pattern spotting and sequencing. Students arrange notes in order and hear the pattern.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The App Store label for the iOS Schools edition reads Data Not Collected, and Apple notes that labels are not verified. The Windows Store privacy details could not be read, and no privacy policy was found. The research assumed work stays on the device and the internet is needed only for setup. A teacher should check the Windows version on a school device and ask the vendor about its data practices.
