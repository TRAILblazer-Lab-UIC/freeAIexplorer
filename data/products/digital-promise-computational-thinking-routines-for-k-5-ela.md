## Overview
Digital Promise offers a toolbox of five computational thinking routines for K-5 English language arts. Each routine has anchor charts, lesson plans, and visuals. The routines were co-designed with seven elementary ELA teachers in New York City. They are unplugged and need no technology.

## Benefits for students
- Students build abstraction, algorithmic thinking, decomposition, and pattern recognition.
- Students use these skills while they read.
- Students work with routines such as Break It Down and Write a Flowchart.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each routine has anchor charts and lesson plans.
- The routines tie to ELA reading instruction.
- No technology is needed.

## Benefits for schools
- The sources found describe the routines as free. The price was not verified on the official page.
- The routines need no devices.
- No IT help is needed.

## Ideas by subject
### ela
1. Break a story into a beginning, middle, and end.
2. Use Break It Down on a hard paragraph.
3. Write a flowchart that shows the order of events in a story.
4. Find a repeating pattern in a poem.
5. Tell a partner the main idea in a few words.

### computer_science
1. Name the steps needed to finish a class task.
2. Draw a flowchart for getting ready for recess.
3. Break a big problem into three smaller parts.
4. Find a pattern in a set of numbers or shapes.
5. Tell which details matter and which can be left out.
