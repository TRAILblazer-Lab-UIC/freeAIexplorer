## Overview
LightBurn is paid design and control software for laser cutters and engravers. It supports hundreds of laser models and offers a 30-day full-featured trial with no watermarks. Licenses are perpetual with one year of updates and there is no subscription. Students design artwork and send it to a laser.

## Benefits for students
- Students design artwork on screen and then see it cut or engraved.
- Students learn how a design becomes a physical part.
- Students practice layout and measurement.

## Benefits for teachers
- The 30-day trial lets teachers test the full software.
- One license covers up to 3 computers.
- The software works with hundreds of laser models.

## Benefits for schools
- Prices were not shown on the fetched page. Schools can ask about educational volume licenses.
- The software is useful only with a supported laser. Most of the cost is hardware.
- The software runs on Windows 10 or later and macOS 13 or later.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find LightBurn on the official LightBurn site.
- [ ] Confirm that the school laser is on the supported list.
- [ ] Ask IT to install the software on a Windows or macOS computer.
- [ ] Start the 30-day trial.
- [ ] Ask about educational volume licenses before the trial ends.
- [ ] Connect the laser and run a test cut on scrap material.
- [ ] Set up the laser safety rules with an adult in charge.

## Where is the AI?
LightBurn has no AI. It turns a design into instructions for the laser. Students build skills in design and measurement.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not say whether an account is needed or whether the internet is used. The policy covers the website, forum, and customers. It says LightBurn does not sell or share personal information and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. That is a data rule. The policy does not describe what the software sends during license activation. A teacher should check the license terms and whether activation needs the internet. A teacher should also supervise all laser use closely.

4. Test two settings and record how each cut looks.
5. Write a work order for a classmate to follow.

### engineering

2. Test the fit and adjust the size.
3. Make a flat design for a small box.
