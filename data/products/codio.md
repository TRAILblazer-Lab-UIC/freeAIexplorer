## Overview
Codio is a commercial platform for computer science, AI literacy, and workplace training. It offers preconfigured coding environments, auto-graded assignments, progress tracking, and rubric-based feedback. It serves higher education, K-12, and enterprise customers. The homepage says AI assistants including Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini are available inside courses.

## Benefits for students
- Students code in a browser with the setup done for them.
- Students get quick feedback from auto-graded work.
- Students can use AI assistants inside the course without a personal account.

## Benefits for teachers
- Faculty can view student prompts to the AI assistants.
- Teachers can use auto-graded assignments and rubrics.
- A free instructor account is available.

## Benefits for schools
- A search snippet of the K-12 page says student licenses start at 20 USD per student per year, with volume discounts.
- The platform runs in a browser, including on Chromebooks.
- IT will likely need to approve it and set up the LMS link.

## Getting started
- [ ] Create a free instructor account or request a school quote.

## Where is the AI?
Codio offers AI literacy assignments and AI assistants for students. The assistants run in the vendor's cloud. Faculty can view the prompts students send. Students use the assistants for help and then complete the work themselves.

## Privacy and safety
Every student needs an account, and the internet is needed at all times. The materials do not say what student data is collected. The research could not read the privacy policy, which returned an error. The homepage says AI assistants from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are offered inside courses. Student prompts likely reach those companies, and the research did not confirm the terms. A search snippet says Codio has a FERPA documentation page, and the research found no signed Student Data Privacy Consortium agreement. A teacher should ask the vendor which AI providers receive prompts. A teacher should also decide whether students may use the assistants.
