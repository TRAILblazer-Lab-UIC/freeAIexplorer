## Overview
Stretch3 is a Scratch 3 editor modified to load custom extensions. The page lists extensions for speech recognition, pose recognition, machine learning on images and sounds with Teachable Machine, and ChatGPT. The site is in Japanese. It is an unofficial community build and is not a product of the Scratch Team or MIT.

## Benefits for students
- Students use familiar Scratch blocks with AI extensions.
- Students train image and sound models with TM2Scratch.
- Students try speech and pose recognition.

## Benefits for teachers
- There is nothing to install.
- About 25 extensions are listed in the README.
- The site page is in Japanese and the English support is limited.

## Benefits for schools
- It is free and the code is open source.
- It runs in a browser on Chromebooks and computers.
- The page loads Google Analytics.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the Stretch3 site in a browser.
- [ ] Read the Stretch3 and TM2Scratch READMEs.
- [ ] Add an extension from the extension menu.
- [ ] Allow the camera or microphone if the extension needs it.
- [ ] Ask IT to review Google Analytics on the page.
- [ ] Skip the ChatGPT extension until the school approves it.
- [ ] Test one project before class.

## Where is the AI?
The AI includes speech recognition, pose recognition, image and sound classification, and a chatbot. TM2Scratch classifies in the browser. Extensions such as ChatGPT depend on outside services. Students train a model and then use it in a block project.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is needed at all times. The page loads Google Analytics, and the research found no privacy policy. TM2Scratch classifies camera and microphone input in the browser, but training happens in Google Teachable Machine. The ChatGPT extension sends text to OpenAI, and browser speech recognition may send audio to a cloud service. The README does not say which extensions use cloud services. A teacher should review which extensions students may use.
