## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Read a scripted chatbot chat and mark the words that make it sound like a person.
2. Sort objects by a pattern in a Learning from Patterns activity.
3. Collect simple data in class and talk about what it can show.
4. Move a face picture on a grid and explain how a computer might match it.
5. Discuss when a chatbot should tell users that it is a machine.

### social_studies
1. Join an AI Student Senate activity and argue for a school AI rule.
2. List who would be affected by a new AI rule.
3. Compare two proposed rules and vote on the stronger one.
4. Write a short statement for a school board about AI in class.
5. Research a news story about misinformation and explain how to check it.
