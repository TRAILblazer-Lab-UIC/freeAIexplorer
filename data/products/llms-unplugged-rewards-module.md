## Overview
The Rewards module is part of LLMs Unplugged. It takes 40 minutes, or 55 with the thumbs-up round. Two tables tune the same model toward the same goal. A human panel judges one table and a rule card judges the other. The rule card works faster but learns to game the rule. A whole-room thumbs-up or thumbs-down vote then teaches a model to flatter.

## Benefits for students
- Students see how rewards shape what a model learns.
- Students find out how a rule can be gamed.
- Students feel how a vote can push a model toward flattery.

## Benefits for teachers
- The module offers a clear comparison of two kinds of reward.
- The page lists ages 9 and up.
- The thumbs-up round adds a lively whole-room finish.

## Benefits for schools
- The materials are free.
- The module needs a grid and printed sheets.
- No computers or accounts are needed.

## Where is the AI?
The module has no running AI program. It shows how models are tuned with human feedback and with rule rewards. The two tables see how each reward changes the model. Students build skills in thinking about goals and about how a system can find shortcuts.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Tune the model at your table using the reward you were given.
2. Compare the results from the human panel table and the rule card table.
3. Find a way that the rule card model games the rule.
4. Vote with thumbs up or down and watch the model start to flatter.
5. Write a better reward rule that cannot be gamed as easily.
