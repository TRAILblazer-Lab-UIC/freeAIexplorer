## Overview
Cuttle is a browser-based vector editor for cut files. It offers live modifiers, reusable components, parametric design, and optional JavaScript scripting. A template library covers keychains, ornaments, and bag tags. A free K-12 plan gives verified schools unlimited teacher and student access to the core editor.

## Benefits for students
- Students design real objects that a machine can cut.
- Students can start from templates and change them.
- Students can learn parametric design and try JavaScript.

## Benefits for teachers
- The K-12 plan gives free templates and unlimited downloads.
- Schools that use Google logins can sign in with Google.
- After approval, all users on the school domain get access.

## Benefits for schools
- The K-12 plan is free for certified K-12 schools.
- It excludes Pro templates, Pro fonts, and Noun Project icons.
- IT should allowlist cuttle.xyz and cuttleusercontent.com.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Cuttle K-12 plan on the Cuttle website.
- [ ] Fill in the school application form.
- [ ] Wait for Cuttle to verify the school email domain. Cuttle replies within five business days.
- [ ] Ask IT to allowlist cuttle.xyz and cuttleusercontent.com.
- [ ] Sign in with a school account.
- [ ] Open a keychain template and change it.
- [ ] Arrange access to a laser cutter or vinyl cutter to make physical output.

## Where is the AI?
This tool has no AI. It builds skills in design, logic, and measurement. Students shape a design with rules and see the result.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed for all users, and the internet is needed at all times. Cuttle holds the school email address and the designs students create in its cloud. For verified school users it does not store first and last names and disables third-party analytics, per its K-12 page. Cuttle states it has signed several Student Data Privacy Agreements. The research could not read the privacy policy page itself. A teacher should check the school domain approval and read the Cuttle privacy policy.

4. Test a design and revise it after feedback.

5. Present a finished product and explain the skills used.

### visual_arts

3. Change fonts and layout on a bag tag.
