## Overview
This is a module in the ASA Data-Driven Mathematics series, published by Dale Seymour Publications with National Science Foundation support. It covers least-squares regression lines, the correlation coefficient, r and r squared, and mean squared error. The authors are Gail Burrill, Jack Burrill, Patrick Hopfensperger, and James Landwehr. The teacher edition carries a 1999 copyright, and the module is labeled Advanced Algebra.

## Benefits for students
- Students work on real problems with real data.
- Students learn to fit a line to data and judge the fit.
- Students build math skills that later help in data science.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teacher and student editions are both provided.
- The module recommends a graphing calculator and includes TI-83 procedures.
- The lessons are built around student discussion.

## Benefits for schools
- The PDFs are free from ASA.
- The module is paper based and needs only a graphing calculator.
- No accounts or IT help are needed.

## Where is the AI?
This module has no AI. The text does not mention machine learning. Students build data skills by fitting lines to data and judging how well they fit.

## Privacy and safety
The module is printed or a PDF and the research found no data collection. No account is needed. A graphing calculator is recommended and it runs locally. No internet is needed after the download. A teacher should check that the 1999 data and examples still fit the class.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Plot a set of real data points and describe the pattern.
2. Draw a line by eye and compare it with the least-squares line.
3. Find the correlation coefficient r for the data.
4. Explain what r squared says about the fit.
5. Compute the mean squared error for two lines and pick the better one.
