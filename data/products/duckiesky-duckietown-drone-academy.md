## Overview
DuckieSky is a drone robotics curriculum that Brown University adapted from a college class for high schools, in partnership with Duckietown. Students build a small quadrotor with a camera and a Raspberry Pi and program it to fly on its own. The course covers position estimation, safety, networking, and communications. A one-week Drone Academy at MassRobotics in Boston uses the same Brown kit.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a real drone from parts.
- Students program autonomous flight.
- Students learn safety, networking, and communications.

## Benefits for teachers
