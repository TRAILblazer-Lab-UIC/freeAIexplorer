## Overview
SMILE LLM is a project of Seeds of Empowerment, a global non-profit. It describes a portable offline AI tutor to train critical thinking in resource-limited settings. The project page says it runs Microsoft's Phi-2 model with llama.cpp on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of RAM. These are the project's own claims. Only a short teaser and a project detail page were read.

## Benefits for students
- Students could chat with an AI tutor that works offline.
- A search snippet lists activities such as Ask.SMILE, Two Words, Up and Down, Guess the Word, and SMILE Coach.
- The project aims at critical thinking.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each classroom could have its own AI assistant, per the project.
- Teachers could use it where internet access is limited.

## Benefits for schools
- The page says a Raspberry Pi 5 costs a fraction of cloud solutions. It gives no dollar figures.
- The device needs a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of RAM. Other parts were not listed.
- IT would need to build the setup. It is not documented.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is Microsoft's Phi-2 language model, run with llama.cpp. The project says it runs on the Raspberry Pi device with no internet after deployment. Students chat with the tutor in activities and use its replies to practice their own thinking.

## Privacy and safety
The project says student data never leaves the device and that no internet is needed after deployment. This is the project's own claim, and the research found no privacy policy or code to check it. The materials do not state whether accounts are needed. A teacher should ask the project to confirm the data claim and review the activities. The project lists AI safety problems such as bias and toxicity.
