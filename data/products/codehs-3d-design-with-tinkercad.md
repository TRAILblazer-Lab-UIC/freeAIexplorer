## Overview
This CodeHS course is for grades K to 5. Students design 3D models in Autodesk Tinkercad. They build a face, a keychain, and an animal, then use Tinkercad Codeblocks. They finish with design challenges such as a bubble wand, an accessible keyboard, a playground structure, and a bridge.

## Benefits for students
- Students turn ideas into 3D shapes on screen.
- Students try block coding with Tinkercad Codeblocks.
- Students practice the design process on real challenges.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course has lesson plans, slides, rubrics, and a teacher guide.
- Lessons take about 90 minutes and design projects about 45 minutes.
- The course lists state CS standards and ISTE Standards for Students.

## Benefits for schools
- The page lists no price and asks schools to contact CodeHS.
- Tinkercad is free.
- Each student needs a Tinkercad account, and data goes to CodeHS and Autodesk.

## Getting started
- [ ] Contact CodeHS for access to the course.

- [ ] Read the teacher guide and the rubrics.
- [ ] Try the face project yourself.
- [ ] Plan about 90 minutes for each lesson.
- [ ] Review the privacy terms of both services.

## Where is the AI?
This course has no AI. It builds skills in design thinking and logic. Students model shapes and use code blocks to build them.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed at both CodeHS and Tinkercad, and the internet is needed at all times. CodeHS holds account data and coursework, and Autodesk holds each student's Tinkercad account. The CodeHS policy says it acts as a school official under FERPA, collects parental consent through schools for users under 13, and does not sell personal information. The research did not review the Tinkercad policy. A teacher should read both privacy policies and check how young students sign in.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Design a bubble wand and test its shape.
2. Build an accessible keyboard and explain who it helps.
3. Plan a playground structure and list its parts.
4. Design a bridge and describe how it holds weight.
5. Improve a keychain after feedback from a partner.

### visual_arts
1. Model a 3D face with shapes and colors.
2. Create an animal from basic shapes.
3. Design a keychain with a pattern.
4. Compare a flat drawing and a 3D model of the same object.
5. Present a model to the class and describe the choices made.
