## Overview
Solarus is a lightweight, free, open source 2D game engine. It is written in C++ and has a Lua scripting API. It comes with a graphical game editor and launcher. It targets top-down action adventure games, and learners create or bring their own art and sound.

## Benefits for students
- Students design and build an adventure game.
- Students write Lua code for game logic.
- Students make or choose their own art and sound.

## Benefits for teachers
- An Endless Studios educator blog suggests it for electives and extracurricular courses.
- It is best for clubs and electives more than core lessons.
- The teacher learning curve is advanced.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- It runs on the computer after download.
- IT approval may be needed for installs.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the engine and editor from the Solarus site.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Install them on one test computer.
- [ ] Gather art and sound assets for students.
- [ ] Open the editor and make a small test map.
- [ ] Plan a club or elective project.

## Where is the AI?
Solarus has no AI. It is a game engine with a scripting language. Students build logic skills as they write code that decides what happens in a game.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used at setup. The engine runs locally. The research found no privacy policy. A teacher should check the license of any art and sound that students bring.
