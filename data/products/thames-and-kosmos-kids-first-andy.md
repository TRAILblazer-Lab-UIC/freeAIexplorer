## Overview
Andy is a screen-free toy robot for children ages 4 and up. Children program it with large buttons on its head. They can press arrow keys directly or enter a sequence of up to 30 moves and press Go. It needs no app or tablet.

## Benefits for students
- Children plan a set of moves and watch the robot follow them.
- Children play a game to guide Andy to find 27 friends.
- Lights, music, and a dance mode keep young learners interested.

## Benefits for teachers
- Nine robot cards support the game.
- Two movement distance modes let a teacher adjust the play.
- Teachers can print optional bonus games from the manual.

## Benefits for schools
- Price listings range from about 25.99 USD at Target to 49.95 USD at RobotShop.
- The robot needs three AA batteries.
- A retailer page says no app, tablet, account, or internet is needed.

## Where is the AI?
Andy has no AI. The robot repeats the moves that a child enters, in the same order each time. Children build sequencing skills as they plan a path and test it.

## Privacy and safety
A retailer page says Andy is programmed with buttons and needs no app or tablet. No accounts or connectivity are described. The manufacturer page could not be reached. The research relied on the retailer page. Programs live on the robot. Andy is a consumer toy and is not marketed to schools. Some Target reviewers reported trouble with programming and a cheap build. Test it first.
