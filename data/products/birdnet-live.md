## Overview
BirdNET Live is a mobile app that identifies animal sounds in real time on a smartphone. The models run on the phone and work offline. It covers about 10,000 species including birds, mammals, insects, and amphibians. Recordings are saved with location and time. Students can use it for field surveys.

## Benefits for students
- Students get live species suggestions in the field.
- Students can run structured surveys and review the audio later.
- Students can export results to share in class.

## Benefits for teachers
- Survey modes make field trips more organized.
- Exports include CSV, JSON, GPX, and Raven Pro files.
- The interface comes in seven languages.

## Benefits for schools
- The code is open source.
- The pages read do not state a price.
- A donation page is mentioned.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is the BirdNET+ model that classifies animal sounds. It runs on the phone. Students record a sound, read the suggestions, and then check them against their own notes. They can review a whole survey session afterward.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The app page says the internet is not required and recordings are stored locally. Recordings carry location and time. The page links a privacy policy that the research did not read. Remote data handling is not confirmed. The app is aimed at researchers and citizen scientists. A teacher should read the privacy policy and decide who holds the phone and who can see the saved locations.
