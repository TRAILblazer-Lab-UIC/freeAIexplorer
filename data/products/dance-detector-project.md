## Overview
Dance Detector is a guided project from the Raspberry Pi Foundation. Students train a movement model with micro:bit CreateAI. The project page could not be read. The steps are unverified. A Raspberry Pi Foundation blog post names it as a good starting point for micro:bit CreateAI.

## Benefits for students
- Students train a model on their own movements.
- Students see how data shapes what a model can spot.
- Students use a micro:bit to test the model.

## Benefits for teachers
- A Raspberry Pi Foundation blog names the project as a good starting point.
- The project needs only a micro:bit and the CreateAI web tool.
- Teachers should read the project page before class because its steps are unverified.

## Benefits for schools
- The project needs a micro:bit. The materials give no price.
- The tool runs in a browser.
- The materials do not say what IT must do.

## Where is the AI?
Students train a machine learning model from accelerometer data. The model is built in the browser through CreateAI and used with the micro:bit. Students record dance moves, train the model, and test how well it tells them apart.

## Privacy and safety
The research could not read the project page. Every detail is unverified. The materials do not say whether accounts are needed, where data goes, or whether the internet is needed. The tool is described as running in the browser, and no privacy policy was found. A teacher should check what CreateAI stores before students record data. A teacher should also make sure students do not record video of classmates.
