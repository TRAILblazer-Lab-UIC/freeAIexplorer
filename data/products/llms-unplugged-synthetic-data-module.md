## Overview
The Synthetic Data module is an extension of LLMs Unplugged by Ben Swift. Students run two relays side by side with pen, paper, and a d10. In one relay each link trains a fresh model on the previous link's output. In the other, everyone adds output to the same sheet on top of the real story. Only the first chain forgets its words, which illustrates model collapse. The module takes 40 to 50 minutes and is listed for Years 9 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Students see what happens when a model learns from machine text.
- Students compare two relays and spot the difference.
- Students learn why real data matters.

## Where is the AI?
The module has no running AI program. It shows model collapse, which can happen when models train on text made by other models. Students run relays of simple models by hand and see words disappear in one chain. They build skills in thinking about data quality.

## Privacy and safety
The module runs on paper with pen and a die. The pages read mention no accounts. The internet is needed only to read or print the module page. The research reviewed no privacy policy. The materials describe no student data collection. A teacher should read the CC BY SA 4.0 terms before sharing adapted sheets. A teacher should also check that students know the earlier modules.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Train a fresh model on the previous student's output and pass it on.
2. Add output to a shared sheet on top of the real story and compare the two chains.
3. Count which words disappear from the first chain.
4. Explain to a partner why the second chain keeps its words.
5. Write a short rule for choosing training data for a real model.
