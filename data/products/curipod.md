## Overview
Curipod is a web platform for K-12 teachers who run whole-class writing and reading lessons. The teacher starts a session and students join. The homepage highlights real-time AI feedback on student writing and lessons aligned to more than 55 curricula. The site says students do not use AI independently.

## Benefits for students
- Students get quick feedback on their written responses.
- Students join a session from any device with a browser.
- Students see revision reports that show how their writing changed.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers lead the session and see all responses in one place.
- Reports show student responses and revisions.
- A free plan covers weekly writing sessions for an individual teacher.

## Benefits for schools
- Schools can sign in with ClassLink, Clever, or Google.
- Schools can request a data privacy agreement.
- School and District plans are custom quotes. Devices need only a modern browser.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Curipod on its official website.

- [ ] Share the join link with students.

## Where is the AI?
The AI gives feedback on student writing and can help generate lessons and read text aloud. It runs in a company's cloud. The vendor says AI calls go to OpenAI, Google, and ElevenLabs without personal data. Students write, read the feedback, and revise their own work.

## Privacy and safety
A teacher account is needed. Students can join without accounts per the pricing page. The vendor collects student responses and optionally student names, and keeps IP addresses for up to 3 weeks. Responses go to the vendor, and AI calls go to OpenAI, Google and ElevenLabs without personal data per the vendor. The policy does not name its sub-processors. A teacher should read the privacy policy, avoid real names where possible, and ask the school for a data privacy agreement.
