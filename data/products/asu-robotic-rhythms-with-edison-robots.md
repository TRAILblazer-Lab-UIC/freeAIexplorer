## Ideas by subject
### music
1. Explore the robot's built-in sounds and describe each one.
2. Build a short rhythm with the percussion features.
3. Repeat a rhythm pattern and clap along.
4. Change the order of sounds and describe the new rhythm.
5. Perform your sequence for the class.

### computer_science
1. Build a sequence of sound blocks in EdBlocks.
2. Run the program on the robot and listen.
3. Debug a block that plays at the wrong time.
4. Use a repeat block to loop a rhythm.
5. Swap programs with a partner and describe what they do.
