## Overview
Elements of AI is a free online course from the University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn. It is made for a general audience and is self paced. Part 1, Introduction to AI, needs no programming or complex math. Part 2, Building AI, recommends basic Python. AI4K12 says it is accessible to upper-level high school students. The course is offered in many languages.

## Benefits for students
- Older students learn what AI is and how machine learning and neural networks work.
- Students explore AI ethics and philosophy.
- Students can work at their own pace.

## Benefits for teachers
- Part 1 needs no programming.
- The course can serve as independent study or enrichment.
- The vendor does not say how many hours it takes.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free.
- It runs in a browser, including on Chromebooks.
- IT should review the data the course collects at sign-up and the analytics services it names.

## Where is the AI?
The course teaches about AI and does not run an AI tool. Students read, answer exercises, and think about how machine learning and neural networks work. They use the ideas to judge claims about AI.

## Privacy and safety
The course runs online and the internet is always needed. The research did not confirm whether an account is required. The privacy policy describes sign-up data such as name, password, email, country, address, zip code, and identification details such as a student number. It also keeps course progress until the user asks for deletion. The policy names outside services for email, analytics, support, and certificate payments. These include Google Analytics and Hotjar. The policy states no minimum age and has no provisions for children. It is aimed at adult learners. A teacher should read the privacy policy and check with IT before students create accounts.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Read Part 1 and write a one page summary of what machine learning is.
2. Compare a neural network with a human brain in a short chart.
3. Complete an exercise on a simple classifier and explain the answer.
4. Try the Building AI exercises with basic Python if the class is ready.
5. Present one idea from the course to the class.

### social_studies
1. Read the sections on AI ethics and list two questions that matter to your community.
2. Debate whether AI should make decisions about people.
3. Write a short essay on a social effect of AI.
4. Compare the course's view of AI with a news article.
5. Create a poster that shows one benefit and one risk of AI.
