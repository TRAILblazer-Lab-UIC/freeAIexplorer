## Overview
Vector is a desktop companion robot with a camera, face recognition, and voice interaction. Anki created it in 2018. Digital Dream Labs now sells Vector 2.0 at 249.99 USD. It is a consumer robot that was not designed for classrooms.

## Benefits for students
- Students watch a robot that sees faces and answers speech.
- Students can use it to talk about how voice AI works.
- Students see timers, weather, and snapshot features in action.

## Benefits for teachers
- The robot is simple to use.
- Wire-Pod is a free community server that restores voice commands.
- The robot suits demonstrations.

## Benefits for schools
- Voice commands need a paid plan at 11.99 USD per month or 99.99 USD per year.
- Wire-Pod avoids the plan but needs a host computer.
- Vector needs WiFi and an account, and IT help is likely.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Vector 2.0 page on the Digital Dream Labs site.
- [ ] Set up the robot on the school WiFi.
- [ ] Choose the paid plan or install Wire-Pod on a computer on the same network.
- [ ] Create the required account.
- [ ] Read the privacy terms for the robot and the plan.
- [ ] Test voice commands in a quiet room.
- [ ] Plan the robot for demonstrations and short student turns.

## Where is the AI?
Vector uses face recognition and voice command recognition. The face work runs on the robot. Voice requests use the vendor's cloud or a Wire-Pod server on the school network. Students speak to the robot and watch how it responds.

## Privacy and safety
An account and internet access are always needed. Cloud AI features require a vendor subscription. The materials do not state what student data is collected. The research could not read the vendor privacy policy because the page returned 404. Wire-Pod runs a server on a local computer. The research did not confirm that its speech processing stays fully local. One source says the vendor servers shut down in January 2024 and another says they work. A teacher should check the privacy terms and decide whether Wire-Pod suits the school network.
