## Overview
Dobot Magician Lite is a lower cost 4-axis desktop robot arm for education. It has a 250 g payload, a 340 mm working radius, and wireless connectivity. The retailer page says it has collision detection for younger students. An AI Teaching Kit and a sensor kit are sold as add-ons.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a real robot arm.
- Collision detection makes the arm safer for younger students.
- Students move from blocks to Python, Lua, or SDKs.

## Benefits for teachers
- Block coding helps beginners start quickly.
- The AI Teaching Kit adds OCR, image recognition, and speech.
- The grade range of 5 to 16 came from a note and was not verified.

## Benefits for schools
- The price is about 995 USD. SCAN UK lists 810.98 pounds with VAT.
- IT will likely need to install the software.

## Where is the AI?
AI is optional and comes with the AI Teaching Kit. It adds OCR, image recognition, and speech. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students use the kit to help the arm read or sort items. Without the kit the arm has no AI. It still builds sequencing and logic skills.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed for the arm, and the internet is used mainly for setup. The Dobot policy says the DobotLab and DobotLink education software collect no personally identifiable information. Accounts for minors under 18 need guardian consent. Face and ID features of some products send encrypted images to a third party cloud service. The research did not confirm where the AI Teaching Kit runs its AI. A teacher should check the vendor terms for the kit and set safety rules because the arm moves with force.
