## Overview
This is a beginner one-quarter course for grades 8 to 12 with two units. Foundations of Generative AI covers neural networks, embeddings, and how large language models are trained. Customizing Language Models covers prompt engineering, retrieval, and model cards. Students use AI Chat Lab to experiment with models and customize chatbots.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how large language models are trained.
- Students practice prompt engineering.
- Students build and test a customized chatbot.

## Benefits for teachers
- The two units can be taught alone or together.
- The course is built for beginners.
- A verified teacher account unlocks AI Chat Lab.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free.
- It runs in a browser on Chromebooks.
- Students need a computer or a Chromebook.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a set of large language models in AI Chat Lab. They run in the vendor's cloud. Students experiment with models, write prompts, and customize chatbots. They use the results to extend their own understanding of how generative AI works.

## Privacy and safety
Student accounts are needed, and a verified teacher account unlocks AI Chat Lab. The internet is needed at all times. Code.org collects account and chat interaction data, and the data goes to the vendor. The policy says it uses third party language model providers and does not describe their retention. It says it does not sell personal data or show ads. A teacher should review the district data agreement before class. A teacher should also give students rules for what not to type.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Explain in a short talk how a neural network learns from examples.
2. Test how small changes to a prompt change the answer.
3. Compare answers from two models and note the differences.
4. Customize a chatbot with a system prompt and test it.
5. Write a model card that lists what your chatbot can and cannot do.
