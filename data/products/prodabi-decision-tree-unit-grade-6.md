## Overview
Search results from TU Dortmund describe a ProDaBi unit for Grade 6 that uses unplugged data cards on food. Students build data-based decision trees from the cards. The official link in the research batch points to a different resource. That resource is a FAU KIKI-Labor station where students build a decision tree for biting and non-biting monkeys. The research team did not read the Grade 6 unit directly.

## Benefits for students

- Students practice classification with food examples.
- Students can try a good monkey and bad monkey game at the KIKI-Labor station.

## Where is the AI?
The activities have no running AI program. They teach supervised learning, where a model learns from labeled examples. Students build decision trees by hand from data cards. They then see how a tree can sort new examples.

## Privacy and safety
The activities run on paper, and the materials describe no student data collection. No account is needed. The research team could not read the Grade 6 unit. A teacher should confirm the unit details and the license before class.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Sort the food data cards by features and note which feature splits them best.
2. Build a decision tree from the food cards.
3. Test the tree on a new card and see whether it classifies the food correctly.
4. Play the good monkey and bad monkey game and build a tree that predicts biting.
5. Compare two trees built from the same cards and tell which is simpler.
