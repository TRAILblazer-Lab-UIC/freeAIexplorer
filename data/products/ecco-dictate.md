## Overview
Ecco Dictate is a free iOS dictation app. According to its App Store listing, it uses push to talk and transcribes when the user releases the button. The listing says it bundles the Whisper Small model and needs no internet to transcribe. It requires iOS 17 or later. These details come from search results because the listing page could not be fetched.

## Benefits for students
- Students can speak their ideas and see them as text.
- Students who find typing hard may draft more easily.
- Students can use it offline.

## Benefits for teachers
- The push to talk button is simple to explain.
- The app is free.
- No independent reviews were found. A teacher should test it first.

## Benefits for schools
- The listing says transcripts stay on the device.
- No account is described.
- The app is about 490 MB and needs an iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later.

The AI is the Whisper Small speech recognition model. According to the listing, it runs on the device and does not need the internet. Students speak, release the button, and read the text. They can then edit the text to improve their own writing.
