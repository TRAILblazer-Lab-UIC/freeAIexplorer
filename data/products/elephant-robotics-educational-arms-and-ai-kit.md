## Overview
Elephant Robotics sells desktop robot arms for education, including myCobot and ultraArm, and an AI kit for vision, grabbing, and automatic sorting. The 2023 AI kit includes a camera with light adjustment and a suction pump. It comes with a textbook called Machine Vision and Robotics, which has five algorithm courses. The source for these details is a sponsored article.

## Benefits for students
- Students see machine vision pick and sort real objects.
- Students follow five algorithm courses in a textbook.
- Students use visualization software built for beginners.

## Benefits for teachers

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. They also do not say where any data goes or whether the internet is needed. The only privacy policy found appears to cover an Elephant Robotics app. It says the app may collect personal information and log data and uses Google Play Services. It also says the app is not directed at children under 13. The research did not confirm that it applies to the arm software. A teacher should ask the vendor about data handling and should supervise students near the moving arm.

### computer_science
1. Work through the first algorithm course and describe how the camera finds an object.
2. Use the visualization software to test an algorithm on different objects.
3. Compare two of the five algorithm courses and list their differences.
4. Record how often the arm sorts an object correctly.
5. Write a short guide that explains machine vision to a younger student.

### engineering
1. Set up the arm and calibrate the camera and suction pump.
2. Arrange objects on the table and measure how well the camera sees each one.
3. Adjust the camera light and compare the sorting results.
4. Design a sorting layout with a bin for each object type.
5. Time the arm over ten sorting runs and calculate an average.
