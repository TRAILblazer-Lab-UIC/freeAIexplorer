## Overview
This CodeHS course for middle and high school teaches JavaScript through p5.js art. It has three modules with 14 lessons, 11 videos, 39 exercises, and 5 offline handouts. Students make drawings, animations, and interactive sketches. The course takes about 20 contact hours over one month and has no prerequisites.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn real JavaScript through art.
- Students see their code turn into pictures.
- Students move from drawing to animation to interaction.

## Benefits for teachers
- A syllabus, lesson plan, and handout are provided.
- Offline handouts support lessons away from screens.
- The course has no prerequisites.

## Benefits for schools
- The page lists no price and links to CodeHS Free, Pro, and quote plans.
- Students need accounts, often created through school rostering.
- Data goes to the vendor.

## Where is the AI?
This course has no AI. It builds skills in sequencing, loops, and logic. Students write code that draws and moves shapes.

## Privacy and safety
Student accounts are needed, and data goes to the vendor on AWS. CodeHS collects name and email or school-assigned identifiers, coursework, saved code, IP address and usage data. The policy says it does not sell personal information and acts as a school official under FERPA. Data for children under 13 is collected only with school or parent consent. A teacher should consider school-assigned identifiers, ask about a state agreement, and read the privacy policy.

## Ideas by subject
### visual_arts
1. Draw a face from simple shapes in p5.js.
2. Choose a color palette and apply it to a drawing.
3. Animate a shape across the canvas.
4. Make a sketch that reacts to the mouse.
5. Show a finished sketch and describe the art choices.

### computer_science
1. Use coordinates to place a shape on the canvas.
2. Use a variable to change the size of a shape.
3. Use a loop to draw a row of circles.
4. Fix a sketch that draws nothing.
5. Explain how a sketch handles a mouse click.
