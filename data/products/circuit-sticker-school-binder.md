## Overview
This is a single Adafruit Learn guide that shows how to decorate a school binder or folder with Chibitronics circuit stickers. The binder can light up, sense light, or react when opened. The page is one build project. Steps are to apply copper tape and stickers over a printed template, add effect templates such as a heartbeat blinker, and fold a paper battery holder with an on/off flap.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a working circuit they can use every day.
- Students add effects such as a heartbeat blinker.
- Students need no coding, soldering, or maker experience.

## Benefits for teachers
- It is a short project with printed templates.
- An optional light sensor turns the lights on when the cover opens.
- Chibitronics publishes separate classroom lesson plans for the same stickers.

## Benefits for schools
- The guide is free.
- Chibitronics sticker kits, copper tape, and CR2032 batteries are bought separately.
- No devices, internet, or IT help are needed.

## Where is the AI?
This project has no AI. It is a simple circuit with a battery and stickers. Students build skills in sensors and logic as they make the lights react to the binder opening.

## Privacy and safety
The project is on paper and uses stickers, copper tape, and a coin cell battery. No software or accounts are involved, and the guide page collects no student data. The guide is read online on Adafruit Learn. The coin cell batteries can be harmful if swallowed. A teacher should keep the batteries stored safely and supervise their use.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Trace the path of the circuit from the battery to the lights.
2. Build the light sensor template and test it in a dark spot.
3. Fix a circuit that does not light.
4. Add a second effect sticker.
5. Explain how the on/off flap closes the circuit.

### visual_arts
1. Design the cover of the binder before adding stickers.
2. Hide the copper tape under a drawing.
3. Choose colors for the lights that match the design.
4. Add a heartbeat effect to a picture of a heart.
5. Show the binder to the class and explain the artwork.
