## Overview
This is Lesson 1 of 5 in the Musical microbit unit, listed for ages 7 to 11. In this unplugged lesson pupils use a musical theme to extend their understanding of algorithms. They read and evaluate algorithms and create a short composition. They then write an algorithm for someone who cannot read music to follow.

## Benefits for students

- Students write steps that a partner can play without reading music.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Read a sample algorithm and judge whether its steps are clear.
2. Write an algorithm for a short tune.
3. Swap algorithms with a partner and follow each other's steps.
4. Fix an unclear step in your algorithm.
5. Compare two algorithms for the same tune and pick the clearer one.

### music
1. Compose a short piece of music with a few notes.
2. Write the piece as steps for someone who cannot read music.
3. Play a partner's piece by following their algorithm.
4. Change one step and listen to how the music changes.
5. Perform the finished piece for the class.
