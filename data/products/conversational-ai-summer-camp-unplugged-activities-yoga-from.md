## Overview
This is a set of unplugged activities designed for a conversational AI summer camp for middle school students. It is described in a SIGCSE 2024 experience report by University of Florida researchers. Two activities target computing concepts and three target conversational AI. In Yoga from Scratch, groups write block programs of yoga poses and the class follows each group's code.

## Benefits for students
- Students act out programs with their own bodies.
- Students learn how conversational AI works through a card game.
- Students move and laugh while they learn.

## Benefits for teachers
- The paper reports use with 50 middle school learners across two summers.
- The activities include Yoga from Scratch and Lego Algorithm.
- The authors report mixed learner reactions, which helps a teacher plan.

## Benefits for schools
- The paper is linked from the University of Florida page.
- Material availability and license were not stated.
- The activities need no devices.

## Where is the AI?
The activities have no software and no AI runs on any device. Three activities teach conversational AI ideas such as intents and training phrases. Students build skills in sequencing and in thinking about how a chatbot understands a request.

## Privacy and safety
The activities are described as unplugged, and the pages read describe no data collection by them. The pages do not say whether an account is needed. The internet is used to read the paper. This is a research report, and the research did not find a packaged lesson set. A teacher should check the paper for activity details and make safe space for movement in the room.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Write a block program of yoga poses and have the class follow it.
2. Find a bug in another group's program and fix it.
3. Build a Lego Algorithm sequence for a partner to follow.
4. Write three phrases that mean the same request for a pretend chatbot.
5. Play a card game that matches a user phrase to the right chatbot answer.
