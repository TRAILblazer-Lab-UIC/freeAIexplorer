## Overview
Makey Makey Classic is an invention kit that connects everyday objects to a computer with alligator clips. A touch on a conductive object registers as a keyboard or mouse press. Students use it with programs such as Scratch and MakeCode Arcade. The official lessons cover circuits, music, literacy, and engineering.

## Benefits for students
- Students turn fruit, drawings, and foil into game controllers.
- Students learn which objects conduct electricity.
- Students see their ideas work within minutes.

## Benefits for teachers
- Beginner and intermediate lesson courses are ready to use.
- The kit works as a USB keyboard and mouse.
- Setup takes minutes.

## Benefits for schools
- Classic is listed at 51.95 USD and the classroom STEM Pack at 724.95 USD on the official site.
- The kit needs only a computer with a USB port.
- No account is needed and no student data is collected.

## Getting started
- [ ] Buy a Makey Makey Classic or a STEM Pack from the official site.
- [ ] Plug the board into a computer by USB.
- [ ] Clip one wire to the Earth pad and one to a key pad.
- [ ] Hold the Earth clip and touch a piece of fruit connected to a key.
- [ ] Open a program or website that reads key presses.
- [ ] Try a lesson from the Beginner course.
- [ ] Note that the site does not state the platform list.

## Where is the AI?
Makey Makey has no AI. The kit builds skills in circuits and input and output. Students learn how a touch becomes a signal that a program can use.

## Privacy and safety
The kit works with no account and no internet. It appears to the computer as a keyboard. The vendor privacy page tried returned a 404. The research found no privacy policy. Vendor education pages mention optional classes with student logins, and the research did not confirm them. The lessons use Scratch and MakeCode Arcade, which have their own data practices. A teacher should check those tools before students sign in.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Test ten objects and sort them into conductors and non-conductors.
2. Draw a circuit that includes a person as part of the path.
3. Predict which foods will work as a switch and test the prediction.
4. Change the length of a wire path and record the effect.
5. Explain in a short report how the Makey Makey senses a touch.

### music
1. Draw a piano on paper with graphite and play it with a piano app.
2. Build a banana piano with four notes.
3. Make a drum kit from foil pads.
4. Compose a short tune and play it on your instrument.
5. Perform for the class and explain how it works.

### ela
1. Craft an interactive story where a touch reveals the next scene.
2. Build a poem generator that shows lines on key presses.
3. Make a touch-and-hear book for a younger reader.
4. Write character lines that play when a prop is touched.
5. Share the story and ask a partner for feedback.

### engineering
1. Complete an invention challenge that uses a Makey Makey.
2. Design a controller for a game with a clear goal.
3. Test the controller and note what to fix.
4. Build a second version that is easier to use.
5. Present the design and explain the choices.
