Elementary AI Explorations are short hands-on activities for grades 3 to 5 from aiEDU. They introduce foundational AI ideas. The page states that students and teachers need no prior AI knowledge. Search results describe them as 15 to 20 minute warmups with teacher guides.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Try the sample exploration about a blind spot and name what the AI missed.
2. Sort objects into groups and talk about how a computer might sort them.
3. Describe one thing a person sees that an AI might not.
4. Draw a picture of an AI helper and explain what it can do.
5. Share one new AI word with the class and explain it in plain language.
