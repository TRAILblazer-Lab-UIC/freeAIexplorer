## Overview
Tynker is a commercial K-12 coding platform with block and text coding courses. The schools page lists 31 elementary courses, 19 middle school courses, and 12 high school courses. Courses cover Python and JavaScript. Students build games and projects and can take Intro to AI and AI/ML courses.

## Benefits for students
- Students move from blocks to text code in one place.
- Students can take AI courses at several grade levels.
- Students can publish and remix community projects.

## Benefits for teachers
- Rostering works with Clever, Google Classroom, ClassLink, Canvas, and others.
- Courses cover AP Computer Science Principles and A.
- Teachers can track student progress.

## Benefits for schools
- Free courses and a free start exist.
- School pricing is by quote and is not listed.
- The platform collects a lot of student data. A review is needed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Tynker schools page and request a quote.
- [ ] Read the privacy policy before any student use.
- [ ] Ask whether the vendor offers a student data agreement.
- [ ] Create student accounts or roster from Clever or Google Classroom.
- [ ] Turn off or avoid optional voice recordings and photos.
- [ ] Try one Intro to AI course yourself.
- [ ] Ask the vendor about Chromebook support.

## Where is the AI?
The AI content is course material. Students learn about AI in Intro to AI courses, and high school students use MediaPipe in an AI/ML course. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students build projects and then test how a model reacts to data.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts and internet access are required. Tynker may collect student name, username, projects, education records, progress, chat logs, optional voice and photos, live class audio and video, age, and device data. The policy says student data is never sold. It shares data with service providers, Google Analytics, and Hotjar, and with other users if a student publishes a project. The policy does not state where data is stored. The research did not verify whether Tynker signs student data privacy agreements or whether AI courses send data to outside AI services. Read the policy and keep the optional features off.
