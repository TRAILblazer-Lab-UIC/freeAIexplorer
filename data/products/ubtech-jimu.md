## Overview
Jimu is a line of building block robot kits from UBTECH. Kits are controlled and programmed from the free Jimu app on a phone or tablet over Bluetooth. Reviews describe consumer kits priced from about 99 to 399 USD. The sweep note linked to a 2019 article that does not mention Jimu. Classroom positioning is not confirmed.

## Benefits for students
- Students snap together blocks and servos to build robots.
- Students control and program the robot from an app.
- Students can build models such as Meebot, TankBot, and BuilderBots.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app is free.
- The setup is a build, a charge, and a Bluetooth pairing.
- The vendor does not say that the kits include teacher guides.

## Benefits for schools
- Reviews list kits from about 99 USD for BuilderBots to 399 USD for Inventor. The prices come from 2017 to 2019.
- One review reported pairing trouble on an iPad Air 3.
- The evidence comes from search snippets of old reviews.
