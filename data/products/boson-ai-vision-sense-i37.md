## Overview
The Boson AI Vision Sense i37 is a zero-code AI vision sensor from DFRobot aimed at children and teenagers. It detects faces and movement. It also has a self-learning mode where a user teaches it a target image with a Learn button. It plugs into BOSON controllers and output modules and gives a digital signal. No programming is needed.

## Benefits for students
- Students teach a sensor to recognize a target with one button.
- Students connect the sensor to other modules without code.
- Students see AI work right away.

## Benefits for teachers
- Setup takes a few minutes.
- Learned data stays after power-off.
- The Boson Starter Kit listing says age 10 and up.

## Benefits for schools

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small vision chip on the sensor. It detects faces, detects motion, and learns a target image. It runs on the device. Students teach it a target, then watch how it reacts and where it makes mistakes.

## Privacy and safety
The sensor has only power and one digital signal pin. It has no documented network connection. The wiki does not say where the processing happens or what chip is inside. The research found one wiki page and no privacy statement. The wiki lists no student data collection and no account. A teacher should decide who may be in front of the sensor for face tasks. A teacher should also use only the supported voltage range of 3.0 to 5.5 V.
