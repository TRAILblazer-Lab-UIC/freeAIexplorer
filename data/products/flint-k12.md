## Overview
Flint is an AI learning platform for schools. Teachers build and assign activities, and students chat with an AI tutor named Sparky. Teachers set how much help Sparky gives and can watch sessions live. The vendor lists its grade range as primary through higher education.

## Benefits for students
- Students get a tutor that answers in a chat window.
- Students work on activities that their own teacher designed.
- Students can take an AI literacy course that comes with the free plan.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers set how much help Sparky gives on each activity.
- Teachers can watch student sessions live and read analytics.
- Teachers can start with a free workspace.

## Benefits for schools
- The free plan covers teachers and their students with no student limit, for up to 3 teachers and admins per workspace.
- School plans cost from 3,000 USD per year.
- Administrators can read every student conversation and turn off personal chats by grade.

## Getting started
- [ ] Go to the Flint website and sign up for the free workspace.
- [ ] Ask IT to review the vendor's privacy terms before students join.
- [ ] Build one activity and set the help level for Sparky.
- [ ] Roster students through Google or Microsoft sign-in.
- [ ] Assign the activity and watch a live session.
- [ ] Read the analytics and adjust the activity.
- [ ] Ask the administrator to set the personal chat controls for each grade.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a chat tutor called Sparky. It runs in the vendor's cloud, and the vendor does not name the AI models it uses. Students chat with Sparky inside activities that their teacher built. Teachers shape the help to push students to extend their own thinking.

## Privacy and safety
Students need accounts and the internet is always needed. The platform collects student chats with the AI, activity work, and rostering data, and this data goes to the vendor. The AI runs in the vendor's cloud and the vendor does not name the AI provider. Flint says it does not share student data with third parties or use it to train AI. These are vendor claims. The privacy policy page tried returned 404. Administrators can read every student conversation. A teacher should ask IT to get and review the written data terms before students join.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Complete the AI literacy course in the free plan and list three things you learned about how AI works.
2. Ask Sparky to explain a coding idea and rewrite the explanation in your own words.
3. Compare two answers from Sparky to the same question and note the differences.
4. Find a mistake in a tutor answer and write a note to correct it.
5. Write a short guide that tells younger students how to use an AI tutor wisely.

### ela
1. Chat with Sparky about a class novel and write a paragraph on what the tutor helped you notice.
2. Ask Sparky for feedback on a draft and decide which suggestions to accept.
3. Practice a debate argument with the tutor and note where your reasoning grew stronger.
4. Ask Sparky to quiz you on vocabulary from a reading and track your score.
5. Write a reflection on how the tutor changed the way you revise.
