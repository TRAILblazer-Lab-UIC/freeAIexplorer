## Overview
Integrating Computing Across the Curriculum is a University of Alabama at Birmingham program. Its lesson plans page lists dozens of PDF lessons for grades 4 and 5. Subjects include science, math, language arts, and fourth grade social studies. Many lessons use Scratch projects.

## Benefits for students
- Students use Scratch to explore ideas in core subjects.
- Students make animations, models, and stories.
- Students practice computing skills in familiar lessons.

## Benefits for teachers
- Lessons were written by researchers and teachers in grades 4 and 5.
- Scratch materials include a presentation template and student handout.
- Teachers can pick the lessons that match their subject.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson PDFs are free to view.
- Some lessons use Prezi, Excel, or video that may have their own pricing.
- Some lessons are based on Alabama standards from before 2010.

## Where is the AI?
The materials describe no AI. The lessons use Scratch and other plain tools. Students build sequencing and logic skills as they code in Scratch.

## Privacy and safety
The lesson page does not state accounts or data handling. Linked tools include Scratch, Prezi, Excel, and YouTube, each with its own terms. The research did not review those terms or open the lesson PDFs. Some linked tools may have changed since these 2013 era materials. Check each tool a lesson uses and confirm what accounts it needs.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Model a food chain and the flow of energy in a Scratch project.
2. Animate how energy moves from the sun to a predator.
3. Label each part of the chain in the code.
4. Change one organism and predict the effect.
5. Present the model and explain it to the class.

### math
1. Use a Scratch project to show a transformation such as a slide or flip.
2. Watch a video on transformations and then code one.
3. Predict where a shape lands after a turn.
4. Combine two transformations in one script.
5. Explain each step in a short write up.

### ela
1. Build an Adjective Animations project that acts out describing words.
2. Choose a noun and add three adjectives to its animation.
3. Write a sentence for each animated scene.
4. Trade projects and name the adjectives in each one.
5. Revise a project to use stronger adjectives.

### social_studies
1. Follow Vulcan's Journey with a Scratch project.
2. Fill in a graphic organizer for each stop on the journey.
3. Retell the story in order using sprites.
4. Add a map backdrop to the project.
5. Present the project and answer questions about it.

### computer_science
1. Open a lesson project and list the blocks it uses.
2. Add a loop to repeat an action.
3. Add a sound or message block to the project.
4. Fix a script with a bug.
5. Remix a project with a new idea.
