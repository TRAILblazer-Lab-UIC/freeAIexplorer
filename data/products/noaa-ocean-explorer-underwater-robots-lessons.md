## Overview
NOAA Ocean Exploration offers a collection of lessons on underwater robots such as ROVs and AUVs. Search results describe lessons on matching robot designs to mission tasks and on how robots gather data. The lessons support NGSS and Ocean Literacy principles, according to an archived theme page. Some lesson PDFs date from 2007 to 2013 expeditions and links may be stale.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how robots explore places people cannot reach.
- Students match robot designs to mission tasks.
- Students study how robots collect ocean data.

## Benefits for teachers
- Lessons are downloadable PDFs.
- They tie to NOAA expeditions.
- Search snippets list grades 5 to 12.

## Benefits for schools
- The lessons are free.
- No devices or accounts are needed.
- No IT help is needed.

## Where is the AI?
This collection has no AI. The lessons teach about robots that explore the ocean. Students practice data skills as they study how robots gather information.

## Privacy and safety
The lessons are downloadable documents, and the research found no account needs and no student data collection. The internet is used to download the PDFs. The lessons page returned an error. The research relied on a theme page and search results. The research did not read individual lessons. A teacher should check the lesson dates and the age fit before use.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Read about how an ROV collects data in the deep sea.
2. List what an AUV can measure about the ocean.
3. Compare data from two ocean locations.
4. Explain why robots can reach places that divers cannot.
5. Write a question that a robot could help answer.

### engineering
1. Match a robot design to a mission task.
2. Explain why one robot suits a task better than another.
3. Sketch a robot for a new ocean mission.
4. List the tools your robot would carry.
5. Present your design to the class.
