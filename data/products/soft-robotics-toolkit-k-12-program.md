## Overview
This is a K-12 soft robotics curriculum from the Harvard Biodesign Lab. Search results describe two phases, a Phase I Starter Curriculum and a Phase II Exploration Curriculum, with thirteen lessons in total. Teachers take a professional development workshop first. The official page returned an error. These details come from search results only.

## Benefits for students
- Students build soft robots by hand.
- Students move from a starter set of lessons to an exploration set.
- Students see engineering and science at work in one project.

## Benefits for teachers
- A professional development workshop comes first.
- Downloadable slides and a bill of materials are listed in search snippets.
- A pilot in India reached 60 students and 11 instructors in six schools over six weeks.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Follow the first lesson to build a simple soft actuator.
2. Test how much a soft gripper can lift.
3. Change one design detail and compare the results.
4. Sketch a soft robot for a real task, such as picking up fruit.
5. Present the design and explain what worked.

### science
1. Observe how air pressure changes the shape of a soft body.
2. Record how much the soft robot bends at different pressures.
3. Compare a soft robot with a rigid one.
4. Ask how living things move without hard bones.
5. Discuss the results of the six-week pilot in India as a class.
