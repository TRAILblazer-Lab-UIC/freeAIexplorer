## Overview
Kira Learning is an AI platform for schools that began as a computer science education platform. It generates standards aligned courses, lesson plans, rubrics, and quizzes. It also offers an AI tutor and grading feedback. Teachers sign in with a Google or school account.

## Benefits for students
- Students get help from an AI tutor.
- Students receive feedback on their work.
- Students use courses from an original computer science library.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can generate lessons, rubrics, and quizzes.
- Tools support intervention and differentiation.
- Sign in uses a Google or school account.

## Benefits for schools
- Tech and Learning says it is free for teachers and students.
- Districts pay a custom rate for analytics.
- District use goes through a demo and a custom agreement.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Kira Learning website.
- [ ] Sign in with a Google or school account.
- [ ] Try the course or lesson generator on one unit.
- [ ] Review every generated lesson before use.
- [ ] Ask IT to review the privacy terms and data agreement.
- [ ] Test the AI tutor with a few sample questions.
- [ ] Decide whether to ask for a district demo.

## Where is the AI?
The AI generates courses, tutors students, and gives grading feedback. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Teachers use it to draft materials and then edit them. Students ask the tutor questions and use the answers as a start for their own work.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed, with sign in through a Google or school account, and the internet is needed at all times. Student work goes to the vendor's cloud. The vendor site says student data stays under district control and the platform is designed to meet FERPA and COPPA requirements. Tech and Learning reports US based AWS servers and Common Sense privacy verification. These are vendor and press statements, and the policy documents were not read. A teacher should read the privacy terms and data agreement and check what the tutor stores.
