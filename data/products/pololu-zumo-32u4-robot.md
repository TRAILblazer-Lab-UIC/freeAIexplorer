## Overview
The Zumo 32U4 is a tracked robot under 10 cm per side with an Arduino-compatible ATmega32U4 microcontroller. It has encoders for closed-loop control and proximity sensors for obstacles. An OLED version replaced the original 8x2 character LCD. The successor is the Zumo 2040 on the Raspberry Pi RP2040.

## Where is the AI?
The robot has no AI. It builds skills in text coding, sensing and control. Students use encoder and sensor data to correct a robot's movement.

## Privacy and safety
The robot works on the device. No account or internet is needed. The materials document no student data collection. A teacher should check that the model is still sold. The Zumo 2040 is the successor.
