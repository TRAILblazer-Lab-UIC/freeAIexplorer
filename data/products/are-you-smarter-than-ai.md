The activity has no software and no AI runs on any device. Students compare their own reasoning with AI style predictions. This builds skills in thinking about data, predictions, and the limits of AI.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Answer a set of trivia questions and then compare answers with the AI's predictions.
2. Mark which questions the AI got wrong and guess why.
3. Write a new trivia question that would be hard for an AI.
4. Tally the class score and compare it with the AI's score.
5. Discuss one task where a human is better than an AI and one where an AI is better.
