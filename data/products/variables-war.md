## Overview
Variables War is a free printable card game for 2 to 6 players that takes about 30 minutes. Each player has two variables, A and B, and wins by reaching exactly 42. The 106 card deck has value, loop, attack, defense and bonus cards. Parents made the project to teach their kids software concepts. The project lists age 7 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Students use variables and loops in a game.
- Students add values and plan to hit exactly 42.
- Students play attack and defense cards in a friendly contest.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each turn draws 2 cards and plays 2 cards.
- Loop cards repeat the next card played.
- The rules are online and short.

## Benefits for schools
- The deck is free to print in English or French.
- A physical deck is listed as coming soon.
- The game uses no devices at play time.

## Where is the AI?
This game has no AI. It builds programming skills with variables and loops. Students change the values of A and B and repeat actions with loop cards.

## Privacy and safety
The game runs on paper and needs no accounts. The research found no data collection described. The internet is used to print the deck from a page that GitHub hosts. Other versions of the code exist as forks on GitHub. Print from the official page.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Play a round and say which variable changed each turn.
2. Use a loop card to repeat the next card and explain what happened.
3. Plan two plays that move A and B closer to 42.
4. Describe your turn as a short list of steps.
5. Invent a new card and write what it would do.

### math
1. Add card values to reach exactly 42.
2. Work out how many more points you need to win.
3. Compare two plays and choose the one that gets closer to 42.
4. Use a loop card and find the total after repeating a value.
5. Keep a running total on paper for A and B.
