## Overview
Breakable Machine is a classroom game that teaches critical AI literacy through spoofing. Students try to fool an image classifier by changing their appearance or surroundings. An explainable AI view shows which image features the model uses. A shared leaderboard lets the class compare strategies. It is designed for learners aged 10 to 15.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn that AI can be wrong with great confidence.
- Students see which parts of an image a model attends to.
- Students compare strategies with classmates on a leaderboard.

## Benefits for teachers
- The game has a teacher projected view.
- The activity is easy to explain.
- It sparks talk about how models fail.
