## Overview
PictoBlox is a Scratch-based block coding environment from STEMpedia that also supports Python. It offers AI and machine learning extensions such as face, object, and pose detection, text to speech, translation, and ChatGPT. A Machine Learning Environment trains image, pose, audio, and text classifiers. It also connects to STEMpedia robots and Arduino boards. The vendor does not state a grade range.

## Benefits for students
- Students use familiar blocks to try real AI tools.
- Students train their own image, pose, audio, or text model.
- Students can connect projects to robots and Arduino boards.

## Benefits for teachers
- The documentation includes examples.
- Third-party listings say a free version or free trial exists.
- The tool runs on desktop, web, and mobile.

## Benefits for schools
- Plan details were not confirmed from the vendor.
- The Machine Learning Environment runs only in the desktop version.
- IT will likely need to install the desktop app.

## Where is the AI?
The AI covers face, object, and pose detection, classifiers, speech recognition, text to speech, translation, and a chatbot. Some work runs on the device and many extensions such as ChatGPT and speech are likely cloud-backed. Students use the blocks to build projects and train their own classifiers.

## Privacy and safety
The research did not confirm whether accounts are needed or whether the internet is required. The ChatGPT extension implies that prompts go to a third party AI service. Other extensions such as speech are likely cloud based. The privacy policy page could not be read. No policy points are confirmed. A teacher should check which extensions use the cloud and avoid them with student faces and voices until the terms are clear.
