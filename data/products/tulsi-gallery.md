## Overview
Tulsi Gallery is a free, open source Android photo gallery with offline text recognition. Users can search photos by the words in them. It is built on a LavenderPhotos base and uses ML Kit for OCR. It is a general consumer app with no education features.

## Benefits for students
- Students can find a photo of a notebook page by searching for a word on it.
- Students can select and copy text from a photo.
- Students can keep photos in an encrypted vault.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can search photos of whiteboards and handouts by keyword.
- The app has album tools for sorting class photos.
- It works offline.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free and open source.
- It needs no internet after install.
- The vendor does not describe any school features. A teacher may need to install it.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Tulsi Gallery on Google Play, F-Droid, or GitHub.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install on school Android devices.
- [ ] Install the app.
- [ ] Grant photo access.
- [ ] Wait for the background text recognition to index the photos.
- [ ] Search for a word that appears in a photo.
- [ ] Turn off network access for the app and test again.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is optical character recognition from ML Kit. It runs on the phone and reads Latin and Devanagari scripts. Students and teachers use it to search their photos by text. It does not describe other photo content.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The developer says all processing happens on the device with no data collection, transmission, or tracking. This is the developer's claim and the research did not verify it. The app includes a one tap Google Lens search that would send an image to Google. Student phones are personal devices and school fit is unclear. Check which photos the app can see and tell students to avoid Lens. Keep student faces and names out of shared galleries.
