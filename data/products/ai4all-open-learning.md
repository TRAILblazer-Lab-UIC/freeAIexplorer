## Overview
AI4ALL Open Learning is a free set of curricula, short lessons, and teacher resources for high school educators. The curriculum includes Explore AI and Sentiment Analysis and Natural Language Processing. Six short Bytes of AI cover drawing, the environment, COVID-19, ethics, facial recognition, and the criminal legal system.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how AI reads feelings in text through sentiment analysis.
- Students explore ethics and facial recognition in short lessons.
- Students see AI used in topics such as drawing and the environment.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teaching guides come with the curricula.
- Professional development is included.
- The Bytes of AI lessons are short and can fit into existing classes.

## Benefits for schools
- The materials are free according to search results.
- The materials run in a browser, and the listing shows no IT install.
- The vendor does not say what a school needs beyond a browser.

## Where is the AI?
The materials cover sentiment analysis, natural language processing, facial recognition, and AI ethics. The vendor does not say where the AI runs. Students look at how AI reads text and faces and then judge the results. The curricula build the skill of spotting patterns in data.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. They do not say where student work goes. The research read no privacy terms and no lesson page.
