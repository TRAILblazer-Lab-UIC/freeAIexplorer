## Overview
Nybble Q is Petoi's pre-assembled robot cat with 11 joints, an ultrasonic sensor, touch sensors, and a gyroscope. The Petoi product page lists it at 435 USD, ages 10 and up, with over 35 movements and up to 10 custom voice commands. It codes with blocks, Python, or C++. The page marks it sold out and says no network connection is required.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a robot cat that walks and reacts.
- Students can move from blocks to Python and C++.
- Students can record custom voice commands.

## Benefits for teachers
- The robot comes pre-assembled.
- Petoi offers educator curricula.
- The Make review notes voice recognition was accurate.

## Benefits for schools
- The product page and the Make review list 435 USD.

## Where is the AI?
AI is optional. The robot has voice commands and an optional AI camera module. The product page also mentions a Gemma model on a phone. Some AI runs on the device and some on a phone. Students teach the cat voice commands and test them.

## Privacy and safety
The robot needs no account, and the product page says no network connection is required. The educator guide says the internet is used for downloads, updates, and online resources. The robot listens through a microphone for voice commands. The product page mentions Gemma on a phone, and the research did not confirm where that model runs or what data it handles. Petoi's privacy policy covers the website and store only and still holds template placeholders. A teacher should check app permissions and any phone AI feature before class.
