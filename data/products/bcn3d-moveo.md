## Overview
BCN3D Moveo is an open-source, 3D-printed robotic arm for education. It was designed with the Departament d'Ensenyament of Catalonia. The repository holds CAD and STL files, an assembly manual, firmware and a bill of materials. It is not sold commercially. Students build and tune the arm, which supports study of mechanical design, automation and industrial programming.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a real robotic arm from printed parts.
- Students tune the arm and learn how motion works.
- Students meet mechanical design and automation in one project.

## Benefits for teachers
- The repository has CAD files, firmware and manuals in English and Spanish.
- BCN3D ran an assembly workshop for 15 institutes in Catalonia.
- The project is advanced and needs careful planning.

## Benefits for schools
- The files are free. Builders source their own parts and printing.
- The repository lists a bill of materials but no total price.
- IT help is likely needed for the firmware upload.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the BCN3D Moveo repository on GitHub.
- [ ] Read the assembly manual in English or Spanish.
- [ ] Download the CAD and STL files.
- [ ] Print the parts on a 3D printer.
- [ ] Buy the parts on the bill of materials.
- [ ] Assemble the arm.
- [ ] Upload the firmware from a computer and test slow movements first.

## Where is the AI?
This project has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is controlling a machine with sensors and commands. Automation and robot control are ideas behind many smart machines.

## Privacy and safety
The arm is controlled by local Arduino electronics and needs no account. The research found no documented data collection and reviewed no privacy policy. The internet is used to download files. A teacher should check electrical safety and pinch points before students run the arm. The repository was last updated in March 2022.
