## Overview
Edge Impulse is a web platform for building datasets, training models, and sending them to edge devices such as microcontrollers and cameras. A free Developer plan is offered to individuals, students, and universities. A university program gives educators of students aged 18 and over free courses and a textbook. It suits college courses and advanced older students.

## Benefits for students
- Students collect sensor data and label it in the cloud.
- Students train a model and run it on a real board.
- Students learn the steps used in embedded machine learning.

## Benefits for teachers
- The university program includes courses and a textbook.
- Educators get an organization for managing students.
- The Introduction to Embedded Machine Learning course is used in degree and diploma classes.

## Benefits for schools
- The Developer plan costs $0 per month with limits on projects and compute time.
- Students need a supported board or phone.
- The terms require users to be 18 or older.

- [ ] Find the Edge Impulse university program page.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is model training and sensor data classification. Training runs in the vendor's cloud and the finished model runs on the device. Students collect data, train a model, and deploy it to see it work on hardware. The platform also lists object detection and synthetic data generation.

## Privacy and safety
Every student needs an account, and the site needs the internet at all times. The terms require users to be at least 18. Customer data is stored on Edge Impulse cloud infrastructure, and the terms grant the company a broad perpetual license to use it. Public projects are visible to anyone and may not include content involving minors. The privacy policy page returned an error, and the research did not confirm the storage region or COPPA handling. A teacher should read the terms and the age rule before anyone signs up.

1. Collect motion data from a board and label three kinds of movement.

### engineering

2. Compare the size and speed of a model on two target devices.
3. Build a sensor project that reacts to what the model detects.
4. Note the limits of the board's memory and plan a smaller model.
