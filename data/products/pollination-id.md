## Overview
PolliNation ID is a free iPhone app from the University of Michigan-Dearborn Environmental Interpretive Center. It is part of the PolliNation citizen science project. Per search results, users identify a pollinator by questionnaire or through a photo AI. Students look for pollinators outdoors and use the app to name them.

## Benefits for students
- Students identify pollinators with a questionnaire.
- Students read an encyclopedia of regional pollinators.
- Students can submit sightings to a citizen science project.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app pairs with the insect hotels of the PolliNation project.
- The questionnaire works without relying on the photo AI.
- Computer science student teams built the app.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free on the App Store.
- It needs an iPhone with iOS 13.2 or later. No Android version is listed.
- The focus is Southeast Michigan. The App Store age rating is 16 plus.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor describes a photo AI as an optional feature, based on search results. The vendor does not say where it runs. Students photograph a pollinator, compare the suggestion with the encyclopedia, and confirm it with the questionnaire.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need or whether the internet is required. The developer's App Store label lists email and name linked to the user, and user ID, usage data, diagnostics, and photos or videos not linked to the user. Apple has not verified that label. The research did not confirm where the optional photo AI runs. The App Store lists unrestricted web access and an age rating of 16 plus. A teacher should check the age rating, the privacy label, and what happens to submitted sightings.
