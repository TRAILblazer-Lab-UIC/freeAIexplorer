## Overview
MyPaint is a free open source raster painting and sketching program for Linux, macOS, and Windows. A built-in brush engine imitates charcoal, pencil, ink, and paint. Users can build custom brushes. It works well with pressure sensitive graphics tablets such as Wacom devices.

## Benefits for students
- Students draw and sketch with natural looking brushes.
- Students use layers and symmetry modes.
- Students work on an unlimited canvas with a fullscreen mode that hides the interface.

## Benefits for teachers
- The interface is minimal, with many brush settings.
- It is a good fit for art classes with a drawing tablet.
- No account is needed.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- A graphics tablet is optional but recommended.
- Installing usually needs IT approval.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the Windows installer or 7z archive, the Linux Flathub or AppImage build, or the macOS MacPorts package.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Connect a graphics tablet if you have one.
- [ ] Try the brush engine with a sketch.
- [ ] Use layers and symmetry on a practice drawing.
- [ ] Use the English page of the official MyPaint site to find downloads.

## Where is the AI?
This program has no AI. It builds drawing skills with digital brushes. Students also practice working in layers. They learn to plan a picture as a stack of parts.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and files are saved on the computer. The internet is used at setup. The research found no privacy policy. The project mentions integrated bug reporting, and the research could not verify telemetry either way. A teacher should ask IT to check network activity after install and set up a place to save student files.
