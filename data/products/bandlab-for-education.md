## Overview
BandLab for Education is a browser-based music creation and classroom management platform. It is separate from the consumer BandLab app. Teachers create classes and invite students into a closed space. Students record, loop and edit music in a cloud studio and submit assignments to the teacher.

## Benefits for students
- Students record, loop and edit music in a browser.
- Students submit assignments to the teacher.
- Students work in a closed space with their class.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers create classes and invite students.
- Only teachers invite students into the space.
- Teachers collect assignments in one place.

## Benefits for schools
- The pricing was not confirmed, and discovery notes say free.
- It runs in the Chrome browser.
- Audio is stored in the vendor cloud, and a help center snippet says US schools must collect parental consent for students aged 13 or under.

## Getting started
- [ ] Create a BandLab for Education account as a teacher.
- [ ] Use the Chrome browser.
- [ ] Create a class.
- [ ] Invite students into the class.
- [ ] Collect parental consent for students aged 13 or under.
- [ ] Give students headphones and a microphone or instrument if they will record.
- [ ] Assign a first short project.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in the education product. Students build skills in sequencing, rhythm and layering. They arrange loops and recordings in a project.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are required for teachers and students and the work is stored in the vendor cloud. The research could not fetch the privacy policy. It did not confirm third party sharing and did not read the full list of data fields. A help center snippet says the product is COPPA and GDPR compliant. For US students aged 13 or under the school must get verifiable parental consent. A signed Student Data Privacy Consortium agreement appears in search results but the research did not read it. A teacher should confirm parental consent and read the privacy policy before inviting students.
