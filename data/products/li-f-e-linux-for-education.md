## Overview
Li-f-e is a Linux distribution for teachers and students based on Ubuntu MATE. It began as an openSUSE project. The newest ISO found is Life-24.04.amd64.iso, about 5.2 GB, dated 2024-05-05. It is an operating system and not a single classroom app.

## Benefits for students
- Students get a free computer system with education software.
- Students see how an operating system works.
- Students can try Linux on an older computer.

## Benefits for teachers
- The system comes with education software.
- The desktop is based on Ubuntu MATE.
- Teachers rate the learning curve as advanced.

## Benefits for schools
- The download is free.
- It needs a 64-bit PC and a 5.2 GB ISO file.
- Replacing a school system needs IT approval.

## Where is the AI?
Li-f-e has no AI. It is an operating system with education software. Students build computing skills as they learn how files, apps, and settings work.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used for setup. This is a full operating system installed locally. Its preinstalled software and any telemetry were not reviewed, and the materials do not say what student data the included apps collect. No release was found after May 2024. A teacher should check each app before students use it. A teacher should also confirm that IT approves the install.
