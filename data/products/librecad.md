## Overview
LibreCAD is a free, open source 2D CAD application for technical drawing. It supports DXF files and runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux. It is available in over 30 languages. It has no trials, subscriptions, or fees.

## Benefits for students
- Students draw to scale like a drafter.
- Students export files that other CAD tools can open.
- Students can use the program in their own language.

## Benefits for teachers
- Support comes from a community of users and developers.
- The tool is moderate to learn.
- Teachers can teach drafting with no license cost.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- The site notes that macOS deployment needs help.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download an installer or the Linux AppImage from the LibreCAD site or GitHub.
- [ ] Ask IT to install it, and ask for help on macOS.
- [ ] Note that the 2.2.0 series needs Qt5.
- [ ] Draw a line and a circle.
- [ ] Add dimensions to a simple part.
- [ ] Save the file as DXF.
- [ ] Plan a first lesson with a simple floor plan.

## Where is the AI?
LibreCAD has no AI. It draws what students define. Students build design and precision skills as they plan lines, angles, and sizes.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used for setup. The site describes a local desktop app with no accounts. No privacy statement was found. A teacher should check where students save their drawings.
