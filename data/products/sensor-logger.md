## Overview
Sensor Logger is a free iOS app that records phone sensors. These include the accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer, GPS, microphone, and camera. It also reads Apple Watch, AirPods, and Bluetooth devices. Recordings appear in interactive plots and export as CSV, JSON, Excel, KML, and SQLite files.

## Benefits for students

- Students see their recordings as interactive plots.
- Students export data and graph it in a spreadsheet.

## Where is the AI?
Sensor Logger has no AI. It records sensor readings. Students build data skills as they collect, export, and graph measurements.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the app works with no internet. Sensor recordings are stored on the device. The App Store label lists device ID for analytics, precise location and crash data. The developer provides that label. The Streaming and Studies features can send data off the device when a user turns them on. A teacher should tell students which sensors to turn on, keep those features off and delete recordings after the lesson.
