## Overview
SelfCAD is a 3D modeling tool that covers sketching, sculpting, rendering, and 3D printing preparation. It runs in a web browser and has a PC version for offline work. A hybrid mode syncs online and offline projects. Interactive tutorials teach the modeling steps.

## Benefits for students
- Students sketch, sculpt, and render 3D models.
- Students prepare models for 3D printing with the built-in slicer.
- Students learn from interactive tutorials and SelfCAD Academy.

## Benefits for teachers
- Tutorials teach the modeling steps.
- The tool covers design and printing in one place.
- Most education praise comes from the vendor blog.

## Benefits for schools
- The vendor blog says schools can use a free educational plan.
- Teachers should confirm how the plan is granted.
- The vendor blog lists a paid plan at 14.99 USD a month or 139.99 USD a year. The pricing page could not be read.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find SelfCAD on its website and register for an account.
- [ ] Ask the vendor how the free educational plan is granted.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the site and student accounts.
- [ ] Sign in and try a tutorial from SelfCAD Academy.
- [ ] Install the PC version only for offline work.
- [ ] Review the privacy policy and its rule about children under 13.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor materials read do not describe any AI. It is a 3D design tool. Students build skills in spatial thinking and in planning a model for printing.

## Privacy and safety
An account is required, and the internet is needed at all times. The policy lists name, company name and email at registration, plus log data and cookies. It says SelfCAD does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, which is a data rule and not a minimum age. The policy does not describe third party sharing or where models are stored. The research found no COPPA, FERPA or student data privacy agreement statement. A teacher should ask IT to review the privacy policy.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Model a simple part such as a phone stand and prepare it for printing in the slicer.
2. Measure an object and recreate it at the same size.
3. Redesign a model and compare two versions.
4. Check a model for problems before printing.
5. Present a design and explain each choice.

### visual_arts
1. Sculpt a small animal with the sculpting tools.
2. Render a model with different colors and compare the results.
3. Sketch a shape and turn it into a 3D object.
4. Design a trophy for a class event.
5. Share a finished model and describe the design process.
