## Overview
LliureX is a Linux system built by the Valencian regional government. A page describes it as a customized Edubuntu system with the KDE Plasma desktop. It is reported to run on over 110,000 school computers in the region. It includes LliureX-Lab for language-teaching classrooms.

## Benefits for students
- Students get a school desktop with the same tools on each computer.
- Language classes can use the LliureX-Lab classroom tools.
- Students see open source software at work.

## Benefits for teachers
- LliureX-Lab supports classroom desktop sharing.
- The system comes in Catalan and Spanish.
- Administrators will do most of the setup.

## Benefits for schools
- The system is described as free. The price was not stated on a page that was read.
- It needs x86_64 hardware.
- Setup is an IT task and can take days.

The official site could not be fetched. The research read no privacy information. The materials do not state where student data goes or whether accounts are needed. They do not state whether the internet is needed. The system may run on desktops and on a school server. A teacher or administrator should check the account setup and the data settings before students log in.
