## Overview
These are Level 1 and Level 2 unplugged puzzle packets from the Root learning library. The packets need no robot. Students solve puzzles about decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithms to reveal a final answer. The official page returned only its title when checked. These details come from search snippets.

## Where is the AI?
This activity has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is pattern recognition. Students look for patterns in puzzles and use them to find answers.

## Privacy and safety
The packets run on paper. The materials do not state whether an account is needed. A teacher should check the library page for sign-in rules before sharing a link. No student data is collected during the puzzles.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Solve a Level 1 puzzle and explain the pattern you found.
2. Break a hard puzzle into smaller parts and solve each part.
3. Write the steps you used as an algorithm for a partner.
4. Spot which details you can ignore and explain why.
5. Move on to Level 2 and compare it with Level 1.
