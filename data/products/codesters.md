## Overview
Codesters is an online environment for teaching Python. It combines a student coding platform, a teacher learning management system, and built-out lessons. The curriculum includes Intro to Codesters, Python parts 1 to 3, Cybersecurity, capstone pathways, and math lessons that use coding. A Colorado state listing gives grades 5 to 9.

## Benefits for students
- Students write Python with drag-and-drop helpers.
- Students follow lessons from intro to capstone projects.
- Students can use a free project page.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers enroll students, assign lessons, and track progress.
- Students can log in with email, Google, Office 365, Clever, ClassLink, or a class key.
- A free 30-day teacher trial and free online training are listed.

## Benefits for schools
- Paid school plans are by quote and no price is listed.
- It runs in a browser on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- Data goes to the vendor's cloud.

## Getting started
- [ ] Start a free 30-day teacher trial on the Codesters site.
- [ ] Take the free online training.
- [ ] Roster students through Clever, ClassLink, Google, or a class key.
- [ ] Assign the Intro to Codesters lessons.
- [ ] Watch the progress dashboard.

- [ ] Review the privacy page, which appears to redirect to Learning.com.

## Where is the AI?
This platform has no AI. It builds skills in sequencing, logic, and data. Students write Python and see the results.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are needed, and the internet is needed at all times. Teachers enter student names or nicknames and grade level, and the platform stores code and progress. Data goes to the vendor. The privacy link redirects to the Learning.com policy. That website policy says it does not cover students under 13 whose data comes through the platform and refers to a separate COPPA Notice. The research did not read that notice. The policy says it does not sell personal information but allows sharing with advertising partners on the website. It does not state where data is stored or discuss FERPA. A teacher should use nicknames and read the COPPA Notice.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Complete the Intro to Codesters lessons and move a sprite.
2. Write a short Python program with a loop.
3. Use a function to repeat a drawing.
4. Debug a program from the Python 1 lessons.
5. Build a small capstone project and present it.

### math
1. Use code to find the mean of a list of numbers.
2. Write a program that draws a graph of a data set.
3. Test a statistics idea with a short program.
4. Compare the output of two programs that use different numbers.
5. Explain how coding helped solve a math problem.
