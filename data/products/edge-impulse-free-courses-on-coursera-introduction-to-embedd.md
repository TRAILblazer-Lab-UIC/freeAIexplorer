## Overview
Edge Impulse publishes three courses and one specialization on Coursera. They include Introduction to Embedded Machine Learning and Computer Vision with Embedded Machine Learning. Edge Impulse says the courses are free for everyone with an optional professional certificate. Coursera pages say a paid Certificate experience is needed for graded work. Free audit access is not confirmed.

## Benefits for students
- Advanced students learn how to run machine learning on small devices.
- Students learn computer vision with real hardware.
- Students can earn an optional professional certificate.

## Benefits for teachers
- The Introduction course comes first and builds the base.
- Courses are self paced and need no classroom time.
- Teachers need strong technical skills to guide students.

## Benefits for schools
- Edge Impulse says the courses are free, but graded work may need a paid certificate.
- Some labs need an OpenMV H7 camera or a Raspberry Pi 4.
- Student data goes to the vendor. IT should review it.

## Where is the AI?
The courses teach embedded machine learning, computer vision, and model training. Training happens in the Edge Impulse cloud. The finished model runs on small hardware such as an OpenMV H7 or a Raspberry Pi 4. Students gather data, train models, and then test them on a device.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are expected for Coursera and for Edge Impulse. The vendor receives course progress and the training data that students upload to Edge Impulse. The research did not read either privacy policy. The courses are written for adults. A teacher should read both privacy policies and check the age rules before students under 18 sign up.
