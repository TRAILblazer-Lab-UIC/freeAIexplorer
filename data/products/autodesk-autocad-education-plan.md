## Overview
Autodesk offers AutoCAD free to eligible students and educators through its Education plan. The license is renewable and needs an Autodesk account with education verification. Autodesk uses the third party SheerID to check enrollment. A batch note suggests grades 9 to 12.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn a professional drafting tool.
- Students practice precise design work.
- Students can build skills for career paths.

## Benefits for teachers
- The tool fits engineering and CTE courses.
- Search results say accredited middle and high schools count as qualified.
- Each user can install on up to three devices per a search snippet.

## Benefits for schools
- The education license is free and renewable.
- IT needs to install the software on Windows or Mac.
- Autodesk pages block automated fetches. Verify terms directly with Autodesk.

## Getting started
- [ ] Create an Autodesk account.
