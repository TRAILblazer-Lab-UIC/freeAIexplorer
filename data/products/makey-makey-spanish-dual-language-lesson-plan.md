## Overview
This lesson plan has two tracks for students in grades 5 to 12 and up. In the Rookie track, students test conductive materials and build a Scratch game where sprites play sounds on key presses. In the Advanced track, students storyboard and build a multi-level dual language game. Students practice Spanish pronunciation and learn if then, forever, timers, scoring, and level design.

## Benefits for students
- Students practice Spanish pronunciation with a hands-on game.
- Students learn coding ideas such as timers and scoring.
- Students share their work with family and staff in the Advanced track.

## Benefits for teachers
- The plan includes Rookie and Advanced tracks.
- Pairs test each other's games for pronunciation.
- The plan is meant to follow the Game Controller Challenge.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson plan is free. A Makey Makey Classic per student or group is a paid purchase.
- Students need computers with a browser.
- The lesson runs over 3 to 5 class days of 1 to 2 hours.

## Ideas by subject
### world_languages
1. Record Spanish words and play each one when a sprite is touched.
2. Test a partner's game and note any pronunciation errors.
3. Build a game that asks questions in Spanish and plays the answer.
4. Match foods to Spanish names using conductive foods as buttons.
5. Share the game with family and staff and teach a few words.

### computer_science
1. Use an if then block to play a sound on a key press.
2. Add a forever loop to keep the game running.
3. Create a timer and a score.
4. Storyboard a multi-level game and build level one.
5. Test the game in pairs and fix each bug.
