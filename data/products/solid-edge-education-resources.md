## Overview
Siemens hosts an educator page for its Solid Edge 3D CAD software. The page lists free software for teachers and students, tutorials, webinar replays, and certification. A search result describes an Hour of Engineering resource for middle school with guided lessons. Students design parts such as a rocket, a bird house, and a building block in 3D CAD.

## Benefits for students
- Students use professional CAD software.
- Students design parts such as a rocket and a bird house.
- Students follow tutorials at their own pace.

## Benefits for teachers
- The page offers tutorials and webinar replays.
- Teachers can request a free license.
- The Hour of Engineering resource was not on the page that was read.

## Benefits for schools
- The software license is free for educators.
- System requirements are on a separate page.
- IT approval for desktop software is likely needed.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in these materials. Solid Edge is a CAD tool. Students build skills in spatial thinking and in planning a part.

## Privacy and safety
The Siemens Learning Center needs registration, and a license request goes to the vendor. The materials do not state what data is collected. The research read no privacy policy. A teacher should read the privacy terms and ask IT what the install collects.
