## Overview
Qobo is a snail-shaped screen-free coding robot for young children. Children arrange puzzle cards that a reader under the robot recognizes. Retail listings describe a 30-piece activity set, a booklet of 15 lessons and a map. The robot follows a black line with two sensors and charges over USB.

## Benefits for students
- Young children learn coding with no screen.
- Children build code by placing cards in order.
- Children watch the robot follow a line.

## Benefits for teachers
- Setup is quick. Charge the robot and lay out cards.
- A booklet of 15 lessons is listed.
- Retailers list ages 3 and up, and 3 to 8 is reported.

## Benefits for schools
- One retailer in Canada showed 127.99 CAD. Prices vary.
- No accounts or internet are needed.
- No independent review was found.

## Where is the AI?
The robot has no AI. Children build skills in sequencing and logic. They put actions in order and see the result.

## Privacy and safety
The robot works on the device and needs no account or internet. One listing mentions an optional app, and the materials do not describe its data use. A teacher should skip the app unless the school has checked it. Small parts need supervision with young children.
