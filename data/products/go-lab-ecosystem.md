## Overview
Go-Lab is an EU funded effort that opened remote science laboratories, data archives, and virtual models for schools. The golabz site lists online labs, scaffolding apps, and inquiry learning spaces. Teachers combine labs and apps into learning spaces on an authoring platform called Graasp. It suits middle and high school science.

## Benefits for students
- Students run experiments in online labs.
- Students follow an inquiry path from question to conclusion.
- Students use apps that guide their thinking.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can browse a large catalog of labs and apps.
- Teachers can author and share their own learning spaces.
- A 2016 source reported over 400 online labs and 40 apps, and a later report cites over 1100 published spaces.

## Benefits for schools
- The portal is described as free. Some labs may need a subscription, which was not verified.
- It runs in a browser and needs no install.
- IT has little to do beyond allowing the site.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the golabz catalog in a web browser.
- [ ] Browse the online labs for a topic you teach.
- [ ] Verify that each lab you want still works. The research notes say to verify availability before use.
- [ ] Create a teacher account on the Graasp authoring platform.
- [ ] Build one inquiry learning space from a lab and an app.
- [ ] Review the privacy policy before students join.
- [ ] Test the space on a student device.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in the materials reviewed. Students build science inquiry skills. They ask a question, run an experiment, and draw a conclusion. The platform runs in the vendor's cloud.

## Privacy and safety
The platform runs in the cloud and needs the internet at all times. An account is optional for browsing, and teachers need an account on the authoring platform. The research could not reach the official site because its certificate had expired, and it read no privacy statement. The materials do not say what student data is collected. Current availability is unconfirmed because the EU funding ended. A teacher should confirm the site loads safely and read the privacy policy and each lab's terms before use.
