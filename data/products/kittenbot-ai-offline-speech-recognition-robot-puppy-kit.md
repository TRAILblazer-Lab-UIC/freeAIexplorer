## Overview
This kit builds a desktop robot dog controlled by a micro:bit V2 and a Sugar ASR voice recognition module. The retailer listing says it wakes on a voice command and responds to instructions such as walking, turning, sitting, shaking hands, wagging its tail, and dancing. Students program how it reacts to each command visually in MakeCode. The title says the speech recognition is offline, but the listing does not explain how.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a robot dog from a block kit.
- Students program each reaction in MakeCode blocks.
- Students see a voice command lead to a physical action.

## Benefits for teachers

## Where is the AI?
The AI is speech recognition by the Sugar ASR module. The retailer title says it runs offline, and the research did not confirm that. Students choose what the dog does after each command and test how well the module hears different voices.

## Privacy and safety
The retailer title says the speech recognition is offline, and the research did not confirm that. No vendor privacy policy or technical documentation was found. The materials do not say whether the internet is needed. The MakeCode editor is a Microsoft web service that was not reviewed. A teacher should check how the robot handles voice recordings and whether MakeCode needs an account.
