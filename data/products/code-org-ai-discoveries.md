## Overview
AI Discoveries is a free beginner course for grades 6 to 8 that runs on a Chromebook or computer. It comes as a 36 week year, an 18 week semester, or a 9 week quarter, and many units stand alone. Units cover critical thinking about AI, machine learning, web development, music programming, games, design, data and society, and physical computing with micro:bit or Circuit Playground.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn what AI can and cannot do through hands-on units.
- Students build web pages, games, and apps with their own code.
- Students can use an AI Tutor for help while they work.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers get lesson plans, slides, activity guides, and rubrics.
- Teachers get an AI Teaching Assistant.
- Teachers can take an optional self-paced professional development course.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free. The physical computing unit needs a micro:bit or Circuit Playground.
- It runs on Chromebooks and computers.
- The course page says Web Lab and AI tools are for ages 11 and up.

## Where is the AI?
Students explore AI literacy, bias, misinformation, machine learning, and chatbot customization. An AI Tutor helps students and runs in the vendor's cloud. Students train models, customize a chatbot, and use the results in their own projects.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts are optional, and the internet is needed at all times. The vendor collects account, progress, and project data, plus AI Tutor interactions. The course page says AI Tutor interactions are stored securely, deleted after 90 days, and never used to train third party models. The privacy policy says it uses third party language model providers and does not describe their retention. It says the nonprofit is not subject to COPPA, and it does not sell personal data or show ads. A teacher should check the privacy terms for the AI Tutor with the school. A teacher should also tell students not to share personal details in chats.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Work through the Thinking Critically About AI unit and note one thing a computer cannot do.
2. Train a model in the machine learning unit and test it on new examples.
3. Customize a chatbot in AI Chat Lab and write rules for its answers.
4. Build a web page in Web Lab about a topic you care about.
5. Build a game in Game Lab and share it with a partner.

### music
1. Write a short program in Music Lab that plays a beat.
2. Change the tempo and notes and describe how the song changes.
3. Add a second track and make the two layers match.
4. Share a track with a classmate and ask for feedback.
5. Explain how a loop helps you make music in code.

### social_studies
1. Study the data and society unit and list one way data affects a community.
2. Find an example of misinformation and explain how to check it.
3. Discuss who should check the fairness of AI tools.
4. Survey classmates about how they use AI tools.
5. Write a short opinion on one rule for AI in schools.
