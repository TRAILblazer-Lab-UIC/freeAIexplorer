## Overview
Bits and Bytes is a commercial card game that teaches basics of coding and algorithmic thinking. The Loopi page lists it for children aged 7 and up. The goal is to guide your program through obstacles to the base. Players issue turn and move commands to steer a character home.

## Benefits for students
- Students plan a sequence of commands before they play.
- Students practice turn and move commands that work like simple code.
- Students play a game with no screen.

## Benefits for teachers
- The game needs only the box and the rules.
- No setup is needed beyond opening the box.
- A teacher can use it as a quiet center activity.

## Benefits for schools
- The game costs about 30 euros per the Loopi page. It is not free.
- It needs no devices, internet, or accounts.
- IT staff have no work to do.

## Where is the AI?
This game has no AI. It builds sequencing, which is the skill of planning steps in order. A parent blogger found the link to programming weak in the simple version.

## Privacy and safety
The game is a physical card set and the Loopi page describes no software. The research found no accounts or student data collection. The age range is 7 and up on the Loopi page and retail listings differ. A teacher should check the age range before buying.

## Ideas by subject

1. Plan a sequence of turn and move commands that steers a character to the base.

5. Write down a winning sequence and teach it to a classmate.
