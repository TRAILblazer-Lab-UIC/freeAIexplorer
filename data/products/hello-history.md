## Overview
Hello History is a mobile app for chatting with historical figures such as Cleopatra, Einstein, and Buddha. An educator review says it offers more than 400 figures on iOS and Android. The company offers a program for teachers and students. Students ask questions and read replies in the voice of the figure.

## Benefits for students
- Students can ask a historical figure questions in a chat.
- Students find the chat format easy to use.
- Students can talk about many figures, from scientists to religious leaders.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can use the chats to start a discussion.
- The company says teachers can ask for help turning a book chapter into an interactive lesson.
- Educators can contact team@humy.ai about the teacher program.

## Benefits for schools
- Free messages come with the download, then the app is paid. The site lists no price.
- The app needs a smartphone and an account.
- The policy says the services are not directed at children under 13.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Hello History in the iOS or Android store.
- [ ] Read the privacy policy with IT before use.
- [ ] Confirm that students are 13 or older.
- [ ] Email team@humy.ai to ask about the teacher program.
- [ ] Create a test account on a teacher device.
- [ ] Try a chat with one historical figure.
- [ ] Plan which questions students should ask.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a chatbot that role-plays historical figures. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Research notes say data goes to OpenAI and ad networks. Students ask questions and compare the replies with real sources.

## Privacy and safety
Students need an account, and the internet is always needed. The app collects email, password, device info, lesson submissions, IP address, cookie and advertising identifiers, and usage data. The policy lists Firebase, RevenueCat, and OpenAI as sub-processors, and says data may be processed in the US and Sweden. The developer says it does not store in-app conversations, but OpenAI may collect them. Advertising tracking is disclosed. The policy says the services are not directed at children under 13. A teacher should read the policy with IT and avoid the app for classes with students under 13.
