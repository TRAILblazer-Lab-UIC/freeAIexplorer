## Ideas by subject
### social_studies
1. Identify causes and consequences in Quebec history and build them into a Twine story.
2. Use primary documents as passages in a branching story.
3. Write a story that shows how one event leads to another.
4. Let readers choose a historical figure and follow their view.
5. Trade stories and list the evidence in each one.

### ela
1. Write a nonlinear story with three paths.
2. Add dialogue to a branching scene.
3. Compare how two endings change the theme.
4. Edit a partner's story for clear links.
5. Present a story to the class and explain the choices.

### library_media
1. Build a story that teaches how to check a source.
2. Plan a research question as a branching map.
3. Add links to reliable sources in each passage.
4. Create a story that shows how to cite sources.
5. Review a classmate's story for source quality.
