## Overview
CodeCombat is a game-based platform for learning Python and JavaScript. The company says it has built AI into most of its learning experiences. It offers AI HackStack, a student-facing platform for AI learning that the vendor calls safe and secure. A Remix feature lets students modify other students' AI projects. The sources give few technical details.

## Benefits for students
- Students can try AI projects such as a food classifier.
- Students can remix other students' AI projects and learn from them.
- Students keep learning Python and JavaScript in the same platform.

## Benefits for teachers
- A high school class used a Food Classifier project to see how AI sorts objects and where it fails.
- Teachers create the class and the student accounts.
- Teachers assign courses from one dashboard.

## Benefits for schools
- The discovery notes describe a free tier and paid classroom plans. Prices were not read.
- It runs in a browser, including on Chromebooks.
- IT may need to approve the vendors that the platform lists.

## Getting started
- [ ] Create a teacher account on CodeCombat.

- [ ] Try the food classifier project yourself.
- [ ] Test the Remix feature.
- [ ] Assign a course to the class.

## Where is the AI?
Students work on classification projects and other AI learning scenarios. The AI runs in the vendor's cloud. Students build a classifier, test it on new examples, and remix a project to change how it behaves.

## Privacy and safety
Every student needs an account, and the internet is needed at all times. The vendor collects a first name or pseudonym, a last initial, optional email, usage data, cookies, and the content students create. Listed processors include Cloudflare, AWS, MongoDB Atlas, and Google Analytics. The policy names no AI model providers, and the research did not confirm where HackStack prompts go. The safety claims come from the vendor and an ORISE listing, and no independent audit was found. A teacher should read the privacy policy with the school and ask the vendor where prompts are sent.
