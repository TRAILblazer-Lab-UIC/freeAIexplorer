## Overview
NLP4ALL is a web tool from Aarhus University for students with no machine learning background. Students analyze political party tweets. They tag tweets by hand in one mode. In another they train a Naive Bayesian classifier and test word features. The tool provides about 4,500 tweets and shows results in a confusion matrix.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how a classifier sorts text.
- Students test which word features help the model.
- Students read a confusion matrix to judge the results.

## Benefits for teachers
- A review paper reports a classroom study with 24 high school students in social studies.
- The tool works in English and Danish.
- The tool needs no machine learning background.

## Benefits for schools

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a Naive Bayes classifier that sorts tweets by party. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Students tag tweets by hand, train the classifier on those tags, test word features, and read where the model gets confused.

## Privacy and safety
An account is required, and the internet is needed throughout. The tool is hosted on a university research server, and the research found no privacy policy. The review does not say how student data is handled. The information comes from a secondary review and not from the tool itself. A teacher should review the political tweets before class and confirm with the authors how student data is stored.

## Ideas by subject
### social_studies
1. Tag tweets by party by hand and explain the clues you used.
2. Compare how different parties talk about one issue.
3. Discuss whether a classifier could be unfair to a party.
4. Read a confusion matrix and describe which parties get mixed up.
5. Write a short report on what tweets can and cannot tell us about politics.

### computer_science
1. Train the Naive Bayes classifier and note how well it sorts new tweets.
2. Change the word features and compare the results.
3. Find a tweet the model got wrong and explain why.
4. Explain in plain words how the classifier uses word counts.
5. Compare your hand tags with the model's tags.
