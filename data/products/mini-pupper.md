## Overview
Mini Pupper is an open-source ROS quadruped robot dog designed for education. It has 12 degrees of freedom and runs on a Raspberry Pi. Students can learn ROS, SLAM, navigation, obstacle avoidance, and AI-based object tracking. The software builds on the Champ quadruped project. The official site returned a 404. Details come from a forum post and search results.

## Benefits for students
- Students work with ROS on a real walking robot.
- Students try mapping and navigation.
- Students try AI object tracking.

## Benefits for teachers
- The code is open.
- The kit can be bought assembled.
- A teacher needs strong Linux and ROS skills.

## Benefits for schools
- Prices vary from about 279 to 940 USD by version and source.
- Lidar is a separate purchase.
- The AI runs on the device and the internet is needed at setup only.

## Where is the AI?
AI is optional. The robot can use object tracking and SLAM for mapping. The AI runs on the robot. Students write and run code that makes the dog follow an object or map a room.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the internet is used at setup. The robot runs ROS locally on a Raspberry Pi. The research reviewed no privacy policy because the official site was unreachable. Some MangDang products mention ChatGPT features, which the research did not check. The robot may carry a camera and lidar. A teacher should decide where it is used, whether any images are saved, and whether any cloud features are turned on.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Run a ROS example and name the parts that send messages.
2. Use object tracking to make the dog follow a colored ball.
3. Read how SLAM builds a map and draw the idea.
4. Change a walking setting in the code and predict the result.
5. Explain how the dog avoids an obstacle.

### engineering
1. Assemble the legs and test each of the 12 joints.
2. Tune the walking gait on a smooth floor.
3. Test how the dog handles a small bump.
4. Add lidar if available and compare the maps.
5. Record the battery life during a set task.
