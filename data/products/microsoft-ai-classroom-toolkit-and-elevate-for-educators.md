## Overview
This is Microsoft's free teacher training for AI literacy. The Introduction to AI literacy path on Microsoft Learn has 5 beginner modules with no prerequisites. It covers what AI can and cannot do, the AI Literacy Framework, free Microsoft Education AI tools, and reflective practice. It is one of two paths toward the Microsoft Elevate Educator community.

## Benefits for students
- The materials are for educators and do not describe a student lesson set.
- Students benefit as teachers learn what AI can and cannot do.
- Teachers can bring that knowledge into class talks about AI.

## Benefits for teachers
- Five modules are self-paced at beginner level.
- The path aligns with ISTE, UNESCO, and TeachAI frameworks.
- Finishing a path leads to the Elevate Educator community.

## Benefits for schools
- The training is described as free.
- Some AI tools need Microsoft 365 Education licenses.
- Learning Zone creation needs Copilot+ PCs.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Introduction to AI literacy path on Microsoft Learn.
- [ ] Sign in with a Microsoft account to track progress.
- [ ] Start with the module on where to use AI in your classroom.
- [ ] Work through the AI Literacy Framework module.
- [ ] Try the free AI tools from Microsoft Education.
- [ ] Ask IT which licenses the school already has.
- [ ] Share what you learned at a staff meeting.

## Where is the AI?
The training teaches about AI and responsible use. The course runs on Microsoft's cloud and the materials describe generative AI tools for educators. Teachers learn what AI can and cannot do, and then apply it to their own planning.

## Privacy and safety
A Microsoft account is needed to track progress, and the internet is always needed. The course is for educators. The research found no student facing lesson set. The cloud AI tools it teaches, such as Copilot, send prompts to Microsoft services under the school's Microsoft 365 terms. The research did not review those terms. A teacher should check the school's rules for Microsoft accounts and license terms before signing in.
