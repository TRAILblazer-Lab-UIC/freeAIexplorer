## Overview
Create ML is Apple's tool for training custom Core ML models on a Mac. It comes as an app that ships with Xcode and as a Swift framework. Users supply labeled data for image, sound, text, or tabular tasks and export a model file. The Apple pages returned little text. These details come from discovery notes and are lightly verified.

## Benefits for students
- Students train a real model from their own labeled data.
- Students can work with images, sound, text, or tables.
- Students can export a model for use in an app.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app offers templates for each task type.
- It fits Swift and app development courses.
- Training happens on the Mac.

## Benefits for schools
- The discovery notes say it is free with a Mac. Xcode and a Mac cost extra and were not checked.
- It works only on a Mac, and not on Chromebooks or Windows.
- IT will likely need to install Xcode.

## Getting started
- [ ] Check that you have a Mac that can run Xcode.
- [ ] Ask IT to install Xcode from the Mac App Store.
- [ ] Open Create ML from the developer tools menu.
- [ ] Pick a template, such as image classification.
- [ ] Add a small set of labeled data.
- [ ] Train the model and test it.
- [ ] Export the model file.

## Where is the AI?
Create ML trains image, sound, text, and tabular models. Training happens on the Mac. Students prepare labeled data, train a model, test it, and use it in their own app.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not say whether accounts are needed. The research did not confirm from an Apple page that training stays on the Mac, and the notes only say no cloud is needed. The internet is needed to install Xcode. The linked policy is Apple's general privacy page. A teacher should keep faces and voices out of student training sets. A teacher should also confirm Apple's terms for developer tools.
