## Overview
MuseScore Studio is free, open source music notation software. Users enter notes with a mouse, keyboard, or MIDI keyboard. Files move to other programs with MusicXML and MIDI. The current version listed is 4.7.7. The separate MuseScore.com site has score sharing and paid features.

## Benefits for students
- Students write and hear their own scores.
- Students enter notes with a mouse, a keyboard, or a MIDI keyboard.
- Students create notation for many instruments and ensembles.

## Benefits for teachers
- The teacher page suggests teaching music literacy to younger students.
- Older students can submit composition assignments.
- Teachers create worksheets, including fill in the blank sheets.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- It runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux but not on Chromebooks.
- The installer may need IT approval.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download MuseScore Studio from musescore.org.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the installer.
- [ ] Skip the MuseScore.com site and its paid offers.
- [ ] Connect a MIDI keyboard if you have one.
- [ ] Turn off or review update checks and crash reports with IT.
- [ ] Try a short score before class.

## Where is the AI?
This tool has no AI. It builds music reading and writing skills. Students practice pattern spotting in rhythm and pitch.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed for the desktop app. Update checks are on by default and send an IP address and basic system details to the vendor. Crash reports send a dump file after a serious error, and the research could not tell whether crash reports can be turned off. The research did not fetch the desktop privacy policy page. A teacher should ask IT about these settings and tell students to avoid MuseScore.com upsells.
