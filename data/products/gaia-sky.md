## Overview
Gaia Sky is a free open-source 3D astronomy visualization platform for desktop and VR. It was developed within ESA's Gaia mission. Users fly through star catalogs, including Gaia data, in real time. An in-app dataset manager downloads data such as Gaia, NGC, SDSS, and open clusters.

## Benefits for students
- Students fly through space in three dimensions.
- Students explore real star catalogs.
- Students can load their own data in VOTable, FITS, or CSV format.

## Benefits for teachers
- The dataset manager downloads star data inside the app.
- VR is supported.
- No documented classroom use was found. Teachers plan their own activities.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- It needs a computer with a capable graphics card. Exact requirements were not read.
- Datasets can be large. The Chromebook platform is not listed.

## Where is the AI?
Gaia Sky has no AI. It builds data and spatial skills. Students explore star positions and spot patterns in the sky.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the internet is used at setup and for dataset downloads. The app runs locally. The site links a privacy policy, but the page could not be fetched and the research did not read it. The materials describe no student data collection. A teacher should read the privacy policy and check the size of the datasets before students download them.
