## Overview
This is the Materials page of Snap!, a block-based language from UC Berkeley. It links to the online reference manual, a PDF manual, and a beginner modules summary from SAP Young Thinkers. It also lists six courses, including The Beauty and Joy of Computing, BJC Sparks for middle school, and Microsoft TEALS Introduction to Computer Science. About ten books are listed, many with free PDFs.

## Benefits for students
- Students can follow courses from middle school through college.
- Students can read free books about Snap!.
- Students use the tool in several languages.

## Benefits for teachers
- The page gathers manuals, courses, and books in one place.
- The Beauty and Joy of Computing is a CS Principles course built on Snap!.
- Pages are available in English, German, Spanish, French, and Chinese.

## Benefits for schools
- The resources are free.
- No account is needed to read them.
- Setup takes only minutes.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the Snap! Materials page.
- [ ] Browse the reference manual and the beginner modules.
- [ ] Choose one course that fits your grade level.
- [ ] Open Snap! in a browser or use the offline version listed under tools.
- [ ] Check that the linked books open on school devices.
- [ ] Plan a first lesson from one module.

## Where is the AI?
These resources have no AI. Snap! is a block coding tool. Students build sequencing, logic, and data skills as they follow the courses.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed to read the resources, and the page needs the internet. The page links Privacy and Terms pages that the research did not read. It also offers Join and Log In links for the Snap cloud. The materials do not state what data the linked sites collect. A teacher should check each linked site and the account rules before students use it.
