## Overview
FarmBeats for Students combines a micro:bit sensor kit with free curriculum on precision agriculture. Section 1 gathers plant data with micro:bit sensors in MakeCode. Section 2 analyzes big data in Excel. Section 3 has students build machine learning models to detect nutrient imbalance and pests. It aligns to AI4K12, CSTA, NGSS, Common Core math, and AFNR standards.

## Benefits for students
- Students monitor real plant health with sensors.
- Students analyze data in Excel.
- Students build machine learning models for farm problems.

## Benefits for teachers
- The curriculum is free and has three sections.
- Free educator training is on Microsoft Learn.
- Teachers need strong data and device skills.

## Benefits for schools
- The curriculum is free. The micro:bit kits are sold separately and prices were not checked.
- Work runs on any device with a web browser.
- Standards alignment helps with planning.

## Where is the AI?
Section 3 teaches machine learning for nutrient imbalance and pests, including computer vision. The materials do not say where the model runs. Students gather sensor data and plant images and then train and test models in the browser. They use the results to decide how to care for plants.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state whether accounts are needed or what data is collected. The lessons use MakeCode and Excel, and the page does not say whether students need Microsoft accounts. It also does not name the tool for the machine learning section or say whether the internet is needed. Sensor data comes from plants, and Section 3 also uses plant images. A teacher should check which tools students sign in to and where the plant images are stored.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Attach micro:bit sensors to a plant and record its data for a week.
2. Compare data from two plants that get different light.
3. Describe signs of a nutrient problem in a plant.
4. Test a model on new plant images and record how well it works.
5. Write a short report on how sensors help farmers.

### math
1. Enter sensor data in Excel and calculate the mean and range.
2. Graph a week of data and describe the trend.
3. Compare two data sets and explain the difference.
4. Use a chart to look for a pattern in plant growth.
5. Predict the next day's reading and check the result.

### computer_science
1. Write a MakeCode program that reads a micro:bit sensor.
2. Store the readings and describe how to clean them.
3. Train a model to detect a nutrient problem.
4. Test the model on new data and note its mistakes.
5. Discuss responsible AI choices in farming.

### cte
1. Explore precision agriculture careers and write a short profile.
2. Compare a farm that uses sensors with one that does not.
3. Plan a small sensor project for a school garden.
4. Interview a local grower about data use.
5. Link a lesson to an AFNR standard and explain the match.
