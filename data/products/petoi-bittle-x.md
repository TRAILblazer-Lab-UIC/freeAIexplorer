## Overview
Bittle X is a palm-sized robot dog on the open-source OpenCat framework. Petoi's educator page lists Bittle X V2 at 319 USD and says it supports block coding, Python, and C++, with a free simulator. The Make review notes built-in voice control, an ESP32-based board, and an optional AI camera module. The educator page lists K-12, and the guide suggests about age 10 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Students build and program a robot dog.
- Students can move from blocks to Python and C++.
- Students can try voice commands.

## Benefits for teachers
- The educator guide says assembly takes about an hour.
- The guide says students work best at 2 to 3 per robot.
- Petoi offers educator curricula.

## Benefits for schools
- Bittle X V2 lists at 319 USD, and the Make review cites 469 USD for the V2 with arm. Educator discounts need a quote request.
- The robot runs on the device and the internet is needed at setup only.
- Chromebook support is limited per the educator guide.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Petoi educator page and request a quote.
- [ ] Choose a kit or a pre-assembled robot.
- [ ] Assemble the kit with groups of two or three.
- [ ] Calibrate the joints with the Petoi app or Arduino IDE.
- [ ] Try a first program in blocks.
- [ ] Try the simulator before using the robot.
- [ ] Check device support if you use Chromebooks.

## Where is the AI?
AI is optional. The robot has voice commands and an optional AI camera module. The AI runs on the device. Students train the robot to respond to voice and can add the camera for vision projects.

## Privacy and safety
The robot needs no account and the internet is used mainly for downloads, updates, and online resources. The robot has voice control. The research did not confirm where voice recognition runs. Petoi's privacy policy covers the website and store only. It does not mention apps and still holds template placeholders. A teacher should ask Petoi where voice data goes and test the web coding blocks on the school network.
