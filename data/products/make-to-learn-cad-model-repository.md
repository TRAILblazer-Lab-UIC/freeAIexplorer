## Overview
The Make to Learn CAD Model Repository is a curated library of open CAD models for educators. The University of Virginia Make to Learn Laboratory built it through three NSF grants starting in 2010. ITEEA is a partner and is building Engineering by Design units on the models. The official page returned 404 on 2026-11-19. The current access path needs a recheck.

## Benefits for students
- Students can open real CAD models and study how they are made.
- Students can 3D print a model.
- Students learn from designs that others have reviewed.

## Benefits for teachers
- Search results describe the models as peer reviewed.
- The models use a Creative Commons Attribution Non-commercial license.
- Models are also deposited in a Dataverse repository with DOIs, per a search snippet.

## Benefits for schools
- The models are described as free.
- Students need CAD software or a 3D printer to use them.
- The access path was not confirmed. Ask IT to check it.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Open a model and list the parts that it contains.
2. Measure a model and compare it to a real object.
3. Change one feature of a model and explain the effect.
4. Print a model and test how it works.
5. Write a design review of a model with two strengths and one improvement.
