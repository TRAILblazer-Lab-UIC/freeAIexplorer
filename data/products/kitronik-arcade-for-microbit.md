## Overview
The Kitronik ARCADE is a handheld gamepad that holds a micro:bit V2 and a 1.77 inch color screen. It has four direction buttons, two action buttons, and a menu button. Students write games in MakeCode Arcade with blocks, JavaScript, or Python. The vendor lists it for age 8 and up.

## Benefits for students
- Students play the games they build.
- Students can start with blocks and move to JavaScript or Python.
- Students share a game by handing over the device.

## Benefits for teachers
- MakeCode Arcade runs in a browser.
- The product page has a datasheet and guides.
- A class pack of ten gamepads is sold.

## Benefits for schools
- The price is 25.90 GBP ex VAT per unit, or 23.90 GBP for 10 or more.
- The micro:bit V2 and the batteries cost extra.
- The micro:bit V1 does not work.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the ARCADE page on the Kitronik site.
- [ ] Buy the gamepad and a micro:bit V2 for each unit.
- [ ] Add three AA batteries and a USB cable.
- [ ] Open MakeCode Arcade in a browser.
- [ ] Build a simple game and download it to the device.
- [ ] Check the guides on the product page.
- [ ] Plan a first lesson with a short game.

## Where is the AI?
The gamepad has no AI. Students write code with blocks or text. They build logic and sequencing skills as they decide what happens when a button is pressed.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed for the board, which runs games locally. MakeCode Arcade is a web editor from Microsoft, and its data handling was not reviewed. The Kitronik privacy page returned a 404. The research found no description of student data collection. A teacher should check the MakeCode Arcade terms and any shared game before it is played.
