## Overview
Digital Schoolhouse AI Unplugged is a lesson for students aged 9 and over. It introduces generative AI with no devices. Students work through activities on how generative AI makes content from a prompt. They also see how it learns by studying training data. The teacher guide suggests ways to adapt the lesson for younger children.

## Benefits for students
- Students see how a prompt shapes what generative AI produces.
- Students learn that AI gets its skills from training data.
- Students work with worksheets and talk with classmates.

## Benefits for teachers
- The download holds a teacher guide, worksheets, and an editable presentation.
- The presentation can be edited to fit the class.
- The teacher guide gives ideas for younger children.

## Benefits for schools

## Where is the AI?
The lesson has no running AI program. It teaches how generative AI works through paper activities. Students act out how a prompt leads to content and how training data shapes what an AI can make. They build skills in prompt writing and in understanding how models learn.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Act out a generative AI by writing new content from a prompt card.
2. Study a set of training examples and guess what an AI would make from them.
3. Change the training examples and compare what the AI would produce.
4. Explain to a partner why a model with poor training data makes poor content.
5. Draw a diagram that follows a prompt from start to finished content.

### ela
1. Write a prompt for a short story and swap it with a partner.
2. Improve a vague prompt by adding detail and compare the two results.
3. Describe how word choice in a prompt changes the answer.
4. Write a set of prompt instructions for a paper AI to follow.
5. Discuss why a clear prompt matters when asking anyone for help.
