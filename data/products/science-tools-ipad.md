## Overview
Science Tools is an iPad app listed as an interactive experiment box. It offers simulations of double-slit interference, double pendulum chaos, optics, and orbital mechanics. Students change settings with hands-on controls and watch the results. The listing says it works offline and suits class demos and self-study.

## Benefits for students
- Students see physics ideas move on screen.
- Students change controls and watch what happens.
- Students can explore alone after class, even offline.

## Benefits for teachers
- The simulations work for a quick class demo.
- The app needs little setup beyond an install.
- The listing is the only source. Try each tool before class.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free to download. A Pro purchase of USD 2.99 adds all tools and future content.
- The app runs on the iPad and needs no internet.
- School device management may need to approve the app.

## Where is the AI?
This app has no AI. The simulations follow fixed physics rules. Students build skills in spotting patterns as they change a setting and watch the result.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the listing says the app works offline. The developer reports to Apple that no data is collected, and Apple notes this is not verified. The research did not read the developer privacy policy linked on the App Store page. The seller shows an individual name and the copyright shows endless ai llc. A teacher should read the App Store privacy details and the linked policy before students use it.

### science

3. Move a lens in the optics simulation and sketch where the image forms.
4. Adjust the orbit settings and explain what makes a planet circle or escape.
