## Overview
trAIn is a free AI literacy resource built by University of Delaware computer science student Julia Stutzke with faculty mentor Debra Yarrington. The informational side covers types of AI, environmental impact, mental health risks, ethical use, and checking whether images and videos are real. The educational side has a lesson plan for middle and high school teachers. The only source is a research symposium abstract.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn the main kinds of AI.
- Students learn how to check whether media is real.
- Students think about the risks and ethics of AI.

## Benefits for teachers
- A lesson plan is included for middle and high school.
- Teachers who log in get extension activities and a forum.
- The topics cover many common questions about AI.

## Benefits for schools
- The abstract calls the resource free.
- The vendor does not say what devices or IT work are needed.
- The abstract gives no link to the tool.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. List the types of AI named in the resource and give an example of each.
2. Discuss one risk of AI and suggest a rule to reduce it.
3. Explain how AI uses energy and what that means for the environment.
4. Debate what ethical use of AI looks like at school.
5. Make a class chart of AI tools you use and what each one does.

### library_media
1. Look at a photo and list clues that it may be fake.
2. Use a checking method on a short video and report your findings.
3. Write steps for verifying an image before sharing it.
4. Compare a real photo with an AI made one and note the differences.
5. Create a poster on how to check media before you trust it.
