## Overview
The MakeCode Offline App is an installable editor for the micro:bit. It runs on Windows and macOS. It is meant for computers without reliable internet. Students program a micro:bit with blocks.

## Benefits for students
- Students code even when the internet is down.
- Students need no sign-in.
- Students use the same blocks as the web editor.

## Benefits for teachers
- The app works without the internet after install.
- Some extensions do not work offline.
- The micro:bit guide says the web editor also works offline after one full load, which suits Chromebooks.

## Benefits for schools
- The app is free.
- Installers are offered for Windows on Intel, AMD, and ARM and for macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon.
- IT may need to approve the install.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the installer or zip for your system.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Accept the license and install.
- [ ] Open the app and make a simple program.
- [ ] Connect a micro:bit and download the program.
- [ ] Test an extension to see if it works offline.
- [ ] Use the web editor on Chromebooks.

## Where is the AI?
The app has no AI. Students program a board with blocks. They build skills in sequencing, logic, and input and output.

## Privacy and safety
No sign in is needed. The download page requires agreeing to the license terms and the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The license says the software may check for updates periodically and some features rely on online services. The research read no specific telemetry terms. A teacher should check which extensions students use and whether the school allows update checks.
