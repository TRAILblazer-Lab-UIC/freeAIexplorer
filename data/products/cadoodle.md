## Overview
CaDoodle is a free, open source, local drag-and-drop 3D CAD application. It is positioned as an alternative to Tinkercad. It runs on Linux, Windows, Mac, and ChromeOS according to a directory listing. Models are stored locally in an open JSON-based file format and export to STL, SVG, OBJ, and others.

## Benefits for students
- Students design 3D models by dragging shapes.
- Students can export models for 3D printing.
- Students keep their files on their own computer.

## Benefits for teachers
- The drag-and-drop style is easy to teach.
- Scripting and OpenSCAD source support give advanced students more room.
- Do not confuse CaDoodle with Doodle3D, which is a different tool.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- No account is needed.
- Models are stored locally, and the installer has an auto-updater.

## Where is the AI?
CaDoodle has no AI. It builds the skill of spatial reasoning, as students combine shapes into a design. It also builds sequencing through the order of design steps.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and search results say the app runs locally and stores models on the computer. The research could not read the official site, and it found no privacy policy. The vendor materials list no student data collection. The installer has an auto-updater, which contacts a server. The app is a pre 1.0 beta. A teacher should check the auto-updater and the offline mode with IT.
