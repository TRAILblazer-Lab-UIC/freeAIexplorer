## Overview
Lightbot's free educator resources page offers a PDF of printable Lightbot command icons for an unplugged activity without computers. It also offers a Lightbot Basics lesson plan for ages 5-9. The lesson plan introduces the basic level set, explains setup, and suggests follow-up questions. The page still says the browser game needs Flash Player. The page looks out of date.

## Benefits for students
- Young students practice giving commands with icons.
- Students learn how a short list of steps moves a robot.
- Students play without a computer.

## Benefits for teachers
- The icons print from a single PDF.
- The Basics lesson plan explains setup and follow-up questions.
- Preparation takes only a few minutes.

## Benefits for schools
- The resources are listed under More Free Resources for Educators, which implies they are free.
- The page does not state a license or an account requirement.
- Game pricing is not stated on the page.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the educator resources page on the Lightbot website.
- [ ] Download the printable icon PDF.
- [ ] Print and cut the icons.
- [ ] Read the Lightbot Basics lesson plan.
- [ ] Draw a simple grid on the floor or table.
- [ ] Let students arrange icons to move a partner across the grid.
- [ ] Ask the follow-up questions from the plan.

## Where is the AI?
This resource has no AI. Students arrange icons to give a robot a list of steps. They build sequencing skills.

## Privacy and safety
The printables are PDFs, and the page does not mention accounts or data collection. The internet is used to download the files. The Lightbot game itself was not reviewed, and no privacy policy was read. A teacher should check the page and the Lightbot game's privacy terms before students use the game.

## Ideas by subject

1. Lay out icons to move a partner from one square to another.
2. Follow a partner's icon list exactly and see where it leads.
3. Find the icon that caused a mistake and fix it.
4. Make a short loop of icons that repeats a move.
5. Compare your icon list with a classmate's for the same goal.
