## Overview
BeepBox is a free browser tool for sketching and sharing instrumental music in a chiptune style. The whole song is packed into the page link. Copying the link saves and shares it. Students place notes in a song editor and listen as they go. A mini player can be embedded in other pages.

## Benefits for students
- Students hear their song at once as they place notes.
- Students can share a song by sending a link.
- Students can export a finished song as an MP3.

## Benefits for teachers
- The editor is easy to learn and quick to demonstrate.
- Teachers do not need to make accounts or manage logins.
- Students can finish a short song in one lesson.

## Benefits for schools
- The tool is free, and the code is open source.
- It works in a browser, and Chromebooks are listed as supported.
- A downloadable offline version exists for schools with weak internet.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the BeepBox website in a browser.
- [ ] Give students headphones.
- [ ] Make a short test song and copy its link.
- [ ] Open the link in a new tab to check that the song loads.
- [ ] Tell students to save their link in a document. Songs live only in links.
- [ ] Download the offline version if the school network is slow.
- [ ] Try the MP3 export once before class.

## Where is the AI?
BeepBox has no AI. It builds the skill of pattern spotting. Students repeat and vary short musical patterns. It also builds sequencing as they order notes in time.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the internet is used at setup. The site says song data stays on the device unless the user shares the link. It also says BeepBox does not collect, track, or share user data. Those are the site's own statements. The research found no privacy policy. A teacher should remind students that anyone with the link can open the song. Students should save links carefully.
