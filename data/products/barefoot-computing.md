## Overview
Barefoot Computing is a UK programme that supports primary computing teaching with free resources, workshops, and live lessons for ages 2 to 11. Resources are grouped as Early Years, 5-7, 7-9, and 9-11. The home page lists Barefoot AI Explorers Level 1 and Level 2, which are unplugged units on AI and how it works. The programme is powered by BCS.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn algorithms, patterns, data, and logic through unplugged play.
- Students explore how AI works without needing a device.
- Students meet ideas in lessons that match their age band.

## Benefits for teachers
- Lesson plans and pupil resources can be downloaded.
- Free workshops and live lessons support teacher learning.
- Teachers can pick resources by age band.

## Benefits for schools
- Resources are described as free on the home page.
- Most units need little equipment and no special devices.
- Some resources use Scratch or micro:bit. Not all are paper only.

## Where is the AI?
The AI content is taught through unplugged lessons. Barefoot AI Explorers teach how AI works and build critical AI literacy. No AI tool runs in the lessons. Students build skills in data and pattern spotting, which sit at the heart of AI.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need. The home page mentions a Barefoot account. Search results say registering gives free access to resources, but the research did not confirm whether it is required to download. Pupil work happens on paper in most units. A teacher should check any Scratch or micro:bit resource for its own account rules. A teacher should also check UK focused language for fit with the class.

3. Write step-by-step instructions for a classmate to follow exactly.
