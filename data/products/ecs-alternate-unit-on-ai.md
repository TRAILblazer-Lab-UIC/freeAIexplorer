## Overview
This alternate AI unit for the Exploring Computer Science course was written by Beverly Clarke. It replaces Unit 5 or Unit 6 and has daily lesson plans. It covers AI terms such as machine learning and deep learning, social and ethical impacts, and smart city, home, and school designs. Students build, train, and test an AI system on an NVIDIA platform.

## Benefits for students
- Students build, train, and test a working AI system.
- Students learn terms such as machine learning and deep learning.
- Students design smart city, home, and school ideas and discuss ethical impacts.

## Benefits for teachers
- Daily lesson plans, handouts, rubrics, and a teacher PowerPoint are included.
- Videos support the unplugged activity on days 5 to 7.
- The unit needs a teacher with strong computing skills.

## Benefits for schools
- The files are offered for download with no fee stated.
- The page does not name the NVIDIA hardware. The cost is unknown.
- The unit is listed at 20 weeks, and IT will likely help with setup.

## Where is the AI?
The unit teaches machine learning, deep learning, and AI ethics. Students build, train, and test an AI system on an NVIDIA platform. The AI runs on that local hardware. Students collect examples, train the system, and test how well it works.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Define machine learning and deep learning in your own words and give one example of each.
2. Build and train a small AI system on the NVIDIA platform.
3. Test the system with new examples and record how often it is right.
4. Join the unplugged activity and explain how it models a neural network.
5. Design a smart school feature and list the data it would need.
