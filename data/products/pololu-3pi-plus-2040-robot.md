## Overview
The 3pi+ 2040 is a palm-sized robot built on the Raspberry Pi RP2040 microcontroller. It has dual encoders, five reflectance sensors, bump sensors, an IMU, a 128x64 OLED display, a buzzer and RGB LEDs. It ships with MicroPython preloaded and example programs. The listed version is a kit that needs soldering.

## Benefits for students
- Students solder and build their own robot.
- Students program in MicroPython with a plain text editor.
- Students use line sensors, bump sensors and an OLED display.

## Benefits for teachers
- Example programs come preloaded.
- The robot also works with C, C++ and Arduino.
- The user guide says ChromeOS is supported per a search snippet.

## Benefits for schools
- Pololu lists 179.95 USD for one kit, 165.55 USD each for five and 152.31 USD each for 25.
- Four AAA batteries and a USB C cable are needed.
- No internet is needed to run it.

## Where is the AI?
The robot has no AI. It builds skills in sensing, logic and data. Students use the line sensors and the IMU to guide movement.

## Privacy and safety
The robot works on the device. No account or internet is needed. The materials document no student data collection. A teacher should plan soldering safety, including ventilation and eye protection.
