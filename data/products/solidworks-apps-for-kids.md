## Overview
SOLIDWORKS Apps for Kids is a free web-based set of 3D design apps and lessons. It is made for teachers, parents, and mentors. A Classroom version lets an educator manage student accounts and share content. Students design 3D objects in a browser.

## Benefits for students
- Students design 3D objects in a browser.
- Students use BlocksCAD lessons that mix block coding and design.
- Students practice radius and height in a cylinder for a watch design.

## Benefits for teachers
- Classroom lets a teacher create a class and add students.
- Lessons line up with math and computer science standards.
- Most coverage is from the vendor, with no independent review found.

## Benefits for schools
- The apps are free.
- They run in a browser and need the internet.
- A teacher can manage students in one place.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the SOLIDWORKS Apps for Kids site in a browser.
- [ ] Try one app yourself.
- [ ] Create a Classroom, invite other teachers, and add students.
- [ ] Ask the vendor whether students need accounts for the plain apps.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the site.
- [ ] Try a STEM or STEAM lesson with BlocksCAD.

## Where is the AI?
The apps have no AI. They are 3D design tools. Students build skills in spatial thinking and in using numbers to shape a model.

## Privacy and safety
The apps run in a browser and need the internet at all times. A Classroom version lets an educator manage student accounts. The materials do not state whether accounts are needed for the plain apps or what data is collected. The research reviewed no privacy policy, and the policy page it tried returned an error. A teacher should read the privacy terms and check the account rules for each age.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Design a simple object in a 3D app and rotate it to check each side.
2. Build a watch case and change its size.
3. Redesign a model to fit a given space.
4. Share a model with a partner and ask for one improvement.
5. Present a finished design and explain the choices.

### math
1. Enter a radius and a height to build a cylinder, as in the watch design lesson.
2. Change the radius and describe how the shape changes.
3. Use BlocksCAD blocks to build a shape with repeated parts.
4. Predict a result before building it and compare.
5. Record the numbers for a model in a table.
