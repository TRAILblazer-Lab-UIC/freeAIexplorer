## Overview
Coding with Bee-Bot is a kindergarten course in the CodeHS CodeHop line. It has 4 units and 14 lessons covering Bee-Bot basics, math and science. Students program floor robots with the buttons on the robot's back and use grid mats. Total time is about 8 hours and no prior coding experience is needed.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a floor robot with buttons.
- Students practice counting, patterns, measurement, shapes and making ten.
- Students explore seasons, weather, living things, and pushes and pulls.

## Benefits for teachers
- Every student lesson includes Spanish slides.
- The course includes a Park Cleanup STEM project.
- Lesson plans and slides come from CodeHS.

## Benefits for schools
- No price is listed. The page directs schools to contact CodeHS.
- Bee-Bot robots are bought separately.
- The lessons are physical robot activities.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in this course. Bee-Bot follows the steps that children enter with its buttons. Students build skills in sequencing and debugging.

## Privacy and safety
The course page does not state whether students log in, and students program the robot with buttons. The CodeHS platform policy lists name, email, coursework, saved code, and technical data. Schools may withhold names and emails or use anonymous IDs. The policy says data on users under 13 is collected only with parental consent obtained by the school, district, or teacher. It says data is hosted on AWS and deleted about 4 years after last login. A teacher should ask CodeHS what applies to this course.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Program Bee-Bot to move along a short path on the grid mat.
2. Predict where Bee-Bot ends and then test the guess.
3. Fix a route that misses the target and explain the change.
4. Plan the Park Cleanup route with arrows on paper.
5. Teach a partner how to clear and press GO.

### math
1. Count the squares Bee-Bot crosses on a route.
2. Move Bee-Bot to make a pattern on the mat.
3. Measure a path in steps and compare two paths.
4. Drive Bee-Bot along the sides of a shape.
5. Use number cards to find pairs that make ten.

### science
1. Drive Bee-Bot through the seasons on a mat and name each one.
2. Move Bee-Bot to weather cards and describe the weather.
3. Sort living and nonliving things on the mat with the robot.
4. Show a push and a pull by moving a small object with the robot path.
5. Plan a Park Cleanup route that collects litter in order.
