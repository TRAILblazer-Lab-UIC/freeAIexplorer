## Overview
Defold is a free game engine for building 2D and 3D games, with game logic in Lua. The site says there are no licensing costs, royalties, or runtime fees. It publishes to desktop, mobile, HTML5, and console targets. Schools can learn it through the project's tutorials and manuals or through paid Craig n Dave school tutorials.

## Benefits for students
- Students write real Lua code for a real game engine.
- Students can publish a game to the web with HTML5.
- Students follow tutorials and manuals.

## Benefits for teachers
- Craig n Dave suggests it for year 12 students as an alternative to Pygame, per a search snippet.
- The editor is available in English, Russian, Chinese, and Portuguese.
- Free tutorials and manuals are available.

## Benefits for schools
- The engine is free.
- Craig n Dave tutorials need a paid membership and do not work with Google Classroom or SharePoint.
- It installs on Windows, Mac, and Linux, and Chromebook support is not confirmed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the editor from defold.com.
- [ ] Ask IT to install it on school computers.
- [ ] Follow a tutorial to build a first game.
- [ ] Read the manuals for Lua scripting.
- [ ] Look at the Craig n Dave courses if the school wants a membership.
- [ ] Test an HTML5 export in a browser.
- [ ] Plan which game idea fits your class.

## Where is the AI?
This tool has no AI. It builds skills in logic, sequencing, and events. Students write Lua code that tells game objects what to do.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The editor runs on the device, and the internet is needed for setup. The homepage does not mention telemetry, and the research did not confirm it. No privacy policy was reviewed. Games students export to HTML5 or other platforms are distributed by the student and sit outside the editor. A teacher should check any tutorial membership terms before students use them.
