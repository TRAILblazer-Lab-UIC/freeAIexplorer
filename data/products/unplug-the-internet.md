## Overview
Unplug the Internet is a Nifty Assignments 2021 classroom simulation. Students act as the DNS, routers or clients to send paper messages through a classroom network. Once the network works, the facilitator adds malicious servers or network failures. Students study attacks such as man-in-the-middle, DNS spoofing, counterfeit packets and DDoS. It targets middle or high school.

## Benefits for students
- Students see how a message crosses a network.
- Students learn how attacks work by acting them out.
- Students talk about internet layers.

## Benefits for teachers
- The activity can run in one 80 minute period or over several days.
- The package includes facilitator directions and printable sheets.
- Advanced students can lead the activity for younger groups.

## Benefits for schools
- The activity is free and needs only printing and cutting.
- It uses no devices.
- The research found no student data collection.

## Where is the AI?
This activity has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is understanding how data travels. Students see how messages move across a network before any online tool uses them.

## Privacy and safety
The activity runs on paper and the research found no student data collection. No accounts are needed. The materials are in a linked Google Doc. Downloading them uses the internet and Google. Remind students that the attacks are classroom role play. Discuss ethical use of network knowledge.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Act as a router and pass a paper packet to the next student.
2. Play the DNS and look up the address for a client.
3. Watch a counterfeit packet enter the network and name the problem.
4. Describe how a network failure changes the path of a message.
5. Suggest a defense for one of the attacks and test it in a second round.
