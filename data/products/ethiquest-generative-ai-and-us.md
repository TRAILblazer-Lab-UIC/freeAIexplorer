## Overview
EthiQuest is a card based game about generative AI. Students make decisions in fictional AI scenarios. Each round raises dilemmas about fairness, authenticity, and responsibility. The page offers a facilitator guide, a printable card deck, and a textbook page. The materials use a CC BY-NC 4.0 International license.

## Where is the AI?
The game has no running AI program. It builds skills in ethical thinking about generative AI. Students decide what to do in fictional scenarios about fairness, authenticity, and responsibility. They practice reasoning about AI use before they meet real choices of their own.

## Privacy and safety
The game runs on paper and the page describes no data collection. The materials are downloaded and printed. The internet is used for the download. ## Ideas by subject
### social_studies
1. Play a round of the card game and explain the choice your group made about fairness.
2. Compare two groups that made different choices in the same scenario.
3. Name who is helped and who could be harmed in one scenario.
4. Write a class rule about responsible AI use from the game discussion.
5. Invent a new scenario card about AI in a community.

### ela
1. Discuss what makes a piece of writing authentic when AI helped to make it.
2. Write a short argument for one choice in a scenario.
3. Rewrite a scenario from the point of view of a different character.
4. Debate whether students should say when AI helped with their work.
5. Write a statement that explains how you would credit AI in a school project.
