## Overview
This is a self-paced online course on building and programming a fleet of light show drones around the Drone Dojo PiHawk kit. A 2024 review says the course teaches Skybrush software. Students solder and 3D print. The review calls it advanced and suited to a small motivated group such as an after-school club.

## Benefits for students
- Students build real drones with soldering and 3D printing.
- Students learn show software called Skybrush.
- Students plan and fly a fleet together.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course is self-paced with about 6 hours of video per a 2024 review.
- It suits a small club.
- Lessons cover the PiHawk build and fleet flying.

## Benefits for schools
- A June 2025 review lists course access at 27 USD per month through the Blue Belt membership.
- The kit costs about 1000 USD per drone. A 10-drone show needs about 10000 USD of gear.
- Both sources come from one outlet and the prices are dated.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Drone Dojo course and the Blue Belt membership.
- [ ] Check the current price of the membership.
- [ ] Ask IT and the club sponsor to review the cost and space.
- [ ] Buy PiHawk kits and extras such as RTK GPS.
- [ ] Start with one drone before buying a fleet.
- [ ] Build, solder and program the first drone.
- [ ] Ask Drone Dojo what student data the membership collects.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in this course. Students build drones and program show flights with Skybrush. A teacher should ask the vendor whether any AI tools are involved.

## Privacy and safety
The review does not mention login or data handling. The course sits behind a paid membership, which suggests an account, but the materials do not state this. The materials do not say which accounts students need. A teacher should check what data the membership collects and set clear rules for soldering and flight.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Build one PiHawk drone and record each step.
2. Solder a part and test the connection.
3. Print a part on a 3D printer and fit it.
4. Test the RTK GPS and note how it changes accuracy.
5. Plan a safe flight area for a small fleet.

### computer_science
1. Design a light show path in Skybrush.
2. Program a short routine for one drone.
3. Sync two drones to the same timing.
4. Change a color cue and predict the show.
5. Debug a show that drifts off its path.
