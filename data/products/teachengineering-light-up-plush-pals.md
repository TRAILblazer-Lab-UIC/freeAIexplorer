## Overview
This hands-on e-textile activity for grades 4 to 6 is by Angela Sheehan and Morgan Ulrich. Each student sews a felt plush toy with three LEDs, a button, and a slide switch. They use LilyPad ProtoSnap parts and conductive thread. The activity covers open and closed circuits, energy forms, and switch choices. It takes about 2 hours 30 minutes and can be split across days.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a working circuit they can hold and show off.
- Students compare a slide switch and a button board.
- Students present the plush and explain their switch choices.

## Where is the AI?
This activity has no AI. Students sew and test a circuit that has no computer in it. They build skills in logic and sequence by tracing how current flows through the switch.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Trace the path of a circuit on the template and mark where it is open or closed.
2. Test the plush with the switch off and on and describe what happens.
3. Name the forms of energy that you see in the plush.
4. Predict what happens if a thread connection comes loose, then test it.
5. Explain to a partner how your circuit works.

### engineering
1. Compare a slide switch and a button board and choose one for your plush.
2. Sew the three LEDs and test each connection.
3. Fix a circuit that does not light up.
4. Redesign the layout of the thread to avoid crossing lines.
5. Present your plush and explain your switch choice.
