## Overview
TETRIX is a metal robotics building system from Pitsco Education. The Pitsco collection lists 98 products with heavy-duty aluminum elements and drive motors. The FTC Competition Set is a FIRST-approved kit for FIRST Tech Challenge teams, according to a Pitsco page seen in search results. Pitsco also sells curriculum packs, including PRIME Engineering for grades 6 to 8 and PRIZM Coding Essentials for grades 9 to 12.

## Benefits for students
- Students build strong robots from aluminum parts.
- Students can use drive motors and spare parts in the same system.
- Students can take part in FIRST Tech Challenge.

## Benefits for teachers
- The kit is approved for FIRST Tech Challenge teams.
- Separate curriculum packs are available for grades 6 to 8 and 9 to 12.
- The curriculum packs are general classroom products and are not FTC specific.

## Benefits for schools
