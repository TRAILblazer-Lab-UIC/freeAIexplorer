## Overview
The DAILy curriculum was created by Irene Lee of MIT for middle school students. Topics include making predictions with machine learning, investigating bias, making art with GANs, recognizing everyday AI, and AI ethics. Activities are hands-on and done on computers. The curriculum pages were not reachable during research. Many details are unconfirmed.

## Benefits for students
- Students train and use machine learning models.
- Students investigate bias in AI systems.
- Students make art with GANs and talk about AI ethics.

## Benefits for teachers
- A published research study followed 89 middle school students taught by first-year teachers who had AI literacy training.
- The topics cover technical ideas, ethics, and careers.
- The vendor does not say how much training teachers need.

## Benefits for schools
- The vendor does not state a price.
- The vendor does not state which devices or accounts are needed.
- A teacher may need to set up the tools.

## Where is the AI?
The curriculum teaches machine learning, GANs, bias, and ethics. The vendor does not say where the AI tools run. Students train models and make GAN art. They then use what they see to talk about how data shapes results.

## Privacy and safety
The curriculum pages were not reachable during research. The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. They do not say where student data goes or where the AI runs. No privacy information was found. A teacher should check each tool the activities use and read its privacy terms before students sign in.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Train a simple model to make a prediction and test it on new examples.
2. Change the training data and describe how the predictions change.
3. Investigate a bias example and suggest how the data could be improved.
4. List three AI systems you meet each day and explain what each one predicts.
5. Debate one ethics question about AI and write a short position.

### visual_arts
1. Make an image with a GAN and describe the steps.
2. Compare a GAN image with a hand drawing of the same subject.
3. Change the settings and record how the art changes.
4. Discuss who should get credit for AI made art.
5. Display the class art with a label that explains how each piece was made.
