## Overview
Goxel is a free open-source 3D voxel editor for Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android. It has layers and no scene size limit. It exports to MagicaVoxel, Qubicle, glTF2, OBJ, PLY, and Build engine formats. One reviewer called it simple enough for a five-year-old. It suits upper elementary and middle school students.

## Benefits for students
- Students build 3D models from small blocks like pixel art.
- Students use layers to organize a model.
- Students can export models for other tools.

## Benefits for teachers
- The tool is simple to teach.
- A roundup lists it in a kids design-app collection.
- Exports to OBJ and glTF2 connect to other 3D projects.

## Benefits for schools
- The desktop version is free. Mobile app pricing was not checked.
- It runs offline after install.
- IT will likely need to approve the install.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download Goxel from goxel.xyz.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Open the app and place a few blocks.
- [ ] Try layers to separate parts of a model.
- [ ] Export a model as an OBJ or glTF2 file.
- [ ] Decide where students save files.
- [ ] Check mobile prices before using the iOS or Android version.

## Where is the AI?
Goxel has no AI. It builds spatial thinking skills. Students plan a shape in three dimensions and build it block by block. It runs on the device.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the internet is used only for setup. Goxel is a local app and models stay on the device. The research did not read a privacy policy. The vendor materials describe no student data collection. A teacher should set rules for model content and file names.

## Ideas by subject
### visual_arts
1. Build a small animal from colored blocks.
2. Compare a flat pixel drawing with a 3D voxel version of it.
3. Use layers to make a character with removable parts.
4. Design a simple scene such as a room or a garden.
5. Choose a color palette and use it across a model.

2. Export a model as OBJ and open it in another program.

4. Measure a model in blocks and write its dimensions.
5. Improve a first model after feedback from a partner.
