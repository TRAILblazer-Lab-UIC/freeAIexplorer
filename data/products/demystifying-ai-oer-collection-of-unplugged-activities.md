## Overview
Demystifying AI is an open educational resource of unplugged AI activities. It is described in an article by Annabel Lindner, Michaela Muller-Unterweger, and Marc Berges in Hello World issue 28. The article appeared in 2025 on pages 70 to 73. The research team could not link the article to the collection with certainty.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn AI ideas through paper activities.
- Students build AI literacy without a device.
- Students talk with classmates about how AI works.

## Benefits for teachers
- The collection is described as an open educational resource.
- The activities need little setup.
- Teachers can find the collection through a free magazine article.

## Benefits for schools
- The materials are free to read, and Hello World is free.
- Paper activities need no devices.
- The materials describe no student data collection.

## Where is the AI?
The collection has no running AI program. It teaches AI literacy through unplugged activities. Possible activities from the related aiunplugged.org site include monkey cards, image cards, and a Turing test style game. Students build skills in data and pattern spotting.

## Privacy and safety
The activities run on paper, and the materials describe no student data collection. The research could not confirm that the aiunplugged.org page is the collection from the article. That site offers print PDFs and online versions of two board games, and no account or privacy statement was seen there. The data handling of the online games is unconfirmed. A teacher should confirm the true source of the collection and read its terms before use.
