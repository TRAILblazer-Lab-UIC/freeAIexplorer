## Overview
This is a YouTube video series from the MIT Media Lab Personal Robots group for middle school students. It launched with two videos called What is AI and How is AI used in your job. Short interviews with 14 professionals show AI in design, space technology, law, soccer, and art. Each video has discussion questions. A high school intern created it.

## Benefits for students
- Students hear real workers describe AI in their jobs.
- Students see AI in many fields, from art to law.
- Discussion questions guide their thinking.

## Benefits for teachers
- The videos suit whole class viewing and discussion.
- They can also serve as homework reflection.
- Discussion questions come with each video.

## Benefits for schools
- The series is free.
- It needs only a browser and a school that allows YouTube.
- No software install is needed.

## Where is the AI?
The series has no AI tool that students use. It teaches what AI is and how workers use it. Students build the skill of spotting where AI is used in a job, and they then talk about it.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is always needed. The videos are hosted on YouTube. YouTube may log viewing, and the research did not review its data practices. The series itself collects no student data. A teacher should check the school's YouTube settings and consider showing the videos from the front of the room.

## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Watch an interview and list the tasks in the job that use AI.
2. Interview a family member about how technology changes their job.
3. Pick one of the 14 careers and write a short description of a typical day.
4. Discuss which jobs may change most as AI grows.
5. Make a poster on a career that interests you and the skills it needs.

### computer_science
1. Define AI in your own words after watching the first video.
2. List examples of AI from the videos and sort them by field.
3. Write three questions you would ask an AI professional.
4. Discuss one way AI helped a person in the videos.
5. Make a class chart of where AI appears in daily life.
