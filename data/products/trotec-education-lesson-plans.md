## Overview
Trotec offers free lesson plans built around laser cutting. The page lists Entrepreneurship, Esports, Architecture, and STEM plans. Entrepreneurship, Esports, and STEM are listed for grades 6 to 12, and Architecture for K-6 and 9-12. Students make items such as custom merchandise, esport themed pieces, and architectural models.

## Benefits for students
- Students make real objects with a laser cutter.
- Students design products for a business idea.
- Students build architectural models.

## Benefits for teachers
- The plans are described as free.
- Four plan topics cover different subjects.
- A search snippet says the plans map to ISTE and CTE pathways, which was not verified.

## Benefits for schools
- A laser cutter is needed and the page gives no machine price.
- The company sells laser machines to schools.
- The page does not name a required machine and mentions the E340 in a closing section.

## Where is the AI?
The plans describe no AI. Students design a piece and then cut it with a laser machine. They practice planning and precision.

## Privacy and safety
Three plans ask the teacher to sign up before download. The page does not say how sign up data is used and links only a general Privacy page. The research did not download the plans. The materials do not state what data is collected or whether the internet is needed. Vendor lead capture is likely but not stated. Read the privacy policy before signing up. Follow the laser cutter's safety rules.

## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Plan a small business that sells laser cut merchandise.
2. Design a product and price it.
3. Make a sample and gather feedback from classmates.
4. Present a pitch for the product.
5. Track costs for materials and time.

### engineering
1. Draw a design for a laser cut part and check its size.
2. Test two materials and pick the better one.
3. Make a STEM project from a plan.
4. Improve a design after the first cut.
5. Explain each step of the process.

### visual_arts
1. Design an architectural model for a place you like.
2. Make an esports themed piece for a school team.
3. Choose a color and material for a design.
4. Display your work and write about it.
5. Compare two versions of a model.
