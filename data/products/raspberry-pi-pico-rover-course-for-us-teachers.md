## Overview
This is a free online course for teachers of grades 4 to 8 on the Pathfinders Online Institute platform. Teachers learn basic circuits and code a Raspberry Pi Pico to run motors and LEDs. They design algorithms that steer a rover through a maze. The 2021 announcement offered a free kit to the first 1,000 US public or charter school teachers.

## Benefits for students
- Students of teachers who take the course get a hands-on robotics unit.
- Students can build circuits and code a rover.
- Students solve a maze task with an algorithm.

## Benefits for teachers
- The platform is described as free.
- Resources cover programming, system design and prototyping.
- Teachers build the rover themselves before teaching.

## Benefits for schools
- The kit offer ran in 2021 and current availability is unknown.
- Other teachers can buy the kit from PiShop.us or gather parts.
- IT may need to help with setup.

## Where is the AI?
The course has no AI. It builds skills in text coding, sequencing and algorithm design. Teachers write steps that steer a rover.

## Privacy and safety
A Pathfinders account is needed and the platform runs in a vendor cloud. The materials do not state what data is collected. A teacher should read the Pathfinders privacy terms. Check them before asking colleagues or students to sign up.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Code the Pico to turn an LED on and off.
2. Write an algorithm that steers the rover through a maze.
3. Test the algorithm and write down each failure.
4. Change one number and describe the new rover path.
5. Explain the algorithm to a partner.

### engineering
1. Build a basic circuit and test a motor.
2. Prototype a rover and record what worked.
3. Design a maze with turns and test the rover on it.
4. Improve the rover after a failed run.
5. Present the system design to the class.
