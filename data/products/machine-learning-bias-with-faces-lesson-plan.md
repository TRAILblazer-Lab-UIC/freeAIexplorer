## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Draw happy and sad faces and train a classifier on them.
2. Test the classifier on a face with a different style and record the result.
3. Remove half the training faces and see how accuracy changes.
4. Read a confusion matrix and explain what each cell means.
5. Write a short note on how to make the training data fairer.

### science
1. Collect data from every student's drawings and look for patterns.
2. Form a hypothesis about what makes the classifier fail.
3. Test the hypothesis with a new set of drawings.
4. Graph the accuracy before and after adding more varied examples.
5. Discuss how human bias enters a data set.
