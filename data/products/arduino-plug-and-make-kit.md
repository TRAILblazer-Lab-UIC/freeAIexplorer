## Overview
The Plug and Make Kit has an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi, seven Modulino nodes and a Modulino Base. The nodes are Knob, Pixels, Distance, Movement, Buzzer, Thermo and Buttons. It includes seven guided projects such as Weather Report, Hourglass and Game Controller. No soldering is needed.

## Benefits for students
- Students snap sensors together with Qwiic cables.
- Students follow seven guided projects.
- Students program a real WiFi board.

## Benefits for teachers
- No soldering is needed.
- The guided projects give a clear path.
- The store page recommends users be 14 or older.

## Benefits for schools
- The kit was 76.13 EUR with VAT on the store page.
- A free Arduino account is required.
- Arduino Cloud features need a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, which some school networks restrict.

## Where is the AI?
The kit has no AI. Students build skills in sensors, logic and sequencing. They combine a knob, a distance sensor and lights to make a working project.

## Privacy and safety
A free Arduino account is required to reach content and Arduino Cloud features. The store page recommends age 14 or older. Under 14 users need a Junior Account with parental consent under Arduino policy. The policy lists sharing with processors, Qualcomm Group companies, and advertising partners. Data goes to both the device and the vendor cloud. A teacher should read the privacy policy and check the age rules before students sign up.
