## Overview
This workshop teaches e-safety through unplugged activities and introduces iDEA Award badges. It was developed by Estelle Ashman at the Gildredge House School Digital Schoolhouse. Pupils start by building digital footprints from the logos of internet services they use. The workshop pack sits behind a login on the Digital Schoolhouse site. ## Ideas by subject
### library_media
1. Place the logos of services you use on a footprint map.
2. Count how many services hold some of your information.
3. Discuss what is safe to share with each service.
4. Answer the quiz on how careful you are about what you share.
5. Write three tips for staying safe online.

### computer_science
1. Sort the services by the type of data they collect.
2. Describe how a service might use that data.
3. Look at the iDEA Award badges and choose one to try.
4. Explain what a digital footprint is to a partner.
5. Create a short checklist for a safe account.
