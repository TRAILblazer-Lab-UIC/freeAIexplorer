## Overview
The Powder Toy is a free physics sandbox. Users build with materials that simulate heat, gravity, and air pressure. Wikipedia counts 258 materials with custom behaviors. It includes a Lua API for scripting. Users can optionally register to save and share creations online.

## Benefits for students
- Students experiment freely with heat, gravity, and pressure.
- Students watch materials react in real time.
- Students can try Lua scripting.

## Benefits for teachers
- It is easy for a teacher to learn and demonstrate.
- A TV Tropes entry notes the chemistry is loose. It may not suit accurate chemistry teaching.
- Local use needs no account.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- It runs on a standard computer with Windows, Mac, Linux, or Android.
- Online accounts may be blocked by school filters.

## Where is the AI?
The Powder Toy has no AI. It runs fixed rules for each material. Students practice observing patterns and testing ideas through simulation.

## Privacy and safety
Local use needs no account and the internet is used at setup. With an account, email and date of birth may be submitted. Technical data such as an IP address is logged. Cookies are required to log in. The policy says content a user submits may be used by third parties and it does not state a minimum age. Online community content is user generated and may be unmoderated. Keep students off online accounts and review community content before class.

### science
1. Build a small model that shows heat moving through a material.
2. Compare how two materials fall under gravity.
3. Test how air pressure changes a structure.
4. Write a prediction before each run and record the result.
5. Explain how the simulation is like and unlike real physics.
