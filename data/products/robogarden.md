## Overview
RoboGarden is an online digital-skills platform with gamified coding lessons. Its offerings include a K-12 Virtual Academy, a Schools program, a Makers program for children, and adult bootcamps. The Juniors program lists Game Development, App Development, and Artificial Intelligence I and II. Course detail for the AI content was not on the pages read.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn coding through game-style lessons.
- Students can take Artificial Intelligence courses in the Juniors program.
- Older learners can join a Machine Learning and AI bootcamp.

## Benefits for teachers
- The site advertises hands-on lessons.
- A Schools program exists for classes.
- No lesson pages were reviewed. Teachers should preview the courses.

## Benefits for schools
- Prices are not listed on the site.
- Paid school plans were suggested but not confirmed.
- Student data lives on AWS.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the RoboGarden site and read the Schools page.
- [ ] Contact the vendor for pricing and onboarding details.
- [ ] Ask for a preview of the Artificial Intelligence I course.
- [ ] Ask IT to review the privacy policy.
- [ ] Check the age rules. The minimum age is 13.
- [ ] Create student accounts through the Schools offering.
- [ ] Test one lesson on a student device.

## Where is the AI?
The site lists machine learning instruction in its AI courses. The vendor does not say where any AI runs. Students would learn about machine learning through lessons. The details were not available on the pages read.

## Privacy and safety
Student accounts are required, and the internet is needed at all times. Parents, guardians, or schools create the accounts, and children under 13 cannot create their own. The site stores a child name, an email, school affiliation, an optional picture, and responses and interactions. This data lives on Amazon Web Services servers and no region is stated. Service providers may use data for analytics, billing, and marketing. The policy does not mention COPPA by name. The research did not confirm whether the AI courses use third party AI services. A teacher should read the privacy policy before signing up.
