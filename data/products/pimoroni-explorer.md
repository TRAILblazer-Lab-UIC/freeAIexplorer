## Overview
Pimoroni Explorer is a fully assembled board built around a Raspberry Pi RP2350B chip. It has a 2.8 inch color display, six buttons, a mini breadboard, four servo outputs, and a piezo speaker. No soldering is needed. It programs over USB-C with MicroPython or C/C++.

## Benefits for students
- Students build and test circuits on a board that is ready to use.
- Students program a screen, buttons, and sound.
- Students can add a Starter Kit with a sensor stick, LEDs, servos, and wheels.

## Benefits for teachers
- No soldering is needed.
- MicroPython firmware and examples are available.
- CNX Software describes the documentation as limited. Plan time to learn it.

## Benefits for schools
- CNX Software reported a pre-order price of about 44 USD and a starter kit at about 75 USD. Check the current shop price.
- The board needs only a computer with USB.
- Student work stays on the device.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in the Explorer. The board runs code that students write. They build skills in sensors, logic, and sequencing.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the board works with no internet. The product page lists no wireless connectivity. Programs load over USB from a local computer. Student programs stay on the board and the computer. A teacher should check the hardware for loose parts before younger students use it.
