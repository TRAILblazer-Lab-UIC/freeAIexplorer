## Overview
The DJI AI Module is an add-on for RoboMaster robots, designed for AI classes at primary and secondary level. Students build datasets and train models on the AI Training Platform in DJI Education Hub. They then download the models to the module to run recognition offline. DJI says it pairs with the RoboMaster EP vision system for tasks such as automatic targeting and pallet sorting.

## Benefits for students
- Students build their own datasets and see how training data shapes a model.
- Students train an image model and then run it on a real robot.
- Students watch the robot act on what the model recognizes.

## Benefits for teachers
- The platform uses a graphical interface. Teachers do not need heavy coding.
- The module pairs with the RoboMaster EP through the universal expansion module.
- The full cycle of data, training, and testing fits one unit of study.

## Benefits for schools
- The module price was not found.
- Training needs a paid Advanced or Professional license. The one-month free trial only shows examples.
- The robot platforms it works with may be discontinued. The module does not import other open-source training tools or other neural network models.

## Getting started
- [ ] Check that the school owns a RoboMaster robot with the universal expansion module.
- [ ] Register an account on DJI Education Hub.
- [ ] Obtain a license for the AI Training Platform.
- [ ] Ask IT to review the account and data terms before students sign in.
- [ ] Collect images for a small dataset.
- [ ] Train a model online.
- [ ] Download the model to the module and test it offline.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is an image recognition model. Training runs in DJI's cloud on the AI Training Platform. The finished model runs on the module itself without internet. Students build the dataset, choose what to recognize, and test how well the model works on the robot.

## Privacy and safety
An account is needed, and the internet is needed for training. Training happens on the DJI Education Hub, and uploaded student images go to DJI's servers. Trained models run offline on the module. The research did not retrieve a DJI privacy policy. Retention and sharing are not confirmed. The materials do not state the minimum age. Sales of DJI education products were reportedly suspended. A teacher should read the terms and check what images students upload.
