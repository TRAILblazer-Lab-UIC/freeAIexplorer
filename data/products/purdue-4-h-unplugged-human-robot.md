## Overview
This is a 45 minute activity for grades K-3 about giving and following step-by-step instructions. Pairs take turns as the robot and the programmer. The robot must follow instructions exactly without improvising. The lesson links the activity to algorithms and to how AI systems depend on clear instructions.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn that a robot needs exact instructions.
- Students practice speaking and listening in clear steps.
- Students move around while they learn.

## Benefits for teachers
- The activity needs no equipment.
- Discussion questions connect the game to AI.
- A companion worksheet supports writing or drawing directions.

## Benefits for schools
- The activity is free.
- It needs only an open space.
- It lists NGSS K-2-ETS1-1 and K-2-ETS1-2.

## Where is the AI?
The activity has no AI and uses no device. It builds a skill that supports AI learning, which is giving precise instructions. The lesson discussion connects algorithms to how AI systems depend on clear instructions. Alexa Groff of Iowa State University is credited as an author.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and no student data is collected. The activity runs on paper and in an open space. A teacher should check the space for safe movement. A teacher should also remind students to treat the robot partner kindly.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Take turns as robot and programmer and give exact steps to reach a target across the room.
2. Draw directions on the worksheet and test them with a partner.
3. Find a step that confused the robot and rewrite it more clearly.
4. Build an obstacle course and write instructions to cross it.
5. Discuss why an AI system needs clear instructions.
