## Overview
Arduino Pro sells this bundle for building edge AI and machine learning applications. The page features a Portenta H7, a Portenta Vision Shield, a Nicla Vision, Arduino Cloud for Business, and a Speech Recognition Engine. The page does not itemize what the bundle contains. Arduino describes it as a professional product and does not design it for K-12.

## Benefits for students
- Students can train image classification models with Edge Impulse.
- The speech engine handles voice commands in 40 languages with no internet and no vocal training.
- Students work with industry-style tools and see how edge AI is built.

## Benefits for teachers
- The boards are programmed in Arduino C++ or in MicroPython with the OpenMV IDE.
- The Vision Shield adds a low-power camera, two microphones, and microSD storage.
- Teachers need strong technical skills. The product is built for industry.

## Benefits for schools
- The page lists no price. Purchase goes through a bundle form.
- Models run on the device. Edge Impulse training happens in the cloud.
- Setup takes days and a teacher will likely need IT help.

## Getting started
- [ ] Request the bundle through the Arduino Pro bundle form.
- [ ] Ask the vendor to list exactly what the bundle contains.
- [ ] Install the Arduino IDE or the OpenMV IDE.
- [ ] Choose the Vision Shield version, Ethernet or LoRa, that fits your plan.
- [ ] Connect the Portenta H7 and run a camera example.
- [ ] Train a small image model in Edge Impulse.
- [ ] Test the speech engine with a few voice commands.

## Where is the AI?
The AI runs on the device. The bundle supports image classification, speech recognition, and gesture detection. The camera is low resolution at about 320 by 320. Students train a vision model in Edge Impulse, load it on the board, and test the results with their own objects and commands.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not say whether accounts are needed. The speech engine is stated to need no internet connection. Edge Impulse training and Arduino Cloud for Business are cloud services whose policies the research did not read. The Arduino website policy says it does not knowingly sell or share data of anyone under 18. A teacher should check the Edge Impulse terms before students upload images. A teacher should also keep the camera pointed away from faces and private spaces.
