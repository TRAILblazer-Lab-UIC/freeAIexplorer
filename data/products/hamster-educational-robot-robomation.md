## Overview
Hamster is a small desktop robot for learning robot programming. A Korean-language teacher e-book from Robomation walks students through a 40 minute line-following lesson with the Entry coding environment. The lesson uses the robot's floor and brightness sensors, a Bluetooth dongle and a PC. Search results say it also works with Scratch, Python and JavaScript. US availability is unconfirmed.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a robot that follows a line.
- Students use floor and brightness sensors.
- Students use block coding in Entry.

## Benefits for teachers
- The e-book gives a 40 minute lesson plan.
- An English site exists at hamster.school.
- The materials rate the teacher learning curve as easy.

## Benefits for schools
- The site lists no price.
- The robot needs a PC and a Bluetooth dongle.
- Most materials are in Korean.

## Getting started
- [ ] Visit hamster.school for the English materials.
- [ ] Confirm that the robot can be bought in your country.
- [ ] Ask IT to install the robot coding program on a Windows PC.
- [ ] Charge the robot and power it on.
- [ ] Plug in the USB Bluetooth dongle.
- [ ] Open the robot coding program and choose Entry offline.
- [ ] Try the line-following lesson on a taped course.

## Where is the AI?
The Hamster robot has no AI. It follows the code students write and reads its floor sensors. Students build skills in sensors and logic by deciding what the robot does on a dark line.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not say whether an account is needed or whether the internet is used. They do not say what data is collected, and the research did not review a privacy policy. The e-book lesson uses the offline Entry program with a Bluetooth dongle on a PC. A teacher should check these points before class. A teacher should also use the offline Entry option that the e-book describes.
