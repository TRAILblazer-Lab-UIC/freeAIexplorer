## Overview
This Bootstrap data literacy course uses the CODAP web tool for analysis. The course page lists about two dozen lessons and seven projects. Students move through the data cycle, bar charts, dot plots, histograms, box plots, scatter plots, correlation, linear regression, and data ethics. Projects include Snack Habits, Time Use, Design a Survey, and a Research Capstone.

## Benefits for students
- Students work with data they care about, such as snacks and time use.
- Students learn to read many kinds of graphs.
- Students study ethics, privacy, and bias in data.

## Benefits for teachers
- A student workbook and teacher lessons are ready to use.
- Answer keys are available through a password request form.
- The course can run as a semester elective or as individual lessons.

## Benefits for schools
- The materials are free and were developed with National Science Foundation support.
- Students work in CODAP in a web browser, and Chromebooks are listed.
- No account is needed.

## Where is the AI?
The course has no AI. It builds data skills, as students collect data, graph it, and interpret it. It also builds pattern spotting through scatter plots and regression.

## Privacy and safety
No student accounts are mentioned, and the internet is needed throughout. The research did not check how CODAP handles data. Some lessons use Google Forms and Google Sheets for class surveys, which sends responses to Google. Teacher keys and research tests need a password requested through a form. A teacher should check which lessons use Google tools and whether the school allows them.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Read a scatter plot from class survey data and draw a line of best fit, as in the Linear Regression lesson.
2. Make a histogram of the class's time use and describe its shape.
3. Compare a dot plot and a box plot of the same data.
4. Use a filter in CODAP to look at one group and report the finding.
5. Describe the strength of a correlation in a sentence.

### computer_science
1. Walk through the data cycle with a question of the class's choice.
2. Design a short survey and collect answers.
3. Load a data set into CODAP and sort it.
4. Use a CODAP transformer such as Filter.
5. Discuss a case of bias in data and propose a fix.
