## Overview
AI Snap! is a set of extensions to the Snap! block language. Seven libraries cover speech output, speech and sound recognition, camera vision, word processing, neural networks, nearest neighbor classification, and extras such as style transfer. A seven chapter teacher guide comes with it. Chapter 6 has students build and train neural networks on TensorFlow.js.

## Benefits for students
- Students build and train neural networks with blocks.
- Students try image recognition, speech recognition, and word embeddings.
- Students make tasks such as converting temperatures or playing Tic Tac Toe.

## Benefits for teachers
- A teacher guide with seven chapters shows each step.
- The libraries can be imported with an example project.
- Chapter 6 says the browser tab must stay visible during training.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- Cloud blocks need API keys, and the AI services may charge.
- A GPU is strongly recommended for many ML features.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open Snap! in Chrome or Edge.
- [ ] Import the AI blocks library or an example project.
- [ ] Read chapter 6 of the AI Teacher Guide.
- [ ] Check devices for a webcam, microphone, or speakers where needed.
- [ ] Keep the browser tab visible during training.
- [ ] Skip cloud blocks unless the school approves API keys.
- [ ] Test one project before class.

## Where is the AI?
The AI includes neural networks, image and speech recognition, nearest neighbor classification, word embeddings, and language model calls. Most run in the browser, and cloud blocks call outside services. Students train a model on their own examples and then use it in a project.

## Privacy and safety
No account with the project is described, and the internet is used at setup. The site does not address privacy. Cloud blocks call outside services and need API keys from providers such as OpenAI, AI21 or Cohere. Data sent to those services goes to those companies. The materials do not say what student data is collected. A teacher should skip cloud blocks until the school has reviewed the services.
