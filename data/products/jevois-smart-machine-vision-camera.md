## Overview
JeVois is an open source smart machine vision camera that processes video on board and sends results to a PC, Arduino, or Raspberry Pi. The JeVois-A33 combines a sensor, a quad core CPU, USB video, and a serial port in a 17 g package. It runs vision software from a microSD card, including OpenCV, TensorFlow, Caffe, and Darknet. The JeVois-Pro adds a 5 TOPS NPU for deep learning models.

## Benefits for students
- Students pick vision modules and read results on a host computer.
- Students can write their own modules in Python 3 or C++.
- Students can send results to an Arduino for robotics projects.

## Benefits for teachers
- The camera detects objects, AprilTags, ArUco markers, and QR codes.
- The Pro adds hand, face, and body pose modules.
- The software is open source.

## Benefits for schools
- The store lists the JeVois-A33 at $49.99 and kits at $59.99 to $69.99. The JeVois-Pro costs $249.99 or more.
- The camera runs without internet and without an account.
- IT should plan an hour for setup. Some optional sensor details come from 2018 news and may be out of date.

## Getting started
- [ ] Buy a JeVois camera from the JeVois store.
- [ ] Load the vision software onto a microSD card.
- [ ] Insert the card into the camera.
- [ ] Plug the camera into a PC over USB.
- [ ] Choose a vision module and view the output.
- [ ] Connect to an Arduino over serial for a robotics project.
- [ ] Try writing a small change to a module in Python.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is object recognition, object detection, pose detection, and face mesh. The Pro also lists vision language models. It runs on the camera itself. Students pick a module, view the results, and send them to a host to extend their own projects.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the pages read describe no cloud connection. Inference runs on the camera module, and results go over serial or USB to a host device. No privacy policy was reviewed. A teacher should check where the camera points and who appears in the video.
