## Overview
codeguppy is a free browser coding platform for JavaScript. It is built on p5.js, p5.play, and p5.scenemanager. It describes itself as for middle and high school students. Students follow tutorials and projects in drawing, pixel art, games, math, and text processing.

## Benefits for students
- Students write real JavaScript and see the result at once.
- Students can start without an account.
- Students can save projects after signing in.

## Benefits for teachers
- Tutorials are grouped by category, such as drawing and games.
- Teachers can use Hour of Code and curriculum pages.
- Class-code accounts for teachers are marked experimental in the privacy policy.

## Benefits for schools
- The homepage says it is 100 percent free.
- It runs in a browser on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- The privacy policy says the site is designed for users 13 and older.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the codeguppy website in a web browser.
- [ ] Run a first tutorial without signing in.
- [ ] Read the privacy policy and note the minimum age of 13.
- [ ] Decide between email accounts and class-code accounts.
- [ ] Set up class-code accounts, which the policy calls experimental.
- [ ] Tell students to choose a username that hides their real name.
- [ ] Share the tutorial category that fits your lesson.

## Where is the AI?
This platform has no AI. Students build skills in sequencing, loops, and logic as they write code. They see the results as drawings and games.

## Privacy and safety
Students can code without an account, and the internet is needed at all times. Email accounts store an email address and projects. Class code accounts store a chosen username, class code, and projects. Data is stored on Google Firebase, and Cloudflare Web Analytics is used without cookies. Programs are private but open to anyone with the URL. The policy says it does not collect real names, ages, addresses, phone numbers, or photos. It says the site is designed for users 13 and older and closes under 13 accounts on request. The research found no FERPA or COPPA certification. A teacher should read the privacy policy and check the ages of the class.
