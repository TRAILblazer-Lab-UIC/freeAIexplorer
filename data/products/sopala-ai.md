## Overview
Sopala is an open source K-12 education package built by SABIER for classrooms with or without internet. A 2026 presentation lists a Raspberry Pi 5, MoodleBox as the learning system, and the Gemma 3 1B model, at a stated cost under 200 dollars. The MoodleBox creates a local network. Students reach it from their own devices. The project began in Ghana's Northern Region.

## Benefits for students
- Students use a learning site and a local AI helper without home internet.
- Students connect with their own devices.
- Students gain access to content in low connectivity settings.

## Benefits for teachers
- The server keeps content and AI in the classroom.
- Teachers can download content over a phone hotspot.
- The setup is technical and needs a server admin.

## Benefits for schools
- Stated hardware cost is under 200 dollars. No software fee was found.
- The project says the data stays on the school server.
- The details come from search text. The presentation PDF would not open.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small local language model, Gemma 3 1B, that assists learners. It runs on the school server, a Raspberry Pi. Students ask questions through their own devices and read the answers. An online Moodle site with AI translation and assessments also exists.

## Privacy and safety
The project says it runs fully offline on a local school server. The research could not check this claim, and the presentation could not be read. The research did not check a FERPA claim from the discovery notes. An online Moodle site with AI translation and assessments is also described, and the materials do not say where it runs or what data it sends. The materials do not say what student data is collected or whether accounts are needed. A teacher or server admin should ask the project to confirm the data claims before use.
