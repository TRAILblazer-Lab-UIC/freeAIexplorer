## Overview
GeoGebra Classic is dynamic math software for graphing, geometry, spreadsheets, and statistics. Classic 5 is the traditional desktop interface with extra options for advanced users. GeoGebra recommends Calculator Suite for new users but says it will keep supporting Classic. Middle and high school students use it to draw and explore math.

## Benefits for students
- Students move points and watch graphs and shapes change.
- Students can graph, build geometry, and analyze data in one tool.
- Students can save work on the computer without an account.

## Benefits for teachers
- One program covers graphing, geometry, spreadsheets, and statistics.
- The main tools work offline after installation.
- Teachers can build and share resources with the advanced options.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free for non-commercial use, including teachers and students.
- Commercial use needs a license.
- Offline installers run on Windows, Mac, and Linux. IT will likely need to install it.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the GeoGebra download page and choose Classic.
- [ ] Check the all download options page for other systems.
- [ ] Ask IT to install it on school computers.
- [ ] Open the program and try the graphing view.
- [ ] Save a test file on the computer.
- [ ] Notice that the online version has a different data flow than the offline app.
- [ ] Decide as a team whether students use Classic or Calculator Suite.

## Where is the AI?
GeoGebra Classic has no AI. It builds math reasoning skills. Students link equations to graphs and shapes. It runs on the local computer.

## Privacy and safety
The apps need no account. An account is needed only to save online or sync, and it needs a username and email. The internet is needed for setup of the offline app. Student files stay on the device in the offline app. The online version uses GeoGebra servers in Austria and third party servers elsewhere. The policy lists Google Analytics, reCAPTCHA, and ad partners on the website. It says no personal data is collected from students under 13 without parent or school permission. A teacher should use the offline app for student work and check which version students open.
