## Overview
The ELEGOO Smart Robot Car V4 is an Arduino UNO R3 based car with a camera, an ultrasonic sensor, a line-tracking module and an infrared remote. Students assemble it, then drive it from a phone or tablet app or program it in the Arduino IDE. The listing gives an age of 13 and up and says basic electronics knowledge helps.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a car from many small parts.
- Students can drive it by app or write Arduino code.
- Students use the obstacle avoidance and line tracking modules.

## Benefits for teachers
- The kit has example sketches and a user manual with tutorials.
- Assembly instructions come on a CD and online.
- The teacher learning curve is advanced.

## Benefits for schools
- The product page showed no price and linked to Amazon.
- Plan for IT help with the Arduino IDE.
- Assembly took one reviewer about 1.5 hours.

## Where is the AI?
The car has no AI. It follows Arduino code and reads its sensors. Students build skills in sensors and logic. The default obstacle avoidance can struggle in tight corners and detects objects at about 20 cm.

## Privacy and safety
The car runs on local hardware and sends video over Wi-Fi to a phone. The materials do not confirm whether the phone app sends data to ELEGOO or needs an account. The ELEGOO privacy policy covers its website and apps. It lists device and usage data, setup location, and cookies and pixels, including the Facebook Pixel. It says services are not designed for children, that users must be 18 or older, and that data may be processed in the US, EU, China, or other countries. A teacher should read that policy and review the app permissions before students install it. A teacher should also supervise the small screws.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Assemble the car from the manual and list each part and its job.
2. Test the obstacle avoidance in open space and in a tight corner.
3. Measure the detection distance with a ruler.
4. Design a track that tests the line tracking module.
5. Write a short report on what changed after a repair or adjustment.

### computer_science
1. Open an example sketch in the Arduino IDE and describe what each section does.
2. Change a value in a sketch and upload it.
3. Drive the car with the phone app and then recreate the moves in code.
4. Use the ultrasonic sensor to make the car stop at a set distance.
5. Add comments to a sketch so a classmate can follow it.
