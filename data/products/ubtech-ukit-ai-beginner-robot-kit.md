## Overview
The uKit AI Beginner is a building kit from UBTECH. Its control box has dual microphones, a speaker, and a Wi-Fi module. Retail listings say it has over 350 pieces, four servos, and 13 robot models. Students in grades 3 to 8 build a model and then use AI blocks for speech recognition and text to speech.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a robot from a kit with many model choices.
- Students program with blocks.
- Students make the robot listen and speak.

## Benefits for teachers
- Blocks keep programming simple for beginners.
- The kit offers 13 models. A teacher can plan several builds.
- No independent review was found. A teacher should test the kit first.

## Benefits for schools
