## Overview
The Falstad Circuit Simulator is a free circuit simulator that runs in the browser. It shows voltages in color and current as moving yellow dots. Users place and edit components, toggle switches, and load many sample circuits from a menu. The site links standalone offline versions.

## Benefits for students
- Students see current move through a circuit.
- Students change parts and see an instant result.
- Students open many sample circuits from a menu.

## Benefits for teachers
- Right-click a part to edit it.
- Sample circuits give ready demonstrations.
- The simulator can be embedded per the source.

## Benefits for schools
- The tool is free and needs no account.
- It runs in a modern browser, including on Chromebooks.
- Standalone versions work offline.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the Falstad Circuit Simulator web page.
- [ ] Load a sample circuit from the menu.
- [ ] Right-click a part to edit its value.
- [ ] Toggle a switch and watch the current.
- [ ] Download a standalone version for offline use.
- [ ] Test it on a student computer.

## Where is the AI?
The Falstad simulator has no AI. It builds logic and cause-and-effect skills. Students change a part and predict how the circuit responds.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed and the simulation runs in the browser. The page has no privacy statement and includes a third party visitor counter from flagcounter.com. The web version loads from the site, and the standalone offline versions avoid network use. The materials describe no student data collection. A teacher should decide whether students use the web page or a standalone version. The GitHub repository has no formal releases.
