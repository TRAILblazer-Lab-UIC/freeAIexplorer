## Overview
Scope is described as a tool that runs a hosted copy of scratch-gui, scratch-www, or hackercat-www on your own system. HackerCat is a mod of Scratch with custom extensions and features. It would suit a school with a server administrator. Students would use the Scratch block editor from that server.

## Benefits for students
- Students would use the familiar Scratch block editor.
- Students could build stories, games, and animations with blocks.
- Students could try the HackerCat mod if the school hosts it.
