## Overview
This is a five-lesson unit on artificial intelligence for Year 5 and 6, ages 9 to 11. It is designed to replace the Year 6 Communication and Collaboration unit in the Teach Computing and Oak Academy curricula. Content covers what AI is, how it learns from data, safe and critical use, bias, hallucinations, and anthropomorphism. Lesson 2 may take longer because of its practical work.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn how AI learns from data.
- Students try online machine learning activities and unplugged activities.
- Students design an ethical AI system.

## Where is the AI?
The unit teaches how AI learns from data, plus bias, hallucinations, and ethical design. The materials do not say where the online machine learning tools run. Students try the tools and then discuss what they saw.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which online tools students must use, whether they need accounts, or where data goes. A teacher should check each tool before the lesson. A teacher should also tell students not to type personal details into any online tool.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Sort a set of cards into groups and explain how the sorting rule works like training.
2. Try an online machine learning activity and describe what the computer learned.
3. Find an example where an AI answer is wrong and explain why.
4. Discuss why it is wrong to treat an AI like a person.
5. Design an ethical AI system for school and write three rules for it.
