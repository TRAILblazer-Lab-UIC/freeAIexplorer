## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Research three AI careers and write a short job profile for each.
2. Interview a local worker about how AI is used in their job.
3. Build a career pathway chart that shows the skills for one AI job.
4. Debate how AI could change a trade that interests the class.
5. Write a resume bullet that describes an AI skill a student has learned.

### special_ed
1. Explore how AI tools can help people with different learning needs.
2. List accommodations that a student might request and discuss how AI could help.
3. Practice using a speech tool and describe what worked well.
4. Discuss safety rules for sharing personal information with AI.
5. Create a picture guide that explains one AI term in plain words.

### computer_science
1. Sort pictures into groups and discuss how a machine could learn the groups.
2. Explain the idea of machine learning in a short poster.
3. Play a game where a classmate acts as a model and learns from examples.
4. List ways that bad data can lead to unfair results.
5. Write rules for safe and ethical AI use in the classroom.
