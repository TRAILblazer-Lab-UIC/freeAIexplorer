## Overview
The Arduino Tiny Machine Learning Kit holds a Nano 33 BLE Sense board, an OV7675 camera module, a Tiny ML shield, and a USB cable. The board senses motion, sound, gestures, proximity, color, and light. Projects use TensorFlow Lite Micro. The kit accompanies two edX courses on TinyML.

## Benefits for students
- Students train real models and run them on a tiny board.
- The kit supports keyword spotting, gesture recognition, and image classification.
- Students learn how sensors and models fit together in one device.

## Benefits for teachers
- The kit follows two companion edX courses, TinyML Applications and Deploying TinyML on Microcontrollers.
- Arduino offers a tutorial for Edge Impulse with the Nano 33 BLE Sense.
- Teachers need strong coding skills. The setup is advanced.

## Benefits for schools
- The Arduino store listed the kit at 61.50 EUR and showed it sold out on the research date.
- The model runs on the device and the internet is needed only at setup.
- A teacher will likely need IT help to install the tools.

## Getting started
- [ ] Check the Arduino store for kit stock and price.
- [ ] Install the Arduino IDE on a test computer.
- [ ] Attach the shield and the camera to the board.
- [ ] Connect the board by USB and run a sensor example.
- [ ] Note that the board lacks the HTS221 temperature and humidity sensor.
- [ ] Follow the edX course or the Arduino tutorials to train a model.
- [ ] Deploy one small model and test it.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small model built with TensorFlow Lite Micro. It runs on the board itself. Students train models for keyword spotting, gestures, or images and then run them on the device. They can change the training data and see how the board responds.

## Privacy and safety
The model runs on the board and the kit needs no account. The two edX courses need an edX account. The research did not review the edX terms or any outside training tools. The Arduino policy says Arduino processes data of people under 18 and Junior accounts under 14 need parental consent. The internet is needed at setup. A teacher should check the terms of edX and any training tool. A teacher should also keep voice and face data out of student training sets.
