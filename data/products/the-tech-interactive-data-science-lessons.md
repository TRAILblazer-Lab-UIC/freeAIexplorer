## Overview
The Tech Interactive offers seven data science lessons and activities. They include Wastewater Data Challenge for grades 7 to 12, Pick and Chews Data Talk for grades 3 to 12, and Analyzing Patterns in Voter Data for grades 9 to 12. A 12 session Data Explorers curriculum is for grades 7 and 8. Most lessons suit grades 7 to 12.

## Benefits for students
- Students work with real datasets on topics such as water and voting.
- Students graph and map data and share findings.
- Students watch videos and discuss drones and global good.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each lesson has a lesson plan PDF.
- The wastewater lesson runs 105 minutes and the voter lesson runs 90 minutes.
- Data Explorers runs 12 sessions of 75 minutes.

## Benefits for schools
- The listing shows no price and the pages read do not state one. The cost is unconfirmed.
- Students need devices for the web-based data tool.
- Data Explorers was developed with TERC and is sponsored by NetApp.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Graph a dataset and describe the shape of the data.
2. Compare two groups and say which has a higher average.
3. Map data and explain what the colors show.
4. Write a question that data could answer and test it.
5. Present your chart and take questions.

### science
1. Study the wastewater data challenge and explain what the data show about a community.
2. Plot data over time and mark a trend.
3. Write a claim and cite data as proof.
4. Discuss what a drone could measure and what data it could add.
5. Share a short summary with the class.

### social_studies
1. Analyze voter data and describe the patterns by place.
2. Act as a data journalist and write a short story from the data.
3. Compare two places and suggest reasons for the differences.
4. Discuss which questions the data cannot answer.
5. Make a map poster of your findings.
