## Overview
RoboMind is an educational programming environment where students write scripts to control a simulated robot. A UK Computing at School lesson uses it for line follower exercises. Search results list builds for Windows, macOS and Linux. Current licensing could not be confirmed.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a robot with no hardware.
- Students solve line follower and maze tasks.
- Students see the effect of each command.

## Benefits for teachers
- A Computing at School lesson gives a line follower exercise.
- No robot needs to be bought.
- The tool runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Benefits for schools
- One result says it is free for 30 days with a free Academy account.
- The status of an older free edition is unverified.
- IT needs to install it.
