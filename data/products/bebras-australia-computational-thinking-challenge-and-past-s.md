## Overview
Bebras Australia is an online computational thinking challenge run by the Australian Maths Trust. It is for Years 3 to 12. The challenge has 15 questions in multiple choice, integer, and interactive formats. Students enter alone or in teams of up to four.

## Benefits for students
- Students solve logic and number puzzles in a short challenge.
- Students can work alone or in a team of up to four.
- Students can practice with past challenges from 2014 to 2024.

## Benefits for teachers
- Past solution guides are free PDF downloads that need no login.
- Unplugged challenge cards are offered for Years 3 to 8.
- Teacher resources, Bebras 365, and mini challenges need no login.

## Benefits for schools
- The main challenge costs AUD 5.50 per student, with bundle discounts.
- Guides, resources, and past challenges are free.
- Students need a web browser, and the unplugged cards need a printer.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Bebras page on the Australian Maths Trust site.
- [ ] Download the past solution guides first, which need no registration.
- [ ] Try the free past challenges with your class.
- [ ] Print the unplugged challenge cards for Years 3 to 8.
- [ ] Register your school and place an order in the AMT admin portal.
- [ ] Note the 2027 challenge window, which runs 8 February to 19 March.
- [ ] Ask your school's IT staff to check that students can open the challenge site.

## Where is the AI?
This challenge has no AI. It builds computational thinking skills such as logic and pattern spotting. Students reason through each puzzle step by step.

## Privacy and safety
The online challenge runs in the vendor's cloud and needs the internet at all times. An account is optional. Teachers register students through the AMT admin portal for the main challenge. The AMT privacy policy says student information is used only for the purpose collected. It says the information is not disclosed without consent unless law requires it. The policy does not list the data collected, name third parties, or say where data is stored. A teacher should read the privacy terms before registering students.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Solve past Bebras tasks and explain the thinking behind each answer.
2. Work in a team of up to four to solve a puzzle together.
3. Use a solution guide to find the key skill that each task tests.
4. Complete an unplugged challenge card and compare answers with a partner.
5. Write a new Bebras-style puzzle for a classmate.

### math
1. Solve a number reasoning task and check the answer with a different method.
2. Find the pattern in a sequence of shapes or numbers.
3. Work out the logic of a puzzle and write each step.
4. Compare answers in a team and decide which is correct.
5. Rate the difficulty of past tasks and explain the ratings.
