## Overview
This is a CSTA 2024 conference session by Vicky Sedgwick that demystifies AI through unplugged activities for early elementary students. The CSTA library page offers only the session description and slides that require a CSTA login. A shared document from the presenter reportedly links to lessons for pre-K to grade 2, children's books about AI, and story and image creation activities.

## Where is the AI?
The session has no software and no AI runs on any device. It builds AI literacy for early elementary students through unplugged activities. The details of each activity are in the slides, which this page could not read.

## Privacy and safety
A teacher account is needed to view the CSTA slides, and the materials do not say what data CSTA collects. The activities are unplugged, and the research found no student data collected. The presenter's shared document mentions a Padlet and AI image creation, and the research did not examine their data flow. The research did not see the activities because the slides need a login. A teacher should check the Padlet and image tools before students use them.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Sort picture cards into groups and tell a partner the rule you used.
2. Read a children's book about AI and talk about what the robot learned.
3. Act as a robot that follows simple steps from a partner.
4. Draw a machine that helps at school and describe what it needs to learn.
5. Make up a short story about a helper that learns from a mistake.
