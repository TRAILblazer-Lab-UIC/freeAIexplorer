## Overview
CoDrone EDU is a programmable classroom drone with a guarded frame and a controller with an LCD screen. It has seven sensors including optical flow, range and color sensors. Students code it in Blockly or Python. Free lessons are hosted on Robolink's Basecamp portal.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a drone that flies.
- Students use sensors to steer and stabilize flight.
- Students move from Blockly to Python.

## Benefits for teachers
- Free lessons are on Basecamp.
- Up to 10 drones can fly in one room on 2.4 GHz.
- Parts are replaceable.

## Benefits for schools
- One drone costs 249 USD. A 12 pack costs 3599 USD and an 18 pack costs 5399 USD.
- Python is not supported on Chromebooks. iPads and tablets are not compatible.
- Flight time is about 7 to 8 minutes per search results.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find CoDrone EDU at Robolink and choose a pack size.
- [ ] Buy drones and pair each with a controller.
- [ ] Use Blockly in a browser, or ask IT to install Python on a Mac or Windows computer.
- [ ] Skip class accounts on Basecamp where you can.
- [ ] Open the Introduction to CoDrone EDU lesson.
- [ ] Mark a flight area and set safety rules.
- [ ] Run the Flight Commands lesson.

## Where is the AI?
The drone has no AI. It builds skills in sensing, logic and sequencing. Students write commands that guide a flight.

## Privacy and safety
Drone flight needs no account. Basecamp is a vendor portal, and its class accounts list a username, password, age, gender, grade, and name. The policy says classroom student information is not public and not shared outside the school context. It names Google Analytics and Amazon as providers and does not name COPPA or FERPA. The policy is dated 2017. A teacher should avoid class accounts or read the policy first. Set clear flight rules and use the guarded frame.
