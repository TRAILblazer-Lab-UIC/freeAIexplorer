## Overview
Edubuntu is an official Ubuntu flavor for schools. It is a full Linux desktop with access to free education software. The site lists Edubuntu 26.04 LTS and 26.10 releases. The traditional bundle includes GCompris, the KDE Education Suite, LibreOffice, and the Linux Terminal Server Project.

## Benefits for students
- Students get learning games and math and science apps.
- Students use a full desktop with office tools.
- Students can use older computers per the site.

## Benefits for teachers
- Education packages come in one bundle.
- The KDE Education Suite includes math and science apps.
- The terminal server tools help with lab setups.

## Benefits for schools
- The system is free.
- The 24.04 LTS release added Raspberry Pi 5 support. Education package support is limited to three years per those notes.
- IT staff are needed for lab deployment.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Edubuntu download page and pick a release.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve a test install.
- [ ] Download the ISO file.
- [ ] Write the ISO to a USB drive.
- [ ] Install it on one test computer.
- [ ] Open GCompris and a KDE Education app to check they run.
- [ ] Plan lab deployment with IT staff.

## Where is the AI?
Edubuntu has no AI. It builds skills in using an operating system and tools. Students learn files, windows, and software on a Linux desktop.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used for setup. The system is a local operating system, and the site calls it privacy conscious but states no policy or data practices. Bundled apps and Ubuntu update services have their own practices, and the research did not review them. A teacher should ask IT about update settings and the three year support limit for education packages.
