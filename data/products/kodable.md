## Overview
Kodable is a coding curriculum for elementary students. Kodable Basics starts at grade K and teaches programming logic through game levels. The company also sells Kodable Creator, Typing, Bug World, and Digital Citizenship. It works on Chromebooks, iPads, and web browsers.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn logic through game levels.
- Students play at their own pace.
- Students can use a Chromebook, iPad, or browser.

## Benefits for teachers
- A dashboard shows progress reports.
- Teachers can roster students through Google Classroom, Clever, ClassLink, or class codes.
- Common Sense Education notes teacher materials that tie coding to ELA, math, and digital citizenship.

## Benefits for schools
- A free teacher account exists.
- School plans and parent plans are paid, and prices were not listed.
- School plans include a standards aligned K-5 curriculum.

## Getting started
- [ ] Register for a free teacher account on the Kodable site.
- [ ] Ask your school before you add student names.
- [ ] Add students through Google Classroom, Clever, or class codes.
- [ ] Have students log in at game.kodable.com.
- [ ] Assign the first Kodable Basics levels.
- [ ] Review the progress reports after a week.
- [ ] Ask the vendor for the price of a school plan.

## Where is the AI?
Kodable Basics has no AI. Students solve game levels with fixed rules. They build sequencing and logic skills as they plan the steps that guide a character.

## Privacy and safety
Student work and accounts live in the vendor's cloud. The privacy page says the game does not collect any personally identifiable information from children, and that the teacher dashboard and website are for adults over 18. Kodable states it is a Student Privacy Pledge signatory and a Student Data Privacy Consortium member. The page does not mention FERPA, the Common Sense review page returned a 404, and the full policy PDF was not read. Teachers create accounts and add students by roster or class code. A teacher should read the privacy terms and ask the district about approval before adding students.
