## Overview
Chibitronics makes peel and stick circuit stickers. These are flexible circuit boards with LEDs and sensors. Learners build circuits on paper with copper tape and stickers. The company sells LED kits, a sketchbook of lessons, and educator products.

## Benefits for students
- Students build working circuits on paper.
- Students light LEDs and add sensors in craft projects.
- Students mix art and electronics in one project.

## Benefits for teachers
- The sketchbook in the Level 1 and Level 2 kits holds lessons.
- The education page offers project templates.
- The materials are small and easy to store.

## Benefits for schools
- Kits are sold by the company, and the pages read show no prices.
- An Educator Kit was sized for 30 students, and batteries were not included per reseller snippets.
- No internet or accounts are needed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Chibitronics education page and read the project templates.
- [ ] Choose a kit and check the price with the seller.

- [ ] Run a first project with a few students.

## Where is the AI?
This kit has no AI. It builds an understanding of circuits and sensors. Students learn how electricity flows through a path to light an LED.

## Privacy and safety
The basic LED kits have no software and no accounts, and they need no internet. The research did not review other Chibitronics products. A teacher should keep coin cell batteries away from young children. The vendor materials read do not describe other safety steps.
