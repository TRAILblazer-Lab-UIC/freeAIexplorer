## Overview
This Spanish language resource from Educ.ar presents teaching and learning dynamics to design, carry out, or assess an educational proposal. Activities are either plugged, using digital devices, or unplugged, without devices or connectivity. It also covers work dynamics such as debate and reflection, sharing and socializing, and documentation. Search results list it as published on 15 May 2023.
