## Overview
ValenceSim is a student built online molecule builder and chemistry teaching tool. It renders 3D molecules instantly from a SMILES string or a search. It also offers NMR and IR spectroscopy, functional group detection, and a stoichiometry calculator. The site says no logins and no installs are needed.

## Benefits for students
- Students can see molecules in 2D and 3D.
- Students can explore simulated NMR and IR spectra.
- Students can check a stoichiometry problem with the calculator.

## Benefits for teachers
- The tool runs in a browser with no logins.
- Teachers can show a molecule to the class in seconds.
- The tool suits high school chemistry.

## Benefits for schools
- The price is not stated. The page suggests it is free but this is unconfirmed.
- No installs are needed.
- The internet is always needed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open valencesim.com in a browser.
- [ ] Type a SMILES string or search for a molecule.
- [ ] Rotate the 3D view and check the 2D view.
- [ ] Try the NMR and IR simulation on a simple molecule.
- [ ] Try the stoichiometry calculator.
- [ ] Confirm the price and terms with the vendor.
- [ ] Ask IT whether the site is allowed on school networks.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI. The tool draws molecules from the text students enter. Students build data and pattern skills as they link structure to spectra.

## Privacy and safety
