## Overview
The Search Sheets module is part of LLMs Unplugged. It runs 10 to 15 minutes for ages 5 and up and works in any room size. Each person gets a sheet of token pairs. Someone calls the last word on the board, and people whose sheet shows it raise a hand. One person is picked at random and reads the next word. The group repeats this until about fifteen words appear.

## Benefits for students
- Students take part in next-word prediction with their own bodies.
- Students see that a text can grow one word at a time.
- Young students can join. The page lists ages 5 and up.

## Benefits for teachers
- The module is short and easy to run.
- It works in halls and large classes.
- It needs one search sheet per person and a whiteboard or slide.

## Benefits for schools
- The materials are free.
- The module needs no computers and no accounts.
- No student data is collected.

## Where is the AI?
The module has no running AI program. The room acts as a language model that looks up the next word from pairs on sheets. Each hand raise stands for a match, and the random pick stands for sampling. Students build skills in seeing how text can be made by prediction.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Hold a search sheet and raise a hand when your pair matches the called word.
2. Read the next word aloud when you are picked.
3. Write down the sentence that the whole room makes.
4. Run the activity again from the same start word and compare the two sentences.
5. Explain why a different person gives a different next word.
