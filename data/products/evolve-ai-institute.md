## Overview
Evolve AI Institute is a small company that sells AI training and consulting to colleges, K-12 schools, and workforce programs. A federal directory lists it as providing free resources in 11 categories. They include classroom tools, curriculum integration, educator professional development, safety, ethics and privacy, and parental guides. The site also offers K-12 lesson plans, a 45 minute ARAD lesson, and free quarterly webinars. It is a resource collection and not a single tool.

## Benefits for students
- Students can use free K-12 lesson plans and toolkits.
- Lessons cover safety, ethics, and privacy.
- The vendor does not say which grades each lesson fits.

## Benefits for teachers
- Free webinars run every quarter.
- A repository gathers many AI education resources.
- A paid course, AI Literacy for Educators, costs $295.

## Benefits for schools
- The directory says all listed resources are free.
- Consulting is sold by quote.
- IT has little to do because the resources are on a website.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI tool. The resources are lessons, toolkits, and webinars about AI. Students and teachers use them to learn about AI safety, ethics, and privacy.

## Privacy and safety
The resources are on a website and the internet is always needed. Lesson plans are for teachers, and students do not use accounts. The materials do not state what data is collected. An educator account may be optional for webinar certificates, according to a search snippet. The consultation form asks for a name and work email and uses reCAPTCHA, and the newsletter asks for an email. The site links a privacy policy that the research did not read. A teacher should read that policy and review each lesson before sharing it with students.
