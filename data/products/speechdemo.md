## Overview
SpeechDemo is a web page where a student speaks into a laptop microphone and sees the recognized text appear. The AI4K12 activities page says it uses Google's speech recognition service. It shows alternative transcriptions of a sentence. Students see how the system handles homophones and other ambiguities. An activity guide covers grades 3-5, 6-8, and 9-12.

## Benefits for students
- Students see speech turn into text as they speak.
- Students compare alternative transcriptions of one sentence.
- Students learn how a computer picks between words that sound the same.

## Benefits for teachers
- The page opens in Chrome in minutes.
- An activity guide covers each grade band.
- No account is needed.

## Benefits for schools
- It is free.
- It runs on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- Audio goes to Google and needs a privacy review.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the SpeechDemo page in Chrome.
- [ ] Download the AI4K12 activity guide for your grade band.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve microphone use and the Google speech service.
- [ ] Tell students that their audio goes to Google.
- [ ] Allow microphone access in the browser.
- [ ] Click MIC ON and say a sentence.
- [ ] Try a sentence with homophones such as their and there.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is Google's speech recognition service. It runs in Google's cloud through the browser speech tool. Students speak, read the text, and compare the alternatives. They use these results to think about how the system chooses a word.

## Privacy and safety
Audio goes to Google through the browser speech tool. No account is needed, and the internet is needed at all times. The page has no privacy statement. The research did not find how the page handles audio or whether it logs it. A teacher should get school approval and use sample sentences that hold no personal details.
