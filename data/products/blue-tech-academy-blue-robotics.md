## Overview
Blue Tech Academy is a field-based marine technology program for high school students. It runs with host schools, aquariums and marine centers. Modules cover bathymetric surveys with uncrewed boats, drop cameras, ROV piloting and introductory Lua control scripts. A capstone field deployment and science communication round out the program. A first pilot ran in November 2025 with Ventura high school students.

## Benefits for students
- Students do real field work with boats and ROVs.
- Students write simple Lua control scripts.
- Students earn a Certificate of Completion.

## Benefits for teachers
- Pilot modules, workshops, curriculum materials and instructor training are offered.
- A school can start without buying equipment.
- Blue Robotics advises on equipment, deployment and training.

## Benefits for schools
- Prices are not listed on the pages read.

## Where is the AI?
This program has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is working with sensor data and scripts. Students collect depth and camera data and write simple control code.

## Privacy and safety
The pages read do not state accounts, data practices, or whether the internet is needed. The only sign in links found are for the Blue Robotics store. Interested hosts fill in an interest form. A teacher should ask Blue Robotics about data handling and software accounts. Field work near water needs adult supervision and clear safety rules.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Run a bathymetric survey with an uncrewed boat and map the depth.
2. Use a drop camera to observe life below the surface.
3. Compare survey results from two spots.
4. Plan a capstone field deployment with a question to answer.
5. Present your findings to a public audience.

### engineering
1. Pilot an ROV through a set course.
2. Plan the equipment needed for a survey.
3. Test and tune a boat for a steady path.
4. Write a pre-launch safety checklist.
5. Suggest a design change after the first trial.

### computer_science
1. Read a short Lua control script and say what it does.
2. Change a value in the script and predict the result.
3. Write a simple script to run a motor for a set time.
4. Debug a script that does the wrong thing.
5. Compare a script with manual piloting and note the differences.
