## Overview
The Raspberry Pi Foundation Projects site is a library of more than 250 free step-by-step digital making projects. Projects cover Scratch, Python, web design, and physical computing. They can be used with or without a Raspberry Pi computer. Some projects are translated into 30 languages.

## Benefits for students
- Students follow clear steps to finish a project.
- Students start from community example creations.
- Students choose Scratch, Python, or physical computing paths.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can pick a project for any class.
- Some projects are translated into 30 languages.
- Teachers can use projects with or without a Raspberry Pi.

## Benefits for schools
- The projects are free.
- Most projects run in a browser.
- Some projects need Scratch, a Python editor, or physical parts.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the Raspberry Pi Foundation Projects site and open the projects catalog.
- [ ] Browse by Scratch, Python, or physical computing.
- [ ] Open one project in a browser and follow the steps.
- [ ] Check what tools the project needs.
- [ ] Ask IT to check the tools and the network before class.
- [ ] Review the example creations at the start of a project.
- [ ] Choose one project for a trial lesson.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in this library. The projects build skills such as sequencing and logic. Students follow steps and then change them to make something of their own. That practice builds a base for later work with AI tools.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. The research could not read the site or its privacy policy. Notes from the research sweep say the online editors send code to Foundation servers, and the research did not verify this. Offline use may be possible with Scratch Desktop and a Python install. A teacher should check each tool before students sign in.
