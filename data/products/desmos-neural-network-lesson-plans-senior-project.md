## Overview
This high school senior project proposes a sequence of lessons built on Desmos graphs. The lessons build intuition for neural networks without programming. Topics move from linear models and gradient descent through loss functions and logistic regression to neural networks and activation functions. The author is a student and the lessons are a work in progress. They were developed over a 10-week blog from February to May 2026.

## Benefits for students
- Students build gradient descent models in Desmos.
- Students see loss functions and decision boundaries on a graph.
- Students build a neural network step by step with no code.

## Benefits for teachers
- Desmos is familiar to many math teachers.
- Video walkthroughs show each Desmos build.
- The lessons are proposals and may be incomplete.

## Benefits for schools
- The lessons and Desmos are free.
- They run in a browser on Chromebooks and other computers.
- No outside review of the project was found.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small neural network that students build inside Desmos graphs. It runs in the browser on the local device. Students adjust values and watch the loss shrink. They use the graphs to see how a network learns to separate two groups. Backpropagation was moved to an appendix because Desmos lacks matrix support.

## Privacy and safety
The project page does not describe accounts or data handling, and the research did not review them. The lessons use Desmos in the browser and need an internet connection to load. No license is stated for the lessons. A teacher should check the school's Desmos policy and whether students need accounts. A teacher should also test each graph before class.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Fit a line to a set of points in Desmos and adjust the slope to reduce the error.
2. Use gradient descent steps to find the lowest point on a loss graph.
3. Compare mean squared error and log loss on the same data.
4. Plot a logistic regression decision boundary and move it to classify points.
5. Explain how an activation function bends a straight line.

### computer_science
1. Describe how a neural network turns inputs into an output using the Desmos build.
2. Change one weight and record how the output changes.
3. Explain why a network needs non-linear activation functions.
4. Compare the Desmos model with a simple program that fits a line.
5. Write a short summary of what a loss function measures.
