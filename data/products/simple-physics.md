## Overview
SIMple Physics is a set of free educational physics simulators for high school. SIMple Mechanics covers kinematics, projectile motion, and basic mechanics. SIMple Gravity covers universal gravitation. Students save and load preset scenes, graph object properties, and export graphs to CSV.

## Benefits for students
- Students run simulations of motion and gravity.
- Students graph properties of objects as they move.
- Students export data to CSV for analysis.

## Benefits for teachers
- A Mechanics Tutorial supports the first lesson.
- Preset scenes can be saved and loaded.
- The author says electromagnetism and waves and optics are not yet available.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free and open source.
- It runs on school computers but not on Chromebooks.
- Development appears slow.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the zip for your operating system from the GitHub releases page.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the program.
- [ ] Extract the zip and run the executable.
- [ ] Work through the Mechanics Tutorial.
- [ ] Save a preset scene and load it again.
- [ ] Export a graph to CSV and open it in a spreadsheet.

## Where is the AI?
SIMple Physics has no AI. It simulates motion and gravity. Students build data skills as they graph properties and read the patterns.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the internet is used at setup. Each simulator is installed as a desktop app from GitHub releases. The about page mentions no data collection, and the research found no privacy policy. A teacher should check that the download comes from the official GitHub releases page.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Launch a projectile at different angles and graph the distance.
2. Change the mass of an object and record how the motion changes.
3. Set up an orbit in SIMple Gravity and describe what keeps it circling.
4. Export a graph to CSV and find the average speed.
5. Predict a result, run it, and explain any difference.
