## Overview
Scratch Encore is an intermediate Scratch curriculum that follows Scratch Act 1. A search snippet describes 14 modules of 2 to 3 sixty-minute lessons for grades 4 to 6. It uses Use-Modify-Create and the TIPP and SEE reading strategy. Students build Scratch projects with growing skill.

## Benefits for students
- Students practice a reading strategy for code called TIPP and SEE.
- Students follow a Use-Modify-Create path.
- Students keep building on skills from Scratch Act 1.

## Benefits for teachers
- The curriculum comes with digital worksheets and intro videos for each concept.
- Automated grading tools support online and asynchronous teaching.
- The curriculum lines up with CSforAll, CSTA, and ISTE.

## Benefits for schools
- The curriculum is free.
- Full resources may need a login.
- The CSTA page says some resources are behind CSTA membership.

## Getting started

## Where is the AI?
This curriculum has no AI. Students build skills in sequence, events, and loops with Scratch blocks. They also practice reading code carefully.

## Privacy and safety
Scratch runs in a browser and stores accounts and projects on Scratch servers. Accounts are optional. The research did not examine the automated grading tools or their data handling. Full resources may need a login. The materials do not state what student data is collected. A teacher should check school rules on accounts and the login for grading tools.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Run a starter project from a module and describe what it does.
2. Modify the project by changing one behavior.
3. Create a new project that uses the same idea.
4. Use the TIPP and SEE strategy to read a classmate's code aloud.
5. Present a finished module project and explain the blocks.
