## Overview
Celestia is a free real-time 3D space simulator created in 2001 by Chris Laurel. Users travel through the solar system, visit over 100,000 stars, and go beyond the galaxy with exponential zoom. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, and several other platforms. NASA and ESA have used it in education and outreach.

## Benefits for students
- Students fly through the solar system on screen.
- Students zoom from a spacecraft to a galaxy cluster.
- Students can explore at their own pace.

## Benefits for teachers
- ComPADRE lists it as an interactive simulation for astronomy education.
- Educational add-ons come with lessons.
- Teachers can use it to show scale and distance.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free.
- It runs on the device, and no account is needed.
- IT may need to approve the install.

- [ ] Plan a short first tour of the solar system.

## Where is the AI?
Celestia has no AI. It builds the skill of pattern spotting, as students notice how planets and moons move. It also builds data skills through real astronomical data.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the program runs on the device. The internet is needed for setup. The research did not read a privacy policy because the official site blocked access. The materials do not say what data is collected. A teacher should check the source of the download and any add-ons before installing.
