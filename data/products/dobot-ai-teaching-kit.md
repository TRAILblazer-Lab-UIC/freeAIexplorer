## Overview
The DOBOT AI Teaching Kit is an add-on for the Magician Lite robot arm. RobotShop says it covers text recognition, image recognition, voice recognition, and face recognition, with online step-by-step tutorials. The kit contents are not itemized on the pages read. It suits middle and high school classes that already own the arm.

## Benefits for students
- Students try four kinds of AI recognition on a real robot arm.
- Students follow step-by-step tutorials at their own pace.
- Students see recognition lead to physical movement.

## Benefits for teachers
- Online tutorials guide the lessons.
- The kit adds AI topics to an arm the school may already use.
- The Dobot site also lists a Basic AI Kit and a Vision Kit as compatible accessories.

## Benefits for schools
- RobotShop lists the kit at $120 as of 2026-10-16.
- The kit needs a Magician Lite arm, which is a separate purchase.
- Software needs were not stated. IT should ask the vendor before buying.

## Getting started
- [ ] Confirm that the school has a Dobot Magician Lite arm.
- [ ] Find the DOBOT AI Teaching Kit at RobotShop or School Specialty.
- [ ] Ask the vendor what software and computers the kit needs.
- [ ] Attach the kit to the arm.
- [ ] Open the online tutorials.
- [ ] Try the text recognition tutorial first.
- [ ] Move on to image, voice, and face recognition.

## Where is the AI?
The AI covers text recognition, image recognition, speech recognition, and face recognition. The vendor materials do not say clearly where the AI runs. Students follow tutorials to set up each recognition task and then connect it to the arm's movement.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need or whether the kit works offline. The Dobot privacy policy says the DobotLab and DobotLink software collect no personally identifiable information. The policy also says face and ID recognition features send encrypted image data to a third party cloud service. The research did not confirm whether this applies to the AI Teaching Kit. A teacher should ask the vendor which features use a cloud service and be careful with face and voice recordings of students.
