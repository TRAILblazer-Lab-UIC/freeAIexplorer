## Overview
This is a short teacher proposal posted on itch.io. The author suggests that teachers build model games in Twine. Each student then builds a Twine game around a very short passage of a poem. The games are shared and can become the basis for an essay.

## Benefits for students
- Students read a few lines of a poem very closely.
- Students turn their reading into choices and links in a game.
- Students can use the game as the base for an essay.

## Benefits for teachers
- The idea is simple and easy to adapt.
- Teachers build model games first and share them as examples.
- The page is free and the assets carry a Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 4.0 license.

## Benefits for schools
- The proposal is listed as free on itch.io.

- The page gives no lesson steps, poem, grade level, or duration.

## Where is the AI?
The materials describe no AI. Twine games follow links that students write. Students build logic and sequencing skills as they decide what choices a reader can make.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. Twine itself can run in a browser without an account. A teacher should check how students will save and share their games before class.

## Ideas by subject
### ela
1. Read a four line passage aloud and mark the words that stand out.
2. Build a Twine game where each choice explores one image in the passage.
3. Play a classmate's game and write what it shows about the poem.
4. Add a passage that changes the meaning of one line and discuss the effect.
5. Turn the game into an outline for a short essay.
