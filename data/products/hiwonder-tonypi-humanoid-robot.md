## Overview
TonyPi is an 18 degree of freedom humanoid robot built on a Raspberry Pi. Retail descriptions say it uses OpenCV for vision with a camera on a 2 axis head. The TonyPi Pro adds ChatGPT for semantic understanding and visual scene description. Kits come with Pi 4B or Pi 5 options. The vendor does not state a grade range, and the robot suits advanced high school students.

## Benefits for students
- Students program a humanoid that walks and moves its head.
- Students run Python vision examples on a Raspberry Pi.
- Students on the Pro version can try voice chat with the robot.

## Where is the AI?
The AI covers computer vision, object tracking, and in the Pro version language model dialogue and voice interaction. Vision runs on the device. The chat features likely use a cloud service. Students write code that makes the robot respond to what it sees.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not say whether an account is needed or whether the internet is required. Basic vision is described as running on the robot. The Pro version's ChatGPT features call an outside model. Voice, text, or camera content may go to a third party. The research did not confirm whether an API key is needed. No privacy policy was reviewed. A teacher should check this and keep the chat features off until it is clear.
