## Overview
This printable PDF is from the ACARA Digital Technologies in focus project. It holds a set of cut out cards that define six computational thinking terms. They are abstraction, algorithms, decomposition, generalisation, pattern recognition, and models and simulations. Each card gives a one line definition. The PDF has definitions only and no classroom prompts.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn six key terms in plain words.
- Students can use the cards as a word wall or a sorting game.
- Students see the same terms across many subjects.

## Benefits for teachers
- The cards are short and easy to cut out.
- A companion set of activity cards with prompts for F-2 is a separate PDF.
- The cards link to the Australian Curriculum Digital Technologies.

## Benefits for schools
- The PDF is free.
- It needs only a printer and scissors.
- No devices or accounts are needed.

## Where is the AI?
These cards have no AI. They build computational thinking vocabulary. Students learn the words that describe how people solve problems for computers.

## Privacy and safety
The cards run on paper, and no student data is collected. No account is needed, and the internet is used only to download the PDF. The grade range is not stated. A teacher should choose the terms that fit the class. The PDF holds definitions and no prompts. Plan your own questions.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Match each card to an example from daily life, such as a recipe for an algorithm.
2. Sort the cards from easiest to hardest and explain the order.
3. Break a class task into smaller parts to practice decomposition.
4. Find a pattern in a set of shapes and name the card that fits.
5. Write your own example for each of the six terms.
