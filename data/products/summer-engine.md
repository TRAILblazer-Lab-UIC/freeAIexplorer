## Overview
Summer Engine is a Godot 4 game engine for 2D and 3D games. It markets itself as the AI game engine. Users describe a game in plain language, and the built-in AI or an outside agent builds scenes, scripts, and assets as a normal project on disk. The desktop app is free and the agent tools are open source under MIT.

## Benefits for students
- Students describe a game and see it take shape.
- Students can edit the scripts and scenes the AI makes.
- Games can export to Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android, and the web.

## Benefits for teachers
- Projects are standard Godot 4 files.
- A command line tool and MCP let outside agents help.
- No classroom documentation was found.

## Benefits for schools
- The desktop app is free with unlimited projects.
- Hosted AI needs an account and paid credits.
- The privacy policy says the product is intended for users 18 and older. School use needs vendor agreement.

## Where is the AI?
The AI generates code, builds scenes, and creates assets from plain language. It runs in the vendor's cloud. Students describe a game, read the scripts the AI writes, and then edit them. The hosted AI needs an account and paid credits.

## Privacy and safety
Accounts store a name, email, password, usage and device data and IP address. Prompts and code snippets go to third party model providers. The policy says Summer Engine is intended for users 18 and older and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Free accounts cannot turn on Privacy Mode. Prompts and code may be used for training by default. The website uses Google Analytics cookies. A teacher should not use it with students under 18 without agreement from the vendor.

3. Find a bug in generated code and fix it.
