## Overview
Transkribus is a handwritten text recognition platform run by the European cooperative READ-COOP. Users upload scans of documents and the platform produces transcriptions. Users can also train their own recognition models. Universities use it in workshops, and the Transkribus Learn platform helps teachers show students how to read and transcribe handwriting.

## Benefits for students
- Students read historical handwriting that is hard to decipher.
- Students can check a machine transcription against the original.
- Older students can train a model on a set of documents.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can bring primary sources into history lessons.
- Transkribus Learn is built for teaching transcription.
- A Leiden project is building open materials for secondary schools.

## Benefits for schools
- A free account with credits is offered. Paid plans exist, and the vendor price is unknown here.
- A scholarship programme gives free credits to research projects.
- The privacy policy excludes users under 16, which affects most K-12 use.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find Transkribus on the READ-COOP website.
- [ ] Ask IT and school leaders to review the privacy policy and the under 16 rule.
- [ ] Create a teacher account.
- [ ] Upload a scan of a historical document.
- [ ] Run recognition and review the transcription.
- [ ] Look at Transkribus Learn for teaching ideas.
- [ ] Use the platform only with students who meet the age rule.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a handwritten text recognition model that runs on the vendor's cloud. Users can also train custom models on their own documents. Students upload scans, read the machine result, and correct it by hand. The corrections show how the model can improve.

## Privacy and safety
An account is needed. Uploaded scans, recognised text, trained models, and usage data go to the vendor's servers in Austria and Germany. The policy names analytics tools PostHog and Sentry. It does not say whether uploads train other models and it does not specify retention for web and desktop data. An on-premises option keeps data local. The policy says the services are not intended for anyone under 16. Check the policy and the age rule with school leaders. Upload only documents that are safe to share.

## Ideas by subject
### social_studies
1. Transcribe a short historical letter and compare it with a printed version.
2. Find places where the machine misread a word and explain why.
3. Study a primary source and write what it tells about daily life.
4. Compare two handwriting styles from different periods.
5. Discuss how machine transcription could open archives to more people.

### ela
1. Read a handwritten page and write the transcription by hand first.
2. Check the machine transcription and mark each error.
3. Write a short summary of a transcribed letter.
4. Discuss how spelling and handwriting have changed over time.
5. Explain how a model learns to read handwriting.
