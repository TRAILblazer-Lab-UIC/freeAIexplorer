## Overview
In this literacy lesson, students program a micro:bit badge to show a story character's feelings, moods, or traits. The badge uses the LED display and sound. The page offers three levels called Mild, Medium, and Spicy. Students describe a character, change an existing program, or write their own. The lesson targets ages 7 to 11.

## Benefits for students
- Students show a character's feelings with an image and a sound.
- Students pick the level that suits them.
- Students link reading and coding.

## Benefits for teachers
- The lesson links to California CCSS ELA RL.3.3 and several California CS standards.
- A recording sheet is offered in English and Spanish.
- Slides and a lesson zip are provided.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson is free. A micro:bit board is a paid purchase.
- Sound needs micro:bit V2 boards. Original V1 boards have no speaker.
- The editor runs in a browser.

## Where is the AI?
This lesson has no AI. Students write a program that shows an image and plays a sound. They build skills in sequencing and logic.

## Privacy and safety
The pages read do not discuss data handling, and they did not require a sign in. The code runs on the micro:bit, and the MakeCode editor runs in a browser or the offline app. The internet is used for setup. The research read no privacy policy for this item. A teacher should check the MakeCode privacy terms, the volume of the sounds, and where students save their programs.

## Ideas by subject
### ela
1. Describe a story character with three feeling words.
2. Choose an image and a sound that show one of the feelings.
3. Find a line in the text that supports the choice.
4. Explain to a partner why the badge fits the character.
5. Compare how two students show the same character.

### computer_science
1. Open an existing program and change the image.
2. Add a sound block to the program.
3. Use a button press to switch between two feelings.
4. Write a program from scratch.
5. Test and fix the program with a partner.
