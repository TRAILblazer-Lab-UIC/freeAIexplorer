## Overview
This three day 5E inquiry lesson comes from The Science Teacher, by James Newland and Sissy Wong of the University of Houston. Students in small groups measure their pulse with an open hardware pulse sensor on an Arduino Uno. They plot the signal in a spreadsheet and calculate heart rate. The main goal is to explain how sensors analyze wave phenomena.

## Benefits for students
- Students build a working sensor circuit.
- Students plot a real signal and calculate heart rate.
- Students connect wave ideas to a body measurement.

## Benefits for teachers
- It follows a clear 5E plan over three days.
- It was piloted in two physics classes.
- The article gives advice on data privacy and opt out options.

## Benefits for schools
- The cost is about 40 dollars per group.
- The lesson needs an Arduino Uno, LED, breadboard, resistor, pulse sensor, and laptop.
- It is not iPad compatible.

## Where is the AI?
This lesson has no AI. It builds data skills, such as reading a signal, plotting it, and calculating a rate. Students also practice working with sensors.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the pulse readings are body data. The readings stay on the group laptop and spreadsheet. Google Sheets is one named option, and it would send the data to Google. The article advises letting students opt out, grouping so no student is the only data source, and using titles that do not identify anyone. A teacher should follow that advice and choose the spreadsheet tool with care.

## Ideas by subject
### science
1. Measure resting pulse and plot the signal in a spreadsheet.
2. Count the peaks and calculate heart rate.
3. Compare pulse before and after light exercise.
4. Explain how a sensor reads a wave.
5. Write a conclusion that uses the data as evidence.

### engineering
1. Wire the pulse sensor to the Arduino Uno and LED.
2. Test the circuit and fix any loose connections.
3. Load the PulseSensor Playground code and run it.
4. Compare readings from two sensors on one student.
5. Suggest one change to improve the setup.
