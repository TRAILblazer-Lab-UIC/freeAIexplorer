## Overview
Teddy Is Lost is an unplugged activity from Idaho National Laboratory, listed for grades K-12. One student acts as the programmer and another acts as the robot. The programmer uses arrow cards to guide the robot across a taped grid to a teddy bear. The robot must avoid other stuffed animals. The official page returned an error. ## Where is the AI?
This activity has no AI. It builds sequencing and the use of commands. Students write a path of arrow cards and watch a human robot follow it.

## Privacy and safety
The activity runs on a taped floor grid. No student data is collected. The materials do not say whether the site needs an account or how the internet is used. A teacher should check the page terms. A teacher should also keep the floor clear of trip hazards, especially around the tape.
