## Overview
AI+ME is a free online course of about one hour for elementary-age students. AI4K12 lists it as the first publicly available course to teach the Five Big Ideas in AI. It is hosted by ReadyAI at edu.readyai.org. The catalog page shows only a course card. The current content was not seen.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn AI basics at their own pace.
- Students meet the Five Big Ideas in AI.
- The course is short at about one hour.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course aligns to the AI4K12 Five Big Ideas.
- It is self-paced and needs little preparation.
- A separate AI and ME book is sold.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free.
- It needs only a browser and internet.
- A teacher can set it up. The vendor does not say whether a login is needed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Open the AI+ME course at edu.readyai.org.
- [ ] Check whether a login is needed.
- [ ] Take the course yourself first.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the site for student devices.
- [ ] Plan one hour of class time.
- [ ] Review the privacy terms of the vendor.
- [ ] Pick a short talk to follow the course.

## Where is the AI?
The course teaches AI literacy and the Five Big Ideas in AI. It runs in the vendor's cloud as an online course. Students work through the course and use what they learn to explain AI in their own words.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need. Any student data goes to the vendor. The course page itself was not opened. Details are unknown. A teacher should check whether a login is required and read the privacy terms before students join.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Name the Five Big Ideas in AI and give one example of each.
2. Sort objects into those that use AI and those that do not.
3. Explain in a sentence how a computer learns from examples.
4. Draw a picture of how a machine sees the world.
5. Share one thing the course taught you with a younger student.
