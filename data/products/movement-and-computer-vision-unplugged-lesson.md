## Overview
This Model AI Assignment is a three-day lesson on basic computer vision and visual AI. It has separate plans for middle school and high school. Students watch a live Quick Draw demo and try short activities about AI weaknesses and ethics. Middle school students program AI behaviors in Scratch with pre-made models, and high school students build a simple custom model.

## Benefits for students
- Students see how a computer vision system can be fooled by adversarial data.
- Students use body, hand, and face tracking blocks in Scratch.
- High school students train a custom model and import it into a Scratch game.

## Benefits for teachers
- The day plans are free to download.
- Separate middle school and high school plans save planning time.
- Optional Teachable Machine variants give extra choices.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson costs nothing.
- It needs only a browser and an internet connection.
- IT involvement is not expected, although the page recommends prior Scratch knowledge.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Watch the Quick Draw demo and guess how the AI decides what a drawing shows.
2. Try an activity with adversarial data and describe how the AI got fooled.
3. Build a Scratch project that reacts to body movement with pose blocks.
4. Test a pre-made model on different faces and discuss where it makes mistakes.
5. Train a simple custom model, import it into a Scratch game, and describe what it learned.
