## Overview
This is a free online course for teachers and school leaders from Raspberry Pi. It takes one to two hours. It has three sections on what AI and AI literacy are, ethics and AI in the workplace, and how to teach AI literacy. A certificate unlocks after completion.

## Benefits for students
- Students gain from teachers who understand AI basics.
- Students gain from lessons on generative AI use.
- Students gain from a school AI policy that staff write with care.

## Benefits for teachers
- The course covers AI and machine learning basics.
- It covers ethics, safety, and social impact.
- Teachers get a downloadable certificate.

## Benefits for schools
- The course is free.
- Leaders can learn how to write a school AI policy.
- No IT work is needed. The platform was labeled beta in search results.

## Where is the AI?
The course teaches AI and machine learning basics and how to teach them. It runs on the vendor's cloud as an online course. The course sits in the Experience AI programme built with Google DeepMind, per a search snippet. Teachers learn the ideas and use them in their own lessons.

## Privacy and safety
A login is required to take the course on the Foundation training hub, and the internet is needed at all times. The platform stores teacher account details with the vendor. The course is for adults and has no student component. The research did not read the privacy policy. A teacher should read the vendor privacy policy before creating an account.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Explain the difference between AI and machine learning in your own words.
2. Train a small model in a class activity and describe how data shapes it.
3. List three places AI appears in daily life.
4. Discuss the ethics of an AI tool that predicts student grades.
5. Draft a class guide on how to use generative AI well.
