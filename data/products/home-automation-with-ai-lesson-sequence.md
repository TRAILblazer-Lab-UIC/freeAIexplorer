## Overview
This is a free lesson sequence for Years 5 to 6 and Years 7 to 8 on how AI can run a smart home. Students sketch floor plans and talk about input, decision, and output. Then they use the MyComputerbrain tool in a browser to train a small neural network. No coding is required.

## Benefits for students
- Students link a familiar setting, the home, to how AI makes decisions.
- Students train a network first with given data and then with their own.
- Students talk about confidence levels and weights.

## Benefits for teachers
- The lesson has two difficulty levels for different learners.
- Part of the lesson is unplugged and needs only paper.
- The lesson maps to the Australian Digital Technologies curriculum.

## Benefits for schools
- The lesson is free.
- It runs in a browser. The research did not confirm whether it needs the internet after the page loads.
- Optional output activities use an Arduino, Raspberry Pi, or micro:bit that the school may own.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is a small neural network in the MyComputerbrain tool. It runs in the browser on the local computer. Students give it training data for four outputs, watch it learn, and then test how sure it is. They can then change the data and see how the answers change.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed. The page says the AI runs locally in the browser and student input is not stored. Reloading resets the experiment. The research did not read the MyComputerbrain site or its privacy terms. It also did not confirm whether the tool needs the internet after the page loads. A teacher should check the tool's privacy terms and have students use made up home examples with no real details about their own houses.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Sketch a smart home floor plan and label the inputs and outputs.
2. Train the network with the provided data and record what it chooses.
3. Make your own training data for four outputs and test the result.
4. Explain what a confidence level tells you about an answer.
5. Change one training example and describe how the network responds.
