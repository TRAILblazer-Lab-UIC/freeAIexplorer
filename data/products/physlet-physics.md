## Overview
Physlet Physics is a collection of interactive illustrations, explorations, and problems for introductory physics. It is the Physlet Physics 3E textbook by Wolfgang Christian and Mario Belloni, hosted by ComPADRE. It has 39 chapters across mechanics, fluids, waves, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, circuits, and optics. Students open a page and work through the illustrations.

## Benefits for students
- Students interact with each physics idea and watch it respond.
- Students can use exploration worksheets.
- Students have 39 chapters to choose from.

## Benefits for teachers
- Teachers can assign a chapter that matches the unit.
- No install or account is listed.
- The materials suit introductory high school physics.

## Benefits for schools
- The material is free for noncommercial use.
- It runs in a browser that has JavaScript turned on.
- Paid eBook versions exist, and the prices were not read.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor does not describe any AI in Physlet Physics. The illustrations run in the browser. Students build data and pattern spotting skills as they change a setting and watch the result.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which accounts students need, and no sign-in was shown. No privacy policy was read. The research did not confirm what data the site collects. The pages need an internet connection at all times. A teacher should check the ComPADRE privacy terms before using the site with students.
