## Overview
Autodesk Fusion for Education is a design tool that combines CAD and CAM in one program. Qualifying students and educators can get it free through a renewable one-year education license. It suits high school engineering and career and technical classes. Students model parts and prepare them for manufacturing.

## Benefits for students
- Students design 3D parts and plan how those parts could be made.
- Students practice with the same kind of tool that manufacturing teams use.
- Students keep one workflow for modeling and for machine preparation.

## Benefits for teachers
- The education license costs nothing and renews each year.
- One program covers design and fabrication lessons.
- Teachers can build projects that fit manufacturing pathways.

## Benefits for schools
- The license is free for qualifying students and educators.
- Autodesk checks enrollment through a third party service called SheerID.
- The notes say the license installs only on personal devices. The research could not verify that. IT should confirm the terms with Autodesk before district approval.

## Getting started
- [ ] Go to the Autodesk education pages and read the license terms.
- [ ] Ask IT to review the terms before any district approval.
- [ ] Create an Autodesk account for each user.
- [ ] Verify education status through the SheerID check.
- [ ] Download Fusion for Windows or Mac and sign in.
- [ ] Confirm which devices the license allows.
- [ ] Start with one small part design as a test.

## Where is the AI?
The vendor materials reviewed do not describe any AI in this tool. Students use it to model and prepare parts by hand. The tool runs as a desktop app that connects to the Autodesk cloud.

## Privacy and safety
An account is needed and the internet is needed at all times. The research could not read the Autodesk privacy statement or product page. Account data and design files going to the Autodesk cloud comes from batch notes and is unverified. Autodesk has a minimum age rule and the research did not confirm the value. A third party named SheerID checks enrollment, according to a search snippet. A teacher should read the Autodesk privacy terms and confirm the minimum age.

## Ideas by subject
### engineering
1. Model a simple bracket and test how it fits a real part the class owns.
2. Redesign a classroom object and explain each design choice in a short write-up.
3. Create a drawing of a part with measurements for a partner to review.
4. Design a phone stand and compare two versions on size and strength.
5. Plan a manufacturing path for a part and list the steps in order.

### cte
1. Build a part that fits a manufacturing pathway lesson and name the machine that could make it.
2. Model a custom tool holder for a shop and present it to the class.
3. Prepare a part for machining and describe each setting chosen.
4. Compare a first design to a revised design and note what improved.
5. Create a small portfolio of three designs for a career fair display.
