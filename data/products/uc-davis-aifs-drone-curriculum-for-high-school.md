## Overview
This is a free high school course on agricultural drones created by high school teachers in a UC Davis AIFS fellowship. The GitBook lists 7 modules. Modules 1 to 4 cover careers, drone basics, safety regulations, manual flight, and programmed flight. Modules 5 and 6 cover mission planning and image processing, and module 7 is a Supervised Agricultural Experience project.

## Benefits for students
- Students learn to fly a drone manually and by program.
- Students plan a flight mission and process images.
- Students finish with a research or business project.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each module has slide lectures, guided notes, quizzes, and hands-on activities.
- Six California teachers designed the modules after training in summer 2022, according to a search snippet.
- AIFS says a September 2025 workshop prepares teachers.

## Benefits for schools
- AIFS describes the course as free, according to search snippets.
- Drones and image processing software are an extra cost.
- AIFS also runs a free High School Summer AI Tech Camp.

## Ideas by subject
### cte
1. Read about careers in agricultural drones and write a short summary.
2. Compare how a farmer might use a drone with other farm tools.
3. Plan a field survey mission for a school garden.
4. Fly the mission and record what the drone saw.
5. Plan a Supervised Agricultural Experience project that uses a drone.

### engineering
1. Describe the main parts of a drone and what each does.
2. Practice a manual flight pattern in a safe area.
3. Program a short flight path and test it.
4. Plan a mission and list the steps in order.
5. Compare two flight plans and pick the better one.
