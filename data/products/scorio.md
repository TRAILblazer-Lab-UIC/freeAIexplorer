## Overview
Scorio is an online music notation service. Users enter notes with a mouse or a MIDI keyboard in a browser editor. Scores are saved on scorio.com. Mobile apps exist for iPad and Android, and the site is largely in German.

## Benefits for students
- Students write real music notation in a browser.
- Students can enter notes with a MIDI keyboard.
- Students keep their scores online and can return to them.

## Benefits for teachers
- A free Test account lets a teacher try the editor.
- A quiz editor is available in beta.
- The interface is mostly German. Expect to translate some labels.

## Benefits for schools
- The free Test plan allows 5 scores and 4 parts with no import or export.
- Pro costs 4.99 EUR per month and Unlimited costs 14.99 EUR per month.
- The editor runs in a browser on Chromebook, Windows, Mac, and Linux.

## Getting started
- [ ] Register once on scorio.com for a free Test account.
- [ ] Open the editor and enter a few notes with the mouse.
- [ ] Connect a MIDI keyboard if one is available.
- [ ] Check which features need a paid plan, such as MIDI and MusicXML import and export.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the site and the student accounts.
- [ ] Review the privacy terms before students sign up.

## Where is the AI?
Scorio has no AI. It is a notation editor. Students build pattern skills as they read and write rhythms and melodies.

## Privacy and safety
An account is required, and the internet is needed at all times. Scores are saved on Scorio servers. The site links a privacy policy in German that the research did not read. The materials do not state the minimum age or what student data is collected. A teacher should read the privacy terms with IT and consider using a teacher-made account.

## Ideas by subject
### music
1. Write an eight-bar melody in the editor and play it back.
2. Enter notes with a MIDI keyboard and compare the result with the sheet music.
3. Copy a simple tune from a printed score and check each bar.
4. Add a second part to a melody and listen to how the parts fit.
5. Save a score and share it with the class for feedback.
