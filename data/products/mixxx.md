## Overview
Mixxx is free, open source DJ mixing software for digital music files. It offers BPM and key detection, sync lock across four decks, chainable effects, and mappings for MIDI and HID DJ controllers. It also supports timecode vinyl control. Students load songs and mix them into their own compositions.

## Benefits for students
- Students blend songs and learn about tempo and key.
- Students try effects and build a mix.
- Students can use a DJ controller or a mouse.

## Benefits for teachers
- A teacher resource page says students load several songs and mix them into their own compositions.
- The page is promotional.
- The interface is easy for students to learn.

## Benefits for schools
- The software is free, and there is no paid pro version.
- It runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux and on Raspberry Pi.
- Installing needs administrator rights. IT approval may be needed.

## Getting started
- [ ] Download the installer from mixxx.org.
- [ ] Ask IT to approve the install.
- [ ] Add a music library of files the school may use.
- [ ] Connect headphones to each computer.
- [ ] Set up the controller mapping if you use a DJ controller.
- [ ] Try a two song mix before class.

## Where is the AI?
This tool has no AI. It detects tempo and key with built-in analysis, and the vendor does not describe this as AI. Students build skills in pattern spotting, such as matching beats.

## Privacy and safety
No account is needed, and the app is a local desktop program. The materials state no student data collection. The privacy policy link on the homepage returned a 404 error. The research did not read a policy. The research also did not confirm whether the app sends any telemetry. A teacher should ask IT to check network activity and confirm that music files are properly licensed. A third-party review calls controller setup a drawback.

## Ideas by subject
### music
1. Mix two songs with the same tempo and describe the blend.
2. Use the sync lock to match beats across two decks.
3. Add an effect to a track and describe how the sound changes.
4. Build a three minute mix with a clear start and end.
5. Present the mix and explain the choices of songs.
