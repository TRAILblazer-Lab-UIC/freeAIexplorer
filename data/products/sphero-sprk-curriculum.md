## Overview
This is a free Orbotix curriculum for Sphero robots, covered in a May 2014 Getting Smart article. The article describes six lessons on rate and time, 2D geometry, percentages, and basic programming. Each lesson has a teacher guide, a student guide, and a worksheet in PDF and Word. Students program Sphero from an iOS or Android app, using MacroLab for some lessons.

## Benefits for students
- Students practice math ideas by driving a robot.
- Students write simple programs for Sphero.
- Students use a worksheet to record what they learn.

## Benefits for teachers
- Each lesson has a teacher guide, a student guide, and a worksheet.
- The article claims Common Core alignment.
- A search snippet says the grade range is 3 to 5.

## Benefits for schools
- The curriculum was free in 2014.
- Sphero robots cost extra. The article did not price them.
- Sphero has since released newer lessons, and the 2014 files may not match current apps.

## Where is the AI?
This curriculum has no AI. Students give the robot direction and speed commands. They build sequencing skills as they plan each move.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state whether accounts are needed. They do not say what data the apps collect. A teacher should check the current Sphero app privacy details with the school before use. A teacher should also check that the old lesson files work with the current app.

## Ideas by subject
### math
1. Use rate and time to predict how far the Sphero will roll.
2. Program the robot to drive a square and discuss the angles.
3. Find a percentage of a full speed setting and test it.
4. Measure the robot's path and compare it with the plan.
5. Draw a shape on the floor and program the Sphero to trace it.

### computer_science
1. Write a short program that rolls the Sphero forward.
2. Add a turn and a pause to the program.
3. Use MacroLab to run a set of commands in order.
4. Change one command and note what changes.
5. Explain your program to a partner.
