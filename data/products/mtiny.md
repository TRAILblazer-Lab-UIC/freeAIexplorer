## Overview
mTiny is a cube shaped USB-rechargeable robot programmed with physical cards and a tap pen. Children order cards that start with a green flag and end with a stop icon, then tap them with the pen. Hidden dot patterns in the ink tell the robot its instructions and which map tile it is on. Makeblock suggests ages four and up.

## Benefits for students
- Children program a robot by tapping cards with a pen.
- Children learn that code runs in order from start to stop.
- Children play with map blocks, a storybook, and animal masks.

## Benefits for teachers
- The kit includes 36 coding cards based on ScratchJr graphics.
- It has 24 reversible map blocks and game cards.
- No screen or app is needed.

## Benefits for schools
- A RobotShop page lists the current Discover Kit at 229.99 USD.
- Older kits were retired, according to snippets.
- The robot charges over USB.

## Getting started
- [ ] Find the mTiny Discover Kit at a retailer.
- [ ] Charge the robot with the USB cable.
- [ ] Lay out the map blocks.
- [ ] Arrange coding cards from the green flag to the stop icon.
- [ ] Tap the cards with the pen.
- [ ] Let the robot run the program.
- [ ] Try the storybook and game cards.

## Where is the AI?
mTiny has no AI. The robot reads the dot patterns in the card ink and follows them in order. Children build sequencing skills.

## Privacy and safety
The robot reads dots in card ink and a reviewer found no companion app. The research found no account needs, no internet need, and no student data collection. The research did not review a privacy policy. A teacher should check the state of the kit before buying. Older kits were retired.
