## Overview
Books and Bots is a Nevada Robotics curriculum that pairs picture books with VEX 123 robot lessons. It serves PreK to grade 5. Search results say the lessons teach literacy and computer science and meet Nevada standards. Each lesson has a School-Home Connection handout. In one kindergarten lesson, children touch-code a VEX 123 robot across letters on a field.

## Benefits for students
- Students hear a story and then code a robot that acts it out.
- Students practice letters and words through robot movement.
- Students take home a handout to share with family.

## Benefits for teachers
- DRI runs hands-on teacher trainings in several Nevada towns.
- Trained teachers can access a lending library.
- Lessons use buttons and cards, which keep coding simple.

## Benefits for schools
- The price of the curriculum was not found.
- Schools need VEX 123 robots. The lending library may help.
- The robots need no internet.

## Where is the AI?
This curriculum has no AI. It builds an AI-related skill, which is sequencing. Children press buttons and place cards to give the robot an ordered list of moves.

## Privacy and safety
The VEX 123 robots run on their own and need no internet. Batch notes say touch coding needs no account and collects no student data. The research could not check this. The official pages blocked direct access. A teacher should confirm this with the program and check that the handouts do not ask families to share personal details. Keep robots on a flat surface and away from table edges.

## Ideas by subject
### ela
1. Listen to a picture book and retell the story in order.
2. Touch-code a VEX 123 robot across letters on a field.
3. Spell a short word by driving the robot to each letter.
4. Pick a favorite scene and move the robot to show it.
5. Tell a family member about the story using the handout.

### computer_science
1. Press the robot's buttons to make it move forward and turn.
2. Place coding cards in order and run them.
3. Debug a card sequence that sends the robot the wrong way.
4. Predict where the robot will stop before it runs.
5. Work with a partner and take turns coding.
