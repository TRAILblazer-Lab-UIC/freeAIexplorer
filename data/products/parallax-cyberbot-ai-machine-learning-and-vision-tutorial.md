## Overview
Parallax publishes a tutorial series on AI, machine learning, and vision for the cyber:bot robot. It comes in a MakeCode block edition and a Python edition. The series builds around the HUSKYLENS AI camera. A separate course teaches students to train the HUSKYLENS to recognize colors, and a Gatekeeper Robot project uses face recognition. The official pages read were empty. Details come from search snippets.

## Benefits for students
- Students train a camera to recognize colors and faces.
- Students choose block coding or Python.
- Students build a Gatekeeper Robot.

## Benefits for teachers
- The tutorials are free per discovery notes.
- Both MakeCode and Python editions exist.
- Tutorials also exist for the Shield-Bot, according to a search snippet.

## Benefits for schools
- The tutorials are free. The robot and camera hardware are extra, and prices were not checked.
- The camera runs recognition on the device.
- The materials do not state account needs. IT should check.

## Where is the AI?
The AI is object, face, and color recognition with on-camera model training. It runs on the HUSKYLENS camera. Students train the camera with examples and then write code that reacts to what it sees.

## Privacy and safety
The materials do not state which tools or accounts students need. The camera is a separate device that does the recognition. The official pages were empty, and the research could not confirm any data handling. The face recognition project would capture student faces. A teacher should check the software the tutorials use and keep face recognition projects to willing student volunteers.

## Ideas by subject
### computer_science
1. Train the camera on three colors and test how often it is right.
2. Write a program that shows the recognized color on a display.
3. Compare the MakeCode version with the Python version of the same task.
4. Train the camera on a face and test it in different light.
5. Describe how the camera learns from examples in a short note.

### engineering
1. Mount the camera on the cyber:bot and test the view.
2. Build a Gatekeeper Robot that reacts to a known face.
3. Adjust the camera angle and compare the recognition.
4. Wire the camera to the robot and write a connection guide.
5. Test the robot on a course with colored markers.
